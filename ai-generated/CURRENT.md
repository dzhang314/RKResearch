## Improved ERK lower bounds over arbitrary rings

The [paid Beta-moment argument](lower-bounds/paid-beta/01-PAID-GRADE-FIVE-SURPLUS.md) raises the arbitrary-ring ERK floors at orders 10 through 17 by at least one stage. At order 12 the [full paid proof](lower-bounds/paid-beta/05-PAID-ORDER-TWELVE.md) raises the floor to 18, matching characteristic zero. At order 14, a [separate paid argument](lower-bounds/paid-beta/04-PAID-ORDER-FOURTEEN.md) raises the floor further to 21, matching characteristic zero. A [finite paid-field argument](lower-bounds/paid-beta/03-PAID-ORDER-EIGHTEEN-NINETEEN.md) gives 27 and 29 stages at orders 18 and 19, two more than the earlier arbitrary-ring bounds. [Its doubling extension](lower-bounds/paid-beta/02-PAID-DOUBLING-SURPLUS.md) raises order 20 from 28 to 30. These are mathematical proofs over all nonzero commutative unital rings using the denominator-cleared order equations; the ordered-field and characteristic-zero columns do not change.

The [terminal detector argument](lower-bounds/paid-beta/06-ODD-EXPLICIT-STAGE-BOUND.md) gives the uniform lower bound \(3d+2\) for every odd explicit order \(2d+1\) with \(d\ge6\). It raises the arbitrary-ring floors at orders 13, 15, and 17 to 20, 23, and 26, respectively. The characteristic-zero floors at orders 13 and 15 also rise to 20 and 23. The odd-order argument does not change the ordered-field bounds at 13, 15 or 17.

The [weighted extension](lower-bounds/paid-beta/07-WEIGHTED-DETECTOR-STAGE-BOUND.md) and its [order-eleven refinement](lower-bounds/paid-beta/08-ORDER-ELEVEN-THRESHOLD.md) prove a uniform \(\lceil3p/2\rceil\) lower bound for ordinary explicit methods at every order \(p\ge11\), with coefficients in any nonzero commutative unital ring. Order 11 now needs at least 17 stages in every coefficient domain; the arbitrary-ring order-16 floor rises to 24. Some orders have stronger separate bounds. These results are not yet formalized in Lean.

The [separated-rank refinement](lower-bounds/paid-beta/separated-pure/README.md) gives 27, 28, 30, and 31 stages at orders 17–20 over every characteristic-zero field. The bounds of 30 and 31 at orders 19 and 20 also hold over every nonzero commutative ring. The stronger ordered-field bounds, where applicable, are unchanged.

The [shift-one](research/WEIGHTED-ORDER-NINE-SHIFT-ONE-ATLAS.md) and [shift-two](research/WEIGHTED-ORDER-NINE-SINGLE-OFF.md) weighted order-nine theorems each give at least **13 stages** over any characteristic-zero field. The censuses are mathematical proofs with separate kernel-checked scalar gates on Lean 4.34.1, 4.35.0, 4.36.0 and 4.37.0-rc1. The resulting [order-eleven cutoff](research/ERK11-AFTER-TWO-WEIGHTED-NINE-FLOORS.md) now meets its weighted stage-size guard; the sharp dense-prefix case still prevents an ordinary ERK11 floor improvement. The [pure-rank-six sharp-prefix analysis](research/ERK11-SHARP-PREFIX-PURE-TAIL.md) excludes a nonendpoint final clock and gives a five-slot terminal pattern in its remaining endpoint case, with separate core Lean gates and a mathematical rooted-order bridge. The [rank-free cutoff](research/ERK11-RANK-FREE-FIRST-ELEVEN-CUTOFF.md) now rules out a genuine late read in every characteristic-zero equality case; only extension of the sharp dense size-six first-eleven chart remains as an ordinary lower-bound question. The [prefix moment](research/ERK11-SHARP-PREFIX-MOMENT-OBSTRUCTION.md) and [Jacobi](research/ERK11-ARBITRARY-PREFIX-JACOBI-GATES.md) arguments exclude the archived repaired prefix and further constrain arbitrary prefixes. The [two-generator endpoint reduction](research/ERK11-TWO-GENERATOR-ENDPOINT-TAIL.md) identifies the final six physical rows in the maximal-rank case and yields an [all-live refinement](research/ERK11-ALL-LIVE-ENDPOINT-REDUCTION.md) and [adjoint support constraints](research/ERK11-ADJOINT-PURE-DUALITY.md). Lean checks the scalar gates on all four tested versions; the tableau reduction is mathematical. The [weighted-transpose criterion](research/ERK11-ONE-SPARE-WEIGHTED-TRANSPOSE.md) and the [low-carrier Jacobi schedule](research/ERK11-LOW-CARRIER-CLEAN-JACOBI-SCHEDULE.md) further constrain the two half-clock and nonhalf branches, respectively.

## Improved DIRK lower bounds over arbitrary rings

The [paid grade-six proof](lower-bounds/paid-beta/DIRK-PAID-GRADE-SIX.md) raises the uniform arbitrary-ring floor by one for every order p≥12, to p + floor((p−1)/2) − 2. At orders 12 and 13 it gives 15 and 17, matching the characteristic-zero lower bounds. No diagonal sign, positivity or nonzero-diagonal restriction is imposed. At order 14, [a sharper proof](lower-bounds/paid-beta/DIRK-PAID-ORDER-FOURTEEN.md) raises the arbitrary-ring floor from 18 to 19. It uses stage chronology and the two paid ERK norms, bypassing the fixed reverse determinant. At orders 18 and 19, [a paid version of the grade-nine proof](lower-bounds/paid-beta/DIRK-PAID-ORDERS-EIGHTEEN-NINETEEN.md) raises the arbitrary-ring bounds to 25 and 27, matching characteristic zero. At order 20, [a flat-band and chronology argument](lower-bounds/paid-beta/DIRK-PAID-ORDER-TWENTY.md) raises the arbitrary-ring floor from 27 to 28. The characteristic-zero and ordered-field floor remains 29. The stronger paid floors have not yet been checked in Lean.

At order ten [eleven stages are optimal in 32 prime and prime-power families](research/dirk10-eleven-prime-families/README.md), starting with 59 and including 419. Two exact rooted-tree replays verify each residue-field tableau and a unit next-order defect. A Hensel argument using the exact algebraic tower gives the endpoint over the corresponding p-adic integers and over all nonzero rings whose finite characteristic is supported on the listed primes. It extends and preserves the earlier [separate characteristic-419 construction](research/causal-profile-reduction/dirk-order10-zmod419.md). Separately, the [exact real-algebraic tableau](research/causal-profile-reduction/dirk-order10-eleven-real-algebraic.md) attains 11 stages over a real number field of degree at most 18, hence over the reals and complex numbers. The full rooted replay is exact, and an eleventh-order star fails. The [real Lean certificate](research/causal-profile-reduction/dirk-order10-eleven-real-lean-certificate.md) proves both the eleven-stage witness and the matching lower bound on the reviewed order predicate in Lean/Mathlib 4.36.0. Over the rationals, [at least **12 stages** are necessary](research/causal-profile-reduction/dirk-order10-rational-lower-twelve.md). The proof combines the native DIRK equality theory, a rational Lobatto obstruction for multiple defect abscissae, and the [genus-two argument](research/causal-profile-reduction/dirk-order10-rational-single-clock-genus-two.md) for one defect abscissa. The full rational lower bound is mathematical, not end-to-end Lean. The [12-stage rational DIRK construction](research/dirk10-twelve-rational-three-ports/README.md) meets the lower bound, giving an **exact rational minimum of 12**, one more than over the reals. It uses three literal row merges. Independent exact checks cover all 1,205 rooted-tree equations and an eleventh-order failure; the tableau also works over every nonzero commutative ring inverting its coefficient denominator. The [upper bound and this specialization are checked end to end in Lean/Mathlib 4.36.0](research/dirk10-twelve-native-upper/README.md); the exact minimum still uses the separate mathematical lower-bound proof. The [two-tableau portfolio](research/dirk10-twelve-field-portfolio/README.md) extends the exact 12-stage upper to every admissible field, and every nonzero commutative local or finite-characteristic ring with 10! invertible. Its [second tableau and the entire coverage transfer are now Lean-checked](research/dirk10-twelve-alternate-native/README.md). A [separate 13-stage tableau](research/dirk10-factorial-thirteen-and-fourteen/README.md) gives exact order ten over every nonzero commutative ring inverting only 10!, reducing the previous uniform upper from 17. The minimum over the rational localization with those denominators is now **12–13**. A [compact version of this arbitrary-ring 13-stage construction](research/dirk10-factorial-thirteen-compact/README.md) is now checked end to end in Lean/Mathlib 4.36.0. A uniform 12-stage upper is still open. The [13-stage](research/dirk10-thirteen-rational-two-ports/README.md) and [14-stage](upper-bounds/rational/dirk-order-ten-overlap/README.md) rational constructions remain available; the latter uses [eight rational endpoint-anchored sites, the minimum for degree nine](research/rational-endpoint-moment-nine/README.md).

At order eleven, an [exact 14-stage real-algebraic DIRK tableau](research/dirk11-width14-real-reflected-shell/README.md) verifies the recorded 13–14 real interval. Its degree-at-most-16 real coefficient tower directly pays all 3,047 rooted conditions and a unit next-order defect. A [separate 14-stage witness in characteristic 43](research/dirk11-width14-f43-reflected-shell/README.md) achieves the same upper stage count in that characteristic. The real-algebraic upper is verified by exact arithmetic, not yet a native upper theorem.

[A complementary exact 12-stage rational DIRK10 witness](research/dirk10-twelve-five-extra-primes/README.md) has only five denominator primes outside 10!: 47, 83, 97, 199, and 647. It is checked end to end in Lean/Mathlib 4.36.0 wherever its denominator is a unit. This is not a 12-stage construction over the factorial localization.

## Exact explicit minima over nonzero commutative rings

- [Order five](research/erk-five-ring-witness/README.md): six stages are necessary and sufficient if 120 is a unit. Otherwise no stage count attains order five.
- [Order six](research/erk-six-ring-minimum/README.md): seven stages are necessary and sufficient if 720 is a unit. A single rational tableau specializes to every such ring, including positive characteristic. This supersedes the eight-stage construction as a minimum, not as a denominator-removal technique.

- [Order seven](research/erk-seven-factorial/README.md): nine stages are necessary and sufficient if 5040 is a unit. The new universal nine-stage tableau is checked against the reviewed Lean order predicate. The earlier [ten-stage tableau](research/erk-seven-ring-witness/README.md) remains a separate construction.

- [Order eight](research/erk8-universal-native-floor/README.md): **eleven stages are necessary and sufficient** over every nonzero commutative ring with 8! invertible. - [Order nine](research/erk9-fifteen-ten-clock/README.md): a **15-stage** tableau has exact order over every nonzero commutative ring with 9! invertible, improving the [16-stage universal construction](research/erk-nine-moment-splice/README.md). The [older rational chart](research/erk9-fifteen-native-upper/README.md), [local-ring theorem](research/erk9-fifteen-local-native/README.md), and [finite-characteristic theorem](research/erk9-fifteen-finite-native/README.md) are already checked end to end in Lean; the new tableau needs no choice of chart. All 486 conditions and exact order now have an [end-to-end Lean certificate](research/erk9-fourteen-native-upper/README.md). The real minimum remains 13.

- [Order ten](research/erk10-seventeen-half-port/README.md): **17 stages** suffice for exact order in every nonzero commutative ring with 10! invertible. An exact physical row merge enabled by a rank-five affine system improves the [18-stage](research/erk10-eighteen-ten-clock/README.md), [19-stage](research/erk10-nineteen-compressed-reverse/README.md), [20-stage](research/erk10-twenty-compressed-reverse/README.md), and [21-stage](research/erk-ten-moment-splice/README.md) constructions. - [Orders eleven through twenty](research/erk-signed-reverse/README.md): exact explicit upper bounds **22, 24, 30, 33, 39, 45, 51, 57, 64, and 70 stages**, respectively, with coefficients in any nonzero commutative ring in which the corresponding factorial is a unit. Signed copies cancel the reverse donor error without dividing by a quadrature weight. [Order twelve](research/erk12-twenty-four-forward-alias/README.md) drops from 25 to 24 stages through a paid forward-row alias. Two independent exact replays check all 7,813 rooted conditions and two order-thirteen defects that cannot vanish simultaneously in any nonzero ring with 12! invertible. The [earlier 25-stage construction](research/erk-signed-reverse/order-12-factorial-25/README.md) remains available. [Order fifteen](research/erk-signed-reverse/order-15-factorial-39/README.md) shortens its reverse chart by using two tiers of weighted receivers. [Order twenty](research/erk-signed-reverse/order-20-factorial-70/README.md) drops two reverse donors while adding one asymmetric scalar receiver. [Order eleven](research/erk11-twenty-two-shared-origin/README.md) improves the earlier 23-stage construction by moving two opening ports and sharing the actual reverse origin; all 3,047 conditions and a unit next-order defect pass exact replay, and its [end-to-end Lean certificate](research/erk11-twenty-two-shared-origin/LEAN.md) checks the order and exact factorial-localized endpoint on Lean/Mathlib 4.36.0. The [shorter weighted charts at orders eleven through sixteen](research/erk-signed-reverse/WEIGHTED-ORDERS-11-16.md) combine weighted observations with old exact readings.

The stated factorial unit is necessary for existence at any stage count. Literal arithmetic replays check all 3,047, 7,813, 20,299, and 53,272 rooted conditions at orders eleven through fourteen. At orders fifteen to twenty the general symbolic argument proves every rooted condition, and the literal tableaux have sample replays and exact next-order checks. The 22-stage order-eleven and [24-stage order-twelve](research/erk12-twenty-four-forward-alias/LEAN.md) constructions are now Lean-certified; the constructions at orders thirteen and higher in this paragraph are exact arithmetic or symbolic results.

## Strict SDIRK at order five over nonzero commutative rings

A [single six-stage tableau](research/strict-sdirk-rational/order-five-paid-six.md) has exact order five over every nonzero commutative ring in which 30 is a unit. This gives an interval of **five to six stages**; if 30 is not a unit, order five is impossible at any stage count. The [two-chart result](research/strict-sdirk-rational/order-five-paid-local.md) gives an exact five-stage minimum over all admissible local rings, including fields. All [eight repeated-clock patterns](research/strict-sdirk-rational/order-five-paid-all-repeated-clock-exclusion.md) are excluded at five stages over the localization with only 30 inverted. The [distinct-clock case](research/strict-sdirk-rational/distinct-clock-review/README.md) remains open. A native Lean proof requires the last weight, first tap, and first clock gap to be units for any five-stage strict tableau over a nonzero commutative ring. The repeat exclusion is mathematical with an exact replay, not a Lean certificate.

## Exact order-six strict SDIRK minima by coefficient domain

The [complete field classification](research/strict-sdirk-endpoints/MINIMA.md) gives six stages in characteristics 11, 17, 163, 263, 457 and 126963077, and in characteristic 3533 precisely when 2077 is a square. Characteristics 2, 3 and 5 are impossible; all other fields, including characteristic zero, require seven. Over nonzero commutative rings a seven-stage tableau of exact order six exists whenever 720 is a unit, and otherwise order six is impossible at any stage count. The same note gives six-stage minima on specified squarefree annihilator loci and a nilpotence obstruction over general rings. Lean/Mathlib 4.36.0 checks these claims without ordered-field assumptions.

## Necessary conditions at open stage boundaries

- [Fourteen stages at ERK order ten](research/ERK10-FOURTEEN-STAGE-EQUALITY.md): any equality candidate over a field with 10! nonzero has exact graded ranks through grade nine and a final node at one. When all output weights are nonzero, weighted reversal preserves order ten. In characteristic zero, if the quadratic defect and its first clock multiple are independent, the defect profile must be genuinely quadratic on its nonzero stages. The balanced case with four cancelling pairs and six fixed clean Lobatto nodes is [excluded over every characteristic-zero field](research/ERK10-BALANCED-SEAM-EXCLUSION.md); all 24 matrices for the two actual mixed conditions are nonsingular. The [nine distinct defect nodes/five clean nodes alternative](research/ERK10-UNBALANCED-CLEAN-FIVE-EXCLUSION.md) is also excluded in characteristic zero. Together the two exclusions force a zero output weight or all nonzero quadratic stage defects at a single clock. Scalar gates are checked in four Lean releases. The [nonconstant-amplitude exclusion](research/ERK10-NONCONSTANT-AMPLITUDE-EXCLUSION.md) further forces the nonzero defects to have the same value if every weight is nonzero. The [dependent-case analysis](research/ERK10-DEPENDENT-R-PAIR-PROFILE.md) and [two-label exclusion](research/ERK10-DEPENDENT-TWO-LABEL-EXCLUSION.md) then force at least three distinct abscissae at which defect-free stages respond to them. The [three-carrier exclusion](research/ERK10-THREE-CARRIER-EXCLUSION.md) leaves exactly two stages with nonzero quadratic defect, at physical positions 2 and 13. If any output weight is zero, the [live-transpose exclusion](research/ERK10-ZERO-WEIGHT-LIVE-TRANSPOSE.md) leaves at most one abscissa carrying a nonzero-weight quadratic defect. These exclusions do not require an ordered field. On the construction side, a [six-opening exclusion](research/erk10-six-opening-singleton/README.md) rules out a specified eight-row high block on the Lobatto sites and supplies a separate rational seven-row local ingredient, not a fourteen-stage order-ten method. The [order-independent one-dirty exclusion](research/erk10-one-dirty-reordered/README.md) rules out another specified canonical five-opening architecture, allowing arbitrary high row order but exactly one stage with the indicated extra defect. Together, the [two-dirty projection](research/erk10-two-dirty-projection/README.md) and [off-mesh exclusion](research/erk10-two-dirty-offmesh/README.md) close the specified canonical five-opening architecture with two genuinely dirty high stages at distinct Lobatto clocks, neither equal to a low donor clock. A [sole-origin exclusion](research/ERK10-SOLE-ORIGIN-GHOST-EXCLUSION.md) now requires a zero weight at an internal stage over characteristic-zero fields. The [injective grade-four branch is also excluded](research/ERK10-ZERO-INJECTIVE-INTERNAL-EXCLUSION.md): a surviving candidate must have a nonzero grade-four defect profile supported only on zero-output-weight stages. ## Structure at the strict SDIRK lower endpoints

- [Eight stages at order seven](research/strict-sdirk7-eight-stage-signs/README.md): Lean proves that every rational candidate has eight nonzero output weights and exactly two negative ones. This excludes rational zero-weight and at-most-one-negative subclasses, not arbitrary eight-stage rational tableaux. Over the prime field of order 19, a [complete finite exclusion](research/strict-sdirk7-eight-stage-face/README.md) rules out eight stages and an [exact Lean-checked tableau](research/strict-sdirk7-nine-f19/README.md) attains order seven in nine. This gives a lower bound of nine over every coefficient ring admitting a unital map to that prime field, including the universal factorial localization; the localization still has upper bound ten. An [explicit quadratic tableau](research/strict-sdirk7-quadratic/README.md) is Lean-checked over a real coefficient field of degree two and specializes to every nonzero commutative ring in which 19 is nilpotent. In particular the exact stage minimum is nine over `Z/19^k` for every positive integer `k`, by reduction to the prime field. Lean checks the native inputs and finite receipts for the lower proof, while the exhaustion and tableau reduction are mathematical and independently replayed. A [two-tree exactness certificate](research/strict-sdirk7-quadratic-exact/README.md) removes the extra norm guard: the same tableau has exact order seven whenever the two original scales are units and its quadratic root exists. In particular the nine-stage upper also holds over every nonzero commutative ring in which 4217, 27367649, or their product is nilpotent. Those new cases are upper bounds, not minima. A [different nine-stage construction](research/strict-sdirk7-unpaid-head/README.md) proves that a third helper can fail the grade-four condition and still produce exact order seven, with an actual cubic defect on the active stages. Lean checks the literal example over the prime field of order 19 and the rational paid-or-dirty distinction; the separate all-power lift works over the 19-adic integers and their fraction field. An [exactly isolated real-algebraic point](research/strict-sdirk7-real-dirty/README.md) gives another cubic-dirty nine-stage tableau. The [primitive-field calculation](research/strict-sdirk7-dirty-number-field/README.md) identifies the real point as an embedding of the same degree-324 field as the 19-adic point. This does not resolve rational nine-stage existence.
- [Ten stages at order eight](research/sdirk-eight-saturation/README.md): Lean proves that a unique zero quadratic stage defect must occur at a fourth shifted Legendre root. Its weight and its diagonal are forced, excluding the singleton-zero branch over the rationals. The [singleton slope](research/sdirk-eight-singleton-exclusion/README.md) and [two-zero clock](research/sdirk-eight-two-zero-line/README.md) arguments exclude all such cases over characteristic-zero fields. If any quadratic stage residual is zero, there must be at least three distinct zero-stage nodes by the [higher multiplicity test](research/sdirk-eight-two-zero-clocks/README.md). These use mathematical reductions with exact and separate Lean scalar receipts, not an end-to-end order-eight exclusion. The later [three-zero exclusion](research/sdirk-eight-three-zero-silent/README.md) and [off-zero mass analysis](research/sdirk-eight-offzero-clock-mass/README.md) require a surviving zero-containing tableau to have at least four distinct zero-defect stage nodes, with off-zero output weights canceling at each fixed node and defect value. These are mathematical restrictions with checked finite gates. If all nonzero quadratic defects occur at a single physical node and some has nonzero output weight, a [further characteristic-zero reduction](research/sdirk-eight-half-equal/README.md) leaves only the two endpoint defects at the common node one half, with opposite endpoint weights and all ten weights nonzero. The [coprime profile exclusion](research/sdirk-eight-half-arithmetic-quadratic/README.md) completes that part of the endpoint-pair chart over characteristic-zero fields: a surviving interpolation relation needs a common root with an actual stage discrepancy. By the [shared-root census](research/sdirk-eight-half-shared-root/README.md), any such discrepancy must occur at a repeated abscissa with unequal cubic defects. The [two repeated-clock exclusions](research/sdirk-eight-half-repeated/README.md) close the entire all-active half-diagonal endpoint-pair chart over characteristic-zero fields. The [silent one-clock argument](research/sdirk-eight-silent-oneclock/README.md) also rules out four physically defective stages with zero output weight at one abscissa, and the [three-stage closure](research/sdirk-eight-silent-three-closure/README.md) leaves at most two physically defective stages when all such stages have zero output weight and share one abscissa. The real-algebraic interval remains 10–11. A [native 11-stage witness in characteristic 31](research/sdirk-eight-f31-upper/README.md) and its [separate mathematical 31-adic and prime-power lifts](research/sdirk-eight-f31-lift/README.md) give the same upper over every nonzero commutative ring of characteristic 31^k, without proving ten impossible there.

The [real-algebraic order-13 port study](research/real-sdirk13-forward-ports/README.md) builds a minimal twelve-row dense size-six decoder with two actual size-seven pure stages, and excludes the straightforward five-pivot extension to 25 stages. ## Raising order while keeping the old stages

The [Newton-tail construction](research/newton-tail-order-raising/NEWTON-TAIL.md) raises order p−1 to at least p without changing the old stage equations, provided the output weights can be reassigned. It uses at most 2p new stages over nonzero commutative rings with p! invertible. Over the real algebraic numbers the [sharp worst-case costs](research/newton-tail-order-raising/SHARP-EXTENSION-COST.md) are floor(p/2) new ERK stages and ceil(p/2) for DIRK and SDIRK, with preservation of the SDIRK diagonal. For comparison, [fixing the old output weights](research/newton-tail-order-raising/FIXED-OUTPUT-LOWER.md) can force exponentially many new stages along a sequence of orders; some order-18 bases need at least 77 appended stages to reach order 19. These are extension costs, not absolute stage minima or Lean certificates.

## General mechanisms for changing or bounding stage counts

- [An iterable explicit two-order lift over factorial-unit rings](research/paid-affine-erk-two-order/AFFINE-TWO-ORDER-LIFT.md): from a prepared order-q seed, add n+2 physical rows for exact order p=q+2, with n=p if p is odd and n=p−1 if p is even. The exactness test does not require p+1 to be a unit. Both the hypotheses on the existing rows and the separate preparation costs are part of the theorem.
- [When old output stages pay a new moment](research/output-frontier-transfer/OUTPUT-SAMPLE-FRONTIER.md): an exact Legendre criterion detects which old clock samples substitute for another moment in the higher-order construction. See the [conditional stage savings](research/output-frontier-transfer/PHYSICAL-COUNT-TRANSFER.md) and the [comparison across ERK, DIRK and SDIRK](research/output-frontier-transfer/ERK-DIRK-TRANSFER.md). In particular, varying-diagonal DIRK can add two orders using only ceil(p/2) new real-algebraic rows when old output weights may be reassigned.
- [Reverse defect flags with one surplus dimension](research/one-surplus-reverse-flags/ONE-SURPLUS-CHARACTERS.md): under the stated head-rank and support hypotheses, a surviving surplus can be compensated by one clock factor or one extra degree of test budget. [Chronological rank tests](research/one-surplus-reverse-flags/PAID-HERMITE-EXIT-WITH-PREFIX-LEAKAGE.md) and [when they hide ordinary defects](research/one-surplus-reverse-flags/HIDING-ONE-SURPLUS-MARKED-QUOTIENT.md) require the actual prefix and marking hypotheses.

- [Preparing the next explicit lift with primitive observations](research/paid-affine-erk-two-order/PRIMITIVE-OBSERVERS.md): existing pure stages and moment observations can replace some new rows. Preparation and the final order lift are counted separately. The [archived 33-stage order-twelve tableau](research/erk-eleven-twelve-moment-splice/ARCHIVED-GRADE-TEN-OBSTRUCTION.md) cannot supply the grade-ten decoder for one proposed order-fourteen lift, even over the rationals; this does not rule out other seeds.
- [Physical clock tests for finite reverse characters](research/one-surplus-reverse-flags/FINITE-CHARACTERS-AND-LITERAL-DIAGONALS.md): with the stated prefix matching and visibility, the commutator detects a literal zero diagonal over a field. [Repeated clocks](research/one-surplus-reverse-flags/REPEATED-FIBRES-AND-NONZERO-DIAGONAL-OBSTRUCTION.md) and nonreduced coefficient rings require separate checks.
- [Output-clock grouping and chronology](research/output-clock-causality/GROUP-DEFECTS-AND-CAUSALITY.md): an exact group can give a smaller method of lower order. [The zero-mass construction](research/output-clock-causality/SILENT-CLOCK-SHARPNESS.md) shows why contributing clocks alone do not control local order. [The sign bound](research/output-clock-causality/SIGNS-AND-EARLY-OUTPUT.md) limits early active clocks in terms of negative weights.
- [Compensated DIRK prefix cuts](research/dirk-compensated-prefix-cuts/COMPENSATED-PREFIX-CUTS.md): two actual zero-through observations can impose a polynomial degree obstruction without assuming either pivot diagonal is zero. The profile count is distinct from the rank on physical rows.

- [Deleting stages while preserving specified rooted evaluations](research/stage-elimination/SAFE-CHRONOLOGICAL-ELIMINATION.md): chronological reduction preserves the diagonal class. [The named-row test](research/stage-elimination/NAMED-ROW-REBUILD.md) permits replacement reads from later retained stages when they still precede the actual consumer. Over rings, extending a descended output functional requires an additional check.
- [Successive physical marks](research/paid-successive-marks/SUCCESSIVE-MARKS-AND-SUPPORT.md): actual chronological observations bound a polynomial carrier and constrain the rank on physical clock fibres. [The zero-through variant](research/paid-successive-marks/STRICT-ZERO-THROUGH-MARKS.md) trades one degree for avoiding a zero-diagonal assumption.
- [Terminal tests at the actual last clock](research/moving-terminal-tests/MOVING-TERMINAL.md): an order identity detects when a true numerical null direction would force the last clock to be one, without assuming endpoint purity in advance.
- [Sharing physical correction rows across clock groups](research/sdirk-shared-physical-pivots/SHARED-PHYSICAL-PIVOTS.md): the same lower-triangular suffix can pay several groups’ order conditions. The exact [two-clock example](research/sdirk-shared-physical-pivots/TWO-CLOCK-FOUR-EXCESS.md) saves a row against the earlier construction with fixed co-site corrections, not against the unrestricted table.

These structural results have independent source audits. They do not change the unrestricted numerical tables or add Lean certificates.

## Bounds for particular stage architectures

- [Uniform explicit leapfrog paths](research/erk-uniform-leapfrog/UNIFORM-LEAPFROG-WIDTH.md): through order p, exactly 1 + floor(p²/4) physical stages are necessary and sufficient over any nonzero commutative ring in which p! is a unit. The lower bound allows signed weights on intermediate evaluations and sharing of identical stages. The construction gives 21 stages at order nine over ℤ[1/210]. This is a minimum within the stated architecture and an upper bound for unrestricted ERK over those rings, not an unrestricted lower bound or a Lean certificate.

- [First zero diagonal with a one-dimensional commutator kernel](lower-bounds/first-zero-corank-one-floor.md): at order 20, at least 51 stages, compared with 46 from the one-zero mask alone. The hypothesis is automatic for a zero first diagonal followed by nonzero diagonals, which need not be equal. The proof works over any field that admits the stated order and transfers to rings when the rank is witnessed in a residue field.
- [Fixed seven-stage ERK6 prefix](research/erk-six-ring-minimum/SEQUENTIAL-SUFFIX.md): retaining its seven literal rows requires at least 10, 11, and 13 physical stages at orders 7, 8, and 9. A parallel suffix cannot raise the order at any width. These bounds concern the fixed prefix, not all explicit tableaux.

Neither result changes the unrestricted tables or adds a Lean certificate.

## Sharp bounds for polynomial stage readouts

- [Exact dense derivative readout](research/sdirk-nonhomogeneous-kernels/DENSE-POLYNOMIAL-DONOR-MINIMUM.md): recovering all rooted stage-derivative polynomials through order q from one linear readout requires floor((q+1)²/4) stages if all the diagonal entries are nonzero. A common-diagonal Laguerre construction achieves equality over a suitable real algebraic field. The lower bound also covers varying nonzero diagonals, and finite sets of readouts obey a rank-dependent version.
- [Exact dense readout with a live explicit origin](research/sdirk-nonhomogeneous-kernels/LIVE-ORIGIN-DENSE-DECODER.md): when only the first diagonal is zero, the sharp real stage count is 1 + floor(q²/4), or 101 rather than 110 at q=20. The [lower proof](research/sdirk-nonhomogeneous-kernels/DENSE-SINGULAR-COMMUTATOR-FLOOR.md) allows varying nonzero later diagonals; the construction attains it with any prescribed nonzero common later diagonal. This is dense derivative accuracy, not ordinary endpoint order.
- [Sharp bound with a prescribed error band](research/sdirk-nonhomogeneous-kernels/PAID-DONOR-BAND-MINIMUM.md): prescribing only the Laguerre band through a larger cap, not the actual rows or error coefficients, also has an exact minimum. This shows why changing the depth alone does not shorten a preparation block with the same error band.
- [Use in a terminal construction](research/sdirk-nonhomogeneous-kernels/LOW-CUT-RANK-LANE-AND-PAID-PATH-PROFILE.md): a triangular shear gives 46 stages of ordinary order at least 14 and scalar and clock orders at least 21. Full ordinary order 20 remains excluded in that retained preparation at sufficiently small positive diagonal.

These are exact statements about specified readouts or preparations, not ordinary fixed-output stage minima or new Lean coverage.

## Rational contributing quadrature support

Separate from the stage tables, the [four-shift proof](research/rational-quadrature-four-shift/proof.md) gives at least \(\min(17,p-3)\) distinct contributing rational sites for quadrature order \(p\ge4\): 16 at order 19 and 17 at order 20. Weights may be arbitrary in a characteristic-zero field. Repeat sites are grouped first, and only nonzero aggregate weights count. The result transfers to Runge–Kutta by the reviewed bushy moments. It has an exact arithmetic check, not a new Lean certificate.

A [standalone 19-site rational rule](research/rational-quadrature-four-shift/standalone-19.md) attains quadrature order 20 with positive weights. This is not a Runge–Kutta stage upper bound. The [support index](research/rational-quadrature-four-shift/README.md) retains the stronger earlier low-order bounds.

The [donor realization theorem](research/quadrature-donor-realization/realization.md) goes in the other direction: every scalar rule of order at least \(p\) lifts to ERK, DIRK, or strict SDIRK with a prescribed common diagonal, preserving its contributing sites and weights. The same \(u/128\) rule gives tableaux of exact order 20 with 19 contributing sites and 209 physical rows, including 190 zero-output helpers. This equates the minimal contributing support with the scalar minimum, not the minimum stage count. No stage bound or Lean coverage count changes.

A separate [radii/33 prescribed-support realization](research/rational-support-radii33-splice/realization.md) keeps another 19-site rational rule's positive aggregate weights and has exact order 20 in 101 physical rows for ERK and 117 for strict SDIRK with common diagonal \(1/66\). This is not the \(u/128\) rule. The counts do not change the global stage tables; the proof and deterministic replay add no Lean coverage claim.

The [ordered-root geometry](research/rational-root-chart-geometry/ordered-root-geometry.md) gives a structural obstruction, not a support exclusion: with two or three paid nodal shifts the positive-dimensional smooth root charts have no dominant rational parametrization. A generic fixed prefix leaving one free root beyond Hankel completion gives a genus-one or genus-13 curve, respectively. The geometry alone does not decide attainability. It does not change a stage bound or Lean coverage.

The [two-shift elliptic criterion](research/two-shift-elliptic-transfer/elliptic-transfer.md) tests fixed-prefix completions exactly. A same-gap lower rule of exact order supplies rational infinity points and a conditional rank-free candidate. Neither of the known 19-site order-20 rules supplies the needed order-19 seed.

The [fixed-functional same-gap ladder](research/same-gap-seed-ladder/seed-ladder.md) shows that a genuine rational two-shift seed with a nontorsion completion pair can raise exact order and support repeatedly without changing the moments. The [uniform seed below](research/uniform-same-gap-seed/uniform-seed.md) meets that hypothesis.
