# Runge–Kutta research archive

This archive concerns the algebraic, classical rooted-tree order conditions. ERK is strictly lower triangular. DIRK is lower triangular, permitting zero diagonal entries. Strict SDIRK has a common nonzero diagonal over fields, and a common unit diagonal in the arbitrary-ring bounds. No stability, stiffness, sign, or node-location restrictions are imposed.

- [Lower bounds by coefficient setting](lower-bounds/README.md)
- [Upper bounds and coefficient fields](upper-bounds/README.md)
- [Lean proofs and reproduction](lean/README.md)

The current tables report established bounds, not claims of literature priority or attainability at the lower bound. Each newly verified proof or construction should enter this archive with its scope and evidence, with the current tables updated in the same change when a bound moves. A conditional theorem, exact arithmetic diagnostic, or proof in progress does not establish a new unrestricted entry. Some older mathematical dependencies are still being collected; a note with a missing dependency is not a self-contained proof bundle.
