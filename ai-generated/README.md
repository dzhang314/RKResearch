# Runge–Kutta research archive

Initial working snapshot, 23 November 2026. The archive concerns classical rooted-tree order conditions for ERK (strictly lower triangular), DIRK (lower triangular, allowing explicit stages), and strict SDIRK (one common nonzero diagonal). No stability assumptions are imposed.

- [Lower bounds and proof fields](lower-bounds/README.md)
- [Upper bounds and coefficient fields](upper-bounds/README.md)

The tables record verified bounds, not priority claims. Proofs and arithmetic replays are distinguished from numerical diagnostics. This is the first batch: references to proof dependencies not yet copied are research references, not a claim that this snapshot is self-contained. Conditional theorems are labeled in their files and do not override the unrestricted tables.
