# Nonlocal reverse DIRK marked-port obstruction

Pinned Lean and Mathlib v4.36.0. The entry point is `RKCert.DirkJointHidden`. Replay with `lake build RKCert.DirkJointHidden`, `lake env lean AuditDIRKJointHidden.lean`, and `lake env leanchecker RKCert.DirkJointHidden`.

Main finite theorem: RKCert.Paired.Terminal.order16_nonlocal_quartic_hidden. For an arbitrary characteristic-zero coefficient field, a 21-row lower tableau with full rooted-tree order ≥16, and the cubic adjoint word's line **not invariant** under diag(1−c), every genuine ordinary vector f in F4 satisfies both b ⊙ f = 0 and τ ⊙ f = 0, pointwise. Neither a scalar-grid assumption, nonzero individual weights, primitive equations, stage deletions, order on the field, nor adjoint RK order is assumed.
The proof earns the actual last physical active row by causally restricting the true tree equations in the hypothetical trailing-zero case. It recovers both directions of marked rank equality, and uses four hard ingredients: (1) weighted ascending and descending reverse Hermite gains, also on the genuine terminal-zero subspace, (2) a single cubic head in the nonlocal reverse band with ≥m−1 distinct actual knots, (3) two *physical* reverse pivots, both original diagonal refunds, and all admissible primitive equations, (4) the fixed quartic gate in [the earlier boundary argument](DIRK-EVEN-BOUNDARY.md) applied to the derived reflected Lobatto knots, across arbitrary characteristic-zero fields.
All even boundaries p=2m,s=3m−3,m≥7 in the nonlocal branch hide F3; F_j is jointly hidden whenever j≥4 and 2j+1≤m. These are properties of the original physical coordinates and do not imply tableau stage removal.
Scope caveat: **the local D3 case is not excluded by this file**; neither this finite theorem nor the general nonlocal result asserts s≥22 at p16 on its own. Separately, the fixed final divisor-moment proof has no bearing on discharge of the local branch.

The [local reverse obstruction](DIRK-LOCAL-REVERSE.md) now proves that the nonlocality hypothesis used here holds for every tableau at this boundary. The joint vanishing still does not license deleting a stage.
