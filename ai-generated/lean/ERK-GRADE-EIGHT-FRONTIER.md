# Native grade-eight: the exact first physical frontier

The stage rank contradiction itself is not yet proved. These three small,
additive modules reduce it to the genuine physical closures, without legacy
tree order, imported ERK exclusions, or arithmetic certificates.

- **NativeFlatClockHead** is generic. A nonempty interval from grade j≥3 to
  j+d with a unit rank budget and actual separating coefficient evaluations
  through j+d admits one maximal invariant core and one head throughout.
  Strict causality gives the outgoing Hermite obstruction at the initial
  and intermediate ranks, and the incoming obstruction at the final rank.
  The next iterate is outside the actual last defect space. The escaping
  numerical blocks (modulo the core) strictly outnumber the included
  powers. No choice of a normalized representative or distinct physical
  nodes is required. This applies to the general doubling argument too.
- **NativeHeadMultiplier** is field-generic. A polynomial evaluated on
  the head, of degree strictly below the number of escaping blocks,
  cannot land in the invariant core unless it is zero. This yields the
  exact nonzero quotient multiplier and its degree budget when an
  actual multiplier preserves the core and the displayed powers.
  It uses native `ClockHeadFibers`, not a wrapper around the old lemma.
- **ERKGradeEightFrontier** uses the checked grade-five surplus. Under
  the hypothetical dim F8≤8, grades 5 through 8 are the genuine flat
  chart with a head of length at least two. F4 either has rank four
  and a flat 4→8 chart (the grade-four quartic branch), or rank three.
  For any v∈F3 outside the grade-five core, pointwise multiplication
  by v preserves the core, and v·w minus P(c)·w lies in the core for a
  nonzero polynomial P of degree at most three. If no such v exists
  and F4 has rank three, the invariant core is exactly F4. These
  conclusions already hold at order 16 on any nonempty strict matrix.
