# Ordered ERK order 16: exact native paid frame

Assume a strictly lower triangular Runge–Kutta stage matrix over an ordered
field and the reviewed `RKResearch.HasOrder A b 16`. Native grade eight
costs nine forward dimensions. The existing even midpoint bound costs
fifteen other dimensions and the rank of the initial reverse seed. Thus
the unconditional lower bound is **24**; **25** follows when the actual
reverse seed `firstSeed A b` (not the output weight vector) is nonzero.

At width 24, the terminal stage is active, its clock is 1, and the reverse
seed vanishes. The grade-eight tree span has rank 18, contains the terminal
coordinate, and grade seven does not yet contain that coordinate. The
degree-six primitive reverse flag saturates its annihilator; all preceding
reverse flags through six have exactly rank equal to their index and agree
with their primitive flags.

This seal gives the native width-24 paid frame. It does not exclude that
zero-seed case and does not claim the unconditional 25-stage endpoint.
It uses the reviewed order directly. No old tableau, tree/order
conversion, or substantial arithmetic replay is required.
