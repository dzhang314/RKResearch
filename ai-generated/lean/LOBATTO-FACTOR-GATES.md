# One weighted primitive rules out small fixed-Lobatto factors

**Theorem.** Let K be any field of characteristic zero. Write L_m for the shifted Legendre polynomial normalized by L_m(1)=1, G_m(X)=X L'_m(X), and J for the polynomial primitive with zero constant term. If (m,D)=(7,4) or (8,5), P divides G_m in K[X], deg P≤D, and u is a nonzero root of G_m in K, then

    (J(X P))(u) ≠ 0.

The divisor need not be monic. Neither the omitted-root hypothesis P(u)≠0 nor the second primitive condition J(X²P)(u)=0 is needed. This is a polynomial theorem over arbitrary characteristic-zero fields, including nonordered ones. It proves the factor obstruction for the corresponding order-14 and order-16 DIRK charts. The polynomial theorem by itself is not an unconditional DIRK stage bound; applying it requires the tableau-to-factor argument.

## Proof

1. Write L'_m(X)=m(m+1) B_m(X), c=lc(B_m), n=m−1 and H(Y)=c^(n−1) B_m(Y/c). Then H is a monic integer polynomial. Respectively c is 429 and 1430. Scaling the roots of G_m by c gives its leading coefficient times YH(Y). Normalize the proposed divisor to leading coefficient one and scale its roots by c. This produces a monic divisor Q of YH(Y) over K and the test site v=cu satisfies H(v)=0. The equation J(XP)(u)=0 is unaffected: more exactly for monic normalization fixed, the weighted moment scales by c^(deg P+2), and multiplying P by a scalar multiplies the moment by that scalar.
2. Use A(Q,v)=Σ_{i=0}^D [420/(i+2)] q_i v^(i+2), where the square brackets are integers: every denominator 2 through D+2 divides 420. Thus A(Q,v)=420 J(XQ)(v). This polynomial expression commutes with every unital ring map, unlike division in unspecified positive characteristic.
3. Integral specialization is legitimate here for a specific reason. The coefficients of the monic factor Q **and its monic complementary factor** lie in the integral closure O of ℤ in K, because their product is a monic polynomial over ℤ. Similarly v is integral, being a root of the monic H. For a selected prime p, lying-over gives a maximal ideal of O above (p). Reducing Q, its complementary factor, and v in the residue field E retains the factorization, monicity, the degree upper bound, the root identity and any alleged A=0. There is no requirement to reduce arbitrary RK tableau coefficients, to preserve distinct roots for an unspecified reduction, or to assume that E is precisely the prime field.
4. For m=7 take p=1237. The six roots of H mod p are 1005,1065,1028,638,601,661. For m=8 take p=1249, with seven roots 561,1121,172,715,9,309,869. The displayed linear products are verified coefficient by coefficient over each prime field. In every extension E, each monic divisor of YH is the product of a sublist of these roots together with the additional zero; each root of H is on the original list. To see the divisor claim, peel off Y−a from one of the two factors after evaluating their product at a; iterate, preserving monicity. No splitting-field assumptions on K nor specialization of an omitted-root inequation are involved.
5. Enumerate the sublists of lengths at most D (99 and 219 respectively), and each candidate endpoint (6 and 7 respectively). For multiplication by X−a, compute coefficients by the recurrence q'_i=q_{i−1}−a q_i; evaluate A modulo the stated prime. All 594 and 1533 results are nonzero. The complete two tables and both prime proofs are checked by ordinary Lean kernel reduction, with no trust in a Python calculation, tactic oracle or native evaluator. The assertion stays nonzero under the injective prime-field homomorphism into E, contradicting the putative vanishing there. This proves the theorem.

## Lean and reuse

The public statements are `RKCert.FixedFactor.lobatto7_first_moment` and `RKCert.FixedFactor.lobatto8_first_moment` in `RKCert/LobattoFactors.lean`, for the project's actual `RKCert.Paired.Terminal.lobatto` and `RKCert.Paired.primitive`. Importing the former also imports the library of isolated general lemmas. The `replay_lobatto_factors.py` auxiliary program independently reconstructs the exact shifted Legendre coefficients from integer binomial coefficients, the monic scaling, splitting nodes and exhaustive checks. It is not imported by Lean.

Pinned Mathlib v4.36.0 supplies `integralClosure.mem_lifts_of_monic_of_dvd_map` (the genuinely nontrivial integrality of the factors), `Ideal.exists_ideal_over_maximal_of_isIntegral` (lying-over), `Ideal.Quotient.field`, `ZMod.castHom`, `Polynomial.scaleRoots`, `Polynomial.coeff_scaleRoots`, `Polynomial.scaleRoots_eval_mul`, `Polynomial.monic_mul_leadingCoeff_inv`, and the actual `Polynomial.shiftedLegendre` coefficients. The specialization adapter `good_prime` and sublist transport `modular_table` are local because they connect those results to this particular executable moment predicate; they do not reimplement integral closure, polynomial scaling, quotient fields or primality theory. The padded moment and integer coefficient recurrence avoid deciding equality of abstract field or polynomial representations.
