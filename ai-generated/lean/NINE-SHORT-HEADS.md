# The genuine two-cheap-head ending through grade nine

Over every characteristic-zero field let A,b be an ordinary lower
triangular tree tableau of order p≥14; c=A1, C=diag(c), F_j its actual
zero-profile stage space, Z=F₉+C F₉. Suppose U is C-invariant,
F₅=U+span(w,Cw), F₉=U+span(w,…,C⁵w), Z is not C-invariant, and all
actual products F₅·F₅ belong to Z. These hypotheses are impossible.
No equality Z=F₁₀, coordinate-product algebra axiom for U, disjointness,
clock-simple recipe, chosen pivot, real-field order or beta-sign
assumption is part of the theorem. The new characteristic-zero
H9 obstruction is a structural branch of the p19 and p20 lower
bound proofs, not a numerical floor by itself.

Write chain_n=U+span(w,…,C^(n−1)w). Its raise satisfies
Z=chain_7. Noninvariance forces at least eight effective distinct
head sites. Correcting w by its old U-fibers preserves every chain.
For any u∈U and x∈F₅, decompose u into its single-clock components.
Each resulting ux belongs to Z by the product payment. Its head
polynomial cannot vanish on all the other effective sites unless it
is zero; hence ux∈U. Thus U is a coordinatewise algebra and a further
U-correction of w makes its physical support disjoint from that of U.

Because w,Cw are both grade-five inputs, w²,Cw²,C²w² are all in Z.
They give polynomials T₀,T₁,T₂ of degree ≤6 representing these
multipliers on the head support. The eight distinct effective
clocks force T₁=XT₀ and T₂=XT₁ as polynomial identities (degree ≤7).
So P=T₀ is nonzero of degree at most four and equals w at every
nonzero head entry. Let e be the true support indicator of U and set
θ=P(c)−w−P(c)e. Its scalar profile is P at grade five: both corrections
are pure. Not only θ, but Cθ, has a trace at grade five. Indeed XP has
degree ≤5, Cw is already paid, and CP(c)e is still in U. If θ_i≠0,
neither w_i nor any U-vector is nonzero there; consequently every
F₉ vector vanishes at that original row. Repeated nodes and zero
output weights were never discarded.

A more general lemma takes θ with a nonzero scalar profile P at grade
j and the clocked trace Cθ at some genuinely earned grade g. For the
first nonzero actual Cθ coordinate u, the native commutator vanishes
at the clean row but triangularity makes its value −a_ii²(Cθ)_i,
so the original diagonal is zero. The analogous test applies to
(C−u)Cθ, now of grade g+1, at its true first survivor v. Both have
physical survivors by exact pairing with nonzero G=X and X(X−u),
paid by j+deg(P)+5≤p. Thus u,v are distinct and nonzero. The
commutator payments are g+3≤p,q, while every one of the m+1 first
and m second ordinary primitive tests fits in q≥g+m+1. This
proves actual evaluations without treating a synthetic terminal row
or an unpriced projection as an ordinary physical stage.

Here j=g=5, deg P≤4, m=3, q=9, p≥14. Hence J[X^(k+1)P](u)=0 for
k=0,1,2,3 and J[X^(k+1)(X−u)P](v)=0 for k=0,1,2. The independently
verified field-valid four-plus-three lemma kills even a quintic,
so P=0, a contradiction. This genuinely refunded clock grade,
not merely the usual grade-six clock trace, is why F₉ suffices.
