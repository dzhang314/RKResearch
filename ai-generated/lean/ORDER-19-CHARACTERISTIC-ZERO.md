# Lower-triangular order 19 requires at least 27 physical stages

**Statement.** For every commutative ring of characteristic zero, a lower-triangular Runge–Kutta tableau satisfying the normalized all-rooted-tree order equations through order 19 has at least 27 stages. The diagonal may be arbitrary, including zero entries; no positivity, real ordering, simplifying assumption, or stage irreducibility is imposed. This is a lower bound, not a 27-stage construction or a sharpness claim. It strengthens the prior automatic 26-stage floor by one.

Work first over a characteristic-zero field. By the previously proved odd-order bound the only size to exclude is 26. Write C for componentwise multiplication by the vector of stage abscissas c, E_j for the ordinary stage tree span, F_j for its normalized pure-defect subspace, f_j for the latter's rank, and B_r for the actual backward word space. Let ε be zero if the ordinary quadratic seed vanishes and one otherwise.

1. **Buy grade ten before dispatching the marked grades.** The grade-three, grade-six and grade-ten pure-rank bounds, ordinary strict growth, and the backward rank budget give
   f3 = 1+ε, f5 ≤ 4+ε, f6 = 5+ε, f8 = 7+ε, f9 = 8+ε, f10 = 10+ε, dim E9 = 18+ε, and dim E10 = 20. In the ε=0 case the actual first row is zero; one extra forward scalar coefficient is paid for by evaluation at this origin. The comparison f10 ≥ f3+9 is decisive: it saturates the full adjoint tail from E10 in *both* branches, not just in the nonanchored case. This bypasses a separate marked early-excess split.
2. **The last coordinate is a test, not a deleted stage.** A zero output at the last stage would allow trimming that terminal stage and contradict the 26-stage odd-order bound. Saturation therefore gives the actual last cardinal in E10, its clock 1, last input column zero and the first two reverse seeds zero. The cardinal kills F8 by exact pairing. Clock escape at F8 and its unit gain imply F9=F8+C F8, so every F9 vector still vanishes on the last stage. Full marked grades 2 through 8 equal B_(j−2); through grade 9 the same statement holds on the terminal-zero slice. For the potential one-dimensional discrepancy at grade 9, the origin-anchored case excludes the last cardinal from E9 and hence loses the extra dimension on terminal restriction. Consequently B7 has rank 7 and B7-perp = E9 + span(last cardinal).
3. **Recover a genuine invariant kernel.** Apply the common paid reverse-word theorem through grade 9: the reverse cubic is nonlocal, the seven-word span consists of polynomial multiples of the physical cubic row τ, and every vector in F4 is killed componentwise by both b and τ. A vector killed by these two rows annihilates B7. On the nonanchored branch E9 has rank 19, so it is exactly B7-perp. All nine-plus-one moments of any such E9 vector vanish, and the Cauchy system forces its scalar polynomial part to be zero: it is in F9. On the anchored branch also require the vector to vanish at the actual zero first row; this still contains F4. The two-endpoint hidden-port lemma removes the extra last cardinal and puts the vector in F9. In both cases this common coordinate kernel W is C-invariant and F4 ⊆ W ⊆ F9.
4. **The reusable rank dispatch.** Let U be the largest clock-invariant subspace of F9; it contains W. A forward outgoing bound initially gives dim U ≤ 6+ε. F7 cannot lie in U: it would coincide with U but its incoming invariant gain of 3 conflicts with f7−f6=1. Quotient growth up to F9, where every intervening gain is exactly one, now places U in F7. Its outgoing gain of 3 improves dim U ≤ 4+ε. Thus F6 is also not contained in U; the same reasoning puts U in F6, then gives dim U ≤ 3+ε. If f4 exceeded 2+ε, F4 would equal U and its outgoing gain of 2 would overspend f5. If f5 were below 4+ε, its unit gain would give F5=F4+C F4 ⊆ U; equality would contradict the incoming gain at F5. Therefore f4=2+ε and f5=4+ε. The rank ceiling places U in F5 by the same quotient argument. Every gain F5 to F9 is one and each grade escapes under C; the one-head normal form makes the full band a common cyclic chain over U, with initial head length 1 or 2.
5. **The product space at the decisive extra grade.** Put Z=F9+C F9. It vanishes at the actual last coordinate and is strictly larger than F9, by the incoming invariant lower bound. F10 is not wholly terminal-zero. Indeed any scalar polynomial profile in F10 has vanishing moments for x^0,…,x^8. If all F10 vectors vanished at clock 1, on the nonanchored branch this endpoint plus the nine moments would separate polynomials through degree 9, giving dim E10 ≥ 10+f10 = 21. On the anchored branch they also vanish at clock zero; the two endpoints and the same nine moments separate polynomials through degree 10, giving dim E10 ≥ 11+f10 = 21 as well. Both contradict 20. These moment detectors are algebraic, including the integration-by-parts proof with two endpoints; no real positivity is used.
   Hence Z is exactly F10 intersected with the last-coordinate kernel, by the known dimensions. It contains every product F5·F5: the product genuinely lies in F10 and its factors vanish at the last coordinate. Saturated E11 and the same forward moment budgets give f11 ≤ 12+ε. If Z were invariant, the outgoing grade-ten bound would force dim Z+5 ≤ f11, impossible. Nothing here promotes Z to the whole of F10, or silently deletes a physical row.
6. **Close.** The checked physical short-head theorem applies to the band from step 4 and the product and escape statements from step 5. The one-head case recovers an actual multiplicatively closed core and invokes the refunded fifth-debut contradiction. The two-head case earns the extra grade-five primitive and triggers the four-plus-three physical pivot exclusion. Thus 26 stages are impossible. The previously proved characteristic-zero field specialization transfers the result to every commutative ring of characteristic zero, preserving all tree identities, lower-triangular zeros, and the stage count. The characteristic-zero conclusion does not identify or exclude any positive characteristic.

**Lean and Mathlib reuse.** The proof uses the existing RKCert field-transfer, marked-word, rank-of-delayed-payment, primitive detector, and physical short-head libraries. Finite-dimensional comparisons use pinned Mathlib's submodules, linear maps, annihilators, lattice operations, `Submodule.finrank_lt_finrank_of_lt`, and `Submodule.eq_of_le_of_finrank_le`, not an alternate rank calculus. The only new reusable rank result, `Paired.Terminal.nine_rank_dispatch`, returns the native clock chain under explicit rank and inclusion hypotheses; it is not itself a stage bound.
