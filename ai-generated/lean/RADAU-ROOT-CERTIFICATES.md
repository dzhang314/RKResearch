# Exact fixed-Radau root and state tests for order nine

Write T(X)=X⁴−20X³/9+5X²/3−10X/21+5/126, the actual project's monic fixed-Radau quartic. All conclusions below hold over **arbitrary characteristic-zero fields**; no ordering, positivity, algebraic closure, or Galois-group information is assumed.

## Specialization of root configurations

The native monic integral scaling is H(Y)=126⁴T(Y/126)=Y⁴−280Y³+26460Y²−952560Y+10001880. In the prime field of order 191 it factors into the four distinct roots 45,57,80,98; modulo 353 the distinct roots are 41,111,161,320.

Let w be any tuple (with any index type) of T-roots. Let a mask specify the pairs of indices required to be different, and let f be any multivariate polynomial over the integers in the tuple coordinates. For *either* listed prime, if f is nonzero on every assignment of its displayed four scaled residues that respects exactly those masked inequalities, then f(126w)≠0. A coordinate need not be different from another unless the mask requires it. No assertion is made about a prime at which a given table fails. In particular these tests do not reduce a proposed Runge–Kutta tableau. The 353 adapter merely transports successfully established predicates; it does not itself assert the separate four-coordinate F/G determinant.

Proof: The elements 126w_i are integral over the integers because H is monic. Reduce in a residue field of the integral closure above the chosen prime. The polynomial identity f=0 and all individual root equations survive. A selected inequality survives as well: if originally x≠y are roots, factor H=(X−x)U over the domain. Evaluating at y gives U(y)=0. If x and y specialize to the same point then H' vanishes there, whereas each displayed root is simple. The residue field can exceed the prime field, but every H-root in that extension is already on the prime-field list. Use the injective prime-field map for the contradiction. This argument is `split_prime_root_tuple`; the native wrappers are `radau_root_certificate` (191), `radau_root_certificate353` (353), and the prime-parameterized `radau_root_certificate_at`, all in `RKCert.FixedFactor`.

## Strong scalar tests and their physical interpretation

1. For every triple a,b,h of roots of T, **including repeated roots**,
   3h²−4(a+b)h+6ab≠0. The polynomial is homogeneous, so an integer scaling of 126 is harmless. Its 4³=64 cases are evaluated modulo 191 by the Lean kernel. The actual theorem is `radau_triple`.
2. For **every** divisor P of XT of degree at most two, at every nonzero root u of T the zero-constant primitive J satisfies J(XP)(u)≠0. Normalize P by its leading coefficient and use the monic integral scaling of the factor XT. The integral moment 12J(XP)(u) commutes with ring homomorphisms. Reduction of the factor and its complementary factor is justified by the integral-closure theorem for monic divisors. Over the split good prime 191, there are 16 possible monic factors of degrees 0,1,2 from the five roots including 0. The 16×4 checks all succeed. This is `radau_divisor_first_moment`; the Python file is only an independent replay of the kernel-checked data.
3. On an actual strictly lower order-nine tableau with five on nodes, assume the aggregate off weights vanish, the fourth defect space is the chain through the quadratic defect, and the degree-at-most-two head P matches that defect at all off rows. Then P **does not divide** the actual on-node hull; even linear factors are excluded. The five-on quadrature gives hull X T. At the first nonzero on row i, each earlier on row is at zero, and every off row obeys the fitting identity. Hence X P agrees on the entire earlier prefix with the clock times the defect. The actual quartic chain forces the incoming action on that clock-defect signal to vanish at i, and gives the exact primitive rule there. Consequently J(XP)(c_i)=0, contradicting item 2. This is `order9_five_on_head_not_dvd`, with `first_nonzero_clean_test` isolating the chronological step.

## The actual N=7 branch is impossible

For a strict twelve-stage order-nine tableau, N denotes the number of distinct abscissas with a nonzero quadratic defect (not the number of rows with nonzero defect), and M counts distinct abscissas with zero defect, counting the two branches separately. The exact theorem `RKCert.FixedFactor.order9_no_seven_off` proves N≠7 under just strict lower triangularity and tree order at least nine, over every characteristic-zero field. This excludes a chart, not every twelve-stage tableau and not a new total stage-count bound.

Suppose N=7. The established fourth-order profile gives a head P fitting every off row with degree at most two. Its off aggregates vanish because seven weighted characteristic-zero moments are paid. The on quadrature needs M≥5; since there are only twelve stages M=5. Thus P does not divide the monic five-on hull Q by the actual first-clean test above.

Here is a short rank argument that avoids having to compute a quotient-algebra multiplication rank. Let R be the monic hull of the seven off nodes. In the actual grade-seven tree span there is an 11-dimensional subspace of profiles p(c)+g q(c), with deg p≤4 and deg q≤5: evaluating a kernel relation on the five on sites first kills p, and evaluating on the seven off sites then kills q. If the seventh-grade space had dimension only 11, both Q(c) and cQ(c) would be in that subspace. Their interpolants p vanish on all five on sites, so p=0 in both. It follows that there are T,U of degree at most five and scalars a,b with Q−PT=aR, XQ−PU=bR. Indeed, each left side vanishes at all seven off sites and has degree at most seven. A degree-at-most-seven polynomial with those seven roots is a scalar multiple of R (for instance, interpolate at one additional field point).

As P does not divide Q, a is nonzero. The first equality then forces deg P=2. Eliminating Q in the two equalities shows that P divides R(aX−b). Since P is nonzero at every off site it is coprime to R, so P divides aX−b: impossible for a quadratic if a≠0. Therefore the seventh-grade space has full dimension twelve. All degree-one backward defects vanish, and the nonzero last weight forces the last node to be one. Moreover, seven off and five on states in twelve rows make the evaluation space of all branch-state functions all twelve-dimensional; a nonzero weighted row therefore cannot be off, since each off aggregate vanishes. The last node would be an on node at one, contradicted by the five-on quadrature. This gives N≠7. The rank calculation is isolated in `seven_off_rank`, the exact interpolation obstruction in `seven_off_pair_obstruction`. No pairwise distinct physical-stage assumption was inserted.
