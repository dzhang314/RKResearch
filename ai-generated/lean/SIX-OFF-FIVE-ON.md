# Order nine: six off abscissas force six on abscissas

For a strictly lower twelve-stage tableau of tree order at least nine over **any characteristic-zero field**, if exactly six distinct numerical abscissas occur at rows where the quadratic defect is nonzero, then exactly six occur at rows with zero defect. The branches are counted separately: the same numerical abscissa may occur on both branches. This rules out (N,M)=(6,5), **not** (6,6), an arbitrary twelve-stage method, or any new total stage lower bound. No physical row is required to be distinct and individual weights may vanish.

The checked statements are `RKCert.FixedFactor.order9_no_six_off_five_on` and `RKCert.FixedFactor.order9_six_off_forces_six_on`. They strengthen the conditional generic rank statement `five_on_state_le` and use a short terminal argument `one_slot_terminal`.

## General state-space rank

Let q≥4. Suppose there are five on nodes and q+2 off nodes, and a polynomial head P of degree at most two agrees with the nonzero quadratic defects at all off sites. Let Q be the monic five-on hull, and assume P does not divide Q. Then **every branch-state function belongs to the actual tree space E_(q+2)**. A branch-state function can prescribe a value separately for each distinct on node and each distinct off node; two different rows in the same branch and node get the same value.

To prove this, take the intersection of the whole branch-state space with E_(q+2). It contains an injective 5+(q+1) dimensional base, spanned by evaluations p(c)+g r(c), deg p≤4 and deg r≤q. An element of the kernel has p=0 by the five distinct on nodes, and r=0 by the q+2 off nodes with nonzero weights g (here g is the defect, **not** the output weight). If there were no extra dimension, Q(c) and cQ(c) would lie in that base: both are genuine scalar profiles of degree at most q+2 and are themselves state-valued. The on sites again kill their degree-four parts. There must be T,U of degree at most q and scalars a,b such that Q−PT=aR and XQ−PU=bR, R the monic off hull of degree q+2. This follows by vanishing on its q+2 distinct roots and the degree bound. Since P does not divide Q, a≠0. Elimination gives P dividing R(aX−b), hence P dividing aX−b since P is nonzero at every root of R. Thus deg P≤1. But then deg(Q−PT)<q+2, contradicting a≠0. One new dimension suffices: there are exactly 5+(q+2) independent branch states. This proof uses no quotient-algebra implementation, positivity or nonrepeat assumption.

## Direct terminal contradiction without a stage merge

In the actual order-nine six-off/five-on profile the established low-degree ranks give the fitted head and fourth-order chain, and the off aggregate weights vanish. The preceding Radau result `order9_five_on_head_not_dvd` excludes P dividing Q. Apply the general state-space theorem with q=4. The state space has dimension eleven and lies in E6. The existing order-nine stage budget gives dim E6≤11; therefore E6 **equals** the state space.

Here is a shorter replacement for a duplicate-pair/merge argument. For any positive-stage explicit tableau, if the backward budget pays j+3≤p, the last weight is nonzero, and at most one ambient dimension remains outside E_j, the existing backward growth theorem forces the linear adjoint residual to vanish. The first backward gain therefore fills Ann E_j. The actual last stage has clock one, and every row in this backward space vanishes on its last coordinate. By the perfect pairing the terminal coordinate vector e_last lies in E_j. No cancellation by an unknown coefficient is used.

Apply this at j=6. Because e_last is in the state space, a nonzero last weight precludes an off final row: an off state singled out by e_last would have that nonzero aggregate weight, impossible. The final node one must be on. The exact five-on-node order-nine quadrature forbids one. Thus (6,5) is impossible. The remaining six-off case must have ≥5 on nodes by quadrature and ≤6 by the physical-row count, hence exactly six.
