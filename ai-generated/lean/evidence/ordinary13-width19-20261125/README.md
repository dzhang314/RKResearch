# The nonzero-first-seed order-thirteen bound

The theorem `twenty_le_of_order_thirteen_of_firstSeed_ne_zero` proves at least twenty stages for any strictly lower triangular tableau satisfying the reviewed `HasOrder 13 A b` over a field of characteristic zero, provided the first reverse seed is nonzero.

The grade-three port forces the first three grades of defects into the clock-invariant core. The resulting two possible head radii are excluded on the actual tableau. At radius three, the third paid square shift lowers the profile degree to three, so eight clocks suffice for the factorization and no separate class census is needed. The zero-first-seed branch is not covered.

The twelve modules in the accompanying order, excluding the audit module, were compiled and independently replayed with Lean 4.36.0 and Mathlib 4.36.0 (Mathlib `fc4f59e577feb1a4956671bc04ac18aaaf8cd842`). All fourteen named statements in the axiom audit use only the standard Lean axioms.
