# Paired clock steps at two paid heights

For an explicit tableau over a characteristic-zero field, a saturated
measured reverse capacity and an independently paid cap at the next
reverse grade give explicit clock step identities for the actual
forward and reverse spaces. The theorem needs both paid heights and
their capacity hypotheses. It requires no terminal mark. It does not
assert an order-18 physical frontier or a new stage lower bound.

The exact [source](../../RKCert/ReviewedNative/PaidClockCapacityStaircase.lean)
and [typed no-output audit](../../AuditReviewedNativePaidClockCapacityStaircase.lean)
both passed Lean 4.36.0 on the pinned Mathlib family.
The [audit output](typed-audit-output.txt) lists only the three standard axioms.
