# Defect clocks and exact reverse capacity

At a paid midpoint of minimal forward defect rank, once the
grade-three clock chain is known, the actual quadratic residual
detects precisely the clean rows. Two nonzero entries of the residual
at unequal clocks suffice to obtain that base. Minimum rank alone is
not used as a substitute. At a live paid endpoint at clock one this
restricts the location and number of other nonzero clean clocks.

Independently, if the measured reverse capacity is attained then both
reverse flags equal the top annihilator, and they agree at each paid
grade with rank equal to the initial rank plus the grade. The basic
exactness theorem uses the reviewed order predicate over a field and
does not require the first seed to vanish.

All four modules compiled and were individually kernel-replayed under
Lean 4.36.0 and pinned Mathlib. Four audits reported only standard
axioms. The separate weak-five prose note contains exact partial
exclusions (degree at most two or reflection-odd). Its rational
calculation script is not a Lean certificate or a complete stage bound.
