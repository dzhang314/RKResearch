# Native explicit order twelve requires at least eighteen stages

`RKResearch.HasOrder.erk_order_twelve_stages` proves the bound for any
characteristic-zero commutative coefficient ring, including rings with
zero divisors. Its hypotheses are the exact human `HasOrder A b 12`
and strict lower triangularity on the original `Fin s` tableau. An
independent consumer excludes all physical widths at most seventeen
without a field, integral-domain, positivity, or distinct-clock premise.
A separate field theorem is also supplied.

## Final nonzero leakage branch

Let M be the actual degree-six terminal mesh, z the disjoint physical
head, Q the degree-at-most-two terminal multiplier, R the
degree-at-most-four square multiplier, δ the actual terminal coordinate,
and G the actual nonzero scalar multiple of the degree-six leak.
Off the invariant core the checked profile has
`M(c)=δ+z Q(c)` and `z²=G(c)+z R(c)`. Since the last value of z is
zero, these give `GQ=z(M−RQ)` on those rows. Divide GQ by M and
subtract the quotient's value at one. The resulting D has degree at
most deg Q, D(1)=0, and `P=GQ−MD` has degree at most six. The two
profile equations make P a paid head evaluation modulo the actual
live core. Exact T10 rank separates the whole degree-six clock
buffer from that head, so P is identically zero.

G's first nonzero ordinary probe is at four. The first four are paid
grade-eight zeros; one more would violate two-endpoint Cauchy
separation. M's probes through four are flat, since its original
degree-six defect is the terminal coordinate at clock one. At probe
`4−deg Q`, the GQ side is the leading coefficient of Q times the
first live probe of G; the MD side is its flat probe value times
D(1)=0. Hence Q=0. This would make M vanish at seven distinct
physical head knots, impossible for the nonzero degree-six mesh.
No multiplicity count, coprime gate or new large scalar calculation
is needed.

## Reusable cancellation

`RotatedClockProbe` proves, over any field:
- `polynomial_degree_cut_at_one`: ordinary division followed by
  subtracting the quotient's value at one still fits the full
  degree-q buffer.
- `probe_mul_clock_polynomial`: multiplication by a bounded clock
  polynomial is a finite convolution of the actual rotated probes.
- `rotated_probe_detects_quotient`: if G's first live probe is m, F is
  flat through m, deg D≤deg Q≤m, D(1)=0, and GQ=FD, then Q=0.
The application to a different order or a marked chart requires
its own actual paid profile and quotient identity.
