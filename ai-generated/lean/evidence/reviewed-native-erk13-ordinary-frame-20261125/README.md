# Native order-13 paid frame and ordinary staircase

Over a field of characteristic zero, strict triangularity and the reviewed `HasOrder 13 A b` force at least nineteen stages. In exactly nineteen stages they give the actual paid rank data and the flat-clock head used in the first-escape argument. When the first reverse seed is nonzero, the ordinary tree spaces in original stage coordinates have ranks 14, 15, 16 and 17 at indices 7, 8, 9 and 10. Each of the three transitions is the sum of the preceding tree space and its image under multiplication by the abscissae. These results do not assert the next step of the staircase or rule out all nineteen-stage methods.

Both proof modules were compiled and separately replayed with Lean 4.36.0 and Mathlib 4.36.0 (Mathlib commit `fc4f59e577feb1a4956671bc04ac18aaaf8cd842`). The report records the axioms of five named statements.
