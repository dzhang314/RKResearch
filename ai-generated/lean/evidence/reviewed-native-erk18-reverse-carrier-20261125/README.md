# Order 18: an actual rank-nine reverse carrier

The [Lean theorem](../../RKCert/ReviewedNative/ERK18ReverseCarrier.lean)
uses a strictly explicit tableau over a characteristic-zero field and the
reviewed `HasOrder 18` predicate. It takes the original last active index,
a vanishing first adjoint seed, support of the quadratic adjoint seed on
exactly one reflected clock, and a dimension cap of nine on the grade-eight
tree annihilator cut by vanishing at that index. These are hypotheses, not
conclusions about all order-18 tableaux.

Under these hypotheses the actual grade-eight reverse flag equals that
cut annihilator and has dimension nine. The actual flags at grades four
through eight have dimensions five through nine. They are generated by
one consecutive clock orbit over the same invariant subspace of
dimension one to three. The theorem retains the original terminal and
tail conditions and gives the corresponding strict escape-site count.
It does not derive the seed or room hypotheses or prove a stage lower
bound.

The body and the unmodified [typed audit](../../RKCert/ReviewedNative/ERK18ReverseCarrierAudit.lean)
both exited zero with Lean 4.36.0 and the pinned Mathlib revision. The body
emitted nine nonfatal lint messages. The separate no-output audit
elaborated five typed clients. Its [raw output](typed-audit-output.txt)
contains four reports, all using only the standard axioms `propext`,
`Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`. The [actual axiom lists](actual-axioms.tsv),
[acceptance ledger](acceptance.tsv), and [34 exact reviewed provider sources](reviewed-providers.sha256)
record the checked cut. No compiled artifacts are included.
