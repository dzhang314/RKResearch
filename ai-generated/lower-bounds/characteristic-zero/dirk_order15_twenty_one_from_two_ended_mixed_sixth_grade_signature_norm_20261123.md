# Candidate unrestricted order-fifteen floor: at least 21 physical DIRK stages

Arbitrary signed and individually zero output weights, repeated or exterior abscissae, and mixed/zero diagonals are retained. This is a lower bound, not an existence claim. ## Terminal deletion and the two real numerical growth alternatives (valid over every field)
