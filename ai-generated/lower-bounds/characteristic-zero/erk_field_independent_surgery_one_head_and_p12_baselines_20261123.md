## 2. Rank-one diagonal escape, without Euclidean projection

Let W be finite dimensional in a coordinate space over K. Suppose dim(W+CW)=dim W+1. Put W_i={w∈W:C^k w∈W for 0≤k≤i}. The map C:W_i/W_(i+1)→W_(i−1)/W_i is injective for i≥1: CW_i⊂W_(i−1), and Cx∈W_i for x∈W_i implies x∈W_(i+1). Since the first loss has size one, each subsequent loss is one until the first plateau. That plateau equals the maximal C-invariant U⊂W. If r=dim(W/U), take w∈W_(r−1) outside U. For 0≤k<r, C^kw lies in W_(r−1−k) but not W_(r−k), since otherwise C^rw∈W. The r successive quotient pivots give
    W=U ⊕ span(w,Cw,…,C^(r−1)w).
No assumption on C was needed for this decomposition. If C is diagonal, U splits on its node blocks; choose within each block a complement and subtract w's U component. The retained head is supported on precisely its *effective* nodes (those where w has nonzero class modulo U). Thus all later shift rank counts are ordinary Vandermonde on those nodes.

Diagonal multiplier refinement. If diagonal D satisfies DW⊂U+wP_(r−1+d) and N≥r+d+1 effective nodes, for h∈U confined to one node the polynomial part of Dh vanishes modulo U at ≥N−1>r−1+d effective nodes, so Dh∈U. For a finite family of such Ds choose a single complement S of U invariant under C and every D, using the coordinate blocks indexed by their joint values: interpolation gives the joint projectors and U decomposes there. (For any prescribed family, finitely many of the coordinate distinctions suffice.) Project the chosen w into S along U. All relevant chain spans plus U are unchanged; now Dw=P(C)w literally, and the successive comparisons of D C^k w against C^k P(C)w at N nodes prove deg P≤d, just as in §7 of worker_log_standalone. If D=diag(v), v∈W and Dw=0, then v∈U: the zero-eigenvalue joint projector kills v as a coordinate vector but fixes w, so in v=u+R(C)w it gives R(C)w∈U∩S=0. For a particular proof requiring fixed w simultaneously for several multipliers, take the joint complement before deriving its multipliers' support restrictions; the set of diagonal operators fulfilling those image inclusions does not depend on w's chosen U component.

## 3. Surgery, Gram ranks, and genuinely real gates

The pair/tree expansion by number of row-surgery marks, the vanishing of one-mark rows in K=Ann V_(N−d), factorization of all allowed two-mark bilinear forms through K/F_d, and the actual zero-column deletion and node-independent earlier interpolation of §§1–3 of erk_distinct_node_ghost_outgoing_energy_and_sdirk_midpoint_activation_20261123.md are polynomial and linear-algebra identities over K. The compact rational tableaux in erk_submidpoint_surgeries_to_exact_six_and_seven_stage_minima_20261123.md and the unique-exterior-site controls likewise remain examples over every characteristic-zero field. For SDIRK the projection involving z=Ae_j retains its diagonal as part of z; only the auxiliary deleted row need lose its diagonal.

The numerical sign-of-inertia payment in erk_submidpoint_signed_surgery_and_ghost_receiver_payments_20261123.md is real (or real-closed); an arbitrary field need not represent zero in an indefinite real-looking diagonal form. Field-safe replacement: a quadratic/bilinear space holding a nondegenerate hyperbolic plane has its isotropic vectors spanning the space. With isotropic e,f and B(e,f)=1, a vector x perpendicular to that plane is a linear combination via x+e−[B(x,x)/2]f, which is isotropic; the radical also consists of isotropic vectors. Therefore in a suffix space S of future pivot-zero directions, if every isotropic vector lies in the next-coordinate hyperplane S', and S' is proper, the quotient by the radical must be anisotropic and the radical must lie in S'. Over algebraically closed K this forces rank exactly one. Moreover across a nested family of suffixes there is at most one nonpivot leading-coordinate exception for a fixed quadratic form: at its earliest exception S has rank exactly one, all its isotropic vectors are its radical, and this radical equals the codimension-one S' (hence all later suffix forms vanish). This is about admissible null vectors over the stated field, not merely rank over a closure.

Caution: positivity at *unknown* variable endpoints must be audited independently. The unrestricted normalized two-endpoint 3+2 determinant for a quartic head is t^6(t−1)^6(15t²−24t+10)/8, up to row/column normalizations; its last factor vanishes at (12±i sqrt(6))/15. There is a nonzero complex polynomial in the corresponding nullspace, so this isolated test does not automatically transfer. This is not a complex RK tableau or counterexample to a stage bound. By contrast, invertibility of a *fixed rational* Gram matrix via its positive real specialization, fixed prescribed Gauss/Lobatto nodes and all-pairs algebraic certificates, and Vandermonde distinctness can validly transfer. For feasibility of a finite rational RKOC polynomial system with combinatorial inequalities expressed by nonvanishing, nonexistence over C implies nonexistence over every characteristic-zero field (embed the finitely generated coefficient subfield, or use the Nullstellensatz after imposing inverse variables).
