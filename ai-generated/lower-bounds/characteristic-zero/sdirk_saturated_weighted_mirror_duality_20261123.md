# Exact bidirectional flag duality at nonsingular RK stage saturation

Scope: classical Runge–Kutta order p with a nonsingular real lower triangular A and no sign condition on the weights. Assume p is even or p≡3 mod 4, and s equals the known nonsingular quadratic lower value (p=2m: m(m+1)/2; p=2m−1: ceil(m²/2)). The flag equalities below do not assume a common diagonal. Equal-diagonal γ≠0 is required only where explicitly stated. These are endpoint-equality restrictions, not an increase in the unconditional single-output lower table. See also sdirk_invertible_commutator_quadratic_barrier_20261123.md and rk_bidirectional_causal_window_capacity_20261123.md.

**Forward and reverse scalar words.** Write N=p−1, c=A1, C=diag c, b a row and B=diag b. The numerical forward E_k and zero-profile forward F_k use all stage tree pairs (v,H), with coordinate multiplication and integration A lifting the corresponding polynomial multiplication and JH(x)=∫₀ˣH. On the reverse side use only scalar words: start at (b,1), right multiply by A with profile operation J*K(x)=∫ₓ¹K, or by C with multiplication by x; allow linear combination and total word length ≤k. Their numerical row and zero-density spaces are W_k,Z_k, respectively. Scalar powers bH(c) are always literal words. Marked-tree grafting for these scalar contexts and any forward feature gives r v=∫₀¹ K H if their grades sum to at most N. In particular Z_j annihilates E_(N−j) and F_j is annihilated by W_(N−j). This does not presuppose that any normalized transposed scheme is of classical order p.

Put R=CA−AC−A²; R is invertible lower triangular with diagonal −a_ii². It sends E_(k−2) into F_k. On the right it sends W_(k−2) into Z_k: since the right action uses J* and x, the operator is J*(xK)−xJ*K−J*²K=0. Taking min(k,N−k) scalar powers in both directions and using positivity of the continuous integral of the square of a polynomial modulo the pure spaces gives both e_k=dim E_k and w_k=dim W_k at least t_k, where t_(−2)=t_(−1)=0 and
    t_k=1+min(k,N−k)+t_(k−2).
The parity choice on p gives t_N=t_(N−1)=s. Therefore every inequality in BOTH recurrences is an equality at every k: e_k=w_k=t_k, and f_k=dim F_k=dim Z_k=t_(k−2) (scalar disjointness gives the upper bound). In particular F_k=R E_(k−2) and Z_k=W_(k−2) R for k≥2. The symmetry of the increment gives
    s−t_(N−k)=t_(k−2)       (0≤k≤N),
starting at k=0,1 and advancing two indices. By dimensions and orthogonality,
    Z_k=Ann(E_(N−k)),       F_k=Ann(W_(N−k)).
Although W_k used single-letter multipliers C and A only, its pure part Z_k therefore already contains every arbitrary zero-density one-mark context through that budget.

**Weighted mirror and enriched integration.** For every f∈F_k the row fᵀB annihilates E_(N−k): multiply its zero-profile forward witness with the test feature at the output. Thus fᵀB∈Z_k, even when some weights vanish. In particular, for p≥4 the degree-two g=Ac−c²/2 has
    gᵀB=−g_s/a_ss² · bR .
Indeed Z₂=span bR, b_s≠0 (trim a zero terminal weight against the lower bound), and (bR)_s=−b_s a_ss². This is an exact adjoint-proportionality condition, not the false statement that D₂ must vanish. If g_s=0 it instead says every nonzero-weight stage satisfies C(2).

If all b_i≠0, the equal dimensions give the full isomorphism B F_k=Z_kᵀ. It identifies the actual PAIRS at each grade: for fixed H, the forward possibilities are H(c)+F_k, while reverse scalar context rows have fixed-density possibilities bH(C)+Z_k. Therefore every W_k context with scalar density K has a forward representative v with vᵀB equal to that context and profile K, and conversely. It follows that the method with coefficients B⁻¹AᵀB, weights b and reversed chronological order is itself a classical order-p DIRK with reflected nodes 1−c. To see this without invoking any generic RK-adjoint assertion, identify the new time variable as y=1−x. Start the new tree at 1, with forward profile 1. Products in the transposed method are the original coordinate products and so preserve represented profiles. For an integration, a represented column v of grade j has vᵀB a reverse scalar context with represented density K(x); (Abar v)ᵀB=vᵀB A, since Abar=B⁻¹AᵀB. This is a reverse scalar context with density J*K. At the next grade the isomorphism turns it back into a forward pair with precisely that density. Since J*K(x) is the forward primitive in y of K(1−y), induction proves correct reflected profiles through grade N. Original quadrature applied at the final output therefore proves all the transposed RKOC. D₁=0 follows since its row annihilates E_(N−1)=R^s, giving the stated reflected nodes. Neither normalization nor the conclusion about a complete transposed method is asserted when any weight vanishes.

Equivalently, with all weights nonzero the original stage-profile calculus through grade N is also closed under the matrix A#=1b−B⁻¹AᵀB acting as the same primitive J, since subtraction of J*K from the constant ∫₀¹K gives JK. Thus every fixed affine combination of A and A# has order p, with the same weights and abscissae (these combinations are not generally triangular). More strongly, integration at separate vertices may independently use A or A# through order p. This mixed-tree conclusion follows by exactly the same induction. It is not the assertion that an arbitrary nonsingular-diagonal RK method is symplectic.

**Both ends of a saturated strict SDIRK.** Assume now all diagonals equal γ≠0 and p≥6 in the tied classes. No nonzero *interior* weight assumption is needed in this paragraph. In addition to [A,R]1=0 at the front, we have b[A,R]=0. In fact b[A,R]∈Z₃, because bAR and bRA are reverse scalar pure rows of grade three. Also Z₃=span(bR,bCR), since W₁=span(b,bC) by D₁=0. The last two coordinates of b and bC are independent: b_s≠0 and D₁ forces a_(s,s−1) b_s=(c_s−c_(s−1)) b_(s−1), where c_s=1−γ. If b_(s−1)=0 it and all reverse words vanish there, so W_N would not have rank s. If c_(s−1)=c_s then a_(s,s−1)=0 and the two-coordinate restrictions of all reverse words remain proportional to the original b restriction, again impossible. Thus b_(s−1)≠0, c_(s−1)≠c_s and a_(s,s−1)≠0. The triangular trailing block of R is invertible, so bR,bCR are likewise independent on the last two coordinates. But [A,R]=[A−γI,R+γ²I] is a commutator of two strictly lower triangular matrices, hence has its first two diagonals zero. Consequently b[A,R] vanishes on the last two coordinates and has to vanish everywhere. (At the front the analogous argument uses E₁ and the first two coordinates, which cannot coincide in a minimal strict method.)

Expansion two indices off the diagonal gives the common chronological factor, up to an irrelevant global sign,
    [A,R]_(i+2,i)=a_(i+2,i+1) a_(i+1,i) (2c_(i+1)−c_i−c_(i+2)).
Hence each consecutive triple at the corresponding outer edge with both neighbor taps nonzero is an arithmetic progression. In fact, for length-three windows there is an alternative sharp affine condition. Let y₁=1−c_s=γ, y₂=1−c_(s−1), y₃=1−c_(s−2). The terminal triple satisfies
    y₃=2y₂−γ   OR   a_(s−1,s−2)=0 and y₂+y₃=4γ.
For the second alternative, D₁ and reverse irreducibility imply b_(s−2)≠0: otherwise a_(s,s−2)=0 also and that column is absent from every reverse word. With the last three weights nonzero, normalize the reversed three-coordinate block in order s,s−1,s−2 by their weights. It is a strictly chronological three-row SDIRK with common γ, row sums y_i by D₁, though not assumed to be an order-p method. Its row-three middle tap is zero. Also y₂,y₃ differ from γ and from each other (otherwise two of the three single-parent rows coincide on all normalized scalar words, contradicting the rank of W_N on the suffix). Write ĝ=Ây−y²/2 on the three coordinates; ĝ₁=γ²/2. The global pure rows corresponding (after this normalization and reversal) to (y−γ)ĝ and (Â−γI)ĝ lie in Z₃ and vanish at the first reversed coordinate. They are proportional, with ratio ĝ₂/ĝ₁ from the second coordinate, since y₂≠γ and the dimension of Z₃ is two. The three-coordinate normalization is legitimate even without inverse weights anywhere else: all entries in the chosen suffix of a row word depend only on that suffix. On row three, with a sole predecessor, this gives ĝ₃=ĝ₂. Directly 2ĝ=γ²+2γu−u² at each one-parent row with u=y_i−γ. Since y₂≠y₃, equality implies y₂+y₃=4γ. The same two alternatives at the first end hold with c₁=γ,c₂,c₃ and tap a₃₂ by the earlier forward calculation. Thus saturation imposes an independently testable three-node incidence at BOTH ends, including in signed and zero-weight charts. This is not nonexistence at the minimum count.
