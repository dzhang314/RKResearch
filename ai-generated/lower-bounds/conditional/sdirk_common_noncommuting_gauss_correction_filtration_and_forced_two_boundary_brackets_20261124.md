# A common physical filtration for noncommuting Gauss corrections, sharp two-ended leading updates, and unavoidable all-depth brackets

24 November 2026. Classical RK only. At every tied nonsingular triangular quadratic equality the actual forward Gauss-subtracted identity, primitive and clock (normalized by the single physical inverse R^-1) jointly lower one ordinary filtration by 2, 1, 1. Quadratic corrected words preserve it. Zero output weights, arbitrary signs and repeated or exterior abscissae present no exception. An explicit per-cut rank allowance applies at EVERY stage count. For strict order 20 at 55 stages the stack of the three lowering operators has actual rank at least 50, not just 45 for the identity correction alone. Two independent endpoint transfers are nonzero in the same primitive correction. In the strict tied case p≥7, a single explicit quadratic-word pencil can be chosen so EVERY iterated bracket with the primitive correction has a nonzero rank-two numerical minor, however deeply nested, even though all these brackets strictly lower the same filtration. This does not improve the unconditional minimum 55.

## Definitions, grades and the simultaneous transfer

Set N=p−1, D=p−2, m=ceil(p/2), L=ceil(floor((p+1)^2/4)/2). Tied means p even or p=3 mod 4. The boundary statements below are at s=L. The matrices are physical ordinary RK matrices: b is a row, B=diag(b), e=1, c=Ae, C=diag(c), g=Ac−c²/2, G=diag(g), R=CA−AC−A². R is lower triangular with diagonal −a_ii² and is invertible whenever the triangular RK tableau is nonsingular. A normalized correction containing R^-1 is a numerical postprocessing of physical coefficients, NOT a claim that this inverse costs zero as an RK tree.

Let V=P_(m−1) with pairing ⟨h,k⟩=∫₀¹ hk. Take any polynomial basis ordered by degree, let X evaluate it at the physical c, let H be its continuous Hilbert Gram, and Y=H^-1 XᵀB. Then YX=I by ordinary quadrature since 2m−2≤N. Write π for the continuous orthogonal projection to V (algebraically, Hilbert projection; real positivity is unnecessary). The scalar shadows of A,C are T_A=πJ, T_C=πx on V. The identity shadow is I; the shadow of an ordered word in A,C is the product of the corresponding T's in exactly that order. For a word P of length k=0,1,2 put
    U_P=R^-1(P−X T_P Y).
Thus U_0 means the identity word; U_A and U_C mean the two one-letter words. By linearity one may use an inhomogeneous polynomial of grade at most k with its same linear combination of shadows. A genuine zero-profile operator such as G of grade 2 can be used with zero shadow. All these matrices exist with B singular.

Write E_j for the ordinary stage feature space through grade j and W_j for the reverse scalar A,C-word row space through grade j; E_i=0 for i<0. Tied saturation supplies e_j=w_j=t_j, t_{−2}=t_{−1}=0, t_j=t_(j−2)+1+min(j,N−j), t_D=L, and s−t_(N−j)=t_(j−2). At larger counts the same lower bounds hold. This is the nonsingular R recurrence: R sends the previous ordinary space injectively into pure features at +2, and the scalar Hilbert pairing separates 1,x,…,x^min(j,N−j) there. The reverse recurrence is identical. In particular R E_(j−2) always annihilates W_(N−j) and at tied equality fills that whole annihilator. Sources: sdirk_saturated_weighted_mirror_duality_20261123.md and sdirk_actual_commutator_jordan_and_first_unpaid_midpoint_product_rank_20261123.md.

The substantive operator statement at equality is
    U_P E_a ⊂ E_(a+k−2)   (0≤a≤D, k=0,1,2).                         (1)
For a+k=D+2 only the codomain E_D=whole space is required. More precisely at EVERY count with tied p and any j≤N, the images U_P E_(j−k), for ANY number of the listed corrected operators with the indicated degrees k≤j, have JOINT rank modulo E_(j−2) at most
    s−dim W_(N−j)−dim E_(j−2) ≤ s−L.                                (2)
All images are inside the very same R^-1 Ann W_(N−j), which already contains E_(j−2); (2) counts their extra directions ONCE. At the boundary j=N+1, the quotient by E_D has dimension at most s−L without a moment claim. For untied orders the same actual-dimension inequality holds when j≤N, with t_(N−j),t_(j−2) used separately rather than the tied simplification.

Here is the shadow cancellation proof. If x∈E_a,w∈W_b have scalar profiles h,k_0 and a+b+k≤N, then
    w(P−X T_P Y)x=0.                                                 (3)
For a,b≤m−1, Yx gives the exact coefficients of h and wX gives the exact pairing with k_0. In the scalar word, insert π between all letters: at each boundary the degree bounds to its two sides sum to ≤N≤2m−1, so at least one side lies in V and inserting π does not change the pairing. Already-inserted projections never increase either degree bound; the adjoints are multiplication by x or integration from x to 1. If a≥m, then b+k≤m−1, so moving the entire scalar word onto the small test gives precisely P_scalar^* k_0 of degree ≤b+k. The remaining Yx need not have ALL correct high coefficients: its pairing with this particular polynomial is exactly b(P_scalar^* k_0)(c)⊙x, paid since a+b+k≤N. The case b≥m is dual: h is small enough that T_P h is the exact polynomial P_scalar h, of degree ≤a+k, and the resulting wX contraction is paid. Both exterior grades ≥m cannot occur. A zero-profile physical operator in place of P makes wPx itself zero. Thus (3) uses no off-budget order condition. Combine it with the preceding R-annihilator equality/containment to obtain (1),(2).

Consequently ANY product of these physical matrices of total drop >D, giving U_0 price 2, U_A,U_C price 1, and normalized grade-two corrections price 0, vanishes at equality: factors may occur in ANY order, and arbitrarily many preserving factors may be inserted. Every positive-drop product has trace zero and is nilpotent. The entire mixed statement is stronger than every scalar pencil being nilpotent: the abstract 3×3 pair in the replay has all its scalar combinations nilpotent but a nonzero trace of (UV)². At larger counts, for a fixed product of h factors whose cumulative drops FIRST exceed D at the end, rank of the whole product is at most (h+1)(s−L). Start in E_D with at most s−L missing dimensions, then apply (2) at each step; errors carried forward cannot grow in dimension under a single fixed linear map. Further arbitrary factors on its left cannot increase rank. In particular one can price a mixed product numerically by s≥L+ceil(rank/(h+1)) in this situation. Do not infer the same allowance for a span of independently propagating products without counting their joint error directions.

An arbitrary polynomial in Z=R^-1G preserves the flags at equality. Its primary spectral projectors are polynomials too, including when several physical defects coincide and Z has nontrivial Jordan blocks. Hence they can be inserted between the factors above, at no filtration drop. For distinct eigenvalues, each nonzero entry of U_A or U_C between corresponding numerical spectral lines descends at least one actual entry grade; U_0 descends two. The graph on simple spectral lines has no directed cycle of positive total descent. For repeated eigenvalues the compression can be nonzero: it is the cycles' positive-drop PRODUCTS that are nilpotent with zero trace, not individual diagonal entries in an arbitrary eigenbasis.

Let M=BA+AᵀB−bᵀb and W=diag(bR). Direct scalar integration by parts gives H T_A+T_Aᵀ H=v vᵀ for v=the vector of integrals of the chosen basis; vᵀY=b since 1 lies in V. Thus at EVERY count satisfying scalar quadrature,

## Two cheap genuinely independent nonlinear M receivers: a separate no-inverse test

At EVERY tied strict p≥6 put q=2c²−Ac. Recall the separately established physical identities cᵀM=α w, w=qᵀM+cᵀM(A−C) and w=bR≠0. If α=0 the grade-two probe qᵀM=w is nonzero; otherwise cᵀM≠0.
