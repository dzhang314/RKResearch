# Ordinary DIRK order fourteen: the fixed reverse determinant can be bypassed

## Claim and exact scope

The argument below proves that a lower-triangular Runge–Kutta tableau satisfying the denominator-cleared order-fourteen equations over any nonzero commutative unital ring has at least nineteen stages, conditional only on the audited paid grade-six/native-count result and the previously audited two fixed q=7 ERK norms. It uses no assumption on the signs or nonvanishing of individual weights, ordering or multiplicity of abscissae, or diagonal. The existing characteristic-zero proof is [the characteristic-zero proof](../characteristic-zero/dirk_order14_nineteen_from_paid_quartic_and_fifth_debut_20261123.md). Two changes yield the paid scope: the final physical two-omitted-root chronology, and an ordinary one-head reduction that bypasses the 140-case reverse determinant instead of presuming its individual integer norm is a unit.

The tall tree pays 14!, so reduce to any residue field. Work over a field of characteristic zero or prime characteristic greater than fourteen, and, for splitting the fixed scalar polynomial if needed, extend to an algebraic closure. Formal primitives from zero are denoted J, and evaluation of a primitive at 1 is the ordinary moment. All Beta moment matrices below are paid by the adjacent-row induction in `dirk_paid_grade_six_and_finite_scope_inventory_20261126.md`; a matrix for x^a(1−x)^b on degree <h needs factorial cutoff a+b+2h−1. In particular no assertion about positivity of the square of an unknown coefficient polynomial is used.

## 3. A grade-four escape is a scalar factor

Assume v∈F4\U. Since C²v∈F6, comparison in the independent head powers gives v=u_v+w R(C), R≠0, deg R≤r−3; hence r≥3. For each j=0,…,r−1 the product v⊙C^j w is F10 and terminal-zero. There are at least three consecutive shifts, so the two-direction leakage is zero by Section 2. Products by v of localized U vectors have all cheap shifts as well. These are node-local head/core vectors; their would-be head polynomial of degree ≤r+3 has ≥r+4 other effective zeros, so they lie in U. Thus diag(v) preserves U, and fitting its action on r consecutive head powers gives a polynomial P of degree ≤4: the E10 head cap is r+3 and each consecutive multiplication drops one from the permitted degree. Take the head in a complement to U on each joint block of (c_i,v_i), by subtracting its component there. Then diag(v)w=P(C)w literally. This joint split is available also in a finite field, by interpolation on the finite distinct labels.

Write d_s=u_s+w Q_s(c), deg Q_s≤r. At an effective head block with P(c)≠0, v=P(c)·1 and v=u_v+wR imply P(c)[1]=R(c)[w]. It does not contain the terminal, whose v value is zero. Thus the terminal representation gives G(c)[1]+Q_s(c)[w]=0. At a head clock where P(c)=0 the first relation forces R(c)=0; the same scalar identity follows. At all ≥r+5 effective head clocks, therefore,
  G R+P Q_s=0.
Its degree is at most r+4, so this is a polynomial identity and P divides G R. Since R≠0, P is nonzero. In the r=3 case R is constant and P is a quartic divisor of G. In the r=4 case, if R is constant then P has degree three or four and divides G; if R is affine, P is quartic and either divides G or is (x−ρ)S, with R=k(x−ρ), k≠0, and S a monic cubic divisor of G. Scale v and P together in the last case to make P monic. The root ρ may repeat a root in S; it is not any of the omitted roots of G/S in the nondividing case.

## 5. Paid closures of the cubic, fixed quartic, and affine cases

**Cubic fit.** This can occur only when r=4 and R is constant, so dim U=1 and U=F3. The first masked contrast x(P−v) has grade at most five including U compensation. Every x^j v for j≤3 is actually in F6 by the constant R and F6=U+wP3, so its polynomial companions below really cost ≤6. By E6 separation the masks x and x(x−u) have distinct nonzero mismatches; the first must be nonterminal, as otherwise the second would vanish modulo U despite its nonzero profile. The refund above at the first and second gives the three local moments of xP at the first and the two of x(x−u)P at the second. Scale u=1. The first three imply P is proportional to 35x³−60x²+30x−4. At t the second pivot ratio, the two residual quadratics (after cancelling nonzero endpoint factors) are 35t²−44t+12 and 30t²−35t+8. Six times the first minus seven times the second is 16−19t; if 19=0 it is itself nonzero, and otherwise substitution gives numerator −84, a paid unit. Contradiction.

**Fixed quartic divisor.** If P divides G, take the masks beginning with x when P(0)≠0, or beginning with 1 when P(0)=0, and successively multiply by the factor of each detected physical clock. There are respectively two or three distinct nonzero omitted D-root candidates, off the support and not terminal. All must occur: if fewer did, masking them all would leave only a terminal multiple modulo U but a nonzero scalar profile of degree <7, impossible by E7 separation and the exact degree-seven terminal G. Order them by their first actual distinctions. The last two masks have grade at most six, with the exception that the first may require a genuinely grade-six U compensation. The last cut always refunds using an earlier interior D-root as in Section 4. If there are three omitted roots, so does the penultimate; if there are two and U⊂F5, the penultimate is the original grade-five x cut and refunds directly. These two primitive equations are exactly I(d)=B(a,d)=0 at the last two distinct physical omissions, excluded by the norms. The only unpaid first-cut possibility here is P(0)≠0, r=3 and U not known contained in F5. Section 6 avoids that refund altogether.

**Affine nondividing quartic.** Here r=4, U=F3, and P=(x−ρ)S as in Section 3. Start masks with x if S(0)≠0 (three nonzero omitted D-roots), or 1 if S(0)=0 (four nonzero omitted D-roots). A single x is already present in S in the latter case, even if ρ=0. Every omitted root must be physically represented before terminal. Otherwise multiply by all actually present nonzero omissions. The resulting input is affordable in E7: at most three further total mask degrees and C³v∈F7. If its profile has lower degree, separation excludes a terminal multiple. At degree seven it is proportional to F(x−ρ), where G=429F(x−d) for the unique missing root d; ρ≠d since P does not divide G, so it still cannot be proportional to G. This proves physical presence of all omissions.

Let d and a be the last and penultimate, M_d the last mask, M_a=M_d/(x−a), and F=M_d S. Then G=429F(x−d) and z_(M_d) has profile F(x−ρ). Subtract e_s/429. The profile becomes (d−ρ)F. Moreover by G R+P Q_s=0 the terminal head coefficient is −429 k M_d(x−d), whereas that of z_(M_d) is −k M_d(x−ρ). After subtraction their top head powers cancel and the difference is a genuinely grade-six pair with head coefficient −k(d−ρ)M_d, whose degree is three. This is a refund of actual grade, not just of polynomial degree. Call the difference divided by d−ρ the corrected last contrast B_d. It is supported modulo U only at d and terminal. Refund the physical diagonal at d using the earlier interior zero of its commutator as in Section 4; its exact primitive gives I(d)=0. The penultimate raw mask is itself grade ≤6 and its diagonal is paid by an earlier interior root. Subtract B_d from that penultimate contrast: this still costs ≤6, and has profile (a−ρ)M_a S, while its earliest mismatch is still a. Use the already paid diagonal there to get B(a,d)=0. These are the same fixed norms. No degree-five assertion for the penultimate difference is required.

## 6. The only expensive core case uses a reverse census, not the old determinant

Here r=3, dim U=2, R is constant k≠0, and P is a quartic factor of D with P(0)≠0. The first forward core correction need not be in F5. Thus ordinary b has an active physical row at each of these eight clocks.

In the full E11 chart each joint block quotient has dimension at most one. If v=P(c) and P(c)≠0, the relation v=k w+u_v makes [1] proportional to [w]. A genuine head block there contributes one head slot. If w=0 and v≠0 then 1 is in U and full E11 forces the whole block to lie in U. If v=0, no effective w is possible when P(c)=0 (k≠0); the quotient slot, if any, is scalar. A mismatching block with v≠0 has zero quotient as well. Thus at most one head slot and one separate scalar-only slot occur per numerical clock, and both can occur only where P(c)≠0. Thus τ has at most eight head clocks plus four distinct zeros of Q, at most twelve clocks. Now use the actual reversed grade-four trace θ=bQ−τL, supported only on τ-clean, b-active rows. If F4⊂U, f4=2: if f4=3 it is itself U and C-invariant, and outgoing two-detector gain gives f5≥5, contradicting f6=5.
