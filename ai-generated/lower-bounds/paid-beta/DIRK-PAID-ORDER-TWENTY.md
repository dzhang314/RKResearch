# Ordinary DIRK of order twenty needs 28 stages over arbitrary rings

The coefficient ring is any nonzero commutative unital ring. The proof does not impose a sign or invertibility condition on the diagonal and does not use the stronger characteristic-zero bound.

## 1. Paid algebra and reversal

The tall-tree order-twenty equation makes 20! a unit. Reduce at any maximal ideal, retaining the number of rows, every rooted equation, and lower triangularity. It suffices to rule out 27 rows over fields of characteristic zero or greater than 20. Work over such a field throughout. All polynomial integrations below are formal; no ordering or sign hypothesis is used on the weights or abscissae. The initial 27-row lower floor, the strict ordinary and pure rank increases, and the paid grade-six inequality are independently audited in [the paid grade-six argument](DIRK-PAID-GRADE-SIX.md).

Set c=A1 and C=diag(c). A grade is tree order minus one. E_j denotes the numerical span of the grade-at-most-j rooted stage columns (with polynomial models), F_j its actual zero-model subspace, and f_j=dim F_j. Clock multiplication and A raise grade by one and induce X· and J, with J the primitive from zero; coordinatewise products add grades. Ordinary order integrates every such polynomial model through grade 19, and in particular kills F_19. We use f6≥f3+4, f3≥1+ε for ε=1 if g=Ac−c²/2 is nonzero and ε=0 otherwise, and strict growth thereafter. The ordinary E increase is strict at each step j→j+1 through E17. When g=0, g_1=c_1²/2 gives the actual zero first row and every pure vector vanishes there.

The fixed Beta Gram with weight X^a(1−X)^b and size h is invertible whenever a+b+2h−1 is paid, by the adjacent-row reduction in the cited audit. In particular the quadrature has at least ten distinct nonzero effective clocks in either the c or reflected y=1−c coordinate. Otherwise let V be the monic hull of the k≤9 nonzero aggregate-mass clocks. The quadrature annihilates XV R for all deg R≤k. The tests have degree ≤2k+1≤19, and the size-(k+1) X-Gram, cutoff 2k+2≤20, forces V=0. This uses aggregate masses, not the signs and not the number of individually nonzero weights.

In reverse time set N=reverse(A transposed), Y=diag(y) in that coordinate order, and v=reverse(b) as a column. Define D_k=N Y^(k−1)v−Y^k v/k. Paid polynomial integration by parts gives D_k⊥E_(19−k); E_h is identified with its reversed coordinate copy in this pairing. After D1=D2=0 is shown, let B_j be the span of the actual D3 words of length ≤j−3 in N,Y, so B_j⊂Ann E_(19−j). These spaces grow strictly. Indeed D3 cannot be zero and a plateau would give a simultaneous invariant subspace killed by the sum functional, containing the two primitive defects D2,D3. The quotient retains lower triangular N and diagonal Y by earliest coordinate elimination within the Y fibres. The class of Yv survives because its sum is 1/2. At its first nonzero coordinate with clock y_i, the two quotient primitive equations would give the same diagonal both y_i/2 and y_i/3, impossible. Thus dim B_j≥j−2, for all the paid grades used below. This also proves the needed strictness when some individual diagonals or weights vanish.

## 2. Exact rank and endpoint budgets

Assume s=27. Trimming trailing zero b entries would violate the already proved 27-row floor, so b_s≠0. Ordinary degree-nine polynomials separate from F9: for a numerical polynomial in F9 use all ordinary moment tests of degree at most nine (grade≤18), and the size-ten unweighted Gram of cutoff 19. If f9≥9, then dim E9≥19 and eight strict rank gains through E17 saturate all 27 coordinates. Thus dim E_j=j+10 for 9≤j≤17. As E17 is full, D1=D2=0. Their terminal coordinates give a_ss=y_s and a_ss y_s=y_s²/2, hence y_s=0 and a_ss=0. Therefore Ae_s=0, Ce_s=e_s, and the reverse first coordinate of D3 and of every word from it is zero.

The preceding B_j lower ranks and the exact annihilator dimensions now agree: B_j=Ann E_(19−j), with dimension j−2, for 3≤j≤10. In particular e_s∈E9. Write e_s=H(c)+z with deg H≤9 and z∈F9. Test it against b(1−c)R(c) for every deg R≤9: the left terminal term is zero since c_s=1, the z term is a paid pure product (9+10=19), and the polynomial term is I((1−X)H R). The size-ten (1−X)-Gram has cutoff 20, so H=0. This would imply b_s=0 since e_s is pure, a contradiction. Consequently f9≤8.

The paid lower f9≥f6+3≥8+ε now forces g=0, f3=1, f6=5, f7=6, f8=7, f9=8. The true zero first row is essential for the next degree. If H(c) lies in F10 and deg H≤10 then H(0)=0. Write H=XP and use every degree-at-most-nine R, with actual product grade 10+9=19. The size-ten X-Gram has cutoff 20 and gives P=0. Thus E10≥f10+11≥20. The seven strict gains through E17 again saturate all dimensions, so f10=9 and dim E_j=j+10 for 10≤j≤17. Repeating the terminal argument gives D1=D2=0, terminal y_s=a_ss=0, and B_j=Ann E_(19−j) with dimension j−2 through j=9. In particular e_s∈E10. The paid product E10⊙F9 shows F9 is terminal-zero since b_s≠0. At F11 use anchored separation of degree-nine scalar columns by testing H/X against degrees at most eight; the actual grade is 11+8=19 and the X-Gram cutoff is 18. Since dim E11=21, this gives f11≤21−10=11.

We have 3≤f5≤4. If f5=4, the preceding exact ranks make [5,10] a unit-gain band with full scalar separation through grade ten. The independently audited lower-triangular flat-band theorem ([flat-band proof](FLAT-BAND-TRIANGULAR-TRANSFER.md)) implies F10 is clock-invariant. Its proof is paid in the present fields: the flat-band indices j=5,H=10 give m=4, the polynomial degrees and primitive divisions are at most 10, and the contradiction is that an ordinary quadratic has a root of multiplicity at least three away from zero. The m=3 square-root exception is not used. Outgoing inclusive Hermite then gives f11≥9+5=14, contradiction. Therefore f5=3 and f4=2.

For clarity, the forward outgoing Hermite lemma from the cited audits gives f_(j+1)≥dim U+floor(j/2) when U⊂F_j is clock-invariant and there are enough detected fibres. A detector is a normalized row at the first physical prefix where the clock block's base-vector restriction fails to match U. Lower triangularity includes the current row, so in the Hermite proof the last derivative must also vanish, or in the equivalent inclusive interpolation form the last factor must be present. The incoming form for invariant F_j compares F_j/F_(j−1), using floor((j−1)/2) detectors and the inclusive last factor. Its map is injective purely by double zeros at the origin and each detector clock: the primitive degree is at most 2r+1. It would independently reject the F10 invariant endpoint, but the stronger outgoing inequality already suffices. The same outgoing proof applies to the reversed weighted base v rather than 1 whenever the polynomial primitive residues lie in the appropriate B_j. A nonzero aggregate weight guarantees detection because the pure space kills the sum and localizes on the clock. Both variants therefore apply to lower-triangular arrays with arbitrary diagonal, including zero diagonal entries.

## 3. Why the reverse D3 is not a single-clock vector

Let τ=D3. Suppose it is supported on one Y-fibre. In reverse chronology eliminate its earliest nonzero coordinate by subtracting a multiple of τ and deleting that coordinate; write π for the quotient projection, E for insertion with zero at the deleted coordinate, and N'=πNE. All earlier coefficients of τ vanish, so N' is lower triangular and π commutes with clock multiplication. The sum and all aggregate masses of the weighted base survive, since τ is on one clock and sums to zero. Projection errors for N lie in πNτ⊂πB4. For the commutator YN−NY−N² they lie in the span of πNτ, πYNτ and πN²τ, hence in πB5. These are actual errors from I−Eπ being a rank-one map into τ, not a claim of RK order for the quotient. If needed, the terminal y=0 row is unaffected because all τ words are terminal-zero.

The projected B4,B5,B6 have dimensions one, two, three. Outgoing weighted Hermite forbids invariance of the first two at these unit gains. Hence they are w, wP1(Y), wP2(Y) for a nonzero w. With v now the projected base and N its projected lower matrix, for deg P≤m and deg q≤m−4,
\[
 N(vP-wq)-vJP\in wP_{m-3}(Y)\quad (m=3,4,5),
 \qquad (YN-NY-N^2)(vP)\in wP_{m-2}(Y)\quad(m=3,4).
\]
The omitted q term at m=3 is zero. These are the monomial identities D_k and the paid projection corrections, with exact grades m+1,m+2.

List the first four distinct nonzero clocks z_t by their first physical occurrence with v_i≠0, available by effective quadrature support, and let u_t=w_i/v_i at the corresponding row. Write V_t=∏_(r≤t)(X−z_r). For t≤3, if u_t=0 the commutator on v X V_(t−1) forces that pivot diagonal zero (it has no prior nonzero coordinate, and the pure result is a multiple of w). For every deg R≤t−1 the primitives of X V_(t−1)R at z_t then vanish. This contradicts the size-t X-Gram after scaling the endpoint, so u_1,u_2,u_3 are nonzero. For t=3,4 the same commutator is actually zero globally: its head polynomial has degree ≤t−2 and vanishes at the t−1 distinct earlier nonzero head values. Thus both the third and fourth pivot diagonal are zero even if u_4 is not.

At t=3,4 put m=t+1 and choose pairs P∈X P_(m−1), q∈P_(m−4) such that vP−wq vanishes strictly before the debut. Charge each distinct clock where normalized w/v values conflict, where a zero-base earlier row has nonzero w, or where an earlier zero clock has nonzero w. On charged clocks impose q=0. There remain at most t−1 equations for P at the previous nonzero active clocks, giving dimension at least m−(t−1)+max(0,m−3−number of charges). Projection to P is injective since the t−1 previous nonzero heads distinguish q. The numerical N image is zero through the explicit pivot. Its true primitive-error multiplier must vanish at those same charged clocks, by the zero-base, conflicting-ratio, or origin equations, so the rank of the primitive values at the t chosen clocks is at most max(0,m−2−number of charges). Thus some nonzero P has vanishing primitive at all t clocks; necessarily JP is a nonzero multiple of X²V_t. Differentiating at previous clocks shows at t=3 that u_1,u_2 are proportional to z_i² V_3'(z_i). At t=4 the affine q is forced by those first two sites to be proportional to X−z_4, so u_3 follows the same formula. Normalize w so u_i=z_i² V_3'(z_i) for i≤3.

Let U=V_2, V=V_3=(X-u)U, u=z_3, and define L(X^k)=X^k/(k+2). The inclusive primitive test XU through the explicit third row gives LU=D V' exactly by its values at three distinct sites and degree at most two. Similarly LV=A_1 V'+B V with A_1 affine and B constant from XV and the first three rows. Applying X∂+2 gives U=D(XV''+2V'), so D=1/12 by leading terms. At z_1,z_2 the corresponding equation for V gives A_1'+B=0. Solving the resulting coefficient equation yields A_1=(X-u)/10 and B=−1/10. Scale u to 1. With B_i=J(X^i V), one has
\[
 B_1=X^2\bigl((X-1)V'/10-V/10\bigr),\qquad
 6B_2=(10X-2)B_1-X^2(X-1)V.
\]
The second equality follows by differentiating and matching the zero primitive at 0. Write B_i=X²(P_i V'+R_(i−1)V). The determinant R_0P_2−R_1P_1=(X−1)²/60. The actual tests XV and X²V vanish before the explicit fourth pivot. Their error multipliers of degree ≤1,2 are determined by the three genuine earlier head values to be P_1,P_2. At z_4 the two equations would require the displayed nonzero determinant times z_4²V(z_4) to be zero, which is impossible at a distinct nonzero z_4. This rules out the local τ alternative. The only numeric divisions in the scalar argument are paid small integers (including 12,10,60), nonzero clocks and differences, and linear pivots explicitly nonzero by construction.

## 4. Fully hidden F4 and the final physical contradiction

In the actual reverse spaces B_j, unit gains and outgoing weighted Hermite exclude Y-invariance of B4 through B8. Together with nonlocal τ this gives B_j=τ P_(j−3)(Y) for 3≤j≤9 by consecutive clock shifts. At j=9 no noninvariance assumption is needed to obtain this span. Identify these coordinate subspaces back in original chronology as appropriate.

For f∈F4, the actual b⊙f annihilates E15 by rooted products, hence is τ L(Y) with deg L≤1. The actual τ⊙f annihilates E12, since f⊙E12⊂E16 annihilated by τ, hence is τ Q(Y) with deg Q≤4. Put θ=vQ−v⊙f in reversed notation. It is zero on the support of τ and equals vQ elsewhere. Its true model is Q with numerical zero-model correction in B4. If Q is nonzero, Yθ cannot vanish: that would put vYQ in B5 and all tests against polynomials of degree at most four would give the paid size-five Y-Gram zero system for Q. After choosing the first nonzero pivot u of Yθ, Y(Y−u)θ likewise cannot vanish: use B6 and the size-six Y-Gram on (Y−u)Q of degree ≤5. The test function degrees are at most 9 and 11, respectively, and fit the annihilators E14 and E13. Thus the second first nonzero pivot v_* has clock distinct from 0 and u.

At these first nonzero physical rows the y-mask commutators are true zero-model vectors in B7 and B8, respectively; they vanish off τ and force the two diagonals zero. Each marked primitive therefore yields zero at its corresponding pivot, since that row has nonzero v base and the preceding numerical inputs are identically zero. Through B9 the paid tests are all degree-at-most-three R in J(y Q R)(u), and all degree-at-most-two R in J(y(y−u) Q R)(v_*). Writing F=J⁴(yQ) gives F=αy⁵(y−u)⁴ with α≠0 by its degree and roots. But
\[
 J^3((y-u)yQ)=(y-u)F'-3F=\alpha y^4(y-u)^4(6y-5u)
\]
cannot have its required distinct triple zero at v_*. This contradiction gives Q=0. Then τ⊙f=0, and b⊙f=0 because b⊙f is supported on τ and f is zero there.

Define a literal coordinate subspace K={z:z_1=0, b⊙z=τ⊙z=0}. These are componentwise constraints, not two scalar pairings. All of F4 lies in K. By the B9 span, K⊂Ann B9=E10. If z∈K, write z=H(c)+f in E10. The origin gives H(0)=0, and testing with bR(c), deg R≤9, gives the anchored size-ten X-Gram on H/X, forcing H=0. Hence K⊂F10 and is clock-invariant.

Let U be the maximal clock-invariant subspace of F10 and k=dim U. Outgoing Hermite gives f11≥k+5, so k≤6. The space F4 is not clock-invariant (its f5 gain is only 1, not ≥2); hence F5=F4+CF4⊂K⊂U, so k≥3. If 4≤k≤6, put S_a=(F_a+U)/U. Once S_a is nonzero, it cannot stall before grade ten: a stall and C F_a⊂F_(a+1) would make F_a+U a larger invariant space inside F10. Thus dim S_a≤a−k−1 whenever nonzero. At a=k+2 this cap is 1, but f_(k+2)=k+1, so U⊂F_(k+2). The next outgoing Hermite gain of at least 3 contradicts f_(k+3)=k+2. Thus k=3 and U=K=F5.

Finally choose the first two distinct nonzero off-K clocks u,v in physical forward chronology; many nonzero aggregate clock sites lie outside K by its definition. On the prefix up through this second debut every off-K clock is 0,u or v. For each R of degree≤1 the vector X(X−u)(X−v)R evaluated on K's support and zero elsewhere is in K, and it matches its full evaluation through that prefix. The two actual lower-triangular A rows therefore take the two corresponding primitive values, because the ordinary degree-five primitive discrepancy lies in F5=K, which is zero at those off-K rows. The primitive value map has rank two: a kernel primitive of degree at most five would have double roots at 0,u,v. Both rows kill F4 on K by A F4⊂F5=K. They cannot give two independent functionals on the one-dimensional K/F4. This rules out s=27 and completes the required paid-ring lower bound.
