## 1. Separation, physical truncation, and the base rank

Here \(j\) denotes tree degree \(|t|-1\). Let \(E_j\) denote the numerical span of actual Butcher weight vectors through size \(j+1\), and \(F_j\) the span of combinations whose formal profile is zero. For a tree \(t\), that profile is \(|t|X^{|t|-1}/t!\). Separation means a relation among numerical vectors through degree \(j\) has zero formal profile. Equivalently,
\[
   E_j=P_{\le j}(\mathbf c)\oplus F_j ,
   \qquad \mathbf c=A\mathbf1 ,
\]
where the polynomial evaluation summand has dimension \(j+1\). Unary extension and rooted sum give the actual inclusions
\[
       A F_j+C F_j\subseteq F_{j+1},\qquad
       F_i\odot F_j\subseteq F_{i+j},\qquad
       C\coloneqq\operatorname{diag}(\mathbf c).
\]
These do not invoke a global output. The formal primitive rule and coordinatewise rule prove them. Separation at an endpoint implies separation at all lower degrees.

Truncating a strictly lower triangular tableau at a physical prefix preserves the restriction of each rooted weight vector, by induction on extension and rooted sum; the same is true for each formal pure difference. It does **not** automatically preserve separation, dimensions or a fitted invariant core. For the transfer theorem those assertions are earned on the prefix by the actual positive weighted reads. If the model of a numerical zero combination there is \(P\), its first coordinate gives \(P(0)=0\), and the available reads \(\ell_{m+1},\ldots,\ell_{m+d}\) give
\[
    \int_0^1 (1-X)^{m+a}P(X)\,dX=0\qquad(1\le a\le d).
\]
With \(P=XQ,\ \deg Q\le d-1\), this is the Gram with weight \(X(1-X)^{m+1}\) on \(Q\); its nonsingularity is paid by \((m+2d+1)!\). Thus the actual prefix model separates afresh. The resulting well-defined linear map \(E_d^{\rm pre}\to K[X]_{\le d}\) can be extended to the full physical prefix coordinate space. It is precisely an exact dense derivative decoder: its value on \(\boldsymbol\Phi_t(A_{\rm pre})\) is the exact profile above. The pure theorem is applied independently to this prefix; no rank equality between outer and prefix \(F_j\) is assumed.

## 2. The two purely separated Hermite rents

In 02-PAID-DOUBLING-SURPLUS.md (same manifest, file eee932b1700f664db7e55e5ee1ef012a0b3a52ef927e357324910ce009827253, lines 11–29) the following are statements about actual separated \((E_j,F_j)\):
\[
 \begin{split}
 U\subseteq F_j,\ CU\subseteq U
     &\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
       \dim F_{j+1}\ge\dim U+\lfloor(j+1)/2\rfloor,\\
 C F_j\subseteq F_j,\quad j\ge5
     &\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
       \dim F_j\ge\lceil3j/2\rceil-3 .
 \end{split}                                                     \tag{1}
\]
The first compresses chronologically on \(P_{\le j}(\mathbf c)\oplus U\). At the retained clock-local annihilators of \(U\), the primitive errors in \(F_{j+1}\) and the actual row \(A\) give the Hermite tests. If a combination of the first \(\lfloor(j+1)/2\rfloor\) nonzero-clock rows killed the restricted \(F_{j+1}\), its \(A\)-successor would kill \(U\) and have earlier support. The derivative of \(X^2\) times the squared factors for all earlier nonzero clocks vanishes on every such possible earlier read, while the polynomial is nonzero at the last surviving clock. The degree budget is \(\deg P'\le j\). This contradiction counts actual rank on the restricted pure data; restriction need not preserve all of \(F_{j+1}\).

For the second compress on \(E_j\) itself. The annihilator of invariant \(F_j\) has one-dimensional summands at exactly \(j+1\) physical clocks; each clock-supported row that kills the constant would also kill all literal polynomial vectors and all \(F_j\), hence be zero. There are \(j\) separate nonzero-clock rows, all with distinct last positions. If a normalized one has clock \(x\), its actual preceding row \(\boldsymbol\lambda_x A\) reads exact primitives up to \(x\) on \(E_{j-1}\), since \(A F_{j-1}\subset F_j\). Dividing the preceding explicit matrix and the output row by \(x\) constructs an ordinary order-\(j\) provider. The independently known baseline \(s_j\ge\lceil3j/2\rceil-2\) (01-PAID-GRADE-FIVE-SURPLUS.md, equation (1), lines 25–34, itself valid for the actual weighted or ordinary row through size \(j\)) thus applies to those derived providers. No ambient order-\(j\) method was assumed. This is the entire scalar-output use in (1).

## 3. Exhaustion of the degree-eight equality branch

The proof is in the [chronological Gram exit](CHRONOLOGICAL-GRAM-EXIT.md) and its [flat-band applications](PAID-FLAT-SEVEN-AND-EIGHT-APPLICATIONS.md):

Assume separation through degree eight and \(\dim F_8=8\). Strict growth and the base rank give \(\dim F_i=i\) for \(5\le i\le8\). None of \(F_5,F_6,F_7\) is \(C\)-invariant, by the incoming-direction count in (1); \(F_8\) itself is noninvariant since (1) would force dimension at least nine. Consequently each of these unit steps is supplied by the actual \(C\)-escape, not merely by a formal polynomial mode. The elementary codimension-one clock-escape calculation gives a common invariant core \(U\) and one head on this interval. In detail, for the initial space \(F_a\), the subspaces of vectors whose first \(i\) clock powers lie in \(F_a\) drop by one until reaching the largest invariant subspace \(U\). Multiplication by \(C\) injects each earlier quotient into its predecessor. Choosing a vector at the last proper drop gives a numerical chain \(w,Cw,\ldots\) of representatives for \(F_a/U\). Each later noninvariant unit step simply appends its next \(C\)-power. No new invariant component can appear before saturation: a clock-local polynomial-head vector would vanish at more other active labels than its degree allows. This proves the common-core assertion. The first rent in (1) applied to \(U\subset F_5\) gives \(\dim U\le\dim F_6-3=3\).

For every \(\mathbf f\in F_3\), the paid Gram exit with \(a=5\), three steps, and degree cost \(e=3\) gives \(\mathbf f\in U\). Here is the precise dense-only content of that exit. For any clock-local \(\mathbf u\in U\), the product \(\mathbf f\odot\mathbf u\) is in \(F_{a+e}\), supported on the same clock. Since the head has more other active labels than its allowed polynomial degree, this product still belongs to \(U\). Choose a complement separately on the physical joint \((c_i,f_i)\) blocks, invariant under both diagonals, and project the original head to it. Products with the successive head powers in \(F_a\) show that the physical values of \(\mathbf f\) on the complementary head are a polynomial \(P\) of degree at most \(e\). Each step of this degree reduction is an interpolating identity on more distinct labels than its degree, because the endpoint is noninvariant.

If \(P\ne0\), set \(\mathbf z=(XP)(\mathbf c)-C\mathbf f\). Its exact model \(XP\ne0\) and separation put it outside \(U\). In each joint block choose physical pivots for \(U\) greedily. At the first mismatch \(i\) of \(\mathbf z\), the corresponding same-clock row \(\boldsymbol\lambda_i\) reads \(\mathbf z\) minus a combination of earlier pivots. All preceding literal coordinates agree with one \(\mathbf u\in U\). At the mismatch \(\lambda_i\mathbf1\ne0\), \(c_i\ne0\), and the complementary head is zero on that joint block, so \(\boldsymbol\lambda_i\) kills the whole \(F_8\). Strict chronology then gives \(\boldsymbol\lambda_i A R(C)\mathbf z=0\) for every \(\deg R\le3\), since \(A R(C)\mathbf u\) is in the paid pure space. The model of that same actual expression is the primitive of \(RXP\). Dilation by \(c_i\ne0\) yields the full weight-\(X\) Gram, so \(P=0\), a contradiction. Once the head polynomial is zero, projection of \(\mathbf f\) on the complementary head is zero as well, since its values are constant on the chosen joint blocks. Thus \(\mathbf f\in U\). This argument uses only the genuine model, \(A,C\), and integer units, not a scalar output.

Strict growth from \(F_2\) and to \(F_5\) leaves only \(\dim F_4=3\) or \(4\):
* If \(\dim F_4=3\), \(\dim F_3=2\). \(F_3\) is noninvariant by the first rent in (1), so \(F_4=F_3+CF_3=U\). Products by each member of \(F_3\) preserve \(U\) by the same single-clock head argument. Zero-constant polynomial interpolation on its diagonal values puts the support indicator of each basis vector of \(F_3\) in \(U\), and multiplication by that indicator preserves \(U\). Taking their union gives the indicator of the full support \(S\) of \(U=F_3+CF_3\). Hence \(P(\mathbf c)\) restricted to \(S\) belongs to \(U\) for every literal polynomial \(P\), and \(S\) has at most three clock labels. Off \(S\), the stage primitive errors through degree four vanish, and \(AF_3\subset U\) forces every off-core receiver's incoming feed on \(U\) to lie on the same one-dimensional annihilator of \(F_3\). At the first nonzero off-core clock \(x\), that line agrees with the integral up to \(x\) on polynomials vanishing at zero. At the first different nonzero off-core clock \(y\), both inputs \(X(X-x)\) and \(X^2(X-x)\) kill all preceding off-core rows, so their primitive ratios have a common scalar. With \(z=y/x\), they are \(z^2(3-2z)\) and \(z^3(4-3z)\), forcing \(3z^2(z-1)^2=0\). Therefore there are at most two off-core clocks, leaving at most five actual clocks in total, contrary to separation through degree five (six literal independent powers).
* If \(\dim F_4=4\), \(F_4\) is noninvariant by (1), and the same one-head chain starts at degree \(a=4\); its core is the \(U\) already identified. Let \(k=4-\dim U\). Choose its genuine generator \(\mathbf f\) so that \(C^i\mathbf f\in F_4\) for \(0\le i<k\). On common joint blocks for the two multipliers \(\mathbf f,C^{k-1}\mathbf f\), the numerical head profiles have degree at most four. They agree as \(P\) and \(X^{k-1}P\) on all effective labels; there are at least \(k+5\) because \(F_8\) is noninvariant. It follows that \(\deg P\le4-(k-1)\), and \(P\ne0\) since a genuine head generator cannot project to zero on all the head blocks. If \(k\ge2\) this degree is at most three. The same two-exit argument rules out degrees at most three even if \(k=1\): the first clock mask \(Q=X\) pays \(\deg R\le2\) at degree eight with the actual source cost four. Degrees at most two are killed by Gram. In degree three, after rescaling this first actual exit to 1, the paid three moments force \(XP\) to be a nonzero scalar multiple of \(H'''\), where \(H=X^4(X-1)^3\), by the weight-\(X\) Gram and integration by parts. The mask \(Q=X(X-1)\) supplies a genuinely different clock \(a'\ne0,1\), paying \(\deg R\le1\). These moments say that \(V\mapsto\int_0^{a'} V H'''\), on \(\deg V\le2\), is proportional to \(V(1)\). This is the contradiction. All these integer divisions and simple-root tests are in the degree-eight horizon, with no sign assumption.

This exhausts the alternatives over the paid field: equality at degree eight can only have \(\dim U=3\), and one genuine quartic head \(\mathbf f\in F_4\). It has the actual flag
\[
              F_{4+i}=U+\operatorname{span}\{\mathbf f,C\mathbf f,\ldots,C^i\mathbf f\}
                  \quad(0\le i\le4)
\]
and \(U\) really is invariant under \(C\) and multiplication by \(\mathbf f\). These are necessary conclusions at degree eight, not assumptions about ambient ordinary order or a held tableau.

Chronological compression in the provider theorem is different from an arbitrary physical prefix: retain independent chronological evaluations of \(E_j\), and redirect any omitted predecessor coefficient only to earlier retained evaluations. Restriction is an isomorphism on \(E_j\) and preserves literal action by \(A\) on retained inputs, so actual rooted data through \(E_j\), pure spans, and the invariant core and head equations survive. In particular the claim is **not** that an extra escape \(CF_j\not\subseteq F_j\) survives a projection outside \(E_j\); the proof uses that noninvariance before compression to obtain the measured head equation. On every joint \((c,f)\) block where the head profile would differ from \(f_i\), the original head is already in the core there, and restriction preserves that relation. Hence the clean/head chart is valid on the compressed data.

If \(\mathbf f\) enters at degree \(a\), the head span through \(j\) has \(j-a+1\) powers. There must be at least \(j-a+1\) distinct clean labels including zero: otherwise choose a nonzero polynomial \(T\) of degree at most \(j-a\) vanishing on all clean labels. Combining the head \(T\) with the polynomial model \(-PT\) would hide a nonzero polynomial model in the core, contrary to separation. At the first nonzero clean row of clock \(x\), the chronological width is at most \(u+\deg P+3\), where \(u=\dim U\). To see the bound, the contrast \(C(P(\mathbf c)-\mathbf f)\), matched to a core vector at the physical pivots, is zero on all previous coordinates. Its primitive response supplies a polynomial \(B_1=J(XP)+P S_1\) of degree at most \(\deg P+2\) vanishing on every preceding head label, and \(J(XP)(x)=0\). The latter excludes monomial \(P\) at \(x\ne0\). If \(B_1=0\), then \(P\mid J(XP)\). Over a splitting field, every nonzero root of \(P\) of multiplicity \(e\) would have multiplicity \(e+1\) in \(J(XP)\), while the zero root has the extra two integrations/powers of \(X\). As \(P\) is not monomial, this exceeds the degree \(\deg P+2\), contradiction. Thus there are at most \(\deg P+2\) preceding head rows, one clean origin, and at most \(u\) core pivots. The bound counts literal preceding physical rows.

For characteristic-zero degree eight, \(j=8,a=4,u=3,\deg P=4\) gives at most ten preceding rows; the established order-eight floor is ten. This saturates the six early head labels and all three core pivots. The first two nonzero clean reads and the saturated primitive residues then give the two incompatible rational quadratics in QUARTIC-CHARACTERISTIC-ZERO-EXIT.md, as independently checked for the generic branch. They are coprime over \(\mathbb Q\), so their Bezout identity remains valid in every extension of characteristic zero; no ordering of the field or absence of square roots is assumed. The argument invokes no tests from an ambient ordinary output. This gives separated \(\dim F_8\ge9\) in characteristic zero.

For paid degree nine assume \(\dim F_9\le9\) and separation through nine. Strict growth makes \(\dim F_9=9\) and \(\dim F_8=8\), so Section 3 gives the only surviving rank-three quartic head. If \(F_9\) became clock-invariant, (1) would give dimension at least eleven. Since \(F_8\) was also noninvariant, the unique new direction is its next actual clock multiple: \(F_9=F_8+CF_8\), so the same head and invariant multiplier data continue through \(j=9\). The first-provider theorem now has \(j=9,a=4,u=3,\deg P=4\), and therefore again gives a provider on at most ten preceding rows. There is at least one nonzero clean row, since the clean-label count is at least six including the origin.

The indexing is important. The clean row \(\boldsymbol\lambda\) annihilates \(F_9\) and gives polynomial evaluation at its nonzero clock \(x\) on \(E_9\). Thus its preceding physical row \(\boldsymbol\lambda A\) integrates exact profiles on \(E_8\), not on \(E_9\). For a tree of size \(n\le9\), this gives
\[
     \boldsymbol\lambda A\cdot\boldsymbol\Phi_t(A)
             =\int_0^x\frac{nX^{n-1}}{t!}\,dX
             =\frac{x^n}{t!}.
\]
Truncate to the actual preceding rows and divide both the matrix and the output row by \(x\). Since the tree vector is homogeneous of degree \(n-1\) in the matrix, this gives exactly \(\mathbf b'\cdot\boldsymbol\Phi_t(A')=1/t!\) for every \(n\le9\). It is an ordinary order-nine method, not order ten. The old paid ordinary baseline at size nine is \(\lceil27/2\rceil-2=12\), contrary to the width ten. This proves \(\dim F_9\ge10\) without the characteristic-zero quadratic comparison. The only denominator, determinant, and multiplicity checks use paid integers; there is no exceptional-field exclusion.

This gives the precise stable pure inputs required by the detector transfer. A particular larger \(d\) inherits the pure surplus by strict growth.
