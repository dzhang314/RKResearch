# How the detector cutoff spends a measured surplus

Use the preceding zero-or-one-spare cutoff. Conditions refer to the actual numerical rooted spaces over a paid field, not to dimensions inferred from assigned task or formal tree counts. There are two consequences: any separated pure-dimension improvement raises the order floor, and an unspent one-stage surplus must appear as a particular actual rank spike if an output genuinely remains late.

## Transfer of a separated pure-dimension theorem

Fix \(d\ge5\) and \(h\ge0\), with \(d>h+4\). Assume the following pure-dimension fact holds for every strictly explicit tableau over the field in question whenever its actual polynomial profile separates through degree \(d\):
\[
                                \dim F_d\ge d+h.                  \tag{1}
\]
The field may have an additional specified paid-characteristic or characteristic-zero hypothesis. Crucially, (1) is a statement about separated numerical data, so it also applies to a prefix admitting an exact dense derivative decoder. An ordinary-order theorem proved using an unavailable global output cannot be substituted here.

**Transfer.** Any actual weighted output of effective odd order \(r=2d+1\) under these hypotheses needs at least \(3d+2+h\) physical stages. An actual weighted output of effective even order \(r=2d+2\) needs at least \(3d+3+h\) stages under the same separated pure-dimension hypothesis at degree \(d\).

Indeed the weighted moments separate \(E_d\): a possible alias \(P=XQ\), \(\deg Q\le d-1\), is annihilated by the \(d\) shifted moments, and the Gram with weight \(X(1-X)^m\) is nonsingular. Thus \(\dim E_d=d+1+\dim F_d\). The usual reverse rank lower bound is \(d-1+\epsilon\); if the last clock is 1, the final coordinate vector is an independent auxiliary direction in \(H_d\), by the weight \(X(1-X)^{m+1}\) Gram. One always has \(\epsilon+\eta\ge1\). Consequently the weighted explicit prefix has at least \(3d+1+h\) rows.

At equality every available direction is accounted for: \(\dim F_d=d+h\), \(\epsilon+\eta=1\), and the ambient spare \(\omega\) of the cutoff is zero. Its actual early prefix ends at \(k=2d+h\). The short-prefix hypothesis \(k+1<N_*\) holds because the weaker old paid weighted floor at effective size \(2d-1\) gives \(N_*=3d-3\), and \(d>h+4\). Thus all the positive taps factor on \(E_d\) through these \(k\) physical coordinates.

Those actual prefix outputs are \(\ell_{m+1},\ldots,\ell_{m+d}\) through degree \(d\). They separate the profile there: at the literal origin the constant coefficient of any alias is zero; the remaining possible alias \(XQ\) is killed by the weight \(X(1-X)^{m+1}\) Gram paid by \((m+2d+1)!\). The exact profile is therefore a well-defined linear map on the prefix's numerical rooted space, which extends linearly to its full coordinate space. This is an exact dense derivative decoder. Its actual pure dimension is at least \(d+h\) by (1), so the prefix needs at least \(d+1+d+h=2d+1+h\) rows, not \(2d+h\). This excludes the equality count. When using only the budget-six version of the penultimate result, restrict to \(d\ge6\).

For even \(r=2d+2\), the actual tap \(\boldsymbol\beta A\) has shifted weight \(\ell_{m+1}\) and effective odd order \(2d+1\), paid by the same \((m+r)!\). Its last read is strictly earlier than the last read of \(\boldsymbol\beta\), which gives the asserted even floor.

### Reusable sources of the pure surplus

The old paid logarithmic surplus is a separated pure-dimension theorem. Its flat-band argument and outgoing Hermite rent use actual \(E_i,F_i\) and model separation, not a global scalar output; its ordinary endpoint providers are explicitly extracted from earlier physical rows. The only starting pure-rank assertion needed is \(\dim F_5\ge5\), available on a dense derivative decoder through size six by the unrestricted dense width theorem. Thus its powers-of-two induction gives
\[
 h=\max\{a\ge0:5\cdot2^a\le d\}
\]
in (1), with the same paid denominators. For clarity, at a flat step from \(j\) through \(2j\) with \(j\ge5\), the original two primitive contrasts enforce invariance of \(F_{2j}\), or there is already an increment of two. The invariant endpoint rank count comes from an actual ordinary prefix provider at each detected clock. Its scalar calculations need integers only through the original paid horizon \(2d+1\). Thus the argument remains available on the dense prefix in this transfer. The condition \(d>h+4\) holds for these logarithmic values. This adds one to the previous odd paid logarithmic stage bound, and applies to a weighted row's actual physical prefix. Without the separate order-eleven extension, take \(d\ge6\).

Two stronger local pure inputs can be used once their cited source proofs are admitted:
* In characteristic zero, the generic quartic-head exclusion gives \(\dim F_8\ge9\) under separation through degree eight. The proof uses actual chronological compression, primitive tests and ordinary order-eight providers, not a separately exact final output. This is 31c1bcf8… and its cited paid flat-band reduction.
* Over paid fields, one more separated degree eliminates that quartic head without exceptional arithmetic. If \(\dim F_9=9\), strict growth and the size-six floor force \(\dim F_8=8\). The paid degree-eight reduction leaves only a rank-three invariant core, with one quartic head generated at degree four. A one-step gain to degree nine cannot make the flag invariant, because the invariant endpoint rent at degree nine is at least \(\lceil3\cdot9/2\rceil-3=11\). Hence the same head persists. The chronological first-provider width theorem gives an actual ordinary order-nine method on at most \(3+4+3=10\) preceding rows, below its paid baseline of 12. This gives \(\dim F_9\ge10\) under separation through degree nine. Both the first-provider proof and the degree-eight structural reduction use separation and exact rooted profiles on actual compressed stages. No fixed high-order tableau or exceptional field arithmetic is used.

Higher \(d\) inherit either extra pure dimension by strict growth. These inputs are separate from the detector's validity; one can apply the transfer with the maximum independently verified separated pure floor.

## One unspent rank: a necessary spike and an endpoint-count cut

Retain the general anchor degree \(g\), paid word budget \(L=r-1-g\), and literal prefix cut \(k=s-L-1\) of the preceding cutoff. Write
\[
 e_u\coloneqq\dim R_u-(u+\epsilon).
\]
In the paid window \(e_u\) is nonnegative and nondecreasing, since the reverse flag grows strictly. The actual band bound is
\[
          \dim H_{g+j}
              \le\dim H_g+j+\omega-e_{L-j-1}
                      \qquad(0\le j\le L-1).                     \tag{2}
\]
In particular an observed reverse excess can consume the spare on a whole initial range of forward grades; it cannot be counted a second time as an independent stage.

If \(\omega=1\), the preceding cutoff proves that a genuine late positive output requires an actual spike
\[
               \dim H_{g+j}=\dim H_g+j+1
                        \quad\text{for some }1\le j\le L-1.       \tag{3}
\]
At any such measured spike the new anchor \(g+j\) has zero spare. Its word budget is \(L-j\) and its physical cutoff is \(k+j\). If \(L-j\ge5\) (or 6 without the threshold extension) and \(k+j+1<N_*\), that cutoff then applies to every positive tap on \(H_{g+j}\).

There is an exact way to transfer this result back to the original late kernel. Let \(z\) be the number of literal clock-1 positions among rows \(k+1,\ldots,k+j\). For every \(\mathbf v\in H_g\) zero through row \(k\), put \(\mu_b=\boldsymbol\beta A^b\cdot\mathbf v\). Then
\[
                           \mu_b=0\qquad(z<b\le L).               \tag{4}
\]
First \(A^j\mathbf v\) is late at the new anchor, so \(\mu_{j+1},\ldots,\mu_L=0\). To descend from \(b=j\) to \(b=z+1\), use a word of length \(j\). At its \(i\)-th step, with \(0\le i<j\), use either \(A\) or \(Y-y_iI\), where \(y_i\coloneqq1-c_{k+i+1}\) is its actual physical clock. Both choices advance the vanishing prefix by one. Choose \(b-1\) occurrences of \(A\), including all \(z\) positions where \(y_i=0\), and use the clock masks at the remaining slots. The final vector belongs to \(H_{g+j}\) and vanishes through \(k+j\), so its read under \(\boldsymbol\beta A\) is zero. Expanding the masks, the product of their nonzero constant coefficients multiplies \(\mu_b\). Every other term has at least one \(Y\), so the adjoint word identity gives a paid scalar multiple of a tap strictly above \(b\). This identity for any mixed word follows by moving each action to the scalar read: \(A\) contributes one upper primitive and \(Y\) contributes one multiplication by \(1-X\). Each adds one to the exponent. The total cost is \(j+1\le L\). The higher taps are already zero by descent, so the nonzero constant coefficient gives (4).

Thus a spike at the first grade leaves at most the first positive tap, and that exception requires the first actual late clock to equal 1. If no literal endpoint clock occurs before a paid early spike, all the original positive taps still factor through \(k\). If the original top tap is nonzero, there can be no spike in this entire paid early window. The spike forced by (3) is then a genuinely late extra numerical direction, not a missed clock capacity guessed from the rank bound.

At \(s=3d+2+h\), one beyond the equality count just excluded by the transferred pure floor, the actual unused rank is
\[
                \omega=1-(\dim F_d-d-h)-(\epsilon+\eta-1).
\]
If the added stage is already spent in pure rank or as an independent final endpoint with nonzero first reverse residual, the output again has the strict early factorization provided that the new prefix still satisfies the strict size guard. At this stage count the old common bound requires \(d>h+5\), not \(d>h+4\). If the stage really leaves one unused rank, then a visible late tap forces (3) and, in the paid window, (4). The exact pure dimension, the final physical clock, and the actual spike count are separate measurable conditions.

## A sharper test for short closure with a top-visible direction

For completeness, suppose \(\omega=1\) and a genuine late vector \(\mathbf v\in H_g\) has nonzero top tap \(\mu_L\). The penultimate proof at budget at least five gives \(\mu_2=\cdots=\mu_{L-1}=0\) on such a vector. Here the stronger statement at budget five follows from the same reciprocal proof after its two-level comparison, as stated in THRESHOLD-ELEVEN.md. If the full clock closure \(W_\delta\) of \(H_g\) is short, \(\delta\le L-2\), it contains each clock component of \(\mathbf v\). Bare last-path chronology on \(W_\delta\subset H_{g+\delta}\) allows at most \(\delta+1\) nonendpoint charges. The \(L-2\) consecutive zero positive moments of powers 2 through \(L-1\), and the nonzero top moment, require at least \(L-1\) charges by Vandermonde. Hence necessarily \(\delta=L-2\).

Moreover \(A^\delta\mathbf v\notin W_\delta\). Were it there, its literal support after \(k+\delta\) would allow just one nonendpoint charge, whose nonzero moment of degree \(1=L-1-\delta\) is ruled out by the penultimate tap. This would kill its top tap too. It follows from the one-spare dimension bound and \(\dim W_\delta\ge\dim H_g+\delta\) that
\[
       \dim W_\delta=\dim H_g+\delta,\qquad
       H_{g+\delta}=W_\delta+K A^\delta\mathbf v .
\]
This is an exact measured extra unary direction at the late grade. More generally, even without a top-visible direction, any short closure with a late visible output must have a single initial clock escape: two initial clock directions would consume the full spare at every step up to closure (clock increments are nonincreasing and every unstabilized step grows), put all the necessary unary iterates inside the clock hull, and give the descending Vandermonde early cutoff. For the one-clock branch at least one actual late unary iterate must escape that hull. This explains precisely why the former three equality charts do not describe a real unspent surplus.

These results are source mathematics. They assert no formal checking or result for a protected calculation.
