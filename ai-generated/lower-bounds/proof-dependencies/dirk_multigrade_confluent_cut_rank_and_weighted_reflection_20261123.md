# A simultaneous chronological rank filtration for arbitrary grades/heads, and its weighted one-mark application

This uses the physical stages, not an order-p assertion for a folded tableau. All statements in the general rank and the weighted single-head sections work over an arbitrary characteristic-zero coefficient field. The integer bounds already proved are separate.

## The multihead, multigrade, multirow cut

Use the genuine ordinary zero-profile spaces F_j and their formal C:x and A:J actions, Jf(x)=∫₀ˣ f. Fix disjoint masked physical classes each on a single numerical site, whose indicators are genuine pure at a grade usable in all budgets below. They need not be the complete support of the pure space and the same numerical site may occur both inside and outside a masked class. The choice of NO masks is always allowed. For an original chronological prefix I list the k distinct nonzero outside abscissae X=(x₁,…,x_k) on that prefix. Define its actual scalar-valued pure trace H_m(I)⊂K^k as follows: u∈H_m(I) if a genuine f∈F_m takes the single value u_j at all seen outside rows at x_j, vanishes at all seen outside origins, and is constant on the seen rows of each masked physical class (different constants per class permitted). The constants on the masked classes can be freely reset, using their genuine indicators. Write h_m(I)=dim H_m(I). This trace is not the rank of the raw pure restriction: it enforces all repeats and within-class splits. At input one may use any subspace of achievable traces as long as the indicated class resets are available separately; at output any containing subspace gives a valid, weaker bound.

Let I⊂I′ be two prefixes, with t distinct nonzero outside sites on I′ and the first k of them the sites on I (k=t is allowed, including I=I′). Suppose the actual principal block of A on I′\I has Lth power zero, L≥1; for unchanged prefixes this is vacuous. In particular a single added physically explicit row is eligible with L=1, an all-zero antichain block is eligible with L=1, and an arbitrary block of n consecutive strictly triangular rows is eligible with L=n. One must not infer the condition merely from zero diagonals of a few sampled debuts while ignoring other intervening rows. Put V_t=∏_(z outside nonzero on I′)(x−z) and m≥t. Then
    dim[H_m(I) + { ((d/dx)^L [x^(L+1)V_t g])(x_i)_(i≤k) : deg g≤m−t−1 }]
        ≤ h_(m+L)(I′) − (t−k).                               (1)
With degree ≤−1 the polynomial space is zero. Thus already at m=t one must gain at least one scalar-valued trace dimension per new distinct nonzero outside site across a nilpotent block, even with many staggered pure heads and arbitrary within-site splits.

Proof: all P∈xP_(m−1) attaining a trace from H_m(I) on the k old points form a space of dimension m−k+h_m(I) by Vandermonde (m≥t≥k). Choose an actual pure lift f, calibrating the masked class constants to P(a), so v=P(c)−f is zero on the complete old prefix. By triangularity and the true block nilpotence A^L v is zero on I′. Therefore (J^L P) evaluated on all t knots belongs to H_(m+L)(I′); it is a pure difference on that prefix, is constant on masked classes and vanishes at every outside origin. Its kernel has dimension ≥m−k+h_m(I)−h_(m+L)(I′). In that kernel exactly J^L P=x^(L+1)V_t g, deg g≤m−t−1, because P(0)=0. Conversely all such g for which the L-th derivative evaluated at the k old points belongs to H_m(I) give a member P of the kernel; the initial L integration constants vanish. Thus the kernel dimension is m−t minus the dimension of the derivative image modulo H_m(I), proving (1). For L=1 the visible confluent columns reduce to z_i² V_t′(z_i) z_i^j. The inclusive k=t cut gives a sum-space budget; the explicit-last k=t−1 cut subtracts exactly one on the right. This accounts for the formerly missing degree in the grade-four calculation.

Multiple different head grades need not be padded: when F_j=U+Σ_α w_α P_(j−d_α) with the local class-constant U, build H_j by giving head α exactly that polynomial degree, equating all sums on repeated outside sites, setting them zero at outside origins, and equating head sums within each observed masked class. Take the image of the resulting evaluated linear map at the nonzero outside representatives. This includes actual cancellations between different heads, varying head values within masked classes and all repeated states; merely summing polynomial dimensions is not valid. It is a linear calculation at each cut, without a choice of a nonzero-coordinate divisor.

## Weighted single-head extension, then true reverse projection

The refunded grade-four four-knot theorem remains valid for a general scalar base v, not just the all-ones numerical stages. Here is an explicit lean assumption package (no masks needed in this version). Write Y=diag(y), N lower triangular, K_N=YN−NY−N², and let w be a head. For each m=3,4,5 and polynomials deg P≤m, deg q≤m−4 (q=0 if degree negative), assume
    N[vP(Y)−wq(Y)] − v(JP)(Y) belongs to wP_(m−3)(Y).
For m=3,4 and all deg P≤m assume
    K_N [vP(Y)] belongs to wP_(m−2)(Y).
Products in these displays mean coordinatewise evaluations. Then there are at most THREE distinct nonzero sites y_i with v_i≠0. In a signed/zero weighted scalar quadrature of order ≥8 with final functional summing the entries, this is impossible by the rational x dx moment test.

On these witnesses the effective head amplitude is w_i/v_i. The scalar inputs v·xV_(t−1) and v·xV_(t−1)² vanish on ALL prior rows as needed, including the potentially many zero-base rows. The two displayed assumptions thus give exactly the nonzero first three amplitudes and explicit third/fourth diagonals of dirk_grade_four_refunded_cut_forbids_four_knots_p12_15_p16_22_20261123.md.
