# Independent audit of the DIRK reverse quotient bound

The quotient and grade arithmetic are sound for real lower triangular A, with arbitrary diagonal, signed or zero output weights, and repeated or exterior real nodes. Let E_j contain the stage tree span through normalized degree j, and m=floor((p−1)/2). If E_j=E_(j+1), the usual tree operations imply invariance under A and C. In reversed coordinates E_j^⊥ is invariant under the lower triangular reverse transpose N and the diagonal Y=I−C. By the spectral projectors of the real diagonal, W=E_j^⊥ splits by reflected node. Within each same-node component use a basis with distinct earliest pivots. The quotient sends each deleted basis coordinate to later retained coordinates at the same node, so it preserves lower triangularity of N and the retained diagonal, and Y remains diagonal.

The functional 1ᵀ kills W, and 1ᵀYv=b·(1−c)=1/2, so [Yv]≠0. Since Y remains diagonal, the first nonzero coordinate i of [Yv] has nonzero label y_i. The reverse defects D2 and D3 annihilate E_j for j+3≤p−1, and their vanishing in the quotient gives N[Yv]=Y[Yv]/2 and N(Y[Yv])=Y²[Yv]/3. At i no earlier entries contribute, so the diagonal entry equals both y_i/2 and y_i/3, impossible. D1 is unused.

The independent forward quotient argument gives dim F_m≥m−2: if any F_j=F_(j+1) for j≥2 at an available grade, invariance under A,C and b·c=1/2 allow the same earliest-coordinate contradiction with the two defects Ac−c²/2 and Ac²−c³/3 (even j=2 includes the latter upon equality). Polynomial-versus-defect separation at m uses a positive square and degree 2m≤p−1, so dim E_m≥2m−1. Reverse strict gains for j=m,…,p−4 then imply dim E_(p−3)≥p+m−4, and hence s≥p+m−4. It strengthens the earlier DIRK p-stage bound at p≥11, giving ≥25 at p20.

Equality forces the first forward defect g=0, since g≠0 adds a dimension at midpoint. It also forces D1=D2=0 because any nonzero such row annihilates E_(p−3), requiring a further coordinate. Thus any equality candidate satisfies C(2),D(1),D(2) globally. D3 cannot vanish globally by the same local diagonal contradiction. Equality therefore has codimension one at E_(p−4), but that does not improve the unconditional integer bound. No external dispatch.
## Endpoint extension: one additional stage, audited

At equality in the old bound, every late E_j, m≤j≤p−3, has codimension p−3−j: the existing one-step increments and starting dimension all must be exact. Trim zero-weight trailing stages. C(2) gives c1=a11=0. D1=D2=0 at last stage imply a_ss=1−c_s and (1−c_s)a_ss=(1−c_s)^2/2, since b_s≠0. Thus c_s=1 and Ae_s=0. The row d3=D3 in original chronology is nonzero by reverse D2,D3 earliest-coordinate argument; d3 annihilates e_s, since Y_s=0 and the last column of A vanishes.

Let U_l be the span of d3 times all right words in A,C of length at most l. Grade orthogonality gives U_l⊂E_(p−4−l)^⊥ with rank cap l+1 for 0≤l≤p−4−m. A stall U_l=U_(l+1) would give a reverse invariant subspace, 1ᵀ kills it, and D2=0 while D3 belongs to it. Quotient D2,D3 again force y/2=y/3 at first nonzero coordinate. Thus the dimensions are precisely l+1 and U_(p−4−m)=E_m^⊥. Every row in U_l kills e_s because d3e_s=0,Ae_s=0,Ce_s=e_s. It follows that e_s∈E_m. Write e_s=P(c)+f for deg P≤m and f∈F_m. Stage one gives P(0)=0 (all forward defects vanish at the first zero row). Test against Q=(1−x)P/x of degree ≤m. Then Q(1)=0, f drops out by degree 2m≤p−1, and 0=∫ x(1−x)(P/x)^2. Hence P=0, so e_s=f contradicts b·f=0 and b_s≠0. This excludes equality and proves s≥p+m−3 when m≤p−4, in particular all p≥9. At p9, m4 and lmax1 give exact lower 10 by the certified upper. At p20 the lower becomes 26. No sign, distinctness, node-location or nonzero-diagonal assumptions.
