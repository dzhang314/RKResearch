# A universal early ordinary forward-rank surplus at grade seven

For every ordinary real lower triangular RK tableau of classical order p≥10, with no assumptions on nodes, individual weights, diagonal or g=Ac−c²/2,
    f₇ ≥ f₃+5.
Thus there is a surplus somewhere in the four ordinary steps from F₃ through F₇. This is independent of all endpoint rank assumptions and tables. A more precise grade-six equality profile and its all-split class extension follow below. No claim on the unnormalized product of two adjoint rows is made.

## Shared rank inputs

Definitions and the elementary unit-step compression, strict growth, outgoing and incoming Hermite bounds are in dirk_two_surpluses_even_horizon_20261123.md and dirk_incoming_hermite_unconditional_dyadic_gain_20261123.md. With p≥8 and j=3,…,6 the strict ordinary forward increases are at least one. If a step out of j≥4 is a unit increase, F_j cannot be C-invariant (the outgoing two-detector gain would be at least two). If a step into j≥5 is a unit increase, F_j cannot be invariant (the incoming two-detector gain applies). These Hermite detections are automatic: any quadrature-active abscissa detects the stage constant modulo an invariant pure space, since an invariant pure space localizes at the node and b kills pure elements at these budgets. The number of active nonzero abscissae is at least floor(p/2), by x times a squared vanishing polynomial. All references to C-invariance are for the diagonal physical abscissa matrix; the formal laws are C:x and A:J=∫₀ˣ. The commutator R=CA−AC−A² has zero profile and at the first nonzero coordinate i of v equals −a_ii²v_i.

Assume for now f₇=f₃+4. Then all four increments are one, F₄,…,F₇ are noninvariant and each F_(j+1)=F_j+CF_j when F_j is noninvariant. If F₃ is invariant denote it by U and take the grade-four vector w in F₄ orthogonal to U. Then F_j=U⊕wP_(j−4), 4≤j≤7. Here and below C-invariance makes orthogonality to U valid separately at every knot. Noninvariance even at F₇ implies at least five different w knots. For any node-local u∈U, v∈F₃, the node-local product uv∈F₆ must lie in U: its head polynomial has degree ≤2 and vanishes at at least four other w knots modulo the separate nodewise orthogonality. Thus U is a coordinatewise algebra. A finite-dimensional coordinatewise algebra (unit not presumed) is spanned on its support by its disjoint class indicators: interpolate a generic member distinguishing its nonzero joint-value classes by polynomials with zero constant. C-invariance locates each class at a single abscissa (localize by polynomial in C). For uw∈F₇ the head degree is ≤3, again too small; hence diag(w)U⊂U, and multiplying the support indicator by w then using orthogonality makes w zero on that support. We have precisely the grade-three indicators and grade-four single head on their complement from dirk_grade_four_refunded_cut_forbids_four_knots_p12_15_p16_22_20261123.md. Its four-knot obstruction and scalar quadrature exclude this branch already at p≥8.

If F₃ is noninvariant, its unit escape into F₄ gives
    F₃=U⊕wP_(r−1),        F_j=U⊕wP_(r+j−4),  j=4,5,6,7,
in the ordinary symmetric one-escape compression (r≥1, w a genuine F₃ element). U is C-invariant and every head power is nodewise orthogonal to U. In particular F₆ contains r+3 independent head powers, and its noninvariance gives at least r+4 distinct effective w knots. Any node-local member of F₆ lies in U: its head term on all other sites is in U there and thus is zero nodewise by orthogonality, so at least r+3 distinct roots kill a head polynomial of degree ≤r+2. For a localized u∈U and v∈F₃, uv∈F₆ is therefore in U. As in the preceding paragraph U is the algebra of its site-class indicators, all in F₃, and w vanishes on its support T. The products w² c^j for j=0,…,2r−2 are all in F₆ (multiply two genuine F₃ elements). Dividing on supp w by w yields polynomials P_j of degree ≤r+2 giving the values c^j w. Since ≥r+4 distinct effective sites occur, P_(j+1)=xP_j identically by comparing degrees ≤r+3 on that set. Thus w=P(c) on its support for a nonzero P of degree ≤4−r≤3. If the bound is negative this branch was already impossible.

For Q=x and then Q=x(x−u), use the actual masked contrast
    v_Q=(QP)(c)−Σ_classes (QP)(a)1_class−Q(C)w
of grade at most 3+deg Q and profile QP. This vanishes on T and supp w. Neither input is identically zero: test its profile against P or (x−u)P respectively, giving the positive integrals of xP² or x(x−u)²P². The highest paired grade is 5+4=9≤p−1. Let u≠0 be the abscissa of the first nonzero coordinate for Q=x and v≠0,u for Q=x(x−u). At either pivot every F₇ vanishes (it is outside T and supp w). Since Rv_Q has grade ≤7, its pivot forces the actual diagonal to be zero. Applying A after extra scalar powers, numerical zero earlier plus this zero diagonal give
    ∫₀ᵘ x^(k+1)P(x)dx=0 (k=0,1,2),
    ∫₀ᵛ x^(k+1)(x−u)P(x)dx=0 (k=0,1).
The derivative-free real contradiction can be checked without a grid: rescale u to one, so P(ux) is proportional to the cubic Z=35x³−60x²+30x−4 orthogonal to degrees ≤2 for x dx on [0,1]. Lower degree would already be zero by positivity. Direct integration gives, for t=v/u,
    6∫₀ᵗ x(x−1)Z= t²(t−1)²(35t²−44t+12),
    6∫₀ᵗ x²(x−1)Z= t³(t−1)²(30t²−35t+8).
The two residual quadratics have no common root: 6 times the first minus 7 times the second is 16−19t, and the first evaluated at 16/19 has numerator −84. Both integrals cannot vanish. This completes the universal grade-seven result.

## Sharpened grade-six equality with arbitrary varying heads inside masked classes

Already at a unit band f₆=f₃+3, F₆ is noninvariant by its incoming gain. If F₃ is invariant, the same F₃ algebra proof uses just F₆ and ≥4 w knots. It provides genuine site-class indicators in F₃, but w need not be shown zero on T at this horizon. Here is the exact extension of the four-knot lemma in that situation: suppose globally F_j⊂U+wP_(j−4) for j=4,5,6, where U is spanned by the genuine grade-three site-class indicators. Allow w to vary inside each class. All scalar-mask tests and commutator refunds in the cited four-knot proof are unchanged (on outside rows U=0). In its paired input/output step for t=3,4 choose in each class with any row strictly before i_t one such witness of w, write its value h_a, and subtract the genuine pure local column (P(a)−h_a q(a))1_class instead of P(a)1_class. When the earlier w values within this class differ, put a into D, along with the already charged outside splits and nonzero-headed outside origins. Then q|D=0 guarantees the input vanishes on EVERY earlier row including that class. On the entire prefix the zero A-image gives JP(c)=u+wR(c) with u∈U constant on the class. At its earlier differing values the same JP(a) forces R(a)=0, so that very same member of D is charged at output too. Coincidence of several classes, an outside split or an origin at that site incurs only one charge, since it is a constraint on q and R at one numerical point. If no row of a class has appeared one may choose h_a arbitrarily. This argument does not require any compatibility with its later rows or multiplying by 1_T at grade greater than available. The full rank comparison and the fatal fourth determinant are unchanged. Thus for p≥8 the grade-six unit band is incompatible with invariant F₃, even without information about F₇.

If F₃ is noninvariant, the F₃ compression, U algebra, w vanishing on T and degree bound deg P≤4−r all followed just from the unit band to F₆ and its noninvariance. Whenever r≥2 one has Cw∈F₃ and deg P≤2; the first contrast xP(c)−Σ (xP)(a)1_class−Cw now costs at most THREE. It is nonzero by the positive xP² test, supported off T and supp w, and at the first nonzero coordinate its true diagonal vanishes by R at grade five. On that row A of the contrast after each extra power 1,x,x² is zero, with discrepancy in F₆ there also zero. This gives ∫₀ᵘ x P(x)² dx=0 by linear combination, impossible for nonzero real u. If r=1 and P(0)=0, take P(c)−ΣP(a)1_class−w of grade ≤3. It is nonzero by testing against P (at grade ≤6), and its first nonzero coordinate u cannot be at zero. The same refund and three moment tests give ∫₀ᵘ P(x)[P(x)/x]dx=0, impossible. Finally if deg P≤1 even the grade-four xP−Cw masked contrast gives ∫₀ᵘ xP²=0 using its two moment tests through grade six.

Therefore in ANY order p≥8 the extremal equality f₆=f₃+3 requires F₃=U⊕span(w) with an actual F₃ site-class mask algebra U, disjoint single cubic head w, w=P(c) on supp w for a quadratic or cubic P with P(0)≠0, and F_j=U⊕wP_(j−3) through grade six. (Here “cubic head” means genuine grade at most three, not degree of P.) In this extremal profile the earliest active clean nonzero knot u already obeys ∫₀ᵘ xP=∫₀ᵘ x²P=0. These are conditional necessary restrictions, not the elimination of all such grade-six equalities. The universal f₇ bound is a different, unconditional statement.
