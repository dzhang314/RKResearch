## Why a refund or a new stage count does not follow automatically

The signed point form F is not generally a new locally-nine stage that one can trivially merge at an unprotected primary site. More generally let U be the known residual space, B the distinct anchor locations, and let spatial columns N represent null linear forms on U in sampled primary coordinates. For k independent carried error responses, a necessity for total cancellation of each arbitrary coordinate is rank k on the restriction N|_B if anchor coefficient rows are the k unit vectors. Adding just one new point zero at a late nonanchor V adds the spatial column e_V, whose restriction to B is zero, when the point conditions on U have full rank. Hence it cannot pay the missing third anchor in the existing p19 two-null design. This argument is about arbitrary coordinates, not a claim the actual three errors are linearly independent. A genuine signed nonlocal observation has the needed anchor component and cannot be replaced by a convenient later point.
