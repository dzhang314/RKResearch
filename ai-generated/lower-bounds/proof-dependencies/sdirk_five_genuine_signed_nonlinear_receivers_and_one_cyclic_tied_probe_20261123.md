# Five genuine signed integration-by-parts receivers, and one cyclic ordinary probe at every tied strict endpoint

23 November 2026. This is an unrestricted numerical nonlinear stage-price theorem, not an improved integer in the minimum table. At p=20, every nonsingular triangular order-20 tableau has an actual joint Hankel rank of at least 55 in five fixed, cheap, zero-density two-branch/commutator receivers. At every strict s=55 candidate ONE explicit genuine receiver already has full joint rank 55. The one receiver is the clock probe unless the physical last defect equals the first defect; at exactly that exceptional value the clock probe is the zero row and a specified grade-two nonlinear probe is cyclic instead. There is also a standalone obstruction to simultaneous left/right invisibility using only one endpoint cubic identity. Signed weights, zero weights, repeated and exterior clocks, and all common diagonals gamma != 0 are allowed. No normalized transposed tableau is used. The algebraic statements below work in characteristic zero. Only the optional Euclidean sum-of-squares description uses real coefficients. ## Actual receivers and the unrestricted identity, even without D(1)

Write b as a row, e=1, B=diag(b), c=Ae, C=diag(c), g=Ac-c^2/2, G=diag(g), R=CA-AC-A^2, P=AR-RA and K=A+C. Scalar polynomial powers of c are coordinate powers. Define the real symmetric numerical defect M=BA+A^T B-b^T b. For an actual ordinary stage field f its row
    f^T M = b diag(f) A + (Af)^T B - (bf)b
is the signed sum of the two actual marked branch placements, with the scalar product correction. It has zero density at grade grade(f)+1: the two densities are the integrals from x to 1 and from 0 to x of the same scalar profile, and their sum is bf when the latter is paid. On any numerical input h this measures b(f * Ah)+b(Af * h)-(bf)(bh). A raw bP is pure because R has zero scalar operator, and (Pe)^T B is a genuine weighted pure ordinary row. These are measured coefficient responses, not symbolic signs or a stage-graph proxy.

Put d=e^T M=b(A+C)-b. The following identity holds for EVERY classical-order-at-least-three RK method, even when A is dense, weights vanish or D(1) fails:
    bR = (2c^2-Ac)^T M + c^T M(A-C) + bP - (Pe)^T B
         - d(A + I/2 + K A + R).                                      (1)
Here 2c^2-Ac is one ordinary grade-two linear combination, not a nonlinear transform of a receiver. For an explicit derivation set A0=eb-A. Then
    A^T B=B A0+M,
    R^T B=BR + b^T d-d^T b + M K-K^T M .
The second follows by reversing the three factors in R, inserting the first identity and using Ae=c and be=1. To expand e^T P^T B use P^T=R^T A^T-A^T R^T. The pure reference term is b[R,A0]=(bRe)b-bR+bP. In a third-order method bRe=-2bg=0, de=0, dc=0 and bc=1/2, directly from be=1, bc=1/2, bc^2=1/3 and bAc=1/6. The term from b^T d-d^T b is -dA-d/2 after left multiplication by e^T. The remaining terms are -d K A-dR+2c^T M A-c^T M K+(Kc-2g)^T M, giving exactly (1). In particular the d-correction cannot just be discarded on arbitrary methods.

Let W be the unital algebra generated by A,C, and let V=W e be the reachable space. Define the five actual receiver rows
    d, c^T M, (2c^2-Ac)^T M, bP, (Pe)^T B .
Stack all rows h u of these five receivers for words u in A,C and evaluate on columns v e for words v in A,C. This is a single signed numerical coefficient Hankel, with genuine ordinary input and genuine raw one-mark outputs. Write r_5 for its rank (computed with finite spanning row/column lists). If triangular A has nonsingular diagonal and the method has classical order p>=3, then
    s >= r_5 >= L(p):=ceil(floor((p+1)^2/4)/2).                        (2)
In fact its row module contains b W, since (1) gives bR and R^-1 lies in W by Cayley-Hamilton. Thus all true scalar-word transfers are covered on V, not just matching rank counts at the two ends. Equivalently s is at least L(p) plus the sum of the number of unreachable physical dimensions and of the reachable dimensions invisible to ALL five receivers and words. The two losses are counted without overlap. For a measurement using the first three rows only, the additional rank supplied by the two endpoint commutator rows is precisely the rank of their stacked word rows RESTRICTED to that first reachable kernel; it is not automatically their full numerical rank.

For a self-contained rank bound on the scalar part let m=ceil(p/2), X_j=c^j, Y_i=bC^i for i,j=0,...,m-1, and Gamma_ij=1/(i+j+1). Gamma is a nonsingular rational Hilbert matrix. The actual operator R costs two and has zero scalar profile by xJ-Jx-J^2=0 for integration J from zero; its diagonal is -a_ii^2, so it is invertible. By the marked/ordinary rooted-tree product rule, the (i,j) entry of H(z)=Y(I-zR)^-1X-Gamma has valuation at least ceil((p-i-j)/2) (the nonnegative minimum suffices). Its determinant has valuation at least ceil(m(p-m+1)/2)=L(p). Pass to the joint R-reachable/observable quotient from X,Y, dimension q. Its R0 remains invertible: inverse is a polynomial in R, hence preserves the invariant subspaces. Sylvester gives det(I-zR0) det H(z)=(-1)^m det(Gamma) det[I-zR0-X0 Gamma^-1 Y0], a polynomial of degree exactly q. Thus q>=L(p). Those numerical Hankel rows and columns are a subset of scalar-word row and column spans, proving (2). This does not assert anything beyond the paid rooted-tree budget for an individual coefficient first appearing above order p.

## ONE cheap probe is cyclic at every tied strict equality p>=6

Tied means p even or p=3 mod 4, s=L(p). Every diagonal is the same gamma != 0. The saturated flags force
    d=0, Pe=0, bP=0, bG=-(g_s/gamma^2)bR,  bW=all physical rows, We=all physical columns.
For completeness, let N=p-1, let E_j,W_j be the ordinary column and scalar-word row flags and F_j,Z_j their respective zero-profile flags. The ordinary scalar-word columns alone already satisfy the lower recurrence for E_j: R transports the previous space injectively to pure grade j, and the scalar profile powers 0,...,min(j,N-j) separate there by the nonsingular Hilbert Gram. The analogous row recurrence uses right R and bC^i. Its minimal dimensions are t_-1=t_-2=0, t_j=t_(j-2)+1+min(j,N-j). At tied saturation t_N=t_(N-1)=s, so all these dimensions are exact, including F_j=R E_(j-2) and Z_j=W_(j-2) R. All genuine marked pure rows are contained since they annihilate E_(N-j) and s-t_(N-j)=t_(j-2). In particular d annihilates E_(N-1), so d=0, and bG lies in span(bR). The last weight b_s !=0 by full word reach, and comparing the s-th component of bG versus bR gives its multiplier as written.

Now P=[A-gamma I,R+gamma^2I] has its first two lower diagonals zero. The first two entries of Pe and last two entries of bP vanish. Pe lies in F_3=R span(e,c); since equal first clocks would make all first-two word evaluations identical, the first two coordinates of that pure space determine it. Hence Pe=0. Similarly bP is in Z_3=span(b,bC)R. The last two coordinates of b,bC are independent: d=0 gives b_s a_(s,s-1)=(c_s-c_(s-1))b_(s-1). A zero penultimate weight would make its coordinate invisible to all scalar words; coincident final clocks give zero final subtap and proportional restrictions of all row words on the last pair. Both contradict full row-word reach. This proves bP=0.

The consequence of (1) is now exceptionally sharp. With alpha=1/2-g_s/gamma^2, the equality d=0 also gives, by direct expansion, c^T M=bG+bR/2=alpha bR. Consequently
    bR=(2c^2-Ac)^T M+c^T M(A-C).                                      (3)
Choose the genuine forward probe f as follows:
    f=c when g_s != gamma^2/2;
    f=2c^2-Ac when g_s = gamma^2/2.
Then f^T M is a NONZERO scalar multiple of bR, and
    rank [ f^T M u(A,C) v(A,C)e ]_(u,v words) = s .                   (4)
Indeed bR W=bW since R is invertible in W. The columns span just as proved above. Each word family can be terminated when the span stabilizes: on the left a length cutoff s-1 always suffices from one cyclic row, and on the right p-2 suffices at equality. Thus (4) exhibits a finite actual nonsingular s-square minor; it is NOT a generic-rank assertion. At p20/s55 this single signed one- or two-grade ordinary probe returns the full rank 55. If one wants the same linear-combination shape on both branches, f=2c^2-Ac+t c has row bR[(1+alpha t)I-alpha(A-C)]. Any real t with 1+alpha(t+c_i-gamma) nonzero for every physical coordinate makes the extra triangular factor invertible in W and gives exactly the same rank. A real sum of the squares of these measured row functionals yields the same rank, but no additional RK conditions come from such a sum.

The entire cheap-word transfer, not merely its span, is identified in (3): for f=c it is the stated multiple of the insertion bR; at the exceptional last defect the degree-two receiver itself is exactly bR. This paid cyclic response does not mean every probe value is nonzero, does not impose classical order above p, and cannot be added to the 55 commutator stages as a separate charge.

## Why a jointly invisible signed sector cannot survive a true nonlinear tree

There is a shorter independent joint-invariant obstruction and a broader clean/ghost statement that does not need both endpoint commutators. Let U be the largest subspace in ker M invariant under A and C (the kernel of the actual M-word stack). K0=U intersect ker B is A,C-invariant: C commutes with B; if x is in K0 then Mx=BAx since Bx=0 and bx=0. It is the maximal wholly ghost A,C-invariant subspace. If U/K0 is nonzero, simultaneous triangularity of A,C supplies a common eigenline on it. Concretely the descending chronological coordinate flag induces a flag with drops at most one on the quotient by K0; intersect it with U/K0 and use the last nonzero flag piece. Take a representative u with Au=gamma u and Cu=delta u modulo K0. Since Bu is nonzero and Mu=0,
    (Bu)^T (A+gamma I) = (bu)b .
The matrix on the left is invertible (all its diagonal entries are 2gamma), hence bu !=0. With h=(Bu)^T/(bu), we have
    he=1, hA=b-gamma h, hC=delta h, delta=1-gamma .
The last equality is hAe=hCe. On EVERY strict SDIRK of order at least two, not necessarily tied or satisfying D(1),
    h g=bc-gamma delta-delta^2/2=gamma^2/2 .
Thus adjoining the SINGLE genuine quadratic row bG to the M-word stack removes every non-ghost joint blind sector. Equivalently
    intersection_w ker([M;bG] w(A,C)) = intersection_w ker(B w(A,C)). (5)
To apply the eigenline argument to (5), take U on the left to begin with; then bG u=(bu) h g contradicts its defining kernel. The opposite inclusion follows from invariance, including all ghosts and any cancellations. This is an exact kernel identity over characteristic zero, not a positive-weight proof. In particular on an arbitrary strict p>=3 tableau, the combined rank of these measurements on genuine scalar-word inputs is at least L(p), since the right stack contains all b-word rows in its span. For non-saturated methods the rows of M are resolved physical probes; the five cheap genuine rows of (1) avoid demanding that all individual coordinate probes be available as cheap trees.

Moreover already the M-stack ALONE has this same kernel if d=0, bRe=0 and Pe=0. Indeed D(1) implies b(A+C)=b, and the displayed h identities give hR=-gamma^2 h. Thus
    h P e = h A R e - h R A e = bRe + gamma^2 = gamma^2 !=0 ,
contradicting Pe=0. At every tied p>=6, b words see every stage, so no wholly ghost invariant subspace exists either. This excludes a simultaneous left/right invariant quotient directly by a true cubic tree identity. It does not use the reverse commutator, an inverse weight, a sign restriction, or a fabricated reverse method.

## Two exact opposite-end balances at the same resolvent, and a compositional endpoint exclusion

The structural cubic identities independently give a short physical transfer test. Write T=A+tI over the rational function field, v=T^-1 e, h=b T^-1. Directly Av=e-tv gives
    Rv=-T(C-tI)v-t^2 v .
Consequently bP v=-bT^2(C-tI)v-t^2-bRe. Similarly hA=b-th and D(1) give hR=h(C-(1-t)I)T-t^2 h, so hP e=bRe-h(C-(1-t)I)T^2 e+t^2. At tied equality p>=6, bP=Pe=0 and bRe=0. Thus for every parameter where T is invertible, and identically as rational functions,
    bT^2 C T^-1 e = bT^-1 (I-C) T^2 e = t/2 .                       (6)
Here b T e=t+1/2 uses ordinary order two. All apparent strict true-tap poles on the left and right of (6) cancel exactly, even with arbitrary mixed weights. This is a consequence of the two actual cubic commutators, not a separate independent stage charge.

In particular at the always-regular t=gamma, compute two scalar physical witnesses with a known NONZERO answer:
    bT^2(C-gamma I)T^-1 e = -gamma^2,
    bT^-1(C-(1-gamma)I)T^2 e = +gamma^2 .                            (7)
No normalized transpose or staged eigenvector search is involved. No single constant first tap column a_i1=2gamma at every i>1 is allowed, whatever b_1: it would give T e_1=2gamma e and (C-gamma I)e_1=0, contradicting the first test. No literal terminal midpoint step with b_s=2gamma and a_sj=b_j for j<s is allowed: e_s^T T=b and c_s=1-gamma would contradict the second. Therefore the whole class obtained by adding a literal common-diagonal midpoint step before OR after an otherwise arbitrary strict scheme pays at least L(p)+1 stages at tied orders p>=6. Other stages may be dense and signed or have zero weights. At p20 this excludes 55 for either kind of composition, not for all strict SDIRK. These opposite nonzero answers are not added as different stages without separate support information.
