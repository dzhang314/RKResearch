# Proofs carried out over the reals

This category is for proofs relying on real-root arguments, real-variable analytic tools or other real hypotheses, or whose direct ordered-field formulation has not yet been audited. Their finite impossibility conclusions still transfer to ordered fields as explained in the index. “Real proof” does not claim existence of a complex counterexample. This initial batch records the real comparison table; further proof files will be catalogued here as their hypotheses are checked.
