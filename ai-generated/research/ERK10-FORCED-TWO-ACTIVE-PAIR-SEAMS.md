## The mean, the literal supported mask, and its scalar residue

\(R(u)=\mu_u/\nu_u\) for one polynomial \(R\ne0\) of
degree at most three. On singleton active rows the literal

## A genuine grade-six variance port

\[
       \Delta=\frac{(va)(wb)}{\nu}(a-b)^2.

\[
     \Delta(\nu-a\omega)=a\nu(R-a)\,(R\omega-\nu).
\]
