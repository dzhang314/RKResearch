       s=u-\alpha r,\quad \alpha=u_3/r_3,\quad

## The exact missing reverse square

The actual reduced reverse pure spaces satisfy
\(\bar F_3=W_2\), \(\bar F_4=W_3+\langle u^2\rangle\).
Its degree-three separation and clock-invariant \(\bar F_3\)
pay the two-dimensional outgoing Hermite gain. Thus
\(u^2\notin W_3\), so it cannot annihilate all of
\[
                      E_5|_I=\mathcal P_{\le5}(c_I)+F_5|_I
\]
with the live \(B_I\)-pairing. We audit exactly which
terms can detect this.

Every ordinary clock polynomial pairs to zero with \(u^2\).
If \(v\ne t\) this is the sum of the two disjoint
\(br^2,bs^2\) single-clock moments, each zero by the paid
annihilators (since \(r,s\in W_2\) and are actual old low
vectors). If \(v=t\), all of \(u^2\) is on one clock,
and \(bu^2=0\) follows again from \(u\in W_2\),
\(u=\alpha r+s\) of actual original grade four.
The ghost coordinate is also invisible.

The \(r\)-pairing of \(u^2\) is zero, because
\(b u(ur)=0\) is a real grade-seven product
(\(r\) grade three and \(u\) has an old grade-four lift)
tested against the normalized annihilator of \(E_7\).
For the \(s\)-pairing, \(b r^2s=0\) by \(r^2\in E_6\)
and \(s\in W_2\); also \(b r s^2=0\), because
\(0=b u(rs)=\alpha b r^2s+b r s^2\) is again
a true grade-seven test. Consequently
\[
                  \langle u^2,s\rangle_B=b s^3 .
\]
For the \(w\)-pairing use \(0=b u(rw)=\alpha b r^2w+b rsw\)
at grade seven. Expanding gives
\[
                  \langle u^2,w\rangle_B=b\,s u w.
\]
Finally if \(v=t\), the \(Cw\)-pairing is \(t\) times
the \(w\)-pairing, and if \(v\ne t\), the \(r,s\)
supports are disjoint and the grade-seven identity kills
the entire \(\alpha^2 b r^2 w\) part before or after
the clock multiplication. In the latter case the
\(Cw\)-pairing is \(v\) times the \(w\)-pairing.

Thus the escaped reverse square requires the necessary
disjunction
\[
                      b s^3\ne0
                         \quad\text{or}\quad b\,s u w\ne0 .
\]
The first is **not** asserted to vanish. Replacing this
disjunction by the crossed test alone would incorrectly
pay a product of total grade ten or higher.

## Consequences for the literal \(s\)-support

The live \(s\)-support has at least **three physical sites**.
The two paid moments \(b s=b s^2=0\) exclude just one.
With two nonzero amplitudes they must be equal and the
weights opposite (the two-point weighted variance equation).
Testing the localized \(b s\) row in \(W_2\) against the
actual \(r\in E_6\) makes \(r\) equal on that pair.
One member is physical 13 with \(r_{13}=0\), so the
pair is disjoint from \(r\). Then \(b s^3=0\), and
\(b s u w=b s^2 w=s_{13}\,b s w=0\) by \(w\in E_6\).
Both charges vanish, contradiction.

At exactly three physical \(s\)-sites, their nonzero
amplitudes must be **pairwise distinct**. Exactly two
amplitude values would each need a zero pooled ordinary
weight from \(b s=b s^2=0\), but one value occurs only
once. If all three amplitudes were equal, the same two
\(s\)-weighted reads of \(r,r^2\) give
\(\sum b r=\sum b r^2=0\) on those three sites.
There are at most two \(r\)-overlap sites (physical 13
is not one). A single overlap is impossible; two
force equal \(r\) and opposing weights. In the latter
case the remaining physical 13 weight is nonzero,
contrary to the zero sum of weights on a uniform
\(s\)-support. With no overlap, the two marked charges
vanish as in the preceding paragraph. Thus exactly
three means three unequal nonzero values. The cubic
charge \(b s^3\) must then be nonzero by the three-moment
Vandermonde. Indeed it equals the product of those three
amplitudes times the ordinary sum of weights on that
three-row support, so that sum is nonzero as well.
Here ordinary weights at any other \(s\)-free rows of
the same clock are not included.

If \(v\ne t\) and the cubic charge vanishes, the other
charge is the genuine \(b s^2 w\ne0\).
There must then be at least four \(s\)-active rows.
At least two of their \(w\)-values are nonzero: \(b s w=0\)
and a single contributing site could not cancel.
There are at least two different \(s\)-amplitudes
among these contributing sites, since otherwise
\(b s^2 w\) would be the shared amplitude times \(b s w\).
Because \(w=A^\vee r\) uses only later old
\(r\)-active rows, both these sites lie before physical 12,
and each has a genuine forward tap to a later
\(r\)-active stage. This is a necessary chronology
condition, not a further exclusion.
