# The two-carrier, three-opposing-active-pair ERK10 branch is impossible

Continue the all-live, two-carrier order-ten equality case over an
arbitrary characteristic-zero field, in the
[remaining carrier atlas](ERK10-THREE-CARRIER-EXCLUSION.md).
Consider the zero-pooled three-label population \((2,2,2)\):
besides carriers at rows 2 and 13, the nonzero values of \(r=As\)
on noncarrier rows are three clock pairs, each uniform in \(r\)
and with opposing weights. This note excludes the entire case.
It does not treat \((2,3,3)\), the five-label case, or
the nonzero-pooled branches. No change to the 14–15 interval
is asserted.

## An actual clean-only hyperplane, without a dirty chronology premise

The ordinary scalar contributions of all four pairs cancel.
There remain six true clean physical coordinates (\(s=r=0\)),
with numerical clocks in chronological order
\(0,u,w,y,v,1\). They are six distinct Lobatto sites, by the exact
ordinary quadrature. These are the only clocks used in the finite
part of the proof; the three dirty labels themselves need not
be found or numerically ordered.

Let \(G\) be the monic cubic of the three active clock labels,
and let \(R\) be the quadratic interpolant of their nonzero
\(r\)-values. Then \(\gcd(G,R)=1\).
The actual grade-three vectors
\(G(c)-G(\theta)s\) and \(R(c)-r-R(\theta)s\)
are supported on the six true clean coordinates and have models
\(G,R\), respectively. (The carrier \(r\)-values are zero.)
Consequently the four-dimensional hyperplane
\[
       W=\operatorname{span}\{G,XG,R,XR\}
                               \subset \mathcal P_{\le4}
\]
consists of models with actual degree-four clean-only lifts,
whose true clean values are exactly the model evaluations.
Independence follows since \(G L_1+R L'_1=0\) would make
\(G\mid L'_1\) for linear factors \(L_1,L'_1\).
Fix a nonzero functional \(\ell\) with \(W=\ker\ell\).
The two-dimensional grade-three clean-only model space
\(W_3=\operatorname{span}\{G,R\}\) satisfies both
\(\ell(W_3)=0\) and \(\ell(XW_3)=0\).
These lifts and this hyperplane need no assumptions on the
placement of any dirty pair within the physical rows.

Let
\[
 \begin{split}
 h_L&=X(X-u),                 & h_R&=(X-1)(X-v),\\
 L_0&=h_L(X-w),&L_1&=X L_0,\\
 R_0&=h_R(X-y),&R_1&=X R_0 .
 \end{split}
\]
Use \(U_L=h_L\mathcal P_{\le2}\) and \(U_R=h_R\mathcal P_{\le2}\)
for the two three-dimensional anchored spaces.
Their intersection is the line generated by \(D_0=h_Lh_R\),
and their sum is all \(\mathcal P_{\le4}\).
The two-dimensional subspaces
\(L=\langle L_0,L_1\rangle,\ R'=\langle R_0,R_1\rangle\)
each intersect \(W\) in a nonzero vector. Choose \(P\in L\cap W\),
\(Q\in R'\cap W\), both nonzero.

For the exact bilinear form
\[
 B(F,H)=\int_0^1 (J F)(X)H(X)\,dX
       =\sum_{i,j}\frac{F_i H_j}{(i+1)(i+j+2)}
\]
the original and reversed **actual** masks now give
\[
 B(P,H)=0\quad(H\in W\cap U_R),\qquad
 B(F,Q)=0\quad(F\in W\cap U_L).
\]
Indeed the clean-only lift of \(P\) is zero through the
first three clean sites and on all dirty rows, so its actual
unary is zero through the first right clean site \(y\).
A clean-only lift of \(H\in U_R\) is zero on the other
right clean sites \(v,1\). Hence their Hadamard product
of grades \(5+4\) is identically zero.
The reverse argument uses the analogous last-left site \(w\)
and integration by parts between \(J\) and \(\bar J\).
The full order-ten weighted transpose exists in this all-live
case by the prior equality theorem.
These facts hold even if the 7|7 physical seam lies elsewhere.

## The hyperplane is quadratic in a projective pair

The finite checks below prove that the matrix
\(K=(B(L_i,R_j))_{i,j=0,1}\) is nonsingular in every possible
chronological order of the four internal Lobatto clocks.
Thus for \(P=aL_0+bL_1\), \(Q\) is proportional to
\[
     Q(a,b)=-(aK_{01}+bK_{11})R_0+(aK_{00}+bK_{10})R_1,
\]
the unique kernel line in \(R'\) for \(B(P,\cdot)\).
Both \(\ell|_{U_R}\) and \(\ell|_{U_L}\) are nonzero:
if either vanished, the corresponding paid zero would
annihilate all \(R'\) or \(L\), contrary to \(K\) nonsingular.
The paid relations therefore identify these restrictions
up to nonzero scale with \(B(P,\cdot)|_{U_R}\) and
\(B(\cdot,Q)|_{U_L}\), respectively.

Put \(d_R=B(P,D_0)\), \(d_L=B(D_0,Q)\).
The finite check also proves that these two linear forms
in \((a,b)\) are independent. In a valid hyperplane they
cannot separately vanish, because both restrictions of
\(\ell\) agree on \(D_0\) and both scalar multiples
are nonzero. Thus both \(d_R,d_L\ne0\).
The hyperplane functional is known up to nonzero scaling:
take it to satisfy
\[
     \ell|_{U_L}=d_R B(\cdot,Q)|_{U_L},\qquad
     \ell|_{U_R}=d_L B(P,\cdot)|_{U_R}.
\]
The two prescriptions agree on \(D_0\), and reconstruct
a functional on all \(\mathcal P_{\le4}\). For computation,
use the basis \(h_L,Xh_L,X^2 h_L,h_R,Xh_R\).
It is a basis because the only overlap of \(U_L,U_R\)
is spanned by \(D_0\), whose \(X^2h_R\) coefficient
in \(U_R\) is nonzero.
Every coefficient \(\ell(X^i)\) of this reconstructed
functional is now a homogeneous quadratic in \((a,b)\).

## The two boundary determinants and their exact residue certification

A nonzero model in \(W_3\) that vanishes at the origin
exists since \(\dim W_3=2\). Its numerical lift is zero
before the first nonzero true clean \(u\), and its unary
there is point exact through grade four. Thus it also
has zero primitive at \(u\).
Likewise a nonzero model in \(W_3\) vanishing at 1 has
zero reverse primitive at \(v\).
Because \(W_3\) is annihilated by \(\ell\) and
\(\ell\circ X\), the resulting necessary determinants
on \(\mathcal P_{\le3}\) are
\[
 \det[\ell;\ell\circ X;\operatorname{ev}_0;
            P_0\mapsto{\textstyle\int_0^u}P_0]=0,\qquad
 \det[\ell;\ell\circ X;\operatorname{ev}_1;
            P_0\mapsto{\textstyle\int_v^1}P_0]=0 .
\]
They are homogeneous binary **quartics** in \((a,b)\).
No equality of the forward and reflected grade-five
pure flags is needed.

The coefficients of all tests belong to the splitting
field of the four interior Lobatto roots. The integer quartic
\(42X^4-84X^3+56X^2-14X+1\) splits into four
distinct roots \(14,23,37,46\) modulo \(59\).
Reduce the splitting field at any prime over \(59\):
one may adjoin the four roots to \(\mathbb Z_{(59)}\)
as integral elements (leading coefficient 42 is a unit),
and take any maximal ideal over 59 by lying-over.
The four images must be the four distinct roots above,
by reducing their factorization, in one of the 24 orders.
All denominators in \(B\) and the two terminal primitive
vectors are paid modulo 59. The nonzero denominators to recover \(\ell\) explicitly
are \(v\) and \((u-1)(u-v)\); they stay nonzero in all
24 reductions. To see the second denominator, if \(l_i=\ell(X^i)\)
and \(a_0,a_1,a_2,b_0,b_1\) are the prescribed values
on the five basis vectors, the first three equations give
\(l_2=a_0+u l_1,\ l_3=a_1+u l_2,\ l_4=a_2+u l_3\).
The last gives
\(l_1=(b_1-a_1-(u-v-1)a_0)/((u-1)(u-v))\),
and \(l_0=(b_0+(v+1)l_1-l_2)/v\).

In [ERK10ThreePairModularGate.lean](ERK10ThreePairModularGate.lean),
the finite coefficient certificates check for each order:
1. \(\det K\ne0\), and independence of \(d_R,d_L\)
   as linear functions of \((a,b)\);
2. at least one quartic determinant at the infinity
   argument \((0,1)\) is nonzero;
3. the two affine quartics at \((1,b)\) have a checked
   Bézout identity with multipliers of degree at most three
   modulo 59. Their coefficients are recovered by a verified
   inverse Vandermonde on the five distinct residues
   \(b=0,\dots,4\).

The finite arithmetic uses kernel evaluation, not floating
point. Conditions 2 and 3 give nonzero reduction of the
fixed-degree 8-by-8 Sylvester determinant: the reduced
polynomials are coprime and at least one has full degree
four. Therefore the map \((U,V)\mapsto U f+V g\) on
two degree-three inputs has trivial kernel by divisibility,
and its square matrix is invertible.
Nonzero reduction implies a nonzero characteristic-zero
determinant. Thus the two affine quartics have no common
root over any extension of the coefficient subfield, and
the infinity case is also excluded. There is no nonzero
projective \((a,b)\), contradiction.

The actual support/filtration argument, polynomial degree,
and splitting-field reduction are justified mathematically
above; the closed Lean checks verify the complete finite
residue arithmetic rather than the entire rooted theorem.
