# Central nine-site regular chart: the opposite exclusive orientation is impossible

**Scope.** Continue the [finite regular central atlas](ERK11-NINE-SITE-HALF-DIFFERENT-CENTER-FINITE.md):
genuine nine-site separation, half–half overlap with
different extras, dormant at physical 9, and
\(\Delta_0\ne0\). In the opposite orientation the
**right-only** clock \(x=v\) is at physical 8 and
the **left-only** clock \(y=u\) is at physical 10.
This closes that orientation over characteristic zero
given those hypotheses, without numerical root scouting.
Together with the matched and rank-one exits it closes
central physical-9 dormancy for different extras in
this specific half–half chart. It does not close an
edge dormant station, a four-distinct calibration,
or ordinary 17-stage ERK11.

## 1. Two literal mixed marks and the nodal reduction

Use the notation of the [matched exit and opposite weighted contacts](ERK11-NINE-SITE-HALF-DIFFERENT-CENTER-MATCHED-EXIT.md).
Thus \(a,b=2-a,h=a/2,d=a-1\), \(y=Y/E\) with
\(E=A_0x+C_0\ne0,\ Y=-(B_0x+D_0)\), \(t\) the
common free extra and \(z=\zeta\) the dormant clock.
The actual two-root modified moment calculation gives
\[
 D_2=m_0m_2-m_1^2\ne0,\quad
 D_1=m_0m_3-m_1m_2,\quad D_0^{\rm nod}=m_1m_3-m_2^2,
 \qquad
 D_2(Z-z)(Z-t)=D_2Z^2-D_1Z+D_0^{\rm nod}.
\]
Here \(D_0^{\rm nod}\) is a nodal coefficient, not
the earlier Möbius constant \(D_0\). The true shared
free and dormant clocks are distinct; all their
scalar masses and all five known active masses
are live. In particular no unseen singular nodal
or repeated-root case is deleted.

The actual difference of repeated-exclusive
anchored grade-five readings, multiplied by the
grade-four partner and appropriate scalar factors,
gave the equations
\[
\begin{aligned}
 I(x,z)\langle D_y,\Phi_R\rangle_N+
    30V_a(y)(y-x)(C_1-zH)\langle\ell_a,\Phi_R\rangle_N&=0,\\
 I(y,z)\langle \Phi_L,D_x^R\rangle_N+
    30V_b(x)(x-y)(C_2-zH)\langle\Phi_L,r_d\rangle_N&=0 .
\end{aligned}
\]
In these \(I(p,q)=I_2-(p+q)I_1+pq I_0\).
The degree-ten grades are \(4+4+2\) for the
masked square-clock product and \(5+4+1\),
\(4+5+1\) for the two primitives. The masks isolate
row 10 in the first and row 8 in the second;
the two clock variables cannot be interchanged
without exchanging their physical roles. These
identities use actual repeated noncarrier rows,
not any equality of their response values. They
require no division by the isolated mixed amplitude,
by a possible zero primitive at an active row,
or by any raw derivative contact coefficient.

To make the coefficient replay explicit, write
\((L_0,L_1)=((a-t)(aE-Y),-tY)\), the actual left
derivative coefficients times \(E\); write
\((R_0,R_1)=((t-d)(x-d),-(1-t)(1-x))\) for the
right derivative coefficients. Let \(K_j(w)\)
be the cubic pairings of \(D_w\) with \(D_R^j\)
after division by \(aw(a-w)\), and \(K_j^R(w)\)
those of \(D_w^R\) with \(D_L^j\) after division
by \(b(1-w)(w-d)\). Let
\[
 A_j=\langle\ell_a,D_R^j\rangle_N,\qquad
 B_j=\langle D_L^j,r_d\rangle_N,\qquad
 \widehat K_j=E^3K_j(Y/E).
\]
These \(A_j,B_j\) are basis pairings, not order
moments or output weights. Divide the original
mixed marks by the respective live clock factors
\(y(a-y),(1-x)(x-d)\), and clear powers of \(E\).
The resulting two bilinear polynomials in \(t,z\)
are exactly
\[
\begin{aligned}
 M_L={}&a I(x,z)(R_0\widehat K_0+R_1\widehat K_1)
 +30Y(aE-Y)(Y-xE)(C_1-zH)(R_0A_0+R_1A_1),\\

\end{aligned}
\]
In the first line use \(V_a(y)=[y(a-y)]^2\),
and in the second \(V_b(x)=[(1-x)(x-d)]^2\).
The first Gram contact gives a quadratic
\(G(t)=L_0R_0+T_4(a)L_1R_0+T_4(b)L_0R_1+

Both \(\Phi\) characters are genuine actual

\[
 U_M=M_{10}-M_{01},\quad
 V_M=D_2M_{00}+D_1M_{01}+D_0^{\rm nod}M_{11}.

Write \(U_L,V_L'\) and \(U_R,V_R'\) for the two

## 2. Exact width projection of the affine rank and norms

A necessary rank/norm system in \(a,x\) now has
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 R_{12}&=U_LV_R'-U_RV_L',\\
 R_L&=D_2U_LV_G-U_GV_L',&
 R_R&=D_2U_RV_G-U_GV_R',\\
 Q_L&=(V_L')^2+D_1U_LV_L'+D_0^{\rm nod}D_2U_L^2,&
 Q_R&=(V_R')^2+D_1U_RV_R'+D_0^{\rm nod}D_2U_R^2.
 \end{aligned}
\]
All five vanish. For the norms multiply the
true nodal polynomial at \(t\) by \(D_2U_i^2\)
and insert \(V_i'=-D_2U_it\). For rank just
subtract the two scalar multiples of the actual
affine equations. The formulas hold even in
a rank-zero coefficient fiber.

Trim only \(a^2 b^{4,2,4,2,6}\) respectively,
each exact and live, and keep the same names.
The bidegrees in \((a,x)\) are respectively
\((83,14),(94,15),(83,15),(116,18),(94,18)\).
The three projected width polynomials are
\(\operatorname{Res}_x(R_{12},R_L)\),
\(\operatorname{Res}_x(R_{12},R_R)\), and
\(\operatorname{Res}_x(R_{12},Q_L)\).
Every actual finite \(x\) gives all three
width equations, regardless of a specialized
degree drop. Their **exact** degrees are
\(2424,2298,2936\) and their common monic gcd is
\[
 a^{112}(a-2)^{74}
 \left(F_4/\operatorname{lc}(F_4)\right)^{49}
 \left(F_6/\operatorname{lc}(F_6)\right)^{52}
 F_{84}^{\,2},
\]
of degree 862. The earlier factors \(F_4,F_6\)
of \(\Delta_0\), and the zero widths \(a=0,2\),
are all forbidden. The exact monic polynomial
\(F_{84}\) is defined below.

Here the large rational resultants are computed
as integer determinants after clearing scalar
denominators and content in each input. For
primitive integer input polynomials \(f,g\) of
\(x\)-degrees \(n,m\), respectively, every
integer coefficient of their \(x\)-resultant
has absolute value at most
\[
 C=(n+m)!\,\lVert f\rVert_1^m\,\lVert g\rVert_1^n ,
\]
where the norms sum all absolute integer
coefficients in \((a,x)\).
This follows directly from the fixed-degree
Sylvester determinant expansion and the
submultiplicative coefficient \(\ell^1\) norm.
The three bounds have bit lengths
\(8157,7775,9835\) in the replay.
For each certified prime, retain the actual
input degrees and compute the corresponding
fixed-degree determinant modulo that prime.
The code reconstructs **every** coefficient
by CRT with modulus product strictly larger
than twice each corresponding \(C\), and
centers the coefficients in this interval.
This uniquely reconstructs the exact integer
polynomials, whose rational monic gcd is
computed thereafter. No assumption that a
tableau or its denominators survives any
prime reduction enters this calculation.

## 3. Every degree-84 width fiber is a forbidden nodal collapse

To define \(F_{84}\), take the monic rational
gcd of the three pairwise \(x\)-resultants
of \(D_0^{\rm nod},D_1,D_2\). Each resultant
has degree 168 in \(a\), and their monic
gcd is exactly
\[
 a^8(a-2)^8
 \left(F_4/\operatorname{lc}(F_4)\right)^4
 \left(F_6/\operatorname{lc}(F_6)\right)^4 F_{84}.
\]
The replay obtains the degree-84 monic factor
by exact rational factorization. Work in
the complete coefficient algebra
\(\mathscr A=\mathbb Z_{(101)}[a]/(F_{84})\);
it is finite free with the power basis.
The exact linear \(x\)-subresultant of \(D_2,D_1\)
has coefficients \(L(a),N(a)\) after reducing
at \(F_{84}\). The replay checks \(L\), \(a\),
and \(a-2\) are units modulo 101 (hence units
in this finite-free algebra). Homogeneous
substitution of \(x=-N/L\) into each nodal
coefficient gives zero exactly in the rational
coefficient algebra. Thus the monic
\(u(x)=x+N/L\) divides \(D_0^{\rm nod},D_1,D_2\)
also in \(\mathscr A[x]\): monic division is
integral, and \(\mathscr A\) injects into the
rational coefficient algebra.

From their formulas \(u\) divides the trimmed
\(R_{12}\), and \(u^2\) divides each of
\(R_L,R_R,Q_L,Q_R\). Indeed \(D_i,V_i',U_G,V_G\)
all have factor \(u\), so the displayed
rank and norm expressions have the stated
orders; trimming \(a,b\) does not change them.
An actual \(u(x)=0\) would force \(D_2=0\),
already ruled out by the genuine two-site
moment support. On any other root \(x\) of
the five equations, all five quotient
polynomials must vanish.

The exact reduction of \(F_{84}\) modulo 101
is squarefree with factor degrees \(78,1,1,2,2\).
In each complete coefficient field the five
quotient degrees are \(13,13,13,16,16\) and
their gcd is constant. In particular the
first polynomial has a leading coefficient
that is a unit throughout the product of
the five fields, hence in \(\mathscr A\).
Make it monic and use its finite-free
power basis. The multiplication maps for
the other four quotients generate the
unit ideal modulo 101 by the checked gcds.
The corresponding linear map over
\(\mathbb Z_{(101)}\) consequently has a
maximal minor that is a unit, and is
surjective in characteristic zero as well.
This excludes an actual common root of
the quotients, including any coefficient
specialization with an apparent affine
rank dropout. No real-root choice and no
restriction on the sign of a weight is used.
