# Four candidate clocks for the sole dormant center are excluded

**Exact scope.** In an order-eleven explicit Runge–Kutta tableau at seventeen physical stages over an ordered field, take the all-live half-clock two-carrier branch with its carriers at physical 2 and 16 and both seventh boundaries genuinely new. Assume \(q=10\) nonzero-first-pool active labels and \(n=12\) active noncarrier interior rows, both full plateau pairs at physical 4,5 and 13,14, and the sole dormant noncarrier interior row at physical 9. Then its clock \(\zeta=c_9\) does not belong to \(\{0,1,a,d\}\), where \(a,d=1-b\) are the two full active plateau labels. This does not assert its clock differs from every active singleton clock, or exclude the full \(q=10,n=12\) topology.

* The plateau aliases \(\zeta=a,d\) are excluded by the [full plateau-alias atlas](ERK11-NONZERO-TEN-TWELVE-PLATEAU-ALIAS-CLOSED.md): the regular width family and every exceptional raw pivot are covered.
* For \(\zeta=0\), the measured literal center direction is \(L_9\propto\ell_a\). The [regular endpoint atlas](ERK11-NONZERO-TEN-TWELVE-ENDPOINT-ALIAS-REGULAR-EXIT.md) covers all live pivots, including the large factor failing the actual unaliased left-pair moment.
* If either initial slope vanishes, use the [right initial exit](ERK11-NONZERO-TEN-TWELVE-ENDPOINT-ALIAS-RIGHT-SLOPE-EXIT.md), or, once the original right slope is live, the independent [left initial exit](ERK11-NONZERO-TEN-TWELVE-ENDPOINT-ALIAS-LEFT-SLOPE-EXIT.md). The first uses a different actual \(R_9/L_7\) chronology, rather than fitting through a vanished slope.
* With both original slopes live, the [right](ERK11-NONZERO-TEN-TWELVE-ENDPOINT-ALIAS-RIGHT-MOMENT-EXIT.md) and [left](ERK11-NONZERO-TEN-TWELVE-ENDPOINT-ALIAS-LEFT-MOMENT-EXIT.md) fixed moment exits exhaust their masks, including the actual temporal row replacements and the measured opposite-profile fallback.
* The remaining raw affine masks are [both excluded](ERK11-NONZERO-TEN-TWELVE-ENDPOINT-ALIAS-AFFINE-EXITS.md). The common-factor branches are kept before dividing, and the exact simultaneous reduced branches also close.

The endpoint proof transfers to \(\zeta=1\) using the measured [mixed pure adjoint duality](ERK11-ADJOINT-PURE-DUALITY.md) and the reflected actual fronts. This does not assume ordinary reverse order eleven. Equal widths already have a dedicated [ordered exit](ERK11-NONZERO-TEN-TWELVE-SYMMETRIC-CENTRAL-ALIAS-EXIT.md). The width factor and scalar algebra are characteristic zero with the inherited live mixed and geometric guards; the full guards are earned over ordered fields. Portable Lean checks verify scalar and residue pieces, not an end-to-end theorem on tableaux.

Clocks outside the four named labels, other dormant placements, non-full plateau cases, other carrier or active-label censuses, and the unrestricted seventeen-stage equality problem all remain open under this synthesis. The unrestricted real stage interval therefore does not change.
