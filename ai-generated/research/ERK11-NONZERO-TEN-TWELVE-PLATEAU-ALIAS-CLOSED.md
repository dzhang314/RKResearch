# A dormant central row cannot share either full active plateau clock

**Theorem scope.** In the seventeen-row order-eleven ERK equality problem, take the all-live half-clock branch with its two quadratic carriers at physical 2 and 16 and both seventh boundaries genuinely new. Suppose there are ten nonzero-first-pool active labels, twelve active noncarrier interior rows, both plateau pairs (physical 4,5 and 13,14) full, and the sole dormant interior noncarrier row is physical 9. Over an ordered field its clock cannot equal either full active plateau, \(a\) or \(d=1-b\). This is a complete exclusion of these **two plateau aliases**, not of the full twelve-active census or the seventeen-row equality problem.

The reflected orientation exchanges the two actual sharp fronts using their paid mixed pure duality; it does not assume an independent ordinary reverse order-eleven tableau. Thus it suffices to take \(\zeta=d\). The inherited two-support geometry gives \(H,x_D,S,U\ne0\), the actual outer clocks \(x=c_8=x_N/x_D,\ z=c_{10}=C_1/H\), and \(a,d,d-1,a-d\ne0\), including the earlier double-zero exclusion. In the affine rows it is the **raw** slope that must be live in the regular branch. Vanishing common factors are treated before reducing the equations, so no masks are discarded by normalization or by a choice of finite place. The independently reduced second-pool line proportionality is not counted as a new width mark.

The full field result uses order only through the earlier mixed and geometric guards (and a separate ordered equal-width exit if used); the exact polynomial, local residue and scalar contradictions are characteristic zero once the needed guards hold. Other active-label and carrier censuses are also outside its scope. In particular the unrestricted real stage interval is unchanged.
