# Invisible constant DIRK10/11: three h-labels excluded

Let an actual eleven-stage, rooted-order-ten lower triangular tableau be defined over characteristic zero. Assume the cubic defect `g=Ac²−c³/3` is physically nonzero only at one numerical clock ξ, all its nonzero values are the same, and all their physical weights are zero. Let `h=(A−ξ/2)g`. This note excludes **exactly three numerical labels carrying nonzero h**, including any h value on a dirty row. The ordinary-Lobatto four-h-label chart and one-dirty-row non-Lobatto charts are not excluded. No full DIRK10 stage-bound claim follows.

Use the exact one-local band `F₄=⟨g,h⟩, F₅=⟨g,h,Ch⟩, U₆=⟨g,h,Ch,C²h⟩` and terminal profile `e_t=H(c)+δg+L(c)h`, with `H(X)=42XJ₄(X)` and deg L≤1. The true terminal has c=1, b=1/30 and zero A column. The first nonzero numerical clock is ξ, has nonzero g=γ=ξ³/6, and has h=0. The genuine reflected cubic G spans the annihilator of E₆ and has a nonzero actual latest active nonunit pivot. All of this is independent of βg, which vanishes here. The genuine paired exterior cut already excludes L=0: then all exterior labels would be fixed, contradicting its first-three-nonzero-exterior cubic on fixed clocks. No unpaid forward quintic is used.

Let n be the number of physical dirty rows. For constant g their restrictions of E₆ are at most affine in h. If n≥3 they have a nontrivial relation supported entirely on invisible dirty rows; it would span the only annihilator direction G, contrary to the genuine active pivot. Thus n≤2. If L(ξ)≠0, the terminal profile fixes h=0 on every dirty row, using the first dirty read; then two dirty rows would already give the same forbidden contrast. Thus n=1. Whenever n=2 the two h values are different by the same independence argument: the first is zero and the other is nonzero. A paid row annihilating g has zero coordinate on a lone dirty row or opposite coordinates on the two dirty rows. The lone dirty row is necessarily before every nonzero ordinary fixed comparator, so a zero D read there is harmless.

## 1. Three h-labels give the full clock decomposition and Lobatto aggregate

Independence of h,Ch,C²h forces at least three numerical labels carrying h. Suppose equality. Interpolation of the three clock profiles yields each individual h projection in U₆. Together with g these projections split U₆ as an invariant clock space; if a projection shares ξ, it is independent of g there since the first dirty row has h=0.

Every off-Lobatto indicator belongs to U₆. For an exterior off label z different from ξ, the terminal equation makes L(z) and h_z nonzero and h constant at all such rows, so its indicator is a multiple of its h projection. At off ξ there are two options. If L(ξ)=0, no exterior row can share ξ (H(ξ)≠0), so g/γ is the full indicator. Otherwise h vanishes on the dirty rows, and the indicator is g/γ plus the normalized exterior h projection if any exterior ξ rows exist. Therefore βU₆=0 gives zero ordinary aggregate at every off label. Ordinary exactness makes μ=ν, the six-node Lobatto measure with all fixed masses nonzero. Interpolating fixed indicators on the six fixed labels and subtracting off projections puts every numerical indicator in E₆. The exact E₆ sum of the clock indicators and U₆ has one physical excess at one clock ζ, the genuine latest active nonunit label. Away from ζ the local E₆ rank equals physical count; at ζ there is one extra physical row. This is the same elementary clock-block argument used in the nonconstant band.

For fixed head moments: away from ξ, all off nonterminal rows at a given label have a common nonzero h, and all nonterminal clock-one head rows have a common h. Since their respective ordinary totals are zero after removing the true terminal, their individual label totals of βh and βh² are zero. Dirty weights also vanish. The only fixed label where exterior h can vary is a shared H/L root τ, where the paid βh and βh² force its two conditional moments to be zero as well. If there are just two different exterior h values at a fixed label of nonzero mass, these two moments imply one value is zero and carries the whole mass; the other is a ghost. This applies at ξ when its exterior head exists (which requires L(ξ)=0 if ξ is fixed and ξ≠1).

## 2. All slack shapes reduce to the existing paid DA obstructions

Count a regular row for every fixed ordinary mass. The following classification handles the possible visible slack profiles; the two invisible cubic rows when n=2 have the same g but different h.

- **Off ζ.** The slack must be an exterior h head with two identical h profiles and opposite active weights: a purely invisible off-ξ block cannot supply an active pivot. If the exterior ζ head shares ξ, there is one extra invisible g row there (n=1) and its G and D coordinates are zero by the g test. The two exterior rows still form the actual G contrast.
- **Clean fixed duplicate.** A fixed ordinary slack has two identical clean profiles. The same pattern occurs at a head with a repeated h=0 class; for a core slack at ξ the clean contrast need not involve the dirty rows. The fixed aggregate at the duplicate's clock is nonzero, not an opposite pair. If ζ=0 the origin is the earlier duplicate and its A row is zero. Use the already proved fixed-origin or fixed-interior duplicate DA argument.
- **Fixed exterior h-head slack.** Outside the dirty rows a fixed head with slack has three exterior profiles. A repeated nonzero h class gives two opposite active weights and a clean regular third row by the first and second h moments. A repeated zero class is the previous clean duplicate. Three distinct values give the Vandermonde shape of the distinct-slack proof: if one is zero, it is the sole active row in the head and G is live at all three; otherwise all three weights are nonzero and their multiples by h are proportional to G. This yields a nonzero local mass debit at all earlier rows as in the marked receiver proof. If ζ=ξ and n=1, its invisible cubic row has G=D=0 and is necessarily early, leaving exactly the same exterior layout. At ζ=0 the true origin supplies the zero h value, so the already proved zero-slack exhaustion applies.
- **Fixed core slack with two dirty rows.** If ζ=ξ and n=2, the four local rows comprise the two actual dirties (h=0,h′≠0) and two exterior profiles. The two exterior h moments and nonzero ordinary mass give either two identical clean profiles or one clean and one nonzero h ghost. In the second case Gg forces opposite G coefficients on the dirty pair; the total forces opposite coefficients on the exterior pair; and Gh ties these pair contributions by the nonzero h differences. Thus all four G coordinates are live, and only the clean row has nonzero weight. The genuine latest pivot is that clean j. Every other local row has `b_s−G_s/(V³/6)≠0`, with V=1−ζ, so the marked fully distinct DA proof applies even though the earliest two g values agree.

In each case the live-read argument is the same two paid h moments, not an extra g-independence assumption. The three numerical h contributions to Dh,DCh are at distinct labels. Apart from a possible slack head, each involves just the unique physical h ghost at its label. At ξ with two dirty rows, Dg ties the two cubic entries and the h contribution uses the nonzero h′ row. With one dirty row Dg makes its entry zero, but that row is before all nonzero ordinary comparators. A zero in one of the three h contributions forces all three to vanish; the polynomial degree-five moments then kill all singleton ordinary D entries and the slack aggregate. D would be supported on the slack block and proportional to G. This is impossible by the usual folding for a repeated-profile contrast, and by the pure last ordinary U read for the fully distinct or mixed core block, exactly as in the marked slack proof. Thus the needed external D-live head labels are present. An opposite pair also has live combined D across its head label. There are at least two distinct live off-slack h labels whenever the marked proof needs them.

All live ghosts precede the actual latest j from triangularity; the possibly dead lone ξ row was already early. Hence the physical tail arguments, including the pure last-three comparison or the guarded one-/two-later marked comparisons, apply unchanged. This excludes exactly three h labels, independently of whether ξ is among them.

## 3. Residual ledger with four h labels and Lobatto aggregate

Assume μ=ν. The same separate weighted h and h² moments at a fixed shared root used above hold without assuming h support three: off exterior h is constant per fiber (and dirty weights vanish), nonterminal unit-head h is constant with zero retained mass, and at most one fixed root of L remains. If a fixed h label is not already paid by an invisible dirty h row, it needs at least one extra physical row in addition to the representative required by its nonzero ordinary mass. An off h label also costs an extra physical row unless its h is already on a dirty row. Thus with n=1 there are at most four h labels, and with n=2 at most four (one of them is paid by the alternate dirty h at ξ). This is a physical count against six fixed masses and eleven rows.

For exactly four h labels the bound saturates. There is one true regular active row at each fixed label. If n=1, the other rows are the first invisible cubic with h=0 and four h ghosts, one per label. If n=2, the invisible cubic rows carry h=0 and h′≠0 respectively, and there are three further h ghosts at the other labels. There is exactly one nonzero h row per numerical label; at a fixed exterior label with two rows the h moment identity gives its regular h=0 row and the zero-weight h ghost. No repeated clean physical profile can be substituted for this count.

The three moments `Gh=GCh=GC²h=0` on four distinct labels make every h read live in G: a zero at any one would kill all four by degree-two Vandermonde. Then Gg would also kill the remaining dirty coordinate, and degree-five clock polynomials would kill G on the six regulars, impossible. The nonzero G support puts all those ghosts before the actual latest active nonunit j; the lone dirty with G=0 is already early. Thus j is physical stage 10. If any G-live ghost were after the penultimate regular interior, its only active nonzero reflected donor label could be the last interior's V. Its zero target in the true first moment forces the weighted V tap to be zero, making its G coordinate zero as well. Therefore the penultimate regular interior is physical stage 9. This chart has **genuinely four** head labels; G need not be supported at one clock. The three-label DA proof does not close it.

## 4. The non-Lobatto ordinary aggregate requires one invisible cubic row

Let Δ=μ−ν. All physical dirty weights are zero. For a nonzero constant L, the same square terminal test as the earlier debit note gives ordinary annihilation by Δ through degree eleven. For an affine L with nonshared root τ, the simple and double reciprocal tests for H/L give degree-eleven annihilation after multiplying each residual mass at z by (z−τ)⁻². There is no residual mass at the pole: if the pole is ξ, only invisible dirties can be there (and the true terminal if it is clock one); if it is one, the true terminal gives the required fixed mass. The paid tests use only βh and βh² (and βCh² for constant L) and again do not require βg to be live.

If n=2, removing the two zero weights leaves nine physical rows including the two actual endpoints. A support debit at the actual prescribed terminal mass leaves Δ on at most 12 sites, so degree eleven forces μ=ν in the nonshared cases. If n=2 and τ is a shared root, necessarily τ=ξ and ξ is a fixed interior. For f=H/L the shared correction
`r=h+f(c)−f(1)e_t−f(ξ)g/γ` is supported at τ and its paid first two moments are ν(τ)f(τ), ν(τ)f(τ)². This follows by expanding them, since all dirty weights vanish and βh,βh²,β h f(c) vanish. After dropping the invisible rows the τ exterior block exists, and if a singleton it carries exactly ν(τ). If m,p count the retained τ and clock-one rows, at most `8−m−p` off labels remain among the nine retained rows (the origin is distinct from both), and at most `4+(m>1)+(p>1)` discrepancies can be at fixed labels because the singletons match. Hence support is at most ten, so μ=ν also in this shared case. Thus non-Lobatto implies n=1.

For a nonzero constant L or a nonshared root with n=1, Δ nonzero requires saturation of the support cap 13 from the endpoint debit. Exactly eight off labels with only the real endpoints can occur, or seven off labels plus one extra nonterminal unit head; all these extra rows have nonzero weight. The nonterminal exterior physical labels are therefore nine distinct clocks, including actual origin 0, and the eight others are live with h≠0. The sole invisible dirty has G=0 by Gg; the actual terminal also has G=0. On those nine exterior labels, E₆ restricts to polynomials of degree≤7 when L is constant, or to polynomials of degree≤7 divided by L when L is affine nonshared. This follows from the genuine h relation `h=−H/L` there and the E₆ band: when L is affine, multiplying the ordinary degree≤6 space by L and adjoining H spans degree≤7 because H(τ)≠0. Similarly E₅ gives degree≤6 after the same scaling. Writing P₉ for the monic product of the nine exterior labels,

```
G_s is proportional to L(c_s)/P₉′(c_s);
β_s h_s is proportional to c_s G_s;
(GA)_s/G_s is affine in c_s.
```

Indeed the first is the nine-site barycentric annihilator, and the E₅ annihilator on those labels has affine numerator; βh vanishes at the true origin and is nonzero on the other eight. Thus G and CG span the full E₅ annihilator. This is an exact remaining shape, not an exclusion. The one-dirty shared-root non-Lobatto branch also remains; none of the reciprocal gains is claimed at a common root.
