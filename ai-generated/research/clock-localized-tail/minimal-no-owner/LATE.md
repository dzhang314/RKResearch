# A spare late row: exact measured final contact and a physical repair

The statements use the actual same canonical early bank and direct late geometry as [one-mark and projection proof](ONE-MARK.md). They concern balanced \(p=2m,\ r=m\) and a real negative diagonal \(\gamma\), with \(\alpha<0\) the actual last early clock. Let \(K_m\) be the genuine first-excess source space; \(q=\dim K_m=\lceil m/2\rceil\). Work at distinct actual late clocks; the scalar output is exactly integrated through degree \(p-1\). An already-paid lower word bank below means every actual contact through length \(m-1\) is genuinely verified, not merely assigned.

## 1. The exact last-shelf freedom is the annihilator of actual rows

Let \(V=V_{m-1}\) be the actual late row space through length \(m-1\), and let \(N\) be its numerical model-zero kernel. The genuine model map exists by the early-null Hilbert test, or directly by the proved one-mark contact criterion here with \({\cal L}_m(\alpha)\ne0\). Put
\[
 B=\{b\,P(C):\deg P\le m-1\},\quad
 {\cal U}=E((X-\alpha)\mathbb P_{\le m-1}),\qquad
 R_F=JF-\frac{(JF)(\alpha)}{{\cal L}_m(\alpha)}{\cal L}_m
       \quad (F\in K_m).
\]
Then \(V=B\oplus N\), since the pure mark rows carry every formal polynomial through degree \(m-1\). At these widths evaluation on \({\cal U}\) has rank \(m\) (at most one distinct site can equal \(\alpha\)). The one-mark theorem gives \(N\,{\cal U}=0\) by actual paid contacts. The exact final contact is
\[
                    T E F-E R_F\in V^\perp
                              \qquad(F\in K_m).             \tag{1}
\]
Indeed, for a true numerical row of model \(P\), its read on \(E R_F\) is \(\ell(P R_F)\) by the already-paid length-\((m-1)\) contact, since \(R_F\) is in the full actual ideal. This equals \(\ell(P JF)\) by ordinary Legendre orthogonality and \(\deg P\le m-1\). Appending a strict primitive at the final shelf must give the same value on \(T EF\). Testing all of \(V\) is precisely (1). Conversely (1) pays all words ending in that final primitive; words with subsequent marks are covered by the shorter-primitive contacts and the true mark degree budget. Thus (1) is necessary and sufficient for completing target order once the lower dictionary is already paid. No auxiliary source has been assumed.

Since \(\dim V\ge L(m)\), with \(d=L(m)+s\) one has \(\dim V^\perp\le s\). In particular just one spare late row has two branches. If \(V\) fills the augmented bank, the old pointwise obstruction remains unchanged. Otherwise \(V^\perp=\operatorname{span}\{u\}\) is one genuine physical direction and (1) becomes
\[
             T E F=E R_F+u\,\theta(F)
\]
for one actual source functional \(\theta\). If the first physical late clock \(x_1\) does not annul every \(R_F\), then \(u_1\ne0\) and \(\theta=-R_{\cdot}(x_1)/u_1\) is forced. At the next physical row, only the first actual late evaluation is available. In particular
\[
       R_{\cdot}(x_2)\in
       \operatorname{span}\{R_{\cdot}(x_1),\,E_{x_1}|_{K_m}\} .   \tag{2}
\]
For fixed \(u\), the sharp top chronology test at every physical row \(i\) is
\[
 R_{\cdot}(x_i)+u_i\theta
      \in\operatorname{span}\{E_{x_j}|_{K_m}: j<i\}.
                                                               \tag{3}
\]
It is sufficient for this top-only interpolation because its coefficients are exactly the actual earlier row taps. Equations (2)–(3) do not erase the requirement \(u\in V^\perp\). If all weights are nonzero, a nonzero \(u\) must have support at at least \(m+1\) distinct sites, since \(Bu=0\) and the literal clock moments give an \(m\)-row Vandermonde test. Thus a one-row spare with ordinary nonzero weights does not provide a localized one-site error.

## 2. Exact joint triangular test, not independent row and column solvability

Let \(A\) be a matrix whose rows are the already verified numerical inputs whose primitive responses must stay fixed, and \(M\) those literal outputs, so preserving the lower dictionary asks \(AT=M\). Let \(Z\) have columns equal to true clock evaluations of a basis of \(K_m\); the target \(TZ=Y\) is computed from (1) with an actual candidate annihilator direction (or several). First solve \(TZ=Y\) row by row using (3), obtaining a literal strict \(T_*\). If those row tests fail there is no joint solution. Any remaining permitted change \(\Delta T\) has each physical row \(i\) supported on \(<i\) and in the kernel of evaluation on \(Z_{<i}\). Write \(D_i\) for this actual space of row vectors, padded with zeros beyond \(i-1\). The joint extension is feasible exactly when
\[
        M-AT_*\in
         \operatorname{span}\{A_{:,i}v:\ v\in D_i,\ 1\le i\le d\}.
                                                               \tag{4}
\]
This is just the range of \(A\Delta T\) from the direct sum of allowed literal row changes. It gives an exact triangular rank test for simultaneous top contacts and lower preservation, including zero transitions and shared supports. Independent two-sided testing would not suffice. When the existing \(A\) records all actual inputs through \(m-2\) and their paid successors, preserving it preserves the true \(V_{m-1}\) and its annihilator; (4) is then not circular.

## 3. An attainable top chronology with at most \(\lceil q/2\rceil\) extra modes

The pure **top** chronology can always be paid with \(s=\lceil q/2\rceil\) candidate modes in \(B^\perp\), at any sufficiently large width (in particular the proposed \(d=L(m)+s\) for the orders under consideration). This assertion is physical, but lower-word compatibility must still pass (4).

There exist \(s\) distinct true leading clocks \(a_i\) such that the paired functionals \(F(a_i),R_F(a_i)\) span \(K_m^*\), and the \(s\) first evaluation functionals themselves are independent. They can be chosen in any real open interval avoiding finitely many prescribed sites. To prove it, write \(c(F)=(JF)(\alpha)/{\cal L}_m(\alpha)\). On \(\ker c\), \(R_F=JF\). For independent \(F,G\in\ker c\) their evaluation determinant \(F\,JG-G\,JF=F\,I_0G-G\,I_0F\) is a nonzero polynomial: otherwise the derivative of \(I_0F/I_0G\) is zero and \(F,G\) would be proportional. Hence one can choose a real-algebraic site contributing two independent reads whenever the remaining subspace has dimension at least three. If its dimension is odd, continue to dimension one and select one nonzero evaluation.

For the even-dimensional case, preserve independence of \(c\) and \(E_0\) on the residual subspace by taking each two-dimensional test inside their joint kernel until the residual dimension is two. Their initial independence on \(K_m\) follows from the actual \(W_h\) and final coefficient impulse \(U\), \(h=m-1\). In the normalized variable with \(\alpha/\gamma=\lambda\), the ratios are \(U(0)/W_h(0)=(\lambda-h)/[h(h-1)^2]\) and \((J_1U)(\lambda)/(J_1W_h)(\lambda)=-(h+1)/[h^2(h-1)]\). Equality would force \(\lambda=1/h\), excluded by the positive reciprocal-root sum \(h\). Thus when the residual space has dimension two, choose \(F\) there with \(c(F)=0,\ F(0)\ne0\), and \(G\) with \(c(G)\ne0\). Their paired determinant is again nonzero. Otherwise the polynomial relation would give \(I_0G=(I_0F/F)G+c(G){\cal L}_m\), hence
\[
                    (G/F)'=-c(G){\cal L}_m'/I_0F.
\]
The left side is regular at zero, but the right side has a simple pole because \(I_0F\) has a simple zero at zero and the Legendre derivative at zero is nonzero. Contradiction. At every step independence on the residual space makes the new raw evaluation independent of all earlier raw evaluations, as required.

Let \(\Theta(F)=(-R_F(a_1),\ldots,-R_F(a_s))\). Choose the first \(s\) rows of a matrix \(U\) to be the identity, choose first \(s\) physical late rows of \(T\) to be zero, and thus \(T EF=ER_F+U\Theta(F)\) holds there literally. The span of the first raw evaluations and these correction functionals is all \(K_m^*\), so for each subsequent row choose coefficients \(U_{i,*}\) to fit the remaining desired read into the span of the available earlier raw evaluations, then choose literal earlier taps by (3). Extend the raw evaluations to an unisolvent set by the first \(q\) physical late sites. At every row after that prefix, \(U_{i,*}\) is freely variable since all of \(K_m^*\) is available, and the strict taps can realize any remaining read. Take a scalar rule with nonzero weights through degree \(p-1\) and choose these free \(U\) entries to enforce \(BU=0\) by Vandermonde; there are \(d-q\ge m\) sites for those \(m\) constraints. Both the top projection and true clocks are now physically realized; the values of \(T\) beyond this specification can vary in the solution spaces. The missing condition for full order is that the actual lower words also be paid and annihilate \(U\), i.e. that their intended actual layout pass the simultaneous test (4). Merely knowing this rank-\(s\) top correction does not give a new stage bound. For \(m=8,10\), this gives concrete two- and three-mode top layouts, respectively.

## 4. An exact attainable last-shelf closure with real pure feeders

There is a complementary constructive remedy whenever a real literal active bank has already reached all true contacts through length \(m-1\), even if its projected top values are not attainable by direct retaps. Keep its strict matrix \(T_0\), actual clock map \(E_C\), and nonzero or signed weights; define the genuine last error
\[
             D(F)=E_C R_F-T_0 E_C F,\qquad F\in K_m .
\]
All actual old rows in \(V_{m-2}\) annihilate \(D\). For such a row of model \(P\), the first term gives \(\ell(P R_F)\) because \(R_F\) is a true lower source, and the second gives \(\ell(PJF)\) from the old paid contact one primitive higher. These are equal.

Add pure feeder stages at real-algebraic clocks \(s_j\), physically after the forward bank and before every active late row, with literal feeds \(E_{s_j}J D_F\), diagonal \(\gamma\), and zero weights. Let \(E_S\) measure their true \(K_m\) values. In this one-way repair, achieving the entire projected correction on the active rows is possible **if and only if**
\[
                 \ker(E_S|_{K_m})\subseteq\ker D .             \tag{5}
\]
When it holds choose a matrix \(L\) so \(L E_S=D\); every column may be chosen in \(V_{m-2}^\perp\) because the resulting map on the actual measurement image already has that range. Extend the strict late matrix by the literal active-to-early-feeder block \(L\); in the active forward tap subtract \(L E_S D_F\), using the old numeric decoder. This is precisely the usual compensated strict early feed, so real clocks and local order remain true.

Every actual word through length \(m-1\) stays unchanged on the active block and zero on the added feeders: the only new outgoing adjoint component is \(aL=0\) for an actual predecessor \(a\) through length \(m-2\). At the last primitive the new active read on a true \(F\) is \(T_0 E_C F+L E_S F=E_C R_F\), which is fully paid as above. Pure clock quadrature is unchanged by zero new weights. Thus this is an actual full last-shelf repair **conditional only on the prior paid bank and (5)**, not a projected numerical guess. The missing previous shelves are not presumed available here.

At most \(\dim K_m\) actual pure feeders are needed: choose that many distinct real-algebraic sites unisolvent for the true polynomial subspace. More sharply the minimum number for exact pointwise restoration is the least number of real site evaluations whose span contains the actual row functionals of \(D\); rank is a lower bound, not a replacement for this condition. One feeder suffices exactly when the nonzero defect factors through a single true evaluation functional. This criterion is physically attainable as proved above, unlike creating one nominal residual mode with no real row. It can be applied to a shorter early-source design with its own verified projected endpoint. No known full lower-word bank or one-eval defect for a target table improvement is asserted in this note.
