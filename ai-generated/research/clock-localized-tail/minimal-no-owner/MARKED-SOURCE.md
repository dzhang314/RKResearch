# Marked early-null rooted profiles

## 1. Exact rank and saturation under root marks

Use the exterior construction in [Active tail and canonical abscissae](ACTIVE-TAIL.md): \(p=m+r\), \(r\le m\), canonical minimum closed early cache through order \(m-1\), \(\gamma<0\), distinct actual late clocks \(x_i>1\), true late local order through order \(m\). Let \(C=\operatorname{diag}(x_i)\), \(J=I_0-\gamma I\), \(J^*=I_1-\gamma I\), and \(E\) evaluate at these literal clocks. The exact first-excess early-null polynomial space is \(K_m=G(\ker\mathbf e\cap\mathcal F_m)\), of dimension \(1+\lfloor (m-1)/2\rfloor\). All its elements vanish at the actual last early clock \(\alpha<0\). It contains \(W_{m-1}\) and the final coefficient impulse \(U\), whose gcd is \(X-\alpha\), by the Laguerre recurrence and interlacing (see §3).

Put
\[
 V=K_m/(X-\alpha),\qquad A=W_{m-1}/(X-\alpha),\qquad
 B=U/(X-\alpha),\qquad a=\deg A=m-2.
\]
These quotients are polynomials, and \(B\) is coprime to \(A\). For each \(\ell\ge0\) let \(R_\ell\) be the subspace of \(K[X]/(A)\) generated by
\[
                  \bar V,\ X\bar V,\ldots,X^\ell\bar V.
\]
Reduction modulo \(A\) is just a companion-matrix calculation of dimension \(a\). Let \(\mathcal M_\ell\) be the polynomial space of true order-\(m\) early-null profiles followed only by root marks of total degree at most \(\ell\). Every such test is an actual rooted linear combination, of size at most \(m+\ell\), still zero numerically in the early bank. Marking multiplies the true model and the actual late values by the clock. Thus
\[
 \mathcal M_\ell=(X-\alpha)V\mathbb P_{\le\ell},\qquad
 \dim\mathcal M_\ell=\ell+1+\dim R_\ell.
 \tag{1}
\]
Indeed \(A\in V\), so the whole kernel \(A\mathbb P_{\le\ell}\) of reduction in degree at most \(a+\ell\) is present; the image is precisely \(R_\ell\).

The dimensions in (1) reach the full common-factor polynomial capacity after only \(\lfloor m/2\rfloor-1\) marks. Multiply \(\bar V\) by the inverse of \(\bar B\), which commutes with multiplication by \(X\), making \(1\) one of its elements. The initial dimension is \(\lfloor(m-1)/2\rfloor\), since the kernel on \(V\) consists exactly of the multiples of \(A\) of degree at most \(a\). A step of the Krylov span cannot be flat before saturation: flatness would give an \(X\)-invariant subspace containing \(1\), hence all of \(K[X]/(A)\). Consequently
\[
 \mathcal M_\ell=(X-\alpha)\mathbb P_{\le m+\ell-2}
                 \quad\text{if }\ell\ge \lfloor m/2\rfloor-1.
 \tag{2}
\]
This improves the previous two-generator guarantee, which took \(m-3\) marks. Below the threshold (1) gives the exact rank of this true marked subspace, not an assertion that there are no other true sources. At or above the threshold it is also the full span of actual source polynomials \(F_t=V_t-D_F\mathbf e(t)\) through order \(m+\ell\): the common-factor upper bound applies to every \(F_t\), and the early-null marked combinations already attain it.

## 2. Hidden channels already needed by summed order

Let \(a_w\) be the true adjoint row of one selected physical \(T\), and \(P_w\) its formal adjoint polynomial. A same-count hidden row at birth grade \(g\) is
\[
 h=\sum_w c_w a_w\ne0,\qquad \sum_w c_w P_w=0,
 \qquad |w|\le g,\qquad\#J(w)\text{ fixed}.
 \tag{3}
\]
The formal relation can mix different numbers of marks. For arbitrary exact rooted order, not just separated weighted matching,
\[
                          h\,E(\mathcal M_{r-g})=0.
 \tag{4}
\]
To see it, use a marked early-null head in \(\mathcal M_{r-g}\). Its actual late values are its exact evaluations because the underlying order-\(m\) head is locally exact and each true mark multiplies by \(C\). All later attachable marks and unary roots preserve the early numerical zero. Grafting each actual word in (3) therefore produces a true summed order equation with exact formal contraction \(\ell(P_wF)\) for the head profile \(F\). Their linear combination proves (4).

On any proposed subset \(S\) of clock labels, a hidden row supported there and invisible to this measured band exists nonzero exactly when
\[
                  |S|>\operatorname{rank} E_S(\mathcal M_{r-g}).
 \tag{5}
\]
The rank can be computed from (1) using the actual node evaluations, not from a count of tree assignments. If \(g\le r-\lfloor m/2\rfloor+1\), (2) applies; the tested rows are a Vandermonde times the nonzero factors \(x_i-\alpha\). Every such nonzero hidden channel therefore needs at least \(p-g\) distinct nonzero clock coordinates. This is a necessary measurement on *any* exact design in the geometry, including one matching only the summed equations. For instance, birth at grade three forces 15, 16, and 17 nonzero coordinates at orders 18, 19, and 20, respectively. This is a comparison of the formula, not exact-order claims for those orders.

For any grade \(g\), not just the saturated range, \(h,hC,\ldots,hC^{r-g}\) are \(r-g+1\) independent actual rows. The subspace \(\mathcal M_{r-g}\) contains \(W_{m-1}\mathbb P_{\le r-g}\), and \(W_{m-1}\) is nonzero on every exterior site. Already these tests force at least \(r-g+2\) active sites of \(h\), and a Vandermonde on any \(r-g+1\) such sites proves the mark-orbit rank. In particular a grade-three seed has orbit rank 7, 7, and 8 at orders 18, 19, and 20. On a specific strict suffix \(>i\), its orbit rank is precisely \(\min(|\operatorname{supp}h\cap(i,d]|,r-g+1)\).

For several seeds and grades use the joint measured block-Krylov rank, not the sum of individual orbit dimensions. At any cap \(s\) and suffix \(>i\), take \(H_{s,i}\) to consist of the true formal-zero generating rows from (3), with their true marked descendants \(hC^\nu\) through \(s\), restricted to that suffix. The basis may use all same-count model kernels through grade \(s\), and any legal further marks then stay within the same kernel. If already retained rows on that suffix span \(U_{s,i}\), the exact number of additional independent row directions needed to retain the proposed hidden data is
\[
           \operatorname{rank}\binom{U_{s,i}}{H_{s,i}}
                      -\operatorname{rank}U_{s,i}.
 \tag{6}
\]
This is the dimension of the measured image modulo the retained data, hence is both necessary and sufficient to reproduce those particular linear observations. It is not a count of new physical stages. If only prescribed future linear observations are of interest, apply those observations before taking the rank, and include all legal mark descendants before quotienting. A projected quotient need not commute with the true diagonal if those descendants are omitted.


## 3. The first-excess generators

Put \(h=m-1\ge2\). Work first with ordinary weights; contracting the identity edges is a triangular change of formal tree coordinates through order \(h+1\), and so gives the same kernel-image polynomials in shifted form. Let \(F_k\) be the full virtual Laguerre polynomial at level \(k\), of degree \(<k\), and let \(D_k\) be the actual cached polynomial. The established Laguerre band of \(G-F_k\), at order \(n\), has first index \(2k+1-n\). The level-\(k\) cache copies the first \(l_k=\max(0,2k-1-h)\) coefficients from the preceding chart. At first excess \(n=h+1\), the copy can therefore create just one new independent missing coefficient at that level, the one of \(W_{l_k-1}\) when \(l_k>0\); lower errors propagate linearly from the preceding chart. The actual high coefficients are determined by literal evaluations of \(F_k\) at the selected nodes. Those evaluations remain true at this excess order because all proper children are still within the paid order \(h\). Equivalently the error \(F_k-D_k\) is the unique degree-\(<k\) polynomial with the propagated low coordinates, the one new missing coefficient, and zero values at that level's actual selected nodes. This follows from the same bounded Vandermonde used in the paid decoder. Iterating this linear representation gives at most one fixed polynomial impulse for each positive \(l_k\). At the final chart, \(G-F_h\) is a multiple of \(W_h\) by its band. Hence the first-excess residual image has dimension at most \(1+\lfloor h/2\rfloor\), and is spanned by those possible impulses and \(W_h\).

Equality of this dimension can be seen without the individual impulse coefficients. In the combined space of numerical columns and exact polynomials through order \(h+1\), apply the actual ordinary commutator \(\Pi=[C,A]-A^2\) to every numerical column through order \(h-1\). Its formal profile vanishes by \([X,I_0]=I_0^2\), and its numerical action is invertible (diagonal \(-\gamma^2\)). A paid decoder through \(h-1\) implies dimension at least \(L(h-1)\) for those lower numerical columns, by the usual least-commutator-power, independent-monomial argument. The combined space also maps onto all polynomials of degree at most \(h\) by true leaf marks or bush trees. Thus its dimension is at least \(L(h-1)+h+1\); subtract at most \(L(h)\) for the numerical projection to get \(1+\lfloor h/2\rfloor\). The residual image of \(G-D_h e\) equals \(K_m\): the smaller paid columns through \(h\) span the whole minimum forward bank, so one can subtract a paid combination with a matching numerical value. Dimension equality now implies that each listed possible impulse belongs to the actual residual space. In particular the final impulse (there is no later propagation) is
\[
 W_{h-2}-\frac{W_{h-2}(\alpha)}{W_{h-1}(\alpha)}W_{h-1}
\]
together with \(W_h\). The minimum cache uses one genuine final node \(\alpha\), at which all its first-excess residuals vanish. On roots of \(W_h\), the three-term recurrence gives \(W_{h-2}/W_{h-1}=(X-\gamma(2h-1))/((h-1)^2\gamma^2)\); its values are distinct, and \(W_{h-1}\) is nonzero there. The displayed impulse and \(W_h\) therefore have only the common zero \(\alpha\). This argument allows every nonzero real-algebraic \(\gamma\), including one selected separately at each order.
