# A literal mixed-clock completion of the length-three gate

The construction uses one exterior abscissa and interior abscissae for the remaining stages of the late block. All continuations of length at most three satisfy their prescribed rooted equations. Consequently the full tableau has order at least \(m+3\); in the balanced cases below this does not reach the target order \(p=m+r\).

## 1. Four independent inputs suffice for the seven-row gate

Use the canonical minimum closed early SDIRK cache through \(m-1\), zero early output, and actual early decoder \(D_F\). Set the common diagonal \(\gamma=-1/6\). Choose the early cache's last clock \(\alpha=\gamma\lambda\) at the largest normalized Laguerre root of degree \(m-1\); \(\lambda\ge m-1\). Assume \(p\ge10\), \(m\ge4\), \(3\le r\le m\), and distinct true late clocks. With the earlier no-owner strict feed \(EJD_F-TED_F\), \(J=I_0-\gamma I\), the true late local order is \(m\), and the actual residual \(F_t\) of each tree belongs to \((X-\alpha)\mathbb P_{\le |t|-2}\). See the direct reverse construction discussed in [Active tail and canonical abscissae](ACTIVE-TAIL.md) for the literal last-row reason and the actual rooted spine recurrence. The late output row \(b\) must be a scalar rule through degree \(p-1\).

Let \(Y=1-X\), \(M=\operatorname{diag}(y_i)\) at the true \(y_i=1-x_i\), and \(L=I_0^Y-\gamma I\) be the formal adjoint primitive on polynomials in \(Y\). Write \(f_a=LY^a\) and \(s=Y-\gamma\). Suppose \(h\) is a nonzero actual row that annihilates \((X-\alpha)\mathbb P_{\le p-4}\) at the clock sites. Pick \(\kappa=-7\). The following four actual primitive equations define the low chart:
\[
 \begin{array}{rcl}
 bT&=&b\,s(M),\\
 bMT&=&b\,f_1(M)-h/2,\\
 bM^2T&=&b\,f_2(M)+h\,\eta_2(M),\\
 hT&=&\kappa^{-1}h\,s(M),\qquad
 \eta_2(Y)=-(Y+\gamma+s(Y)/\kappa)/3.
 \end{array}
 \tag{1}
\]
The seven rows in [Active tail and canonical abscissae](ACTIVE-TAIL.md) are generated by these four. In fact with \(u_j=bT^j,\ q_j=bM^jT\), the first line gives \(u_1=b(M-\gamma I)\) and hence \(u_2=q_1-\gamma u_1\), \(u_1M T=q_2-\gamma q_1\). Also
\[
 h=b\Omega,\qquad
 \Omega=(T+\gamma I)M-M(T+\gamma I)-(T+\gamma I)^2.
\]
Indeed \(b\Omega=u_1M-q_1-u_2-2\gamma u_1-\gamma^2 b=b(M^2-2\gamma M)-2q_1=h\). For the three formal-zero words of the seven-row gate, direct substitution gives \(a_\sigma=h\,s(M)=\kappa u_1\Omega\), \(a_\rho=(\kappa-1)u_1\Omega\), and \(a_\tau=0\). For example \(q_1T=q_2/2-\gamma q_1-(hT)/2\); substitute this and \(u_1MT=q_2-\gamma q_1\) into \(a_\tau=u_2M-2u_1MT+q_1T\) to get zero by the displayed \(\eta_2\). Also \(u_1\Omega-hT=a_\tau\). These checks establish the required grade-three numerical identities, not just their model values.

Every actual word row through length \(k\le3\) then equals \(bP_w(M)+hQ_w(M)\), where \(P_w\) is its exact formal polynomial and \(\deg Q_w\le k-2\) (zero if \(k<2\)). This is immediate from (1) and actual marking by \(M\): the pre-primitive inputs through length two lie in the span of the four displayed input rows with their stated models. For a true source of order at most \(p-k\), the marked hidden polynomial \(Q_wF_t\) is in \((X-\alpha)\mathbb P_{\le p-4}\). Its contraction is zero, and the \(bP_w\) contraction is exact by scalar precision since \(\deg(P_w F_t)\le p-1\). Thus **all** separated word contacts through length three are paid. The actual single-large-child recursion consequently proves ordinary rooted order through \(m+3\): every paid tree in that window has no more than three total spine operations above any true source. There is no assertion of full order at sizes \(m+4,\ldots,p\).

## 2. Four rational anchors give a literal strict pivot

Work in the chronology of the reverse reads, namely the opposite of the physical plus order. Set \(\beta=y/\gamma\), \(H=h/(b\gamma^2)\) pointwise where \(b\ne0\). Choose the first four reverse sites
\[
 \begin{array}{c|rrrr}
 j&0&1&2&3\\ \hline
 \beta_j&1&-1&-3&-5\\
 X_j&7/6&5/6&1/2&1/6\\
 H_j&-1&7&147/5&3451/53 .
 \end{array}
 \tag{2}
\]
Thus exactly one is exterior. For a row at normalized site \((\beta,H)\), let \(R_k\) be its normalized strict tap to an earlier reverse site \(k\). Precisely, if it is physical index \(i\) and the earlier reverse site has physical index \(j>i\), set \(T_{ji}=\gamma b_i R_k/b_j\). The four equations (1) in this column become the following actual input moments:
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 m_0&=\sum R_k=\beta-1,\\
 m_1&=\sum R_k\beta_k=\beta^2/2-\beta-H/2,\\
 m_2&=\sum R_k\beta_k^2=\beta^3/3-\beta^2-H(6\beta+8)/21,\\
 m_H&=\sum R_k H_k=H(\beta-1)/\kappa .
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{3}
\]
Use the empty row at site 0, the single tap \(-2\) to site 0 at site 1, the taps \((-28/5,8/5)\) at site 2, and \((-508/53,140/53,50/53)\) at site 3. Substitution in (3) verifies all sixteen values exactly. For example \(m_2=-4,\ m_H=84/5\) at site 2, and \(m_1=-798/53,\ m_2=82/53\) at site 3. All three initial adjacent strict taps are nonzero.

The quadratic interpolant of the first three \(H\) values is \(P_2(\beta)=9\beta^2/5-4\beta+6/5\). At the fourth site,
\[
                    H_3-P_2(-5)=-288/265\ne0 .
\]
Hence the four actual vectors \(1,\beta,\beta^2,H\) form an invertible pivot. Every later reverse row may take arbitrary taps to already available sites outside these four, then solve one literal \(4\times4\) system on these four earlier physical sites to attain (3). This leaves the common diagonal unchanged; the primal plus row clock is the true clock because the earlier strict feed \(EJD_F-TED_F\) cancels the \(T\)-leaf contribution. Thus the construction uses no acausal auxiliary projector.

If \(d\ge p+1\), take any other distinct interior clocks and choose a scalar rule \(b\) with all weights nonzero and a failed degree-\(p\) moment. The \(p\) scalar equations are Vandermonde; each coordinate is variable and the degree-\(p\) row independent. Prescribe \(h_i=\gamma^2 b_i H_i\) at the four sites (2). The remaining \(d-4\ge p-3\) distinct sites solve the \(p-3\) linear common-factor moment equations for \(h\), since \(X_i-\alpha\ne0\). The hidden row is not zero, and no reserve of silent tail coordinates was imposed.

If \(d\ge p+2\), one can also ensure a nonzero strict adjacent tap at every subsequent reverse slot. At the very next slot prescribe \(H\) avoiding one exact value: the coefficient of its tap to slot 3 is obtained by subtracting the \(P_2\)-moment from \(m_H\) and dividing by \(H_3-P_2(-5)\). Its dependence on \(H\) has a nonzero coefficient proportional to \((41-13\beta)/35\) at every interior normalized \(\beta\le0\). The resulting fifth prescribed hidden coordinate is allowed because \(d-5\ge p-3\). At still later slots set the adjacent tap freely nonzero outside the first four and solve the four pivots. This gives a full genuine strict path, not a hidden shallow cutoff. No claim about this additional adjacency at \(d=p\) or \(d=p+1\) is made.

## 3. The boundary count \(d=p\) also works

Write \(\xi_0,\ldots,\xi_3\) for the four fixed physical clocks (2), \(G_4(X)=\prod_{j=0}^3(X-\xi_j)\), and \(C_j(X)=G_4(X)/((X-\xi_j)G_4'(\xi_j))\) for their cardinals. The other \(p-4\) clocks will be simple interior roots of a monic polynomial \(N\). Put \(G=G_4 N\). The unique scalar weights on the \(p\) clocks obey
\[
 b_i=\ell(G/(X-x_i))/G'(x_i),\qquad \ell=\int_0^1 .
\]
The complete common-factor annihilator at \(d=p\) consists of rows
\[
                 h_i= Q(x_i)/((x_i-\alpha)G'(x_i)),\qquad \deg Q\le2 .
\]
This follows from the leading cardinal coefficient identity and dimension three. To impose the four prefix values \(h_i=\gamma^2 H_i b_i\), interpolate the requested \(Q\) values at \(\xi_j\). The degree-three coefficient vanishes exactly when
\[
   \ell(N A)=0,\qquad A(X)=\sum_{j=0}^3 H_j(\xi_j-\alpha) C_j(X).
 \tag{4}
\]
Thus all four prescribed ratios fit if and only if this one scalar equation for the remaining nodal hull is paid. The common factor is the actual source clock, not a formal truncation.

### Positivity of the new weight

The cubic interpolant of the four \(H_j\) in \(\beta\) is \((6\beta^3+495\beta^2-1066\beta+300)/265\). With \(\epsilon=1/6=-\gamma\), \(t=-\beta\in[0,6]\), \(a=1+\epsilon\lambda\), subtract the one degree-four nodal multiple from \(H(\beta)(1-\alpha+\epsilon\beta)\). Its degree-three remainder is \(A\). In normalized coordinates this gives
\[
 265A =a(-6t^3+495t^2+1066t+300)
             +(-447t^3-1150t^2-348t+90)/6 .
\]
Use \(t^3\le6t^2\). The combined quadratic lower coefficient is \((459\lambda-1078)/6>0\) since \(\lambda\ge m-1\ge3\); the linear and constant coefficients are positive as well. Hence \(A>0\) on \([0,1]\).

Let \(n=p-4\). The monic degree-\(n\) orthogonal polynomial for the positive measure \(A(X)\,dX\) exists by the nonsingular weighted Gram solve and is real-algebraic. It has \(n\) simple roots in \((0,1)\): otherwise the product of its sign-changing roots in the open interval would be a polynomial of degree \(<n\) with nonzero signed integral against it, contradicting orthogonality. It belongs to the hyperplane (4). Thus the real affine hyperplane (4) has a nonempty relatively open subset on which all roots are simple and interior. We work in a small such neighborhood, labeling roots continuously. Its translation directions are \(\delta N\) of degree \(\le n-1\) with \(\ell(A\delta N)=0\).

### Every listed degeneracy can be avoided on that hyperplane

- **Collision with a fixed site.** Evaluation at \(\xi_j\) is not proportional to \(\delta N\mapsto\ell(A\delta N)\): the square \((X-\xi_j)^2\) has evaluation zero and strictly positive integral against \(A\). Hence \(N(\xi_j)=0\) is not forced by (4). Perturb first to avoid all four.
- **Zero weight at a fixed site.** Its numerator is the functional \(\ell(N G_4/(X-\xi_j))\). The cubic \(G_4/(X-\xi_j)\) is not proportional to \(A\), since it vanishes at the other fixed sites whereas \(A(\xi_l)=H_l(\xi_l-\alpha)\ne0\). The Hilbert pairing through degree \(n-1\ge3\) ensures these functionals are independent on the translation domain prior to imposing (4). Thus no such numerator is identically zero on the hyperplane.
- **Zero weight at a moving root.** At any simple moving root \(z\), write \(N=(X-z)Q_z\) with monic \(Q_z\) of degree \(n-1\). Keep \(z\) fixed and perturb \(Q_z\) by a polynomial \(D\) of degree \(\le n-2\) with \(\ell(A(X-z)D)=0\). Its weight numerator is \(\ell(G_4 Q_z)\), and can be changed by such a perturbation: \(G_4\) is not proportional to \(A(X-z)\) since the latter is nonzero at every fixed site. Both have degree \(\le4\), within \(n-2=p-6\). By the nondegenerate Hilbert pairing there is a direction changing this numerator while preserving (4). Sufficiently small changes keep the roots simple, interior, and noncolliding. Thus the zero-weight locus for each continuously labeled moving root has empty interior in the admissible open set. It is closed there by the explicit weight formula.
- **Degree-\(p\) scalar error accidentally zero.** The error is \(\ell(G_4 N)\) for the monic full nodal hull, up to the convention for the error sign. It is not identically zero on (4), since \(G_4\) has degree four and cannot be proportional to \(A\) of degree at most three; again the translation domain includes degrees through four.

The complement of this finite collection of degeneracies is nonempty and open in the admissible affine hyperplane. The hyperplane is defined over the real algebraic numbers, whose points are dense in its real affine coordinates, so choose a real-algebraic \(N\) there. Its simple real roots are real algebraic as well. Then every scalar weight is nonzero, the degree-\(p\) scalar moment fails, and the unique quadratic \(Q\) from (4) gives the required true hidden row. Finish every actual reverse row by (3). This proves the boundary \(d=p\) case without assuming the sign of the discrete weights or using a numerical root search.

## 4. Exact scope through the requested orders

For balanced even targets with \(r\) even and \(d=L(r)\), the relevant mixed-clock projections are:
\[
 \begin{array}{c|c|c|c|c}
 p&m&r&d&\text{proved actual rooted order}\\\hline
 12&6&6&12&\ge9\\
 16&8&8&20&\ge11\\
 20&10&10&30&\ge13
 \end{array}
\]
Each has **all** length-three word contacts at its stated target grade budget, not just those needed by the smaller concluded order. At \(16,20\) the whole strict adjacent path can be made nonzero by §2. At \(12\) only the three specified initial adjacent taps are certified; the rest are not asserted. These are not order-12, order-16, or order-20 upper bounds. The degree-\(p\) scalar failure certifies the next bush only if the remaining genuine mixed shelves are subsequently paid. The reduction to four real input rows and the exact affine selection of interior clocks give a physical starting point for those shelves, without reviving either a frozen exterior reverse chart or a silent trailing pivot block.
