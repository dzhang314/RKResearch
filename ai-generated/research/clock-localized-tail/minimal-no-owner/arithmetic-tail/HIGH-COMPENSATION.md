# Seven physical taps preserve the high prefix and the rank-nine fourth shelf

For \(n\ge4\), seven causal pivots realize the minimum word dimensions six and nine at lengths three and four while preserving the named exceptional contacts.

## 1. Five literal compensation clauses have full rank

Use the first five actual reverse sites from RANK-NINE.md, with the paid four low equations and its composite input
\[
 \varphi=bM_0^3-\lambda h_0M_0,\qquad
 \varphi T_0=bf_3(M_0)+h_0P_2(M_0).
\]
Their measured functions are \(1,\beta,\beta^2,H,\phi\), where
\(\phi(\beta,H)=\beta^3-\lambda\beta H\), and their restriction has rank five. Denote by \(P\) the true model of the genuine negative-prefix row \(v\), and keep its shorter primitive row \(w\) and its established mark. The true required high equations are
\[
            v=b(P-J^*Q)(C)+bQ(C)T,\qquad vT=w,\qquad
            \deg Q\le r-3,                                  \tag{1}
\]
with \(J^*=I_1-\gamma I\), in physical \(X\). Put \(f(\beta)=Q(1-\gamma\beta)\) and \(\widehat P(\beta)=P(1-\gamma\beta)/\gamma\).

At the first five physical suffix sites \(v=0\). Thus the first equation of (1) is the actual discrepancy \(\mathcal D_i f=\widehat P(\beta_i)\), where \(\mathcal D_i f=L_1 f(\beta_i)-\sum_{k<i}R_{i,k}f(\beta_k)\). For any polynomial \(f\), interpolate its actual values on the first four sites by
\(A^{(4)}_f(\beta)+c^{(4)}_f H\). The four-tap formula in PAID-HIGH-PIVOTS.md gives
\[
 \mathcal D^{(4)}(\beta)f
  =L_1(f-A^{(4)}_f)(\beta)+K_f^{(4)}(\beta)\,H_4(\beta),\qquad
 K_f^{(4)}=(A^{(4)}_f)_1/2+(A^{(4)}_f)_2K
                    -c^{(4)}_f(\beta-1)/\kappa .             \tag{2}
\]
Here \(H_4=N_4/D_4\) is the exact rational continuation after prescribing the grade-four composite. For each of the first five sites (2) gives its genuine \(\mathcal D_i\): for the old sites their actual taps and measured values satisfy the four equations, and the affine interpolation evaluates \(f\) exactly on every available prior node. At the new fifth site the same four taps were used. The rational \(H_4\) agrees with the old \(H_i\)'s at the four earlier sites by (4) of RANK-NINE.md, with nonzero denominators there for large finite \(g\). Its limit Taylor coefficients at zero are
\[
                   H_4=2-10\beta+(14/3)\beta^2+O(\beta^3).
 \tag{3}
\]

Let the clocks \(u,\beta_3,\beta_4\) coalesce to zero in the limit only. Replacing their discrepancy rows by zeroth, first and second Newton divided differences is an invertible transformation at finite distinct sites. Formula (2) shows that these transformed rows converge to the first three Taylor functionals of (2). The interpolation \(A^{(4)}_f,c^{(4)}_f\) also has a continuous confluent limit by divided differences, as shown in PAID-HIGH-PIVOTS.md. The two unclustered columns are at \(1,-1\).

We check explicitly that the five limiting homogeneous conditions on polynomials through degree five have only constants in their kernel. Write \(I\) for the quadratic interpolant at \(1,0,-1\), and \(B=f-I=(\beta^3-\beta)S\). The constant of \(I\) is immaterial. Denote the linear and quadratic coefficients of \(I\) by \(I_1,I_2\). The value and first derivative conditions from (2) impose
\[
                      I_1=-I_2,\qquad B'(0)=I_2/3 .
\]
Indeed the first discrepancy is \(I_1+I_2\), and then the derivative is \(-B'(0)+I_2/3\). Under these conditions \(c^{(4)}_f=-I_2/3\), \(A^{(4)}_f=I+(I_2/3)(6\beta^2-9\beta+2)\), and \(K_f^{(4)}=(I_2/3)(\beta-1)\), by the same interpolation derivative. The coefficient of \(\beta^2\) in (2) is therefore
\[
 [\beta^2](I_0^\beta B)-[\beta^2]B+
   (7/2)I_2-(44/9)I_2=-[\beta^2]B-(11/9)I_2 .
\]
Here \([\beta^2](I_0^\beta B)=B'(0)/2=I_2/6\); the two other coefficients are obtained from \(-\frac{I_2}{3}L_1(6\beta^2-9\beta+2)\) and \(\frac{I_2}{3}(\beta-1)H_4\), using (3). The third cluster test sets this to zero. Thus
\[
          S(0)=-I_2/3,\qquad [\beta]S=(11/9)I_2.             \tag{4}
\]
The two unclustered discrepancy equations give
\(\int_0^1 B=I_2/6\) and \(\int_0^{-1} B=17 I_2/6\), from the actual limiting \(H\)-values \(-1,17\) and the polynomial factor \(I_2\beta/6\). Subtracting them asks for the odd part of \(S\) to contribute \(-8I_2/3\). If \(\deg f\le5\), then \(\deg S\le2\); its sole odd coefficient in (4) gives instead \(-44I_2/135\). These agree only if \(I_2=0\). Then \(I_1,S(0),[\beta]S=0\), and the nonzero even integral coefficient at \(\beta^2\) gives \(S=0\). This verifies rank five through degree five in the limit and therefore at sufficiently large finite \(g\). In particular we can solve the five actual inhomogeneous high clauses with a polynomial \(f_0\) of degree at most five.

## 2. A homogeneous high direction genuinely changes the sixth pivot

Set \(s=r-3=2n-2\ge6\). A monic degree-\(s\) polynomial \(f_\infty\) satisfying the five limiting homogeneous conditions can be chosen with \(I_2\ne0\). Use \(I=I_2(\beta^2-\beta)\) up to a constant, (4), and put \([\beta^{s-3}]S=1\). If \(s=6\), the difference of the two integrals above now has the extra contribution \(-4/35\) from the cubic coefficient of \(S\). It forces
\[
 (316/135)I_2=4/35,\qquad I_2=27/553\ne0 .
\]
The quadratic coefficient of \(S\) then solves the sum of the two integrals. If \(s>6\), choose \(I_2=1\); the additional free cubic coefficient solves the odd difference, and the quadratic solves the even sum. These are independent because \(\int_0^1(\beta^3-\beta)\beta^k=-2/[(k+4)(k+2)]\) and the integral to \(-1\) multiplies it by \((-1)^k\). Correct \(f_\infty\) by a degree-\(\le5\) polynomial to the exact homogeneous kernel at finite sites using the preceding rank. The correction tends to zero in the normalized divided-difference equations. Call the resulting monic polynomial \(f_R\).

On the first five **actual** sites interpolate any \(f\) now by
\[
 f(\beta_i)=A_f(\beta_i)+c_f H_i+a_f\phi(\beta_i,H_i),\qquad \deg A_f\le2 .
 \tag{5}
\]
For \(f=f_R\), the coefficient \(a_f\) has a nonzero confluent limit. One way to compute it is to subtract the first-four interpolant from \(f_\infty\) and from the composite \(\phi\), then take their coefficients of \(\beta^2\) at the fifth \(H_4\)-branch. The residuals vanish at the two distinct old cluster sites, so the quotient of their second divided differences is exactly \(a_f\). For \(f_\infty\), its residual is \(B+c_f^{(4)}(6\beta^2-9\beta+2-H_4)\). By (3) and (4) its quadratic coefficient is \(-5I_2/3\). For \(\phi\), its residual is \(\beta^3-A_2-(\lambda\beta+c)H_4\), from (4) of RANK-NINE.md; the quadratic coefficient is \(11/52\). Thus \(a_{f_R}\longrightarrow-(260/33)I_2\ne0\). This also controls continuity of the whole measured interpolation (5) after the same divided differences.

Define polynomials
\[
 U_f=A_f+a_f\beta^3,\qquad W_f=c_f-a_f\lambda\beta,\qquad
 K_f=(A_f)_1/2+(A_f)_2 K-c_f(\beta-1)/\kappa-a_f P_2 .
\]
Thus \(f=U_f+H W_f\) on the measured five sites. At a next column, the five actual low and composite equations imply
\[
          L_1 f-R\cdot f=L_1(f-U_f)+H K_f.                  \tag{6}
\]
Indeed plug (5) into those five literal responses; the composite term contributes \(f_3+H P_2\). The degrees of \(W_f,K_f\) are at most one and two. Write \(f=f_0+\eta f_R\), while requiring \(\eta\ne0\) and avoiding \(K_f\equiv0\). This is possible because \([\beta^2]K_{f_R}=-a_{f_R}[\beta^2]P_2\ne0\) at large finite \(g\).

Also avoid values of \(\eta\) for which the polynomial
\[
 \Gamma_f=(f-U_f)K_f-W_f\{\widehat P-L_1(f-U_f)\}
\]
vanishes identically. It has degree at most \(s+2\), and the coefficient of \(\eta^2\) in its leading degree is
\[
 [\beta^2]K_{f_R}+\frac{[\beta]W_{f_R}}{s+1}
   \longrightarrow
   \frac{a_{f_R}}{156}\left(11-\frac{25}{s+1}\right)\ne0 ,
\]
where on the right \(a_{f_R}\) denotes its limiting value. This follows from the monicity of \(f_R\) and the \(P_2,\lambda\) limits in RANK-NINE.md. The term with \(\widehat P\) is at most linear in \(\eta\), even if its physical coefficients are large. Thus an algebraic choice of \(\eta\) can meet all conditions.

The polynomial \(\Gamma_f\) vanishes at all five earlier sites because both the measured interpolation and the earlier high clauses hold there. Pick an additional distinct interior site avoiding the roots of \(K_f,\Gamma_f\), at most \(2+(s+2-5)\) extra exclusions. The exact scalar grid has enough sites. Set
\[
      H=\{\widehat P-L_1(f-U_f)\}/K_f
\]
there; its five earlier low and composite taps now satisfy the high clause by (6). Its measured input \(f-U_f-H W_f=\Gamma_f/K_f\) is nonzero. Thus the six actual inputs \(b,bM_0,bM_0^2,h_0,\varphi,bQ(C)\) are independent on six real physical sites.
