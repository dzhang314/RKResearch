# Several completed weighted clock fibres impose one Hermite rank on a leading tableau

Let \(A,\mathbf b\) be an ordinary order-\(p\) lower triangular RK tableau over a field with \(p!\) a unit. Write \(q\) for the number of distinct literal clocks carrying a nonzero output weight, including labels whose entire aggregate weight cancels. Assume \(q\le p\), and put \(a\coloneqq p-q\). The numerical rooted span \(E_j\) uses actual Butcher weight vectors through degree \(j\), that is, order \(j+1\). For every visible clock \(\theta\) write
\[
     w_\theta\coloneqq\sum_{i:c_i=\theta}b_i\mathbf e_i^{\rm row},
       \qquad \bar b_\theta\coloneqq w_\theta\cdot\mathbf1 .
\]
The paid clock-isolation argument in MINIMAL-QUADRATURE-OUTPUT-CLOCKS.md says
\[
            w_\theta\cdot\boldsymbol\Phi_t(A)
                 =\bar b_\theta P_t(\theta),\qquad
   P_t(x)\coloneqq|t|x^{|t|-1}/t!,\quad |t|\le a+1.                 \tag{1}
\]
A repeated clock is not given separate exact stage profiles by (1). Only the actual weighted combination is exact, including the possible zero aggregate.

Take a literal leading prefix of \(k\) stages, with no zero diagonal when using the nonsingular conclusions below. A fibre is completed at this cut if every one of its actual nonzero weights is on the leading rows. Its row \(w_\theta\) is then truly supported on this prefix. Count by \(c\) the completed labels with \(\bar b_\theta\ne0\) and \(\theta\ne0\). Write \(\eta=1\) if the completed clock zero has nonzero aggregate, and \(\eta=0\) otherwise. Write \(z\) for the number of completed visible zero-aggregate fibres. Clocks zero and one have different roles: this proof's origin special case is \(\theta=0\), not the \(\theta=1\) endpoint in weighted tail descent.

## Exact joint scalar rank

For \(0\le j\le a\) all actual completed-fibre rows \(w_\theta A_{PP}^u\), \(0\le u\le a-j\), are exact on rooted grade \(j\) for the scalar functional \(\bar b_\theta(J^uP)(\theta)\), where \(J\) is the zero-origin primitive. They use only rows of the unchanged leading matrix. The zero-aggregate rows produce only zero scalar functionals. The scalar rank of all these reads is exactly
\[
                    H_j\coloneqq
                 \min\{j+1,\ c(a-j+1)+\eta\}.                    \tag{2}
\]
To prove this, put \(v\coloneqq a-j\) and let \(F=J^vP\) for a polynomial \(P\) of degree at most \(j\). Then \(F\) ranges over all polynomials of degree at most \(a\) divisible by \(x^v\). At each distinct nonzero completed live clock, vanishing of the \(v+1\) exact primitives is equivalent to \(F\) having a root of multiplicity \(v+1\) there, since those reads are the derivatives of orders \(0,\ldots,v\). At a live clock zero only \(P(0)\) need be added, and that changes the required power of \(x\) to \(x^{v+1}\). Thus the simultaneous kernel is exactly
\[
                 x^{v+\eta}\prod_{\theta\ {\rm live},\,\theta\ne0}
                         (x-\theta)^{v+1} K[x]_{\le a-v-\eta-c(v+1)}.
\]
Negative degree denotes the zero space. The restricted input dimension is \(j+1\), proving (2) over paid fields without any ordering of the so-called clocks.

These are all scalar directions generated by arbitrary paid words in \(A_{PP}, C_{PP}\) starting at the completed rows. Indeed \(J^u(xP)=xJ^uP-uJ^{u+1}P\) by induction from \(xJ-Jx=J^2\); evaluation at a fixed clock therefore reduces the additional clock multiplications to the same primitive family. This claim concerns scalar rank, not ambient row rank, which may be larger.

## Forward placement, including zero-sum fibres

On a nonsingular leading matrix its genuine commutator has diagonal \(-a_{ii}^2\), so it embeds \(E_{j-2}\) in \(F_j\). The scalar directions (2) are exact and kill the latter pure space. Hence the leading rooted dimension through degree \(J\le a\) is at least the sum of \(H_j\) through \(J\) in its own parity. In addition the \(z\) completed zero-aggregate rows are independent physical annihilators of \(E_a\), because their actual supports are nonempty and disjoint. Thus
\[
                  \boxed{\quad
      k\ge z+\max_{\epsilon=0,1}
              \sum_{\substack{0\le j\le a\\j\equiv\epsilon\pmod2}}H_j .
                         \quad}                                  \tag{3}
\]
One may also count the actual scalar dependencies among the live completed rows at degree \(a\): if \(c+\eta>a+1\), there are a further \(c+\eta-(a+1)\) independent annihilators because their ambient row supports are disjoint. The same additional annihilators kill each smaller rooted space as well, so their count can be added to either parity. With a mixed diagonal, subtract the actual graded right-kernel intersections of the leading commutator in each two-step sum; the completed zero-sum annihilators are unchanged.

For one nonzero completed live clock and no live zero clock, the parity sum in (3) is the usual nonsingular \(Q(a+1)\), because \(H_j=\min(j+1,a-j+1)\). This improves the \(Q(a)\) lower bound obtained merely from the normalized local order-\(a\) leading method. If the completed clock is a singleton at \(\theta\ne0\), that local normalization still preserves class but scales a common diagonal to \(\gamma/\theta\); it does not preserve its numerical value. For several singleton clocks at one cut, (3) gives a simultaneous bound on their positions. It equally applies when each exact receipt is a full repeated nonzero-aggregate fibre. A future nonzero weight at the same clock prevents using a partly completed fibre unless a separate rooted read is actually paid.

## The local and tail information combine degree by degree

Let \(V_j\) be a proved retained scalar rank at degree \(j\) from the ordinary weighted tail cut for the **same** physical prefix, such as \(a_j-d_{p-1-j}\) in MASKED-DENSITY-GROWTH.md. For \(j\le a\) the scalar rank is at least \(\max(H_j,V_j)\), not their sum. For \(j>a\) take \(V_j\). These numbers can be summed in one commutator parity on that prefix. This can be stronger than taking the maximum of two already completed stage bounds, since the stronger row data may change with the degree. Completed zero-sum annihilators may only be added at a stopping degree at most \(a\), unless their higher-grade annihilation is separately measured.

For a concrete scope check take a nonsingular order-\(14\) method with exactly seven nonzero stage weights, and cut immediately after its second nonzero weight. The ordinary scalar quadrature forces these seven labels to be distinct nonendpoint Gaussian labels. Here \(a=7,c=2,\eta=z=0\), and there are five distinct visible labels in the suffix. The Hermite-only lower bound (3) is \(14\); the uniform triangular trailing bound gives \(Q(14)-T_5=13\). In odd degrees \(1,3,5,7\) the joint picks are respectively \(2,4,6,3\): the last rank comes from the ordinary tail density while degree five uses the completed-clock jets. The same literal leading prefix therefore has at least \(15\) stages. This is a weighted-stage-position constraint on a sparse-output architecture, not an unrestricted order-\(14\) stage bound.

A fully measured version takes the rank of the union of the actual scalar functionals rather than the pointwise maximum. In monomial \(x^\ell\) the jet row for \(J^u\) at \(\theta\) is \(\ell!\theta^{\ell+u}/(\ell+u)!\). A surviving density \(P(y)=\sum_u d_u y^u\) from a literal tail certificate gives the row \(\sum_u d_u\,u!\ell!/(u+\ell+1)!\). All terms are paid at the specified word budget. Importantly, a raw mask \(\mathbf b P(Y)\) that vanishes on the visible suffix labels is already the actual row \(\sum_{\theta\ {\rm completed}}P(1-\theta)w_\theta\); its primitive descendants are already within the corresponding completed-fibre words when their grades are paid. Thus neither the mask nor those descendants generally contribute a second independent rank. This identity is the exact overlap test behind the joint calculation.
