# Exact refund of shifted fibre rows, and the limit of linear words

Use the rooted and physical hypotheses of the companion notes.

## The first three shifted reads have one literal refund

Let \(w\) be a raw complete fibre row, with \(wC=\theta w\), and let its last actual weighted stage have nonzero diagonal \(\delta\). Put
\[
 \xi\coloneqq w(A-\delta I),\qquad L\coloneqq\theta I-C .
\]
The shifted row is physically supported before the last weighted index. The recovered point and two first primitive rows can be chosen simultaneously as
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \widehat w_2&\coloneqq \xi L=wA^2+wM,\\
 \widehat w_1&\coloneqq(\widehat w_2-\xi A)/\delta
                       =wA+\delta^{-1}wM,\\
 \widehat w_0&\coloneqq(\widehat w_1-\xi)/\delta
                       =w+\delta^{-2}wM .
\end{aligned}                                                       \tag{4}
\]
The first follows from \(AL-LA=A^2+M\) and \(wL=0\); substitution proves the others. If \(w\) is paid at degree \(a\), all these equalities are physical, and the recovered rows act through degree \(a-2\) by the original unshifted scalar evaluations of \(1,J,J^2\) respectively, including zero aggregates. The three differences have precisely the same refund row. At every split where \(w\) belongs to the older paid space, the difference lies in the older right-\(M\) image; a recovered row and its original counterpart must not be used to claim an additional reverse direction from this difference.

For \(u\ge2\) the recovered higher primitive is \(wAL^{u-1}/(u-1)!\) when the relevant factorial is paid. Its difference from \(wA^u\) is in the span of words with at least one \(M\) and total cost at most \(u\) after \(w\). Indeed \(AL=LA+A^2+M\); modulo the two-sided ideal generated by \(M\), \(A^nL=LA^n+nA^{n+1}\). Moving \(L\)'s left then gives \(wAL^{u-1}=(u-1)!\,wA^u\) in that quotient, as any term with a left \(L\) is killed by \(w\). The commutation uses exactly total cost \(u\). A two-sided ideal word need not belong to the prior **rightmost** \(M\) image on a real tableau. At a zero-surplus split it must do so by the exact ledger, and at positive surplus its unrefunded rank is measured by (2), not declared zero. For instance
\[
 \widehat w_3-wA^3
       =\tfrac12 w(MA+AM+ML).
\]
This is a paid rank question rather than an assertion of formal commutation on the stages.

## Linear adjoint orbit can hide arbitrarily large genuine rooted contrasts

Here is a general physical example, not an assertion about a global stage minimum. Start with a triangular prefix \(P\) whose actual clocks contain two different values. Fix a nonzero \(\gamma\), equal to the prefix's common diagonal if the whole result is to be strict SDIRK. Choose two prefix feeder rows \(u,v\) with
\[
 u\cdot\mathbf1=\theta-\gamma,\qquad
 v\cdot\mathbf1=0,\qquad v\cdot\mathbf c_P\ne0 .
\]
Such \(v\) exists as a difference of two coordinates with different clocks. Append \(n+1\) rows with distinct parameters \(\lambda_0,\ldots,\lambda_n\) and no mutual off-diagonal feeds, all of diagonal \(\gamma\) and all fed from the prefix by \(u+\lambda_i v\). Their actual clock is \(\theta\).

Write \(f_t(\lambda)\) for the \(t\)-profile of an appended row with parameter \(\lambda\). The genuine recurrence on the whole tableau reads
\[
 f_\bullet=1,\qquad
 f_{[t]}(\lambda)=(u+\lambda v)\cdot\boldsymbol\Phi_t(A_P)
                       +\gamma f_t(\lambda),\qquad
 f_{t\#t'}=f_t f_{t'} .
\]
The degree in \(\lambda\) is at most \(\lfloor(|t|-1)/2\rfloor\). To check this by induction, the first extension from \(\bullet\) has constant value \(\theta\), while the extension of any higher tree adds a polynomial of degree at most one to \(\gamma f_t\). Rooted sum adds the tree degrees \(|t|-1\) and multiplies the profile polynomials, preserving the bound. For \(t_0\coloneqq[[\bullet]]\), \(f_{t_0}\) is affine of nonzero slope \(v\cdot\mathbf c_P\). Rooted sums of \(t_0\) therefore supply every power of this affine polynomial up to the degree limit. Thus the actual finite restrictions of \(E_j\) to this cohort have rank exactly \(\min(n+1,\lfloor j/2\rfloor+1)\).

Take \(n\ge2\), and let the actual row weights on this fibre be the barycentric difference
\[
 \alpha_i\coloneqq\prod_{h\ne i}(\lambda_i-\lambda_h)^{-1},\qquad
 w\coloneqq\sum_{i=0}^n\alpha_i e_i^{\rm row},
\]
where the row indices here are the appended coordinates. Interpolation of the leading coefficient gives \(\sum_i\alpha_i\lambda_i^\ell=0\) for \(\ell<n\) and \(1\) at \(\ell=n\). In particular the weights have zero aggregate and cancel both \(u\) and the \(\lambda v\) feed. Hence \(wA=\gamma w,\ wC=\theta w\); the entire ordinary linear \(A,C\) orbit is one-dimensional and \(wM=-\gamma^2w\). Nevertheless \(w\) annihilates \(E_{2n-1}\) and fails on the rooted sum of \(n\) copies of \(t_0\), at degree \(2n\), by the exact value \((v\cdot\mathbf c_P)^n\). The first distinction is at order \(2n+1\), not detectable at all by the dimension of its ordinary \(A,C\) orbit. In contrast the truth-preserving rooted diagonal \(B_{t_0}\) sees the actual \(n+1\) profile values; its powers on \(w\) have increasing numerical rank.

If \(P\) was already order \(p\le2n\), appending the rows and adding these weights preserves that order, because the preceding rooted error is zero through the original order. Choose \(\theta\) not used by any preceding weighted stage if a separate whole cancelling fibre is desired. This example is not stage-minimal and is not used to infer a global obstruction. It shows why one cannot use a one-dimensional linear donor orbit to assert equality of the repeated stages' actual profiles or repeatedly debit a zero row after it has entered the older right-\(M\) image.

A related fully general necessary condition does not require affine feeds. For a complete fibre paid through degree \(a\), any test tree \(t\) of positive degree \(d\) supplies
\(\sum_i b_i\zeta_i^\ell=\bar b_\theta \zeta_*^\ell\) for \(0\le\ell\le\lfloor a/d\rfloor\), where \(\zeta_i\) and \(\zeta_*\) are as in the preceding note. After aggregating equal \(\zeta_i\), subtract the target weight at \(\zeta_*\). If the remaining signed measure is nonzero, its support has at least \(\lfloor a/d\rfloor+2\) distinct values, by the Vandermonde test. This conclusion covers genuinely different and signed types, not merely a positive-weight or Gaussian output. If every such aggregate cancels, no mathematical difference of those traces has been proved.
