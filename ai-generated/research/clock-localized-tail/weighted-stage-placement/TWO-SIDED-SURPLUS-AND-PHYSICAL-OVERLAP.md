# Two-sided paid surplus and literal donor intersections

Work over a field in which the factorials of the stated ordinary RK order are units. Use the actual rooted spaces \(E_j,F_j\) through order \(j+1\); \(F_j\) is generated by formal combinations with zero scalar counterpart. A tree \(t\) has model \(P_t(x)\coloneqq |t|x^{|t|-1}/t!\). The formal model of \(A\) is the zero-origin primitive \(J\), and that of \(C\coloneqq\operatorname{diag}(A\mathbf1)\) is multiplication by \(x\). Write
\[
 M\coloneqq CA-AC-A^2.
\]
The rooted commutator payment gives \(M E_j\subseteq F_{j+2}\). When all diagonal entries of the lower triangular \(A\) are nonzero, \(M\) is invertible and has diagonal \(-a_{ii}^2\). The statements below using this inversion are for nonsingular lower triangular tableaux, including strict SDIRK, and not for ERK or arbitrary mixed zero diagonals.

## A split identity with actual row kernels

Take a literal leading tableau of width \(k\) and a parity ending at degree \(J\). Suppose \(\mathcal R_j\) is a physical row space on this tableau for each degree of that parity. It consists of paid row functionals on \(E_j\): each row acts on a tree vector by its specified linear functional of \(P_t\). In particular it annihilates \(F_j\). Require the actual inclusion \(\mathcal R_{j+2}M\subseteq \mathcal R_j\) whenever \(j+2\le J\). Let \(\mathcal R_{J+2}=0\), and interpret negative \(E\) subscripts as zero. Write
\[
\begin{split}
 e_j&\coloneqq\dim E_j,\qquad d_j\coloneqq\dim\mathcal R_j,\qquad
 h_j\coloneqq\operatorname{rank}(\mathcal R_j\longrightarrow E_j^*),\\
 K_j&\coloneqq\mathcal R_j\cap\operatorname{Ann}(E_j),\qquad
 G_j\coloneqq\mathcal R_{j+2}M,\\
 f_j&\coloneqq e_j-e_{j-2}-h_j,\qquad
 u_j\coloneqq d_j-d_{j+2}-h_j,\qquad
 v_j\coloneqq k-e_j-d_j+h_j .
\end{split}
\]
Here \(G_j\subseteq K_j\), since \(M E_j\subseteq F_{j+2}\) and all next-grade rows kill \(F_{j+2}\). The three displayed surplus terms are nonnegative. More precisely, \(u_j=\dim(K_j/G_j)\), and \(v_j=\dim(\operatorname{Ann}(E_j)/K_j)\); forward nonnegativity uses \(M E_{j-2}\subseteq F_j\) and the scalar rank on \(E_j/F_j\). If
\[
 S\coloneqq\sum_{i\equiv J\ (2),\ 0\le i\le J}h_i,
\]
then at every split \(j\) on this parity,
\[
 \boxed{\quad k-S=
 \sum_{i\equiv J\ (2),\ i\le j}f_i+
 \sum_{i\equiv J\ (2),\ i\ge j}u_i+v_j.\quad}                         \tag{1}
\]
Indeed \(k=e_j+d_j-h_j+v_j\), and the two recursions telescope in opposite directions. This counts the scalar rank at the split once. It is not a second addition of the old forward and reverse parity bounds.

At equality in the scalar parity sum the entire common kernel of \(\mathcal R_j\) on stage space is \(F_j=M E_{j-2}\), and \(\operatorname{Ann}(E_j)=K_j=G_j\) at every split. At gap \(g=k-S\), the whole annihilator contains \(G_j\) with codimension \(u_j+v_j\le g\). Any unrefunded physical zero row occupies one of those dimensions, regardless of whether its zero model arose from a repeated or a cancelling clock.

## Exact overlap calculation

Take any supplied paid donor subspace \(W\subseteq\mathcal R_j\), and put \(Z\coloneqq W\cap\operatorname{Ann}(E_j)\). For any linear map \(\Pi_W\) with kernel exactly \(W\), the portion of the reverse surplus certified by \(W\) is exactly
\[
\begin{split}
 \Delta_j(W)&\coloneqq\dim((Z+G_j)/G_j)\\
 &=\dim W-\operatorname{rank}(W\longrightarrow E_j^*)
                -\dim G_j+\operatorname{rank}(\Pi_W|_{G_j})
 \ \le u_j.                                                         \tag{2}
\end{split}
\]
The subtracted intersection is \(G_j\cap W=G_j\cap Z\), not the intersection of their model densities or an overlap of lists of stage labels. Several donors at the same degree must first be pooled in \(W\). Across different degrees on one parity, the corresponding \(\Delta_j\) can be summed in (1): each is measured modulo its own older commutator image. In particular a zero row recovered from an older one by a scalar eigenvalue of \(M\) will not be charged again after it enters that image.

For raw complete clock rows \(w_\theta\coloneqq\sum_{c_i=\theta}b_i e_i^{\rm row}\), choose the last actual nonzero weight \(i_\theta\) as anchor. If \(W\) is spanned by the chosen raw rows, \(\Pi_W\) can be obtained by recording:
- all coordinates outside the union of actual weight supports of the chosen fibres;
- in each chosen fibre, the coordinates \(u_i-(b_i/b_{i_\theta})u_{i_\theta}\) at the other actual nonzero weight indices.
The resulting kernel is precisely \(W\). Thus the calculation checks both silent columns and within-fibre weight ratios at physically repeated clocks. A pivot-set test is weaker but cheap: write \(\operatorname{piv}(L)\) for the possible greatest support index of a nonzero row in \(L\). An invertible lower triangular \(M\) preserves every such index, so
\[
 \Delta_j(W)\ge
 \#\bigl(\operatorname{piv}(Z)\setminus\operatorname{piv}(\mathcal R_{j+2})\bigr).
\]
Different pivot indices give independent classes. Equal pivot sets do not establish equal physical row spaces; the actual contrast map in (2) may still see a deficit.

For the completed-fibre Hermite construction one can take \(\mathcal R_j\) to be all words of cost at most \(a-j\) on the completed rows, where \(a=p-q\) for \(q\) actual visible clock labels. All these rows are physical prefix rows, and the scalar ranks are the previously proved \(H_j\). Shifted future rows supported on the same prefix may be added as new seeds only with their actual shorter paid horizon. These examples use (1) and (2) directly; no stage at a repeated clock is presumed individually exact.
