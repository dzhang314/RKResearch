# Exact scope of the support-indicator shortcut

This correction leaves the original fibre-transfer seal and Lean files
unchanged. The rank and stage results with the explicit hypothesis
\(L(S)\subseteq F_H\) are unchanged.

Clock stability and pointwise product closure of \(F_H\) give the
indicator of its **actual physical support** \(S_0\), and its genuine
clock projections give the supported fibre indicators within \(S_0\).
Thus the shortcut proves \(L(S)\subseteq F_H\) when \(S=S_0\) or when
the support cover enlarges \(S_0\) only at zero-clock rows. Such rows
do not affect \(L(S)\).

If an arbitrary cover \(S\) contains an extra unsupported nonzero-clock
row, clock or product stability cannot create a nonzero entry there.
Indeed the weighted evaluation of the constant polynomial has value
\(c_i\ne0\) at such a row \(i\in S\), so \(L(S)\subseteq F_H\) is then
impossible. This remains true if that added physical row has the same
clock as a supported row.

The separate output-mass criterion still requires the indicator of the
**whole original fibre**, or independent proof that its full aggregate
weight is zero. An indicator on only the supported part of a repeated
fibre shows nothing about the weights on its omitted copies. The parent
reviewer's scope observation is incorporated without modifying the main
conditional comparisons.
