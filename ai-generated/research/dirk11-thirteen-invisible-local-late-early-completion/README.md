# Late silent slack excludes a completion before the fourth ordinary debut

**Result.** Work in the normalized characteristic-zero DIRK11/13
[four-marked-pair plus slack chart](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-local-slack-chronology/README.md).
If the silent nondirty slack lies after the fourth distinct
weighted nonendpoint reverse debut, no marked pair can complete
before that fourth debut. In the surviving no-completion prefix,
the fourth diagonal is necessarily zero and its \(G/b\) sample
differs from the first three's genuine cubic interpolation.
This statement allows zero \(G\) at the fourth; it does not assert
that all later slack fits are impossible. The unrestricted real
stage interval remains **13–14**.

## 1. An actual marked repeat supplies the old contact

Use \(Y=1-c\), \(N=A^{\mathsf T}\) in literal reverse chronology,
\(I\) the zero-constant primitive and \(W(U)=b_{\rm wt}U(Y)\).
Let \(G\) be the actual reflected cubic.
The first three weighted nonendpoint debuts are \(v,u,w\);
the older cut excludes marked completions before the third.
Their \(G/b_{\rm wt}\) samples are live: the first two reflected
moments give the first, and \(NG,N(YG)\) subtract the earlier
live distinct donors if the next heads are zero. This is the
same genuine low prefix calculation as in the
[middle cut](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-local-slack-chronology/README.md#4-a-completed-pair-with-slack-in-the-next-gap-is-impossible).
Put \(R=(Y-v)(Y-u)(Y-w)\), \(P_0=YR\).
Match the three samples with \(P(0)=0\), \(\deg P\le3\);
\(D_0=G-W(P)\) has zero prefix through \(w\).
The same true fit proof (before use of any slack row) gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 ND_0&=G\ell-W(IP),\\
 N(YD_0)&=G(Y\ell-\rho)-W(I(YP)),\\
 NW(P_0)&=W(IP_0)-GW_0,\qquad \mathcal C D_0=0 ,
 \end{aligned}
\]
where \(\mathcal C=YN-NY-N^2\), \(\ell\) is affine, and
\(\rho,W_0\) have degree at most two. Their old interpolated
values are \(I^2P/P,IP_0/P\). In particular \(\mathcal C D_0\)
is paid in \(Z_5=G\mathcal P_2\) by \(3+7+1=11\) for the
weighted term and \(G\in E_7^\perp\) for the other term.
Three actual old zero coordinates force it to vanish globally.
Nothing here assumes that the slack is next.

Suppose a marked pair has completed before fourth \(V\).
Two such completions would give two double roots of \(IP_0\)
in addition to zero (at each pair compare the actual
\(NW(P_0)=0\) entries and distinct \(G/b_{\rm wt}\)).
That would exceed degree five.
Thus the literal prefix is \(T,v,u,w,i,V\), where \(i\) is the
repeat of \(a\in\{v,u,w\}\). At \(i\) the first nonzero
\(D_0\) coordinate is \(b_i(k_i-k_{\rm first})\), because
the two weights are live and the normalized heads differ.
Then \(\mathcal C D_0=0\) gives \(d_i=0\).
At the repeated row and its original mate,
\(ND_0=N(YD_0)=0\). Comparing their two actual weighted
fits therefore gives \(\ell(a)=\rho(a)=0\),
\(IP(a)=I^2P(a)=0\). At both members \(NW(P_0)=0\),
so \(W_0(a)=0\) and \(IP_0(a)=0\) as well.
There is no zero-weight substitution at either pair member.

The resulting three Hermite equations are exactly the
[old-contact degree-24 ingress](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-local-slack-old-twin/README.md#1-the-exact-old-contact-clock-algebra),
with \(a\) replacing the numerical silent-clock anchor there.
The valuation argument gives a true cubic \(P=Ym\) in a monic
gauge, \(\ell=\lambda(Y-a)\) with \(\lambda\ne0\), and
\(\delta=1/4-\lambda\ne0\).
The equations now give
\[
 m=Y^2-\tfrac65aY+\tfrac3{10}a^2,\qquad
 R=(Y-a)[Y^2-(2t+3/5)aY+t a^2],\qquad
 \lambda=t/(4t-6/5).
\]
The ordinary Hankel tests use exactly the same distinct live
ordinary labels and the degree-ten hull budget, so the same
irreducible degree-24 \(a\) field, live \(t\) ratio and later
quadratic apply. The actual silent clock \(z\) is not used in
this reconstruction. In particular the full rank-48 model
retains both assignments of the later \(V,B\).

## 2. What can physically follow the fourth?

Use the marked column
\[
 q=\frac{(V-a)(k_V-P(V))}{P_0(V)},\qquad
 Q_4=(Y-a)P+qP_0,\quad D=(Y-a)G-W(Q_4),\quad
 L=(Y-a)\ell-\rho+qW_0 .
\]
Both physical members of the repeated clock \(a\) have zero
\(D\) (the mark and \(Q_4\) vanish there), so the actual
inclusive prefix through \(V\) is zero.
The true identity is \(ND=GL-W(IQ_4)\).
If \(G_V=0\), this would force \(IQ_4(V)=0\) with
\(q=-(V-a)P(V)/P_0(V)\).
Thus the cleared gate
\[
 I((Y-a)P)(V)P_0(V)-(V-a)P(V)IP_0(V)=0
\]
is necessary. It is a unit in the full rank-48 reduction.
This first step permits the fourth to have absent \(G\)
before the arithmetic gate, rather than presuming another
marked row.

With \(G_V\ne0\), now
\(\mathcal C D,\mathcal C W(P_0)\in Z_6=G\mathcal P_3+K H\)
by \(4+6+1=11\) and the actual affine mark on \(G\).
On a zero-prefix element \(GQ+\theta H\), the actual two
\(a\)-rows have distinct \(G/b_{\rm wt}\) and common live
\(H/b_{\rm wt}=H_*(a)\), \(H_*(Y)=YJ_4(Y)\), so
\(Q(a)=\theta=0\).
For \(\mathcal C D\) the zero prefix also includes \(V\),
and all four distinct live \(G\) samples force \(\mathcal C D=0\).
For \(\mathcal C W(P_0)\) the zero prefix is through the
repeat, giving \(\mathcal C W(P_0)=\chi GR\) globally.
These are exactly the two predecessor identities in the
[old-twin repeat gates](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-local-slack-old-twin/README.md#2-the-first-two-literal-repeat-gates);
the decoupling here uses two live weighted entries, not
a notional silent \(a\)-row.

If the next physical row after \(V\) is another marked
completion \(x\), it cannot be the already completed \(a\).
The exact preceding repeat gates exclude either a repeat
of \(V\) or a different old clock. In detail, its
\(D_i=b_i(x-a)(k_i-k_{\rm first})\ne0\) gives \(d_i=0\);
the rank-48 unit \(IP_0(V)\) and the two proportional

Otherwise \(IP_0(x)\ne0\) (by double roots at \(0,a,x\))
and its actual commutator forces \(d_V=x-V\).
In both cases the inherited cleared fourth hinge is a unit.

If the next physical row is \(B\), the
[old-twin consecutive-fifth exclusion](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-local-slack-old-twin/README.md#3-actual-fifth-split-and-two-distinct-quadratics)
likewise applies. The actual \(\mathcal C D\) gives
\(d_B^2D_B=0\). On the nonzero branch use the ordinary
matched \(V/B\) quartics; these do not require \(G_B\ne0\).
On the zero branch the true quintic with input \(W((Y-V)P_0)\)
has zero inclusive input through \(B\), hence a paid error
\(GQ_5+\theta H\). Comparing the two weighted \(a\)-members
forces \(Q_5(a)=0\) and \(\theta=I((Y-V)P_0)(a)/H_*(a)\),
precisely the datum that the old-twin proof obtained from
its zero-weight/live-weight pair. All three old quotient
fits and the fourth fit otherwise use the same actual
weighted values. Its undivided determinant excludes the
zero diagonal as before. No arithmetic at an absent later
silent row is transposed into these tests.

The actual dirty and origin rows are later than \(B\)
in reverse. The only possible remaining next row after
\(V\) is therefore the silent nondirty slack.

## 3. All immediate silent labels are impossible

At the literal slack \(s_*\) after \(V\), its unweighted
entry is \(D_{s_*}=(z-a)G_{s_*}\ne0\):
\(G_{s_*}\) is live and \(z\) cannot be a marked \(a\).
The true \(\mathcal C D=0\) then gives \(d_{s_*}=0\),
and \(ND=GL-W(IQ_4)\) gives \(L(z)=0\).

Writing \(S(Y)=(Y-a)\ell(Y)-\rho(Y)\), the fourth weighted
match gives the degree-at-most-two equation in \(q\)
\[
 F(q)=
 [P(V)+qP_0(V)/(V-a)] [S(V)+qW_0(V)]
       -I((Y-a)P)(V)-qIP_0(V)=0.
\]
The silent equation is \(S(z)+qW_0(z)=0\).
Their fixed cleared scalar
\[
 F_0W_0(z)^2-F_1S(z)W_0(z)+F_2S(z)^2
\]
is necessarily zero even when \(W_0(z)=0\).
The only allowed \(z\) are the actual origin clock \(1\)
or the unmarked ordinary interior label. Thus \(z\) can be
\(1,V,B\), or one of the two other old labels, but not \(a\).
The full finite-free unit test checks \(1,V,B\) at rank 48
and **both** remaining old roots at rank 96. Each is a unit.
There is no surviving late continuation of an early
marked completion.

## 4. The remaining fourth must be a new zero-diagonal mismatch

If no pair completes before \(V\), the literal prefix
through \(V\) is \(T,v,u,w,V\).
The initial old fit \(IP-\ell P=\delta P_0\) still holds
for some constant \(\delta\), possibly initially zero.
In fact \(\delta=0\) is impossible in this chart.
If \(IP=\ell P\), valuation at zero forces
\(\ell(0)=0\), and comparing coefficients shows
\(P=\kappa Y^n,\ \ell(Y)=Y/(n+1)\) for \(1\le n\le3\).
The entire \(ND_0=G\ell-W(IP)\) then equals
\(\ell(Y)D_0\). At the first nonzero physical \(D_0\)
entry, the diagonal of \(N\) is the nonzero
\(\ell(Y_i)\), contradicting its forced zero diagonal
from \(\mathcal C D_0=0\).
There is a nonzero \(D_0\) entry because the silent slack
has weight zero and live \(G\), and every possible first
support has nonzero \(Y\) (the true terminal has \(G=0\);
the only dirty row has zero weight and zero \(G\)).
Hence \(\delta\ne0\), which also forces a genuine cubic
degree for \(P\): smaller degree could not have its
nonzero \(IP-\ell P\) divisible by \(YR\).

If \(D_{0,V}=0\) then the literal \((ND_0)_V=0\) gives
\(IP(V)=\ell(V)P(V)\), contrary to \(\delta P_0(V)\ne0\).
Thus \(D_{0,V}\ne0\), so \(d_V=0\) by the commutator.
Then all actual fits at \(V\) are
\[
 k_V\ell(V)=IP(V),\quad k_VW_0(V)=IP_0(V),\quad
 k_V\rho(V)=I^2P(V),\qquad \ell(V)\ne0 .
\]
They use the literal earlier zero prefixes and its zero
diagonal, and do not divide by \(k_V\).
The last live assertion follows from
\(IP(V)-\ell(V)P(V)=\delta P_0(V)\).
In this surviving chart, \(G_V\) is still allowed to
vanish, and later pair completions and slack positions
are not excluded by this packet.
