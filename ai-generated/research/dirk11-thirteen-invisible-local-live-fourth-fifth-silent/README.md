slack. The unrestricted real interval remains **13–14**.

## 1. The genuine silent row is at \(x=b\)

Scale \(X=Y/V\), \(N,G\) by \(1/V,1/V^3\) in the monic gauge.
Write \(z=V\) for the literal fourth, \(b=B/V\) for the normalized
fifth, and \(x=b\ne0,1\) for the normalized silent clock.

[universal fourth-clock pin](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-local-late-fourth-pin/README.md):
