# Normalized four-pair invisible-cubic DIRK11 slack census

This is a characteristic-zero-field exclusion within the normalized
four-marked-pair chart at thirteen stages. It does not establish that
unrestricted DIRK methods of order eleven need fourteen stages.

- A silent slack before the third ordinary weighted nonendpoint reverse
  debut is excluded by the [chronology ingress](README.md).
- Between the third and fourth debuts, slack may have an old, fourth,
  fifth, or origin clock. The [old twin](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-local-slack-old-twin/README.md),
  [fourth twin](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-local-slack-fourth-twin/README.md),
  [fifth twin](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-local-slack-fifth-twin-closure/README.md),
  and [origin](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-local-slack-origin-closure/README.md)
  exclusions close those four possibilities.
- For later slack the [early-completion exclusion](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-local-late-early-completion/README.md)
  rules out completing any marked pair before the fourth debut. The
  genuine fourth diagonal is zero and its reverse sample differs from
  the cubic drawn through the earlier samples. The [fourth-return test](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-local-fourth-return/README.md)
  excludes an immediate return to this fourth clock, and the
  [absent-head exclusion](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-local-zero-fourth-closure/README.md)
  forces its reverse head to be nonzero. An immediate completion of an
  earlier marked pair [requires a nonzero companion](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-local-old-repeat-companion/README.md).

The remaining immediate next rows are a marked old completion with live
companion, a silent slack at the origin, an earlier clock or the future
fifth clock, or the consecutive fifth weighted nonendpoint debut. This
record does not assert that any candidate of those types exists. The
new exclusions have exact rational and finite-free residue replays,
not end-to-end Lean proofs.
