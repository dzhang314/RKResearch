## 2. Two genuine weighted debuts and the conditional early macro

The slack occurs after at least two distinct live nonzero
weighted numerical debuts in reverse: if its inclusive positive
power donors were confined to one nonzero label, the true first

The polynomial \(P=Y(Y-u)(Y-v)\) and \(YP\) have zero
inclusive weighted inputs then, regardless of a repeated
physical donor. Their primitive errors are affine and quadratic

\(G\) entries and left sides \(b_i IP(s)\), \(b_i I(YP)(s)\).
If their cross coefficient \(b_i G_{i'}-b_{i'}G_i\) is
nonzero, then both primitives vanish. The actual values at
\(s=u\) are \(u^3(2v-u)/12\) and \(u^4(5v-3u)/60\);
they cannot both vanish for \(u\ne0\), and likewise at \(v\).
The cross coefficient is live for a marked pair by its
opposite \(G\)'s and live ordinary aggregate. It is also
live for a silent slack with live \(G\) sharing one of those
two labels with its weighted mate. Thus no marked pair can
complete before the third weighted debut, and an early slack
cannot share either of the first two weighted labels.

rows in reverse, since it is the first nonzero forward

nonzero tap to \(j\) and places it before \(j\) in reverse.

silent stages in this slot count. The first weighted

its own first two moments. At the second weighted debut
\(u\), \(G_u=0\) would make \((NG)_u=0\) because \(NG\in U_0\).
The only prior live \(G\) donor \(v\) would then have zero
tap, contradicting the half and third pure diagonal reads
on \(u\). This includes the possibility that an unmarked
singleton was proposed as the second debut.

Suppose for contradiction the slack is before the third
weighted debut \(w\). The literal reverse prefix is now
\(T,v,u,s_*,w\). These are exactly the inputs of the
[late macro](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-six-late-ghost/README.md#2-the-ghost-forces-a-literal-zero-pivot):

In particular the alternative \(G_w=0\) was handled by
the actual double-zero primitive and Radau-factor check
