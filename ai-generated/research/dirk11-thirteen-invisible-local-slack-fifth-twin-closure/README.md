leaves only the actual origin clock for any middle slack. The

## 3. A single true fourth compatibility excludes all old repeats

In the normalized \(X\) coordinate set \(h=q(1+\lambda)-\delta\).
Integration by parts yields
\[
 (1+\lambda)I^2P=\lambda(X-1)IP+\delta IP_0,\qquad
 (1+\lambda)\rho=\lambda(X-1)\ell+\delta W_0 .
\]
The second is the interpolation of the first at the three actual
old weighted clocks. Consequently
\[
 (1+\lambda)L=\lambda(X-1)^2+hW_0,\quad
 (1+\lambda)IQ_4=(X-1)IP+hIP_0,\quad
 (1+\lambda)ND=(X-1)ND_0-hNW(P_0).
\]
At the literal completed old clock \(r\), \(L(r)=0\) therefore
gives
\[
 h=-\lambda(r-1)^2/W_0(r),\qquad
 q=(h+\delta)/(1+\lambda).
\]
Both \(h\) and \(W_0(r)\) are necessarily live: the opposite
product equals the nonzero \(-\lambda(r-1)^2\).
No primitive at the fifth weighted row has yet been used.

At the literal fourth, \(ND_V=0\) (its inclusive \(D\) input
is zero), and the true weighted \(P_0\) first appears there,
so \((NW(P_0))_V/b_V=d_V P_0(u)\). On the other hand
\((ND_0)_V/b_V=k_V\ell(u)-IP(u)=(\lambda q-\delta)P_0(u)\).
The whole-row identity now forces the normalized diagonal
and normalized head to be
\[
 d_V=\frac{(\lambda q-\delta)(u-1)}h,\qquad
 k_V=P(u)+qP_0(u)/(u-1).
\]
For those exact values, the same literal \(P_0\) fit requires
the scalar gate
\[
 IP_0(u)-k_V W_0(u)-d_V P_0(u)=0.
\]
This gate depends only on the already reconstructed ordinary
clock field, and the normalized old repeat \(r\). To retain
every possible choice of \(r\), use the monic cubic extension
by \(R\), of rank \(60\cdot3=180\) over \(\mathbb Q\).
The exact checker proves this scalar a unit in the full
rank-180 reduction at 101, independently at 103. In particular
its image cannot vanish at any actual old root.
There is no fifth-row matching or later-stage assumption.
