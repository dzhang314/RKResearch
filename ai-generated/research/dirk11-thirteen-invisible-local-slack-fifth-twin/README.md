# Live fourth pivot for the middle silent slack

**Results.** On the normalized DIRK11/13
[four-marked-pair plus slack face](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-local-slack-chronology/README.md),
assume the slack is strictly between the third and fourth distinct
weighted nonendpoint reverse debuts.
The [fourth-twin](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-local-slack-fourth-twin/README.md)
and [older-twin](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-local-slack-old-twin/README.md)
packets exclude those contacts. The two remaining options here are
the origin clock \(z=1\) and the fifth weighted debut's clock \(z=B\).
Both force a **live fourth diagonal** \(d_V\ne0\) and
\(\mathcal C D=0\) for the true matched quartic below.
For the fifth twin, nevertheless \((ND)_B\ne0\).
Consequently that fifth twin cannot immediately follow \(V\):
at least one marked pair must complete before it, with zero
diagonal at the first such completion. This does not exclude
the origin chart, fifth twin with an intervening completion,
or a later silent slack. The unrestricted real stage interval
remains **13–14**.

## 1. Common true prefix and matched quartic

Write \(Y=1-c\), \(N=A^{\mathsf T}\) in literal reverse chronology,
\(I\) for the zero-constant primitive, \(W(U)=b_{\rm wt}U(Y)\),
and \(G\) for the genuine cubic, in the common gauge specified
below. The actual prefix is \(T,v,u,w,s_*,V\), by the preceding
[middle-slack no-completion gate](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-local-slack-chronology/README.md#4-a-completed-pair-with-slack-in-the-next-gap-is-impossible).
Put \(R=(Y-v)(Y-u)(Y-w),\ P_0=YR\).
Fit \(P\) with degree at most three and constant term zero to the
three live old \(G/b_{\rm wt}\) samples. The exact paid
[D0 fits](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-local-slack-fourth-twin/README.md#1-actual-reversed-prefix-and-graded-rows),
before the special fourth-twin read, give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 D_0&=G-W(P),\\
 ND_0&=G\ell-W(IP),\\
 N(YD_0)&=G(Y\ell-\rho)-W(I(YP)),\\
 NW(P_0)&=W(IP_0)-GW_0 .
 \end{aligned}
\]
Here \(\ell\) is affine, the other fits have degree at most two,
and \(\ell(z)=\rho(z)=W_0(z)=0\). The first two roots are the
actual slack zero diagonal from \(\mathcal C D_0=0\),
\(\mathcal C=YN-NY-N^2\); the last is the actual silent
\(W(P_0)\) prefix with live \(G\) at slack. This does not assume
anything about the numerical fifth twin being before a possible
pair repeat. As in the same argument, valuation at zero makes
\(P\) a true cubic and \(\ell=\lambda(Y-z)\ne0\).
Scale \(G,P\) by the cubic leader so \(P=Ym\) has monic \(m\);
then \(IP=\ell P+\delta P_0,\ \delta=1/4-\lambda\ne0\).
The invariance of the displayed fits under this common rescaling
follows by changing \(W_0\) accordingly.

Let \(k_V=G_V/b_V\). The actual column
\[
 D=(Y-z)D_0-q W(P_0),\qquad
 q=\frac{(V-z)(k_V-P(V))}{P_0(V)}
\]
vanishes through \(V\), with nonzero denominator at the distinct
ordinary debut. Put \(Q_4=(Y-z)P+qP_0\) and
\(L=(Y-z)\ell-\rho+qW_0\).
The true fits give \(ND=GL-W(IQ_4)\), hence
\(k_VL(V)=IQ_4(V)\). There is no assumption on \(d_V\).

## 2. A zero fourth diagonal would force an impossible slope

The old and fourth nonendpoint clocks are distinct from \(z\) in
both remaining middle cases. Suppose \(d_V=0\).
The only earlier possibly nonzero entry of \(D_0\) is at the slack;
its \(ND_0\) is zero by its own zero diagonal. Thus
\((N^2D_0)_V=0\) because \(d_V=0\), and the literal
\(\mathcal C D_0=0\) gives
\((V-z)N_{V,s_*}G_{s_*}=0\). The clock difference and slack \(G\)
are live, so the slack-to-fourth tap is zero. This yields
\((ND_0)_V=0\), hence \(k_V\ell(V)=IP(V)\).
Also \((NW(P_0))_V=0\), since the earlier weighted inputs vanish
and \(d_V=0\).
On all four distinct weighted sites \(x=v,u,w,V\) we now have
\[
 k_x\ell(x)=IP(x),\qquad k_x W_0(x)=IP_0(x).
\]
Put \(F=IP/Y\), \(L_0=IP_0/Y\), and write \(W_0=(Y-z)w_1\)
with affine \(w_1\). Cancel the factors \(Y\) and \(Y-z\) on
these four nonzero sites, using \(\ell=\lambda(Y-z)\).
Then \(F w_1-\lambda L_0\), of degree at most four, vanishes
at all four sites. It also vanishes at zero: both \(F,L_0\)
are divisible by \(Y\). Thus it is identically zero.
Since \(\lambda\ne0\), this gives
\(IP_0=(4Y/5+b)IP\) with the slope read from the two live monic
leaders \(1/5,1/4\).

Differentiate this exact polynomial identity and use
\(IP=\lambda(Y-z)P+\delta P_0\):
\[
 (1-4\delta/5)P_0
   = [(4/5)(1+\lambda)Y+b-(4/5)\lambda z]P .
\]
At the old three distinct clocks \(P\) is live and \(P_0=0\).
The affine bracket is identically zero, hence \(\lambda=-1\).

In fact \(\lambda=-1\) is incompatible with either remaining
ordinary clock geometry. In that case \(\delta=5/4\); after
scaling \(Y=zx\), write \(m(x)=x^2+rx+s\),
\(F_*=I_x(xm)/x\). The exact normalized cubic hull is
\[
 R_*=\tfrac45[F_*+(x-1)m].
\]
The genuine \(\rho(z)=0\) gives
\[
 r^2=(r+6)s .
\]
For reproducibility, multiplication by \(m\) on \(1,x,x^2\bmod R_*\)
has determinant \(-s^2(2r^2-9s)/225\). It is live, because \(m\)
is nonzero at each of the three distinct old roots. Applying its
adjugate to the coefficients of \(I_x^2(xm)/x\bmod R_*\), the sum
of the resulting coefficients is \(s^2(r^2-rs-6s)/2250\).
It must vanish because the true quadratic fit \(\rho_*(1)=0\).
No cancelling polynomial is presumed live without this check.
The constant fit gives \(s\ne0\), and \(r+6=0\) is incompatible
with \(r^2=(r+6)s\), so use \(s=r^2/(r+6)\).

Return to the literal \(Y\) and put
\[
 m(Y)=Y^2+r zY+s z^2,\quad
 \widehat R(Y)=(r+6)\tfrac45[IP/Y+(Y-z)m(Y)] .
\]
Thus \(\widehat R=(r+6)R\) contains no \(r\)-denominator after
using \(s=r^2/(r+6)\).
If \(z=B\), the moment sequence
\(M_j=\int_0^1Y^{j+1}(1-Y)(Y-z)\widehat R(Y)\,dY\)
must satisfy \(M_{j+1}=VM_j\) for \(j=0,1,2,3\).
Let \(F_{ij}=M_iM_{j+1}-M_{i+1}M_j\). The exact gcd of
the three resultants in \(r\) of \(F_{01}\) with
\(F_{02},F_{03},F_{12}\) is proportional to \(z^{12}\).
This excludes \(z\ne0\), including zero first moments
or degree drops. No leading coefficient of a minor is inverted.
If \(z=1\) is the origin, omit the extra \(Y-z\) factor:
use \(J_j=\int_0^1Y^{j+1}(1-Y)\widehat R(Y)\,dY\) with
\(J_{j+2}-(V+B)J_{j+1}+VB J_j=0\) for the same four \(j\).
The \(3\times3\) Hankel minors with rows \(012\) and \(013\)
are exactly coprime in \(r\). Both orthogonality systems test
only the actual degree-seven ordinary hull and its first three
marks, not an assumed live weight at the real origin.
The self-contained **project.py** checks all those identities
over \(\mathbb Q\). Therefore these two actual middle charts
always have \(d_V\ne0\).

## 3. Whole commutator zero and true fifth crossing

Both \(\mathcal C D\) and \(\mathcal C W(P_0)\) are in
\(Z_6=G\mathcal P_3+K H\), by \(4+6+1=11\) for the weighted
part and the degree-one mark on \(G\) pairing still with \(E_7\).
They both have zero prefix through the slack. Among such rows
the coefficient of \(H\) is injective: if it is zero, the
cubic multiplying \(G\) vanishes on the four distinct live
\(G\) clocks \(v,u,w,z\), hence is zero. Therefore this prefix
kernel has dimension at most one. The literal \(V\) value of
\(\mathcal C W(P_0)\) is \(-d_V^2 b_V P_0(V)\ne0\);
it spans that kernel. But \(\mathcal C D\) also vanishes at \(V\),
hence it is zero globally.

At the numerical fifth \(B=z\), \(L(B)=0\), so
\[
 (ND)_B=b_B\,[I^2P(z)-q\,IP_0(z)] .
\]

Suppose \((ND)_B=0\). Integration by parts and the actual affine
primitive yield the exact identities
\[
 (1+\lambda)I^2P=\lambda(Y-z)IP+\delta IP_0,\qquad
 (1+\lambda)\rho=\lambda(Y-z)\ell+\delta W_0 .
\]
The second follows from the first on all three old clocks with
live \(P\) and is an exact equality of degree at most two.
With \(S=I^2P(z)\), these imply
\([\,\delta-q(1+\lambda)\,]S=0\).
If \(S=0\), the
[exact remainder pin](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-local-slack-fourth-twin/README.md#2-the-cubics-clock-shape-is-forced)
applies: \(z\notin\{0,v,u,w\}\), the exact affine fit holds,
and \(\rho(z)=0\). It gives \(m(Y)=Y^2-zY+z^2/5\).
The same [degree-eight ordinary contradiction](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-local-slack-fourth-twin/README.md#3-seven-ordinary-labels-contradict-the-exactly-pinned-shape)
uses only the five distinct numerical nonendpoint clock labels,
the old hull and the shape of \(m\); interchange the names of
its last two clocks so the other clock is here \(V\).
No distinctness or order assumption on intermediate completions
is used by that arithmetic.

If \(S\ne0\), the relation gives \(\delta=q(1+\lambda)\),
so \(q\) and \(1+\lambda\) are live. The same integration by parts
and checked interpolation identities imply
\[
 (1+\lambda)L=(Y-z)\ell,\qquad
 (1+\lambda)IQ_4=(Y-z)IP .
\]
The actual match at \(V\ne z\) thus gives \(k_V\ell(V)=IP(V)\);
substitute \(IP=\ell P+\delta P_0\) and the definition of \(q\)
to get \(\lambda q=\delta\), contradicting
\(\delta=(1+\lambda)q\ne0\).
This proves \((ND)_B\ne0\), including the slope \(\lambda=-1\).

In the fifth-twin chart, if there were no marked completion
between \(V\) and \(B\), then \((ND)_B=d_BD_B\) would be nonzero,
contradicting the literal \(\mathcal C D\) read
\(-d_B^2 D_B=0\) at this first possible supported row.
Thus there is at least one intervening completion.
At the first such literal row \(i\), with clock \(r\in\{v,u,w,V\}\),
the ordinary debut matches \(D\), while the marked completion
has a different normalized \(G/b\). Since \(r\ne z=B\),
\(D_i=b_i(r-z)(k_i-k_{\rm first})\ne0\).
The actual \(\mathcal C D\) read then forces its own
diagonal \(d_i=0\). This is a necessary restriction on
the surviving fifth-twin chronologies, not their exclusion.
