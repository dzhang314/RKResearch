It does not exclude slack at the actual origin, at another numerical

**13–14**. The deductions below are characteristic-zero and do not use an
order or positivity on the tableau field.

## 1. Actual reversed prefix and graded rows

\(u,v,w,V\) are distinct weighted nonendpoint clocks; the single
unweighted slack has \(Y_{s_*}=z=V\notin\{0,1,u,v,w\}\).
The actual \(G/b_{\rm wt}\) samples on \(u,v,w\) are all live, and
\(G_{s_*}\ne0\). The cubic-dirty row is later in reverse even if
its numerical clock coincides with another row's. No other
zero-weight or duplicate physical row occurs in this prefix.

the unique polynomial of degree at most three with zero constant

 N(YD_0)=G(Y\ell-\rho)-W(I(YP)),\qquad
 \rho=\frac{I^2P}{P}\bmod R,\qquad \deg\rho\le2 .

## 2. The cubic's clock shape is forced

The affine \(\ell\) is not zero: if it were, \(IP\) would
vanish at \(u,v,w\) and have a double root at 0, impossible
for its nonzero degree-at-most-four polynomial.
Thus \(\ell=\lambda(Y-z)\) with \(\lambda\ne0\).
If \(\deg P<3\), the difference \(IP-\ell P\) has degree
at most three but vanishes at \(0,u,v,w\), hence is zero.
That contradicts valuation at zero since \(\ell(0)=-\lambda z\ne0\):
taking the primitive raises the order there by one.
The same valuation argument gives a live coefficient of \(P_0\)
in the degree-four case.

Scale \(Y=zx\) and divide out the live cubic leader and
powers of \(z\). Write the scaled \(P\) as \(xm\),
\(m=x^2+rx+s\), with monic scaled old hull \(R_*\).
Let \(I_x\) be the primitive in \(x\) and
\(F=I_x(xm)/x\). The exact affine fit becomes
\[
 \delta R_*=F-\lambda(x-1)m,\qquad \delta=1/4-\lambda\ne0 .
\]
The constant relation and \(R_*(0)\ne0\) force \(s\ne0\).
The double primitive at 1 gives \(3+5r+10s=0\).
Now \(I_x^2(xm)-\rho_*(xm)\) is divisible by \(x(x-1)R_*\):
at the old three clocks it is zero by the true fit,
at 1 both the scaled second primitive and quadratic
\(\rho_*\) vanish, and at 0 the factor \(x\) is literal.
All five roots are distinct. Its degree is at most five,
so the quotient is a scalar, without requiring a live
degree-five leader. Dividing by \(x(x-1)\) and reducing
modulo monic \(m\) shows that the remainders of
\[
 F=I_x(xm)/x,\qquad H_*=I_x^2(xm)/(x(x-1))
\]
are proportional: both reductions are scalar multiples of that
of \(R_*\), with the \(F\) multiplier \(\delta\) known live.
This does not invert \(m(1)\) or assume \(m\) is squarefree.

With \(r=-3/5-2s\), their exact ascending coefficient vectors,
scaled by 300 and 1500 respectively, are
\[
 (50s^2+15s,\,-100s^2+15s-9),\qquad
 (100s^2-45s,\,-200s^2-45s+27).
\]
The determinant taken as \(F_1H_{*,0}-F_0H_{*,1}\) is
\(2250s^2(5s-1)\) in these integer scales. Therefore
\(s=1/5,r=-1\). Returning to the literal \(Y\) coordinate,
the monic part of the cubic after removal of its \(Y\) factor is
\(m_a(Y)=Y^2-zY+z^2/5\), and writing \(F_a=I(Ym_a)/Y\)
gives \(\delta R=F_a-\lambda(Y-z)m_a\).

## 3. Seven ordinary labels contradict the exactly pinned shape

independent of where any repeats occur after \(V\).

 \int_0^1 Y^{k+1}(1-Y)(Y-z)(Y-B)

 (13860 z^8-60984 z^7+113736 z^6-117054 z^5
   +72486z^4-27594z^3+6310z^2-800z+45).

Because the unknown vector has first coordinate 1 and \(z\ne0\),

are rational nonzero or explicitly live before division;
neither clock integrality nor a residue embedding is assumed.
