# A middle silent slack cannot share an old ordinary clock

**Result.** In the normalized DIRK11/13
[four-marked-pair plus slack chart](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-local-slack-chronology/README.md),
suppose the silent nondirty slack is between the third and fourth
distinct weighted nonendpoint reverse debuts. It cannot share the
numerical clock of one of the three old weighted debuts. The argument
covers both fifth-diagonal alternatives and every chronology of the
four marked completions. It works over characteristic zero given the
stated rooted normalization. It does not cover a middle slack at the
origin or at the fifth ordinary label, or any later slack. The real
unrestricted interval stays **13–14**.

## 1. The exact old-contact clock algebra

of \(v,u,w\), and \(V\) is the fourth distinct weighted label.

the zero-weight slack of live \(G\). Both \(V\) and the fifth ordinary

 ND_0=G\ell-W(IP),\quad

\]

## 2. The first two literal repeat gates

## 3. Actual fifth split and two distinct quadratics

At literal \(B\) the zero earlier \(D\) and global
\(\mathcal C D=0\) say \(d_B^2D_B=0\).
Also \(ND_B=d_BD_B=0\), for **either** diagonal case:
thus \(k_B L(B)=IQ_4(B)\) without an extra cut assumption.
Define the affine expression
\[
 k_x^*(q)=P(x)+qP_0(x)/(x-z),\quad x=V,B .
\]
The clocks \(V,B\) are distinct from the old \(z\), so this
expression never divides by a contact. \(k_V=k_V^*(q)\), and the
matched \(V\) polynomial
\(F_V(q)=k_V^*(q)L(V)-IQ_4(V)\) has degree at most two.

* **\(d_B\ne0\).** Here \(D_B=0\), so \(k_B=k_B^*(q)\), hence
  \(F_B(q)=k_B^*(q)L(B)-IQ_4(B)=0\).
  The fixed quadratic Sylvester determinant for \(F_V,F_B\) is a
  unit in the whole rank-48 model.
* **\(d_B=0\).** Let \(P_5=(Y-V)P_0\). The true weighted quintic
  fit \(W(IP_5)-NW(P_5)=G Q_5+\theta H\), \(\deg Q_5\le3\),
  uses \(5+4+2=11\). Its input vanishes through \(V\); at \(B\)
  its first nonzero input has zero diagonal. Hence the primitive
  error equals the literal weighted primitive throughout the
  prefix. The silent slack forces \(Q_5(z)=0\); its old weighted
  twin fixes \(\theta=IP_5(z)/H_*(z)\) with \(H_*=YJ_4\).
  Put \(E=IP_5-\theta H_*\) and \(Q_{\rm old}=E/P\bmod R\).
  The exact degree bound gives \(Q_5=Q_{\rm old}+\gamma R\)
  and the true \(V\) read is \(k_VQ_5(V)=E(V)\).
  Evaluating at \(B\) yields the affine polynomial
  \[
  V_B^*(q):=k_VQ_5(B)
     = k_V^*(q)\left[Q_{\rm old}(B)-
             Q_{\rm old}(V)\frac{R(B)}{R(V)}\right]
       +E(V)\frac{R(B)}{R(V)} .
  \]
  The necessary \(B\) reads \(k_BQ_5(B)=E(B)\) and
  \(k_B L(B)=IQ_4(B)\) then imply
  \(G_B(q)=k_V^*(q)E(B)L(B)-V_B^*(q)IQ_4(B)=0\).
  We did not divide by \(Q_5(B)\), either diagonal, or a leading
  coefficient of \(Q_5\). The fixed quadratic determinant for
  \(F_V,G_B\) is a unit in exactly the same whole rank-48 model.

In both cases the fixed \(4\times4\) determinant is computed
on the coefficient columns of \(F_V,qF_V,F,qF\), in ascending
powers of the formal unknown \(q\).
A common root gives a nonzero left vector
\((1,q,q^2,q^3)\), even if an apparent quadratic leader has
dropped. Thus either unit contradicts the actual scalar root.
