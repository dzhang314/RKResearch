# Repeat caps after the live fourth debut of a middle silent slack

**Results.** Work in the normalized characteristic-zero DIRK11/13
[four-marked-pair plus slack chart](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-local-slack-chronology/README.md)
with the slack strictly between the third and fourth distinct
weighted nonendpoint reverse debuts. Take either remaining contact:
the slack's numerical clock is the fifth ordinary label \(z=B\),
or the origin \(z=1\). These are the
[live-fourth cases](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-local-slack-fifth-twin/README.md).
The first marked completion between fourth \(V\) and fifth \(B\)
cannot repeat \(V\) itself. In the fifth-twin chart there is
**exactly one** intervening marked completion, at one of the old
three distinct labels, and its diagonal is zero. In the origin
chart there are at most two intervening completions; if there are
two, \(V\) is the unmarked singleton (\(G_V=0\)) and \(B\) is marked.
These are necessary chronologies only. They do not exclude the
remaining cases or alter the unrestricted real interval **13–14**.

## 1. Common paid names and repeated fourth exclusion

zero-constant primitive \(I\), \(W(U)=b_{\rm wt}U(Y)\),
and the actual reflected cubic \(G\) in the same monic gauge
as the cubic fit \(P=Ym\) of the old three \(G/b_{\rm wt}\) values.
The prefix is \(T,v,u,w,s_*,V\); all three old \(G\)'s and the
silent slack \(G_{s_*}\) are live. The \(z\) of the slack is
distinct from \(v,u,w,V,0\), and the fifth distinct weighted
nonendpoint debut is \(B\). The actual dirty and origin rows
are later than \(B\) in this reverse prefix. Once \(V\) has
debuted, before \(B\) only marked completions can intervene.
No ordinary weights are moved between physical pair members.

Write \(R=(Y-v)(Y-u)(Y-w)\), \(P_0=YR\). Retain the true fits
\[
 D_0=G-W(P),\quad ND_0=G\ell-W(IP),\quad
 NW(P_0)=W(IP_0)-GW_0,\quad
 \mathcal C D_0=0,\quad \mathcal C=YN-NY-N^2 .
\]
The actual slack has diagonal zero and
\(\ell(z)=W_0(z)=\rho(z)=0\), where
\(\rho=I^2P/P\bmod R\).
Thus \(\ell=\lambda(Y-z)\) with \(\lambda\ne0\), and
\(IP=\ell P+\delta P_0,\ \delta=1/4-\lambda\ne0\).
The prior live-fourth proof excluded \(\lambda=-1\)
by the ordinary clock pencil in both these contacts.
Let \(k_i=G_i/b_i\) for a weighted row. The actual quartic
\[
 q=\frac{(V-z)(k_V-P(V))}{P_0(V)},\quad
 Q_4=(Y-z)P+qP_0,\quad D=(Y-z)G-W(Q_4)

is zero through \(V\) and satisfies, by the same paid proof,
\[
 \mathcal C D=0,\qquad
 ND=G L-W(IQ_4),\quad L=(Y-z)\ell-\rho+qW_0 .
\]
At the first marked completion \(i\) of clock \(r\) before \(B\),
the earlier \(D\) entries vanish, and
\(D_i=b_i(r-z)(k_i-k_{\rm first})\ne0\).
Indeed the distinct \(G/b\) values are from the exact signed pair,
and \(r\) is a nonendpoint marked site, distinct from \(z\).
The literal diagonal of \(\mathcal C D\) at this first support
is \(-d_i^2D_i=0\), so \(d_i=0\).

Suppose \(r=V\). For the original fourth row and its putative
repeat, let
\[
 n_j=k_j\ell(V)-IP(V),\qquad
 t_j=IP_0(V)-k_jW_0(V),\qquad
 a_V=d_V,\quad a_i=N_{i,V}b_V/b_i .

Their \(NW(P_0)\) fits are \(t_j=a_jP_0(V)\): the first nonzero

On \(D_0\) the only possible earlier nonzero inputs are slack

As the original \(V\) and its repeat share a clock, their literal

\[
 (V-z)N_{j,s_*}G_{s_*}/b_j = a_j n_V .
\]
This uses the first row's own diagonal for \(j=V\) and the

\[
 n_j=a_j[(k_V-P(V))+n_V/(V-z)] .

Their \(2\times2\) determinant with the \(t_j\) reads vanishes.
Comparing the two different \(k_j\)'s, without a diagonal

\[
 \ell(V)IP_0(V)=IP(V)W_0(V).
\]
This same relation already holds at all three old weighted
sites by the true primitive fits. Write \(W_0=(Y-z)w_1\).
The degree-at-most-four polynomial
\((IP/Y)w_1-\lambda(IP_0/Y)\) vanishes at those four distinct

Exactly the differentiating argument in the live-fourth packet
now gives \(\lambda=-1\), already excluded. Thus the first
marked completion, if any, is some \(r\in\{v,u,w\}\).

## 2. A second completion forces the true weighted quintic to commute

At the first completion \(r\), \((ND)_i=d_iD_i=0\).
At its original mate also \((ND)_{\rm first}=0\).
The different \(k\)'s in their fits give \(L(r)=IQ_4(r)=0\);
we already have \(L(z)=0\).
If \(L\) were identically zero, \(IQ_4\) would vanish at \(v,u,w,V\)
and have a double root at zero since \(Q_4(0)=0\).
Its degree is at most five, so \(IQ_4=0\), contradicting the live
old sample \(Q_4(x)=(x-z)P(x)\). Thus \(L=a(Y-z)(Y-r)\)
with \(a\ne0\).
Put \(P_5=(Y-V)P_0\). At the four old-and-fourth ordinary
samples \(IQ_4=a(Y-r)Q_4\) from their true \(ND=0\) and
\(D=0\); the difference also vanishes at zero. Degree comparison
then gives \(IQ_4=a(Y-r)Q_4+\eta P_5\) for some scalar \(\eta\).
In particular
\[
 ND=a(Y-r)D-\eta W(P_5).
\]

Define \(E=(Y-r)D\). It is zero through the first repeat.
Using \(\mathcal C D=0\) in the preceding identity gives
\[
 (1+a)NE=(Y-r)ND+\eta NW(P_5).
\]
If there is a second completion \(s\) before \(B\), it is the
next literal row; the \(Y=s\) member belongs to a different
marked pair from \(r\). The entry \(E_s=(s-r)D_s\) is live.
Both \(W(P_5)\) and \(NW(P_5)\) are zero through this entry,
since their weighted donors have only the ordinary labels
\(0,v,u,w,V\). Thus the literal equation
\[
 (1+a)d_s E_s=a(s-r)E_s
\]
forces \(d_s\ne0\), including the otherwise apparent
\(1+a=0\) exceptional case (which cannot occur here).
Hence \((\mathcal C E)_s=-d_s^2E_s\ne0\).

The whole actual grade-seven reverse deficit space is
\[
 Z_7=E_3^\perp\cap\{T=0\}=G\mathcal P_4(Y)+H\mathcal P_1(Y).
\]
Indeed \(E_3=\mathcal P_3(c)+K g\) is the true dimension-five
space from \(C(2)\) and the actual single nonzero cubic direction.
The terminal cardinal is independent of that low space, so \(Z_7\)
has dimension seven. The five multiples of \(G\) are paid here
by \(G\in E_7^\perp\), and \(H\mathcal P_1\) is paid since
\(H\in E_4^\perp\). Both are terminal-zero.
They are independent by numerical class sums: \(G\)'s sums are
zero and \(H\) has live sums at every weighted nonendpoint label.
The five \(G\) clocks are distinct even with the allowed physical

Both \(\mathcal C E\) and \(\mathcal C W(P_5)\) belong to this
\(Z_7\). The charge for the weighted term is \(5+5+1=11\)
on \((CA-AC-A^2)E_3\subset F_5\); the two marks on \(G\)
still pair only with \(E_7\).

Factor out \(Y-r\); the remaining polynomials have degrees
three and zero. On the four other clocks among \(v,u,w,z,V\)
this row is zero. If all four \(G\)'s are live, the coefficient
of \(H\) is injective (a zero coefficient leaves four roots of
a cubic). If \(G_V=0\), its nonzero \(H_V\) instead kills the

Now the second repeat has \((\mathcal C E)_s\ne0\),
whereas \((\mathcal C W(P_5))_s=0\) by zero input. In the same

## 3. Paid quintic and exact ratio separation

The actual weighted primitive of \(P_5\) has error
\[
 W(IP_5)-NW(P_5)=G Q_5+\theta H,\qquad \deg Q_5\le3
\]
by \(5+4+2=11\). At every two-member completion before \(B\)
the whole weighted input is still zero; comparing their
different normalized \(G/b\)'s gives \(Q_5=0\) at that clock,
and \(IP_5/H_*=\theta\), with \(H_*(Y)=YJ_4(Y)\).
At the silent slack also \(Q_5(z)=0\).

* At the fifth twin \(B=z\), the actual \(B\) read gives
  \(IP_5(B)/H_*(B)=\theta\) as well, since \(Q_5(B)=0\).
* At the origin slack \(z=1\), if \(B\) is the unmarked
  singleton then \(G_B=0\), giving the same ratio at \(B\).
  If there are at least three intervening completions,
  their distinct three roots together with the slack root
  force \(Q_5=0\) identically, again giving the ratio at \(B\).

In each of these proposed branches the ratio agrees at \(B\) and
at two distinct ordinary clocks among \(v,u,w,V\).
We show that even this scalar condition is impossible.
Ordinary quadrature gives the monic five-clock hull
\(S_5=(J_5+\alpha J_4)/132\). Indeed \(S_5\) is orthogonal
to degree at most three under \(Y(1-Y)dY\), the fixed rational
Gram matrix is invertible, and the two displayed Jacobi
polynomials span its degree-at-most-five orthogonal space.
The script verifies this rational Gram, the orthogonality,
and their coprimality, hence \(J_4(B)\ne0\).

Set, using the actual fifth clock \(B\) (not necessarily \(z\)),
\[
 F(Y)=\frac{J_5(Y)J_4(B)-J_4(Y)J_5(B)}{Y-B},\quad
 L_5(Y)=I(YF)/Y,\quad
 Q(Y)=\frac{L_5(Y)J_4(B)-J_4(Y)L_5(B)}{Y-B}.
\]
Here \(F/(132J_4(B))\) is the monic four-clock complement of \(B\).
Thus \(P_5=YF/(132J_4(B))\), and the two equal ratios
give two distinct common roots of the two quartics \(F,Q\).
Both leaders are live at \(J_4(B)\ne0\).
Their resultant is zero, and the degree-one polynomial obtained
by three exact pseudodivisions must vanish identically: it is a
polynomial combination of the quartics and would have two
distinct roots. The exact gcd of the resultant and its two
coefficients is proportional to \((B-1)^4J_4(B)^4\).
This contradicts the nonendpoint \(B\) and \(J_4(B)\ne0\).
Pseudodivision identities are checked before specialization,
so none of their possible intermediate pivots has been inverted.

This rules out two completions for the fifth twin, or for
the origin slack with \(B\) unmarked; it rules out three in
the remaining origin case. The previous live-fourth packet
proved that a fifth twin must have at least one completion,
so exactly one old marked completion remains there.
For the origin slack, if two occur then \(B\) is marked,
and \(V\) is the sole available unmarked label because the old
three \(G\)'s are live. The manuscript does not assert that
any surviving chronology is realized.
