# The normalized no-late invisible-six alpha-one chart is empty

**Result.** Over a characteristic-zero coefficient field, no normalized
invisible-six no-late DIRK11/13 candidate has \(\alpha=1\), the single
physical ghost in the first open reverse gap. This audit covers all three
middle counts, both reciprocal branches, and every possible forward carrier
degree. It does not exclude any other normalization or chronology, and the
unrestricted real DIRK11 stage interval is still **13–14**.

## 1. Inputs and the count/degree split

Use the actual order-eleven lower triangular tableau, signed
\(r=0,\ d=z_{\rm count}=6\) support and true paid ranks of the
[no-late census](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-six-no-late/README.md)
and [forward masks](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-six-prefix/README.md).
There are six zero-weight ghost rows with distinct numerical clocks and
live cubic heads, and seven distinct clean clocks, including the actual
endpoint rows. The five ordinary nonendpoint weights are live. In literal
reverse order the rows are
\[
 T,v,u,w,z,V,[\beta\text{ ghosts}],B,[\gamma\text{ ghosts}],O .
\]
Here \(z\) is the first-gap ghost. The older three regular \(v,u,w\)
have live reverse cubic \(G\), and the no-late count supplies
\(\gamma\ge3\), \(1+\beta+\gamma=6\). This means exactly
\((\beta,\gamma)=(0,5),(1,4),(2,3)\). Coincidence of a ghost clock
with an unrelated clean clock is not removed from the normalized chart.

The actual forward cubic \(g\) has profile \(U\) on the six ghosts,
where \(U\ne0\) and
\(\deg U\le3\). This is a conclusion from the grade-six decomposition,
not a polynomial ansatz. The zero polynomial is inadmissible since the
ghost values are live. With \(\phi=U(c)-g\), the paid barycentric
identity is \(G\phi=\tau H_{\rm bary}\).
At the old three regular rows \(\phi=U(c)\) and \(G\) is live.
If \(\tau=0\), all three distinct old forward sites are roots.
Thus \(U\) must have degree exactly three and, after one live
rescaling, \(U(X)=-R(1-X)\) for the old reverse hull
\(R(Y)=(Y-v)(Y-u)(Y-w)\). The nonold clean sites \(V,B,O\)
have live \(U\), hence \(G\) zero; the terminal \(G_T=0\)
is actual. At every ghost \(U\) is live, so its clock cannot be
an old site. This excludes only those three contacts, not contacts
with \(V,B,0,1\). In particular the nine-support-clock barycentric
argument in the zero-multiplier proofs really uses nine distinct
clocks, even when such a nonold contact occurs.

For **any nonconstant** profile the true grade-five primitive of
\(C\phi\) gives \(I_X(XU)+UQ_2=0\) at each \(\gamma\) initial forward
ghost, with \(\deg Q_2\le2\). All inclusive donors of this column
are zero there (including the origin, multiplied by its zero clock).
If \(\gamma>\deg U+2\), the polynomial must vanish identically.
Then \(U\mid I_X(XU)\) forces \(U\) to be a monomial: trailing and
ordinary degrees give quotient a multiple of \(X^2\), and at each
live coefficient of \(U\) that multiple is \(1/(i+2)\).
This contradicts the origin liveness of a positive-degree carrier
(or the two paid first-clean primitive marks).
Thus \(\gamma\le\deg U+2\) whenever \(\deg U\ge1\).
Nothing here assumes a natural ordering of the coefficient field.

## 5. Independent zero-reciprocal prefix companion

The zero case also has a useful complementary prefix injection.
In \(Z_6=G\mathcal P_3(Y)+K_{\rm coeff}H_{\rm bary}\), take a
row \(GQ+\theta H_{\rm bary}\) with zero literal prefix through \(V\)
under \(\tau=0\). At \(V\), \(G_V=0\) and \(H_{{\rm bary},V}\ne0\),
so \(\theta=0\). Then \(Q\) is zero at the three old regular
clocks and \(z\), all distinct with live \(G\). Thus \(Q=0\).
No reciprocal division by \(G_V\), diagonal, or unrelated ghost–clean
difference is used. The native module checks this exact scalar
injection as well as the zero-reciprocal cubic factor and the count split.

For example in the actual weighted prefix set \(P=Ym,\ P_0=YR\),
\(K_*=(V-z)(G_V/b_V-P(V))/P_0(V)\),
\(Q_4=(Y-z)P+K_*P_0\), and \(D=(Y-z)G-W(Q_4)\).
Its zero prefix through \(V\) follows from old matching, the
mark at \(z\), and the chosen \(V\) fit; \(K_*\) uses no \(1/G_V\).
The transposed commutator \(\mathcal C=YN-NY-N^2\) sends this
particular \(D\) into \(Z_6\). For the \(W(Q_4)\) term this is the
actual charge \(4+6+1=11\); for the \(G\)-term it is the extra marked
grade in \(E_7\), annihilated by \(G\). Its terminal entry vanishes
and the literal triangular prefix stays zero. The new injectivity
therefore gives \(\mathcal C D=0\).
When \(\beta\ge1\), the next literal entry is
\(D_x=(x-z)G_x\ne0\), so the actual commutator diagonal
\(-d_x^2\) gives \(d_x=0\). The same old matching
\(ND=GL-W(IQ_4)\) then gives the true matched quadratic
\(L=\lambda(Y-z)(Y-x)\), \(\lambda\ne0\): if \(L=0\)
the primitive \(IQ_4\) vanishes at \(0,v,u,w,V\) and is a multiple
of \((Y-V)YR\); the derivative at zero forces it to vanish
identically, contradicting the old \(Q_4\) samples.
At \(\beta=0\) the next entry is \(-b_BQ_4(B)\), instead giving the
undivided \(d_B^2 Q_4(B)=0\), not a zero diagonal when that
input vanishes. None of this optional complementary reasoning is
needed as an input to the older exact zero-multiplier replays.
