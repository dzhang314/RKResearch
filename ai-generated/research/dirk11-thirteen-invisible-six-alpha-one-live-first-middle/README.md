## 1. Two actual formulas for the first middle diagonal

Match \(m=G/(Y b_{\rm wt})\) on the three old sites,
set \(P=Ym\), \(P_1=YP-\kappa P_0\),
\(\kappa=[Y^2]m\), and use
\[
 \ell=IP/P\bmod R,quad \rho=I^2P/P\bmod R,quad
 \rho_1=Y\ell-\rho-\kappa W_0.
\]
The genuine unmarked and marked weighted cubic errors
give these old fits. Here \(\ell\) is affine, and
\(W(IP_0)-NW(P_0)=G W_0\) is the quartic error.
The first ghost gives \(W_0(z)=\rho(z)=0\) and \(d_z=0\).

At \(x\) the two true comparisons \(N(G-W(P))\)
and \(N(YG-W(P_1))\) have only the unmatched inclusive
donors \(z,V,x\). With \(k=G_V/b_V\),
\(r_x=N_{x,V}b_V/G_x\), and \(e=N_{x,z}G_z/G_x\),
their reads are
\[
 \ell(x)=d_x+e+r_x(k-P(V)),\qquad
 \rho_1(x)=x d_x+z e+r_x(Vk-P_1(V)).
\]
The quartic at \(x\) gives
\(r_x P_0(V)=-W_0(x)\): only \(V\) can be a nonzero
weighted input, and the output weight is zero.
Thus with \(K_*=(V-z)(k-P(V))/P_0(V)\), subtraction
cancels the \(\kappa\) terms and gives
\[
 (x-z)d_x=(x-z)\ell(x)-\rho(x)+W_0(x)K_*.
\]

Z_6=G\mathcal P_3+H_{\rm bary}K_{\rm coeff}\)

\((x-V-d_V-d_x)N_{x,V}\). Therefore
\((x-V-d_V-d_x)W_0(x)+Q_c(x)=0\).

Set \(F=W_0/(Y-z)\), \(\rho_* =\rho/(Y-z)\),

## 2. Exact closure of the zero quartic signal

If \(W_0(x)=0\), its old inverse fit is not identically
zero: otherwise the degree-five \(IP_0\) and its
derivative would vanish at all three old roots.
So \(W_0\) is quadratic with distinct roots \(z,x\),
and its quartic read gives \(N_{x,V}=0\).
The actual commutator also gives \(Q_c(x)=0\).

By the ordinary determinant in the
[two-ghost debit](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-six-alpha-two-debit/README.md#1-a-nonzero-old-determinant),
\(W_0,Q_0,M\) are independent as quadratics,
where \(Q_0=(L_1/m)\bmod R\) and
\(M=(J_4/m)\bmod R\). Evaluation at \(z,x\) has
kernel the line of \(W_0\), so the evaluations of
\(Q_0,M\) are independent. The genuine quintic
error for \(P_5=(Y-V)P_0\) kills \(z,x\) because its
entire weighted prefix is zero there. Its old fit
is \(Q_0-VW_0-\theta' M+\nu R\). Thus \(R,M\)
have independent evaluations at \(z,x\) as well.
It follows that the two coefficients of \(Q_c\)
in their span are zero. The full literal row
\(\mathcal R^{\mathsf T}W(P_0)\) vanishes, and at
\(V\) this gives \(d_V=0\).

The genuine \(Q_5\) is \(W_0\) times an affine
polynomial. Multiplying by \(m\) at the old three

\(\det(J_4,L_0,YL_0,L_1)\bmod (Y-V)R=0\).

[corrected beta-one alpha-two proof](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-six-alpha-two-live-cubic-beta-one/README.md).
