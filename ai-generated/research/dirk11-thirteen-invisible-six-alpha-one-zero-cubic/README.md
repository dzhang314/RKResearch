# The remaining alpha-one zero-multiplier cubic counts are impossible

**Result.** In the original normalized no-late invisible-six
DIRK11/13 census, a cubic carrier and \(\tau=0\) cannot have
\((\alpha,\beta,\gamma)=(1,1,4)\) or \((1,2,3)\).
This holds over every characteristic-zero field, with no
ordering or sign assumptions. Together with the earlier
\((1,0,5)\) exclusion this removes the alpha-one cubic
zero-multiplier chart. This is not a general thirteen-stage
exclusion. The unrestricted real interval remains **13–14**.

Use the literal normalized hypotheses and tree spaces of the
[early ghost](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-six-alpha1-five-zero/README.md),
[old profile census](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-six-early-profile-census/README.md),
and [forward mask](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-six-prefix/README.md)
papers. In particular there are six distinct individually
live cubic-dirty ghost clocks, all of weight zero, and seven
distinct ordinary clean clocks. The five nonendpoint
ordinary weights are live; no division by the origin weight
will be made. In literal reverse chronology the rows are:
\[
 T,v,u,w,\ z,\ V,\ x_1,\ldots,x_\beta,\ B,\ [\gamma],\ O.
\]
Here \(T\) and \(O\) are the true terminal and origin.
Use only the displayed order; \(x_i\) are physical middle
ghost rows. Write \(Y=1-c\) for the reverse clock,
\(N=A^{\mathsf T}\) in reverse order, \(W(P)=b_{\rm wt}P(Y)\),
and let \(I\) denote the zero-constant primitive.

The three old ordinary roots have hull
\(R(Y)=Y^3+c_2Y^2+c_1Y+c_0\). In forward coordinates set
\(U(X)=-R(1-X)\), \(a=1-V\), \(b=1-B\), and
\(\phi=U(c)-g\). These follow from the proved reciprocal
zero-head description: \(U\) is the old forward cubic,
\(G_V=G_B=G_O=G_T=0\), and the support of the true reverse
\(G\) consists of the three old ordinary and the six ghost
rows. Ghost clocks cannot coincide with any old ordinary
clock, because \(U\) is live at the ghosts. Contacts of
ghosts with \(V,B,0,1\) are **not** ruled out by that
argument and are kept in every proof below.

## 1. A common paid hull and the actual adjacent fourth-clean mark

Let \(m\) be the old quadratic-or-lower interpolant of
\(G_r/(r\,b_r)\) at the three old reverse clocks. Every
old value of \(m\) is live. Set
\[
 K=J_4(Y)(Y-1)(Y-V)(Y-B),\qquad P_0=YR.
\]
In the old cubic algebra the reverse six-ghost hull \(R_6\)
(up to one live scalar) has residue \(K/m\).
Indeed the nine distinct support coordinates of \(G\) and
its eight moment vanishings make it the barycentric row of
the nine-root hull \(R R_6\), up to scale. The true clean
head \(H_{\rm bary}\) is the barycentric row on the old
three and \(V,B,1\), and on nonendpoint clean rows its
weighted ratio is \(H_{\rm bary}/b_{\rm wt}=YJ_4(Y)\)
in a common gauge. Comparing the two inverse derivatives
at an old root cancels \(R'\), giving the asserted identity.
Both \(K\) and \(m\) are units in this split old algebra.

Use the actual grade-five input \(C(C-b)\phi\).
All inclusive donors to the first five **forward** ghosts
are killed by the mask. These ghosts occur before \(a\),
and only the origin and \(b\) can be clean
donors there. Its value and its primitive residual at \(b\)
fix the constant terminal-head multiplier. With
\(H=XJ_4(X)\), put
\[
 F_b=I[X(X-b)U],\qquad K_b=H(b)F_b-F_b(b)H .
\]
The \(F_6\) shape
\(g\mathcal P_3+(e_T-H_T)K_{\rm coeff}\) shows that

affine polynomial, possibly zero. The omitted sixth ghost

 E_6=\det\big(K,YK,(Y-z)mK_b(1-Y)\big)\bmod R=0.          \tag{1}
\]
Each column is reduced modulo \(R\) before taking the

For contrast, the naive grade-seven continuation is
**not independent** on \(\tau=0\). The column

## 2. One middle ghost: a true quintic divided difference

Use the actual reverse quintic \(P_5=(Y-V)P_0\). Its
primitive error is \(W(IP_5)-NW(P_5)=GQ_5+\theta H_{\rm bary}\)
with \(\deg Q_5\le3\) by the grade-six space \(Z_6\).
Through all old and middle rows every weighted input is
zero: the old ordinary roots, terminal, and \(V\) annihilate
\(P_5\), and each ghost weight vanishes. At \(V\),
\(G_V=0\) and the weighted clean head is live, hence
\(\theta=IP_5(V)/(VJ_4(V))\).
Let
\[
 D_5=J_4(V)(IP_5/Y)-(IP_5(V)/V)J_4 .
\]
On the old sites the reduced residue of \(Q_5\) is
\(D_5/(mJ_4(V))\). A common live scale clears \(J_4(V)\)
and the old multiplication norm of \(m\), leaving
\(Q^0=(\operatorname{adj}(m) D_5)\bmod R\).
Here \(\operatorname{adj}(m)\) denotes the polynomial
for the adjugate of the three-dimensional multiplication
map evaluated on 1. It satisfies
\(m\operatorname{adj}(m)=\operatorname{Norm}(m)\pmod R\).
For \(\beta=1\), \(Q_5(z)=Q_5(x_1)=0\), with \(x_1\ne z\).
Since \(R(z)\ne0\), the cubic
\[
 Q^\#=q^3 R(z)Q^0 - q^3 Q^0(z)R
\]
is a known live scalar multiple of \(Q_5\) (also allowing
the zero polynomial). All coefficients in this formula are
polynomial in \(p,q\), since \(\deg Q^0\le2\).
If \(Q^\#=c'_0+c'_1Y+c'_2Y^2+c'_3Y^3\), the divided
difference at \(z\), times \(q^2\), has coefficients
\[
 d_0=q^2 c'_1+p q c'_2+p^2c'_3,\quad
 d_1=q^2c'_2+p q c'_3,\quad d_2=q^2c'_3 .
\]
It vanishes at \(x_1\), exactly by the two distinct physical
ghost clocks.

Separately, the true grade-five primitive
\(A(C\phi)=J+gQ_2\) with \(J=I(XU)\) is zero at the
four initial forward ghosts before \(b\). Thus the
degree-at-most-five polynomial \(J+UQ_2\) is their hull
times an affine polynomial. The other forward ghosts
are exactly the reflected \(z,x_1\). On the old residues
this gives
\[
 \det\big(K,YK,(Y-z)(Y-x_1)mJ(1-Y)\big)\bmod R=0 .
\]
Compute \(g_1,g_0\) from the determinants with third
columns \((qY-p)mJ(1-Y)\) and
\(Y(qY-p)mJ(1-Y)\). Then the actual linear gate is
\(g_0=x_1g_1\). The joint divided-difference test is
\[
 B_1=d_0g_1^2+d_1g_0g_1+d_2g_0^2=0.                         \tag{3}
\]
This uses no division by \(g_1\), the quintic leader
or a weight on a ghost. In particular it includes
\(g_0=g_1=0\), or \(Q^\#=0\), in the exact calculation.

## 3. Two middle ghosts: an actual weighted commutator

All actual commutator inputs through \(x_2\) are zero

\(\eta(Y-V)\). At \(B\) the first nonzero input

\[
 \eta J_4(B)=-d_B^2 R(B),\qquad d_B b U(b)=J(b).

If \(Q_5=0\) the first two residue columns vanish and

\[
 B_2=J(b)^2\det(D_5,YD_5,(Y-V)J_4)\bmod R=0.              \tag{4}

It covers the zero \(d_B\) alternative without an
additional gate, and does not assume distinguishability
of a ghost clock from a regular non-old clock.
