# The first primitive pin transfers to the live zero-fifth charts

**Results.** Over a characteristic-zero field, the following
normalized no-late invisible-six DIRK11/13 **cubic, live reciprocal**
charts are empty:
* \((\alpha,\beta,\gamma)=(1,2,3)\), for every old fit;
* \((\alpha,\beta,\gamma)=(1,1,4)\) with \(d_B=0\), including
  every alternate old fit.

The first statement uses the existing universal cubic beta-two
zero-fifth-diagonal theorem, not the beta-zero chronology. The
second assumes that literal diagonal is zero and does not assert
that it always is. This result transfers only a first primitive
pin, not a second reverse read across a nonexistent gap. It does
not decide beta-one nonzero-fifth alternate fits, quadratic carriers,
other normalization charts, or the unrestricted interval **13–14**.

## 1. Two independent prefixes, both before the variable middle donors

Use forward \(C=\operatorname{diag}(c)\), reverse
\(Y=1-c\), and \(N=A^{\mathsf T}\) in *literal reverse*
row order. The normalized chronology is
\[
 T,v,u,w,z,V,[\beta\text{ ghosts}],B,[\gamma\text{ ghosts}],O
       \quad\text{in reverse}.
\]
The forward first nonorigin clean row is \(b=1-B\).
The actual old three regular clocks and \(V,B\) are distinct
nonendpoint ordinary labels with live weights, and the
ghosts each have zero weight. Numerical coincidences
between a ghost and an unrelated ordinary row are not
assumed absent. The live reciprocal normalization gives
\(G/b_{\rm wt}=YJ_4/U_r\) at the regular sites, with \(U_r\)
the reflected carrier.

**Forward prefix at \(b\).** With the true
\(\phi=U_f(c)-g\), \(U_f(X)=U_r(1-X)\), the
[actual grade-five forward mask](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-six-prefix/README.md#1-a-true-forward-head-and-its-first-two-primitive-errors)
pays for
\[
 A(C\phi)-I_X[XU_f](c)\in F_5=g\mathcal P_2(c).
\]
Both sides are actual columns of the original rooted order
calculus: the pure polynomial primitive error is paid at
grade five and the marked \(g\) input remains in \(F_5\).
At \(b\), prior forward rows are only \(O\) and the
\(\gamma\) initial forward ghosts. The initial ghosts
have \(\phi=0\); the origin factor in \(C\phi\) kills
the \(O\) donor whether or not its output weight is zero.
The own diagonal reads \(d_B b U_f(b)\), and the
residual \(g\mathcal P_2\) vanishes at this clean row.
Thus \(d_B=0\) gives the genuine affine primitive
\[
 J_b(t)=I_X[XU_f](b)=\int_B^1(1-Y)U_r(Y)\,dY=0 .
\]
This uses neither the count of middle ghosts nor the
beta-zero five-prefix hull; the middle ghosts occur
**after** \(b\) in forward chronology. No terminal-profile
correction from \(F_6\) is present in this grade-five read.

**Reverse prefix at \(V\).** Let
\(R=(Y-v)(Y-u)(Y-w)\), \(P=Ym\), \(P_0=YR\) and
\(P_1=YP-\kappa P_0\), \(\kappa=[Y^2]m\).
Both \(P,P_1\) have degree at most three and match the
respective old scaled heads; the quartic \(P_0\) is zero
on the old ordinary and terminal sites. The
[actual old primitive fits](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-six-alpha1-five-zero/README.md#1-the-actual-first-reverse-gap)
pay in \(Z_4=G\mathcal P_1\) for the first cubic,
and in \(Z_5=G\mathcal P_2\) for the marked cubic and
old quartic. Using \(W(f)=b_{\rm wt}f(Y)\), the whole
row identities are
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 N(G-W(P))&=G\ell-W(IP),\\
 N(YG-W(P_1))&=G(Y\ell-\rho-\kappa W_0)-W(IP_1),\\
 NW(P_0)&=W(IP_0)-GW_0 .
 \end{aligned}
\]
The marked polynomial uses the exact
\(IP_1=YIP-I^2P-\kappa IP_0\). These paid fits use
only the old exact samples to identify their polynomial
coefficients, not a first-middle ghost, and they do not
divide by \(1+\ell_1\).

At \(V\) put \(k=G_V/b_V\),
\[
 K_*=(V-z)(k-P(V))/P_0(V),\quad
 Q_4=(Y-z)P+K_*P_0,\quad D=(Y-z)G-W(Q_4).
\]
The denominator \(P_0(V)\) is live. The actual
\(D\) is zero through \(V\): the terminal and old
rows match, \(Y_z-z=0\) and \(b_z=0\) kill the
first-gap ghost, and the chosen \(K_*\) makes the
ordinary read at \(V\) match. These facts also hold at
\(V=z\), when \(K_*=0\). Lower triangularity gives
\((ND)_V=0\) without a diagonal condition or any
knowledge of \(D_B\). From the displayed identities,
combining the marked cubic, minus \(z\) times the first,
minus \((\kappa+K_*)\) times the quartic, gives the
exact row formula
\[
 ND=G L_{\rm match}-W(IQ_4),\qquad
 L_{\rm match}=(Y-z)\ell-\rho+K_*W_0 .
\]
Consequently \((IQ_4)(V)=k L_{\rm match}(V)\).
This uses only indices through \(V\), **before**
any of the variable middle ghost rows. It does not use
\(\mathcal C D=0\), the first-middle pivot,
\((ND)_B=0\), or a live fourth diagonal.

## 2. Exact common clearing and independent first-pin replay

In the old adjugate gauge \(U=nU_r=\widehat M+tR\)
with live \(n\), the reflected integral remains
\(J_b(t)=j_0+j_1t\) up to the same live factor.
Put \(z=p/q\), \(Z=qY-p\), and define
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 c_q&=P_0(V)U(V),\
 k_q&=Z(V)[n V J_4(V)-P(V)U(V)],\
 Q^\#&=c_q ZP+k_qP_0,\
 L^\#&=c_q(Z\widehat\ell-q\widehat\rho)+k_q\widehat W_0 .
 \end{aligned}
\]
Then \(Q^\#=c_q qQ_4\) and
\(L^\#=c_q q n L_{\rm match}\).
The literal first reverse primitive gives the exact
degree-at-most-two row
\[
 H_V(t)=U(V)(I Q^\#)(V)-V J_4(V)L^\#(V)=0.
\]
This is precisely the first gate in the beta-zero finite
engines in their unscaled reverse units. Neither
\(H_B\) nor its vanishing is used.
Clearing a common root of \(H_V=h_0+h_1t+h_2t^2\)
and \(J_b=j_0+j_1t\) gives the necessary scalar
\[
 P_V=j_1^2 h_0-j_0j_1h_1+j_0^2 h_2=0 .
\]
This fixed homogeneous quadratic uses no division by
\(j_1\) and retains all apparent leading-degree losses.

The exact old ordinary/profile lifts are kept intact;

## 3. Exhaustive old-profile ingress for the two stated conclusions

The old-profile census and its affine resultant apply
before the middle count. The following audit explicitly
separates each closure from the transferred calculation.

1. **Monomials and finite contacts.** The old actual
   monomial \(m=Y\) would force \(z=0\) with nonzero
   old ghost-gate numerator \(-3c_0^2/10\), impossible.
   \(m=1,Y^2\), \(c_2s=c_0\) when \(rs\ne0\), and
   the lost ratio \(c_1+6s=0\) of the \(F_n\) branch
   have the [finite old lifts](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-six-alpha-one-beta-zero-finite-contacts/README.md),
   all now checked by the first pin alone. These profiles
   were also treated separately for beta one/two in the
   [older finite proof](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-six-alpha-one-live-cubic/README.md).
   The mixed \(r=0\) contact yields \(\ell_1=-1\).
2. **Slope and direct profiles.** Old slope \(-1\)
   is excluded in beta one/two by the
   [first-ghost bridge](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-six-alpha-one-slope-minus-one-bridge/README.md)
   and its [direct-ratio slope exclusion](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-six-alpha-one-direct-fn-minus-one/README.md).
   This covers both the \(r=0\) contact and the direct
   \(F_1\) root. Direct \(F_2\) makes \(m\mid R\),
   impossible because the old \(m\) values are live.
   Direct noncontact \(F_n\) with live ratio is excluded
   by the **original beta-one/two**
   [zero-fifth proof](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-six-alpha-one-live-fn-zero-fifth/README.md).
   It uses genuine first-middle gates in those counts
   and allows any fourth diagonal. The beta-zero
   direct-Fn proof is **not** transferred, since its
   exact clock projection genuinely used \(H_B\).
3. **Alternate \(F_1,F_2\) when \(A_1=0\).** These
   [F1](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-six-alpha-one-beta-zero-alternate-f1/README.md)
   and [F2](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-six-alpha-one-beta-zero-alternate-f2/README.md)
   old factor/field reductions use only the true early
   fits, the ordinary Jacobi clock polynomial, and
   exclusion of slope \(-1\). That last exclusion is
   supplied by item 2 in these counts. Their finite
   replays use only \(P_V\) in the audited transfer,
   regardless of whether the alternate factors coincide
   with another factor of the affine resultant.
4. **Alternate \(F_n\) outside \(F_1,F_2\).** If the
   vanished affine pivot \(A_1=0\) leaves a true root,
   \(A_0=0\) follows. Once the contact and \(F_1,F_2\)
   factors are excluded by items 1 and 3, the
   [quadratic-in-affine-pivots resultant](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-six-alpha-one-beta-zero-alternate-fn/README.md)
   forces \(F_n=F_n'=0\) at the actual \(s\).
   Its \(c_2=0\) fallback is the already excluded
   lost ratio of item 1. Both exact discriminant fields
   and both old roots are retained. The rank transfer uses
   \(P_V\) alone on both. No disjoint-factor assumption
   precedes the exclusions of the overlapping earlier
   factors.

For **beta two cubic**, the
[universal beta-two fifth-diagonal theorem](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-six-alpha-one-beta-two-zero-fifth/README.md)
uses the three actual ghost roots \(z,x,y\) in the
weighted quintic prefix, the old ordinary determinant,
and the literal diagonal of the quintic commutator at
\(B\). It assumes a live reciprocal and cubic carrier
but no old-profile type or fourth pivot. Its contradiction
for \(d_B\ne0\) gives exactly the diagonal used in
section 1. Together with items 1–4 this covers all
live cubic old profiles in the beta-two count.

For **beta one cubic**, the same closure of items 1–4
works under the explicit hypothesis \(d_B=0\);
no universal beta-one zero-diagonal theorem is asserted.
In particular the remaining live beta-one alternate
cases must have **nonzero** fifth diagonal. No claim
about quadratic carriers or earlier zero-multiplier
and other-normalization cases is made here.
