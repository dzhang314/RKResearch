## 1. The retained unmarked companion is a second genuine crossing

For \(j=0,1\), let \(P_j^*\) be the earlier degree-at-most-four
weighted interpolants agreeing with \(Y^jG/b\) at the old three
ordinary rows and at \(V\), and zero at the true terminal.
They are obtained by correcting the old cubics with multiples
of \(P_0\). Write \(r_j^*=IP_j^*/k\bmod R_3\) for their quadratic
fits. True grade-five weighted and marked payments imply
\[
 W(IP_j^*)-NW(P_j^*)+N(Y^jG)=G r_j^*(Y).
\]
Indeed on each old row the two tap terms cancel, determining
the quadratic. They also cancel on the whole inclusive prefix
through \(V\), so \(IP_j^*(V)=k(V)r_j^*(V)\) as well.
On the gap ghost block before \(B\), this identity says
\(N_D(Y^jG)=G r_j^*(Y)\), where \(N_D\) is the actual block
of literal taps among those ghosts.

Suppose at least two ghosts are in that gap. Write \(z,w\)
for the first and second distinct physical ghost clocks and
\(d_z,d_w\) for their literal diagonals. The two true grade-six
commutator images
\[
 L_j=\mathcal R^{\mathsf T}(W(P_j^*)-Y^jG)
\]
are in \(Z_6=G\mathcal P_3+K H_{\rm bary}\), with the actual
rooted pairing budget \(4+6+1=11\). They vanish through
\(V\) by the inclusive cut. Four distinct live clean zeros
kill the effective cubic on the clean sites; the reciprocal
identity turns the remaining \(Z_6\) combination into a
single scalar times \(gG\) globally. As in the fourth-clean
packet, the first ghost reads calibrate the scalars in ratio
\(z\), since both have the same diagonal read multiplied by
\(z^j\) and \(g_zG_z\ne0\). Therefore \(L_1-zL_0=0\).
On the second literal ghost this kills every earlier donor
and gives \((w-z)G_w d_w^2=0\), so \(d_w=0\).
Consequently \(d_z=r_0^*(z)\), and
\(N_{w,z}G_z/G_w=r_0^*(w)\).

Now retain \(L_0\) **itself**, not just \(L_1-zL_0\).
Write its normalized ghost values as \(\nu U_{\rm rev}\).
At \(z\), the \(L_0\) scalar read is \(d_z^2\).
At \(w\), the only remaining read is
\(-(w-z-d_z)r_0^*(w)\): the ghosts are physically consecutive
and \(d_w=0\), so the \((N^2)_{w,z}\) entry is just
\(d_z N_{w,z}\). The two calibrations imply the undivided gate
\[
 \boxed{\
 U_{\rm rev}(z)(w-z-d_z)r_0^*(w)+U_{\rm rev}(w)d_z^2=0.\
 } \tag{1}
\]
There is no division by \(z,w,d_z\), or an intervening tap.
This is separate from the independently paid quintic
\(Q_{\rm eff}\) crossing of the previous packets.

For example an **algebraic residue specimen**, not a tableau,

 (d_z,r_0^*(w),U_{\rm rev}(z),U_{\rm rev}(w))=(33,6,6,31).
\]

This illustrates precisely which earlier residue filter

## 2. The second-root quadratic pivot is actually live

Both \(z,w\) are roots of \(q_z(Y)=r_1^*(Y)-z r_0^*(Y)\).
If its quadratic coefficient were zero, those two distinct
roots would force \(q_z\) to be identically zero.
Put \(P=P_1^*-zP_0^*\). The previous exact weighted matches
imply \(IP\) vanishes at the old three ordinary clocks and
at \(V\), and it has a double root at zero since \(P\)
has zero constant. These are six distinct-root-with-
multiplicity conditions on a polynomial of degree at most
five. Hence \(IP=0\) identically, so \(P=0\) in characteristic
zero. But \(P(r)=(r-z)k(r)\) on the three distinct old roots,
with all \(k(r)\ne0\). This is impossible. Thus the quadratic
leader for locating the second ghost is a genuine live
pivot, independently of any residue test. This does not
assume that the cubic \(r_1^*(z)-z r_0^*(z)\) in \(z\)
has nonzero leader.

## 3. Five initial ghosts: an undivided crossing with the first cubic

For \(\gamma=5\) there is a single gap ghost before \(B\),
of reverse clock \(z=1-\xi\). In the carrier cubic quotient
write \(J=I(XU)\), \(M=XJ\), \(S=S_6\) (or their reflected
coefficients in the same quotient). The genuine first
five-prefix hull gives \(M-\xi J\) proportional to \(S\),
and the actual first gap reads give \(C(z)=0\), with
\(C(Y)=r_1^*(Y)-Y r_0^*(Y)\).
For any two coefficient indices \(i,j\), set
\(D_{ij}=J_i S_j-J_j S_i\) and
\(E_{ij}=M_i S_j-M_j S_i\).
Then \(D_{ij}z=D_{ij}-E_{ij}\). If \(C_k\) are the
coefficients of the degree-at-most-three polynomial \(C\),
\[
 \sum_{k=0}^3 C_k (D_{ij}-E_{ij})^k D_{ij}^{3-k}=0
 \tag{2}
\]
is necessary without division by \(D_{ij}\).
The old determinant of \(J,M,S\) is also required to be zero.
Thus (2) adds three homogeneous first-gap tests and preserves
every vanishing forward-location pivot in their construction.

## 4. Joint units at two fixed ordinary clock residue classes

At each fixed pair \((p,B_0)=(101,79),(37,33)\), form
the same monic \(V\)-quartic and \(s\)-cubic ordinary
clock tower as in the finite-clock proof, of rank
\(4\cdot3=12\). Recover \(r\) using the affine pivot,
then \(q=k(V)\), the clean carrier, and both corrected

appropriate residue algebra. For the multighost case
adjoin \(z\) by the monic first-gap cubic and use the
second-root quadratic coefficients to recover \(w\);
the required intermediate leaders are unit-tested
at these fixed residue classes. This is rank 36.

In rank 12, the sum of the old five-prefix determinant
and the three tests (2) is a unit in both classes.
At \(p=101,\bar B=79\) the prefix determinant **alone**
is not a unit. In rank 36, the old quintic crossing
alone is not a unit in either class, but its sum with
twice (1) at \(101,79\), and once (1) at \(37,33\),
is a unit in the whole algebra. Each combination is
legitimate because **both** terms must vanish in the
same actual clock component. These are fixed ordinary
clock multiplication-algebra unit certificates, not
comparisons of independent projected roots.

Start over the localization of \(\mathbb Z_{(p)}[B]\)
at \((p,B-B_0)\). The above residue inverses lift by
the multiplication determinant and adjugate at each
monic step, as in the finite-clock proof. Thus whenever

specialization (for example a rational \(B\) with
denominator prime to \(p\) and residue \(B_0\), or an
algebraic \(B\) at an integral place with that residue),
the local algebra evaluates to the actual field.
The necessary joint quantity would have to vanish,
contradicting its lifted unit. No tableau coefficient,
weight, or ghost is assumed integral. Arbitrary other
