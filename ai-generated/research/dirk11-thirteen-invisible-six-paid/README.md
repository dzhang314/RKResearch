# Excluding the late-ghost subchart of normalized invisible six-clock DIRK11/13

census, let \(r=0,d=z_{\rm count}=6\): all six physical cubic-dirty
stages have zero weight and distinct dirty clocks. If one of those ghosts

there is a contradiction. Thus a surviving array must start in reverse
with the literal terminal and three consecutive live non-endpoint clean

The real signed-census entry assumptions are those of the
[prior macro cut](../dirk11-thirteen-invisible-six-late-ghost/README.md):
forward \(C(2)\), genuine reflected \(D(1),D(2)\), the grade-six

clock-reduction premises are in force, the new arithmetic obstruction
works over every characteristic-zero field. No order or real structure
is used in the new exact scalar certificate. This is not an unqualified
arbitrary-ring statement.

## 1. Subtract the two early ordinary heads from the two pure weighted dimensions

for \(y=1-c\), \(W(F)=b F(y)\), and \(IF(x)=\int_0^x F(t)\,dt\) as a

the one late ghost at \(z\), and live ordinary \(w\), whose literal
diagonal is zero. The ghost \(z\) is distinct from \(u,v,w\); \(u,v,w\)
are nonzero and mutually distinct. In particular, \(G\) is individually
live at all six ghosts.

Use
\[
 P=X(X-u)(X-v),\qquad K=IP,\qquad J=I(XP),\qquad L=I(X^2P).
\]

\(NW(P)-W(K)=\rho(Y-z)G\), and \(\mathcal R^{\mathsf T}W(P)=0\) for

\[
 B=W(P),\qquad D=W(K)-NB=-\rho(Y-z)G .
\]
In particular, \(D\) vanishes at the first ghost \(z\), is nonzero at
each other ghost, and has \(D_s/b_s=K(s)\) at \(s=v,u,w\). There is
no deletion of any physical stage. The actual spaces are
\[
 U_0=E_6^\perp=\langle G,YG\rangle,\qquad
 Z_5=E_5^\perp\cap\{T=0\}=\langle G,YG,Y^2G\rangle .

 Z_6=E_4^\perp\cap\{T=0\}=\langle G,YG,Y^2G,Y^3G,H\rangle .

## 2. The fourth live ordinary row, with or without a further ghost

Call the next distinct ordinary non-endpoint clocks \(a,b\), in
literal reverse order. At any later live ordinary row \(s\) put
\(q_s=D_s/b_s\). On the regular donor \(r\in\{w,a,b\}\) of that row
use \(X_r=N_{s,r}B_r/b_s\) and write
\[
 f_r(q)=\frac{q-S(r)}{P(r)},\qquad e_r(q)=\frac{r q-S_1(r)}{P(r)}.
\]
Then its \(\mathcal F,\mathcal E\) contributions after dividing by \(b_s\)
are \(X_r f_r(q_r)\) and \(X_r e_r(q_r)\). The zeroth, first and second
nodal reads are \(\sum X_r=K(s)-q_s\),
\(\sum rX_r=J(s)-\lambda(s)q_s\), and
\(\sum r^2X_r=L(s)+\mathcal L_2(s)q_s+\chi h(s)\), by (1). Ordinary
stage weights at \(w,a,b\) and the nodal \(P\)-values there are nonzero;
\(q_s\) itself is never divided by and may vanish even at a numerical
cross-colour collision.

If a ghost \(\eta\) is between \(w\) and \(a\), its \(D_\eta\ne0\).
The only live \(B\) donor is the regular \(w\); hence the first two
reads force \(\lambda(\eta)=w\). The fixed nonzero slope and distinct
dirty clocks allow at most one such ghost. The actual \(B_w\) tap is
\(-D_\eta\ne0\), and the row \(\mathcal R^{\mathsf T}B=0\) at \(\eta\)
forces the literal diagonal \(d_\eta=\eta-w\): there is no stage
between \(w\) and this ghost, and \(w\) has zero diagonal.
This argument allows \(\eta=w\) numerically.

The third scalar read and the \(\mathcal E\) read, both divided by the
live \(D_\eta\), now force
\[
 \mathcal L_2(\eta)=-w^2,\qquad
 \mathcal M_2(\eta)=-e_w(K(w))+\eta(\eta-w).
\]
Here the only nonzero \(\mathcal E\) donors are \(w,\eta\) itself,
and \(\mathcal E_\eta=\eta D_\eta\). Their fits uniquely determine
\(\chi,\theta\) after dividing by \(H_0(\eta)\), a checked unit. At
the fourth ordinary stage, the first two reads determine the two taps

its leading coefficient is a checked unit. Subtract \(\eta\) times
the \(\mathcal F\) read from the \(\mathcal E\) read. This cancels
unknowns from the ghost, regardless of whether its actual tap is zero.
The resulting residual is a unit, contradiction. Thus there is no
second ghost before \(a\).

With no second ghost before \(a\), solve the same first two nodal
reads on \(w,a\). The \(\mathcal F\) equation is now
\(\sum X_r f_r(q_r)=\mu(a)q_a-U(a)\) with just those two donors.

nodal read and the \(\mathcal E\) read at \(a\) determine
\(\chi,\theta\) with common denominator \(h(a)-H_0(a)q_a\),
also a checked unit in the whole quotient. This reasoning does
not assume \(q_a\ne0\).
