# Exact order-twelve ordinary DIRK in 21 factorial-paid stages

This is the order-twelve specialization of the archived implicit-midpoint
extrapolation family. Use six independent midpoint branches with one through
six equal substeps. Branch \(m\) has length \(m\), diagonal \(1/(2m)\),
strictly lower in-branch entries \(1/m\), and no cross-branch entries.
Its total output weight is
\[
   w_m=\prod_{\substack{1\le j\le6\\j\ne m}}
              \frac{m^2}{m^2-j^2},
   \qquad b_{m,i}=w_m/m.
\]
There are \(1+2+\cdots+6=21\) actual stages. The distinct first two
diagonals \(1/2,1/4\) make this genuine ordinary DIRK, not an ERK or a
strict SDIRK method. There is no stage padding or common-diagonal claim.

The [literal rational tableau](tableau21.json) is generated by
[build.py](build.py). The independent integer checker [verify.py](verify.py)
reads only that literal, and verifies every one of the **7,813** rooted
conditions through size twelve. With stage scale \(D_A=120\) and weight
scale \(D_b=19958400\), the checked equation on a size-\(n\) tree is
\[
  t!\,\mathbf b_{\mathrm{int}}^{\mathsf T}
      \boldsymbol{\Phi}_t(\mathit{A}_{\mathrm{int}})
   =D_b D_A^{\,n-1}.
\]
The common denominator is \(19958400=12!/24\), and \(D_A\mid D_b\).
Thus the same literal formula has order at least twelve over every
commutative unital ring inverting \(12!\), by specializing the integer
identities; no rational flag-coordinate denominator is required.

The actual thirteenth tall-tree cleared defect is \(-231/1024\).
It is a unit in that ring since the only numerator primes are
\(3,7,11\) and the denominator is a power of two. Consequently exact
order twelve holds over every nonzero such ring, including the rationals.
The thirteenth bush defect is also nonzero over \(\mathbb Q\), but is not
needed for arbitrary-ring exactness.

This is a **21-stage factorial-unit upper witness**, not a rational or
factorial-localized minimum. The 15-stage real witness and its minimum
are separate and do not assert a rational 15-stage tableau. The six-branch
construction is standard, not a new extrapolation principle. This receipt
records the exact arithmetic; native Lean status is tracked separately.
