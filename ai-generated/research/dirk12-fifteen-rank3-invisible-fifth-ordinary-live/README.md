# DIRK12/15: the genuine fifth ordinary live clock has at most three choices

Continue the real ordinary fifteen-stage order-twelve \(C(2),f_5=3\) weighted-invisible four-distinct-dirty-ghost frame in either the [nine-site early reverse return](../dirk12-fifteen-rank3-invisible-nine-site-early-return/README.md) or [ten-site earliest extra reflected zero](../dirk12-fifteen-rank3-invisible-ten-site-early-zero/README.md) chronology. For every actual first-four prefix of distinct nonzero ordinary live clocks \((a,b,c,e)\), the **fifth** such clock \(f\) has at most three choices, given by a nonzero polynomial of degree at most three. In the non-Legendre-root branch with nonzero fourth diagonal, the degree is at most two. Together with the [geometry-free first-four proof](../dirk12-fifteen-rank3-invisible-all-ordinary-prefixes/README.md), both actual ordinary chronologies have only finitely many first-five live prefixes, without restricting individual ghost geometry.

This is an exact additional stage condition, not a claim of nonexistence of the finite set or a new global bound. Real DIRK12 is still **15–16**. There is no positive-weights assumption, degree-twelve moment payment, or reduction of an unknown tableau.

## Three-mode evaluation rank is genuine even at a first Legendre root

Use \(T=\mathbf c/a\), \(\bar A=A/a\), \(\bar R=(CA-AC-A^2)/a^2\), \(x=b/a,y=c/a,t=e/a,u=f/a\), and \(J\) for normalized scalar integration from zero. The first five nonzero ordinary live clocks are unique physical rows in these reverse chronologies: in the nine-site chart the repeated pair is in the first four reverse live nonterminal rows and hence too late in ordinary order to interfere with these first five nonzero live rows (there are ten nonterminal live physical rows). The ten-site chart has no repeated nonterminal clock. The ordinary origin is the only earlier live zero in this prefix, and all ghost rows precede \(b\).

The genuine nonterminal live profiles of \(F_7\) belong to

Now take \(L_6'(a)=0\). All ghosts are before \(a\) and the same first-root proof gives actual first diagonal zero. Use

All scalar factors are nonzero: \(x=0,1\) are invalid; \(x=2/3,1/3\) would give \(y=0,1\); and the quartic bracket is \(((36(x-1/2)^2-1)^2+8)/72>0\). Therefore \(Z(y)\ne0\) when \(k(y)=0\). Together with \(r,k\), this proves genuine full three-mode evaluation rank on \(1,x,y\) also in this case. No unverified exceptional modal pivot remains over the real numbers.

## The extra paid degree-six input at the fifth physical row

Let \(\mathcal A(F)\) denote the actual ordinary live-only normalized read, excluding the four ordinary ghost taps. For any scalar \(F\) of degree at most six and with zero constant, its difference from \(JF\) on nonterminal live rows lies in \(\mathcal G\). Indeed its actual scalar primitive discrepancy belongs to genuine \(F_7\) at that input degree; subtracting the four literal ghost taps still changes it by a member of \(\mathcal G\) by their two-mode formula. If in addition \(F(1)=F(x)=F(y)=0\), its actual first three live reads are zero by their physical support. Thus \(\mathcal A(F)=JF-\mathcal L_F\), where \(\mathcal L_F\) is the unique member of \(\mathcal G\) matching \(JF(1),JF(x),JF(y)\). This uses exact three-mode rank just proved, not a guessed scalar approximation at a future row.

Put
\[
        K=T(T-1)(T-x)(T-y)(T-t).
\]
Both \(K\) and \(TK\) belong to this input domain, of degrees five and six. Let
\[
             E_{\rm fifth}=\bigl[\mathcal A(TK)-T\mathcal A(K)\bigr]/K.
\]
This is a polynomial on an actual first-four prefix: at \(0,1,x,y,t\) the numerator vanishes by the literal first four live rows (and by all modes and primitives vanishing at zero). In particular the actual \(t\)-row reads are zero because all available live scalar input values, including its own, are zero. The numerator has degree at most eight, and \(K\) is monic degree five. At the actual fifth nonzero live row \(u\), each input can only read its own scalar value times its physical diagonal. Therefore \(E_{\rm fifth}(u)=0\).

## The fixed quartic prevents an identically zero fifth condition

The numerator of \(E_{\rm fifth}\) is \(-J^2K-\mathcal L_{TK}+T\mathcal L_K\). If it is identically zero, its degree-eight term forces \(\mathcal L_K\) to have no top \(sL_7'(as)\) term.

The nonzero-\(\gamma\) fact is from the actual mixed reverse grade-six comparisons using the actual unpaid \(\varepsilon_{12}\), not an extra scalar exactness premise.

Consequently \(J^2K/T\) lies in

It has a double zero at the literal numerical origin, since \(K\) has a zero there before the two primitives. The two-jet rank in this three-dimensional span is exactly two. In the nonroot case with actual fourth diagonal nonzero, the masked Möbius result already gives \(\mathcal D(K)=0\), so no degree-eight term remains and the degree bound here is at most two.
