# DIRK12/15: a terminal and repeated-clock restriction for four invisible ghosts

Consider the actual ordinary real order-twelve fifteen-stage \(C(2),f_5=3\) equality tableau. Assume its weighted forward cubic is invisible, \(\beta\mathbf h=0\), and **exactly four stages are unweighted, all four nonzero in \(\mathbf h\), at four distinct numerical clocks**. Assume also that the candidate scalar grid has exactly seven live labels (including the literal endpoints). Then the scalar grid is fixed Lobatto, and:

1. The actual terminal is the **only live physical row at clock \(1\)**, with weight \(1/42\).
2. There must be **at least three distinct repeated live scalar labels**.
3. The true reverse pure spaces through grade five vanish at every unweighted stage and every singleton live scalar label, and sum to zero separately at each remaining live label.

This does not exclude the whole four-ghost chart or the invisible face, and the general interval remains **15–16**. Here a repeated live label is a clock with at least two nonzero-weight physical stages. The ghost clocks may coincide with any live scalar clock; no noncollision assumption is used.

Write \(\mathbf h=A\mathbf c^2-\mathbf c^3/3\), \(C=\operatorname{diag}(\mathbf c)\), \(\beta\mathbf v=\mathbf b\odot\mathbf v\), \(T_j,F_j\) for genuine ordinary numerical/pure spaces and \(\mathcal B_j\) for the actual weighted reverse pure flag. Grade means tree order minus one. All eleven other rows have nonzero weight and are physically cubic-clean. The real scalar quadrature therefore uses exactly their seven live numerical labels. The candidate count and the exact shifted Legendre orthogonality give the fixed seven-node Lobatto grid with positive **aggregate** weights; no sign restriction is made on individual weights within repeats.

## The pure ghost coordinates and the terminal shape

The genuine \(\mathbf hP_3(\mathbf c)\subset F_6\) is exactly the four-dimensional ghost coordinate subspace: each ghost amplitude is nonzero, and the four distinct ghost locations allow full cubic interpolation. Since \(f_6=5\), the numerical restriction of \(F_6\) to all live rows has rank at most one. This does not assume that \(\mathbf h^2\) supplies an additional independent grade-six direction.

The actual terminal coordinate \(\mathbf e_t\) belongs to genuine \(T_6\). Take any actual paired scalar profile \(P\in P_6\) for it. The literal zero origin gives \(P(0)=0\). Paired tests against every \(R\in P_5\) are genuinely paid at \(6+5=11\), so \(I(P R)=b_t R(1)\). In the exact quadrature on the fixed live grid, test all \(R\) on the six nonzero labels; degree-five interpolation realizes every such test. For each label \(z\neq0\) this yields \(m_z P(z)=b_t{\bf1}_{z=1}\), where \(m_z\) is the positive aggregate scalar weight at \(z\). Thus \(P\) is zero at every live label except \(1\), and \(P(1)=42b_t\). The actual discrepancy \(\mathbf k_t=\mathbf e_t-P(\mathbf c)\in F_6\) is therefore supported, on live rows, only at clock \(1\).

Suppose there is another live stage at clock \(1\). Because \(b_t\ne0\), the restriction of \(\mathbf k_t\) to the live rows is nonzero, and so spans the whole at-most-one-dimensional live \(F_6\) restriction. Consequently every grade-six pure column vanishes at every live abscissa distinct from \(1\).

Let \(a,b\) be the first two distinct nonzero live clocks in actual forward chronology **excluding \(1\)**. They are distinct interior Lobatto clocks, so \(0<a,b<1\). Earlier live rows through the first occurrence of \(b\) have clock in \(\{0,1,a,b\}\). Consider the two-dimensional scalar space
\[
                       X(X-1)(X-a)(X-b)P_1 .
\]
At the four actual ghosts \(z\), a quadratic \(Q\) can match \(S(z)/h_z\) if the leading cubic interpolation coefficient is zero, one scalar linear condition. Therefore choose a nonzero \(S\) from this space admitting that fit. The actual grade-five input \(S(\mathbf c)-\mathbf hQ(\mathbf c)\) vanishes numerically through the first occurrence of \(b\), including intervening ghosts and repeated live \(1\)-rows. By chronology and the true grade-six live pure restriction, \(JS\) vanishes at \(a,b\); it also vanishes at \(0\). All three roots are double since \(S\) vanishes there. Its degree is at most six, so
\[
                         JS=\lambda X^2(X-a)^2(X-b)^2
\]
with \(\lambda\ne0\). But \(S(1)=0\) says the derivative of the right hand side at \(1\) is zero. Each differentiated product contribution there is positive before multiplication by \(\lambda\), since \(0<a,b<1\). Contradiction. Thus the terminal is the unique live clock-\(1\) row and \(b_t=m_1=1/42\).

## The reverse pure flag must see three different repeated live labels

For any \(\boldsymbol\zeta\in\mathcal B_5=\operatorname{Ann}T_6\), its four ghost entries vanish because the ghost coordinate vectors already belong to actual \(F_6\). Independently, an arbitrary \(P_6\) scalar profile may be made supported on live rows by subtracting a genuine \(\mathbf h Q(\mathbf c)\) with \(Q\in P_3\), via interpolation on the four ghosts. Since \(P_6\) ranges independently over the seven fixed live sites, the annihilation by \(\boldsymbol\zeta\) gives zero aggregate separately at each live site. In particular \(\boldsymbol\zeta\) vanishes at any singleton live site. Therefore the whole true \(\mathcal B_3,\mathcal B_4,\mathcal B_5\) is supported inside repeated live sites only, whether or not the normalized reverse cubic equals the forward cubic (it does not here).

Let \(\mathbf G=N\mathbf W(X^2)-\mathbf W(X^3)/3\) be the actual reverse cubic, where \(N=A^{\mathsf T}\) in reversed physical order, \(\mathbf y=\mathbf1-\mathbf c\), and \(\mathbf W(P)=\beta P(\mathbf y)\). The [actual equality frame](../dirk12-fifteen-frame/README.md) gives both leading weighted primitive identities with no deweighting: \(N\mathbf W(1)=\mathbf W(X)\), \(N\mathbf W(X)=\mathbf W(X^2)/2\). At the first live nonzero reflected physical row \(i\), all earlier live source clocks are zero. The second leading identity at that row therefore gives the literal diagonal \(N_{ii}=y_i/2\). Thus \(G_i=b_i y_i^3/6\ne0\). This proves \(\mathbf G\) is genuinely nonzero in this invisible-forward chart, and that its first live nonzero reflected clock is a support label.

The true reverse rank gains through grade five are one: the paid inclusions \(\mathcal B_j\subseteq\operatorname{Ann}T_{11-j}\) have upper ranks \(1,2,3\) at \(j=3,4,5\) by \(\dim T_8,\dim T_7,\dim T_6=14,13,12\). The actual strict primitive-flag rank gains after the nonzero cubic give the matching lower ranks. This is the [weighted reverse prefix lemma](../dirk12-fifteen-weighted-reverse-band/README.md), checked in DIRKOrderTwelveFifteenReverseRanks; it applies to the true weighted transpose and assumes no individual nonzero weights. Thus \(\dim\mathcal B_3,\dim\mathcal B_4,\dim\mathcal B_5=(1,2,3)\), with \(\mathcal B_3=\langle\mathbf G\rangle\). The terminal and cluster arguments above do not need this last rank/debut reasoning. Suppose at most two distinct live labels were repeated. If \(\mathbf G\) used both, then \(\mathcal B_4=\langle\mathbf G,Y\mathbf G\rangle\) would be clock-invariant, contrary to the actual outgoing weighted rank gain to \(\mathcal B_5\) of only one. Here \(Y=\operatorname{diag}(\mathbf y)\).

If \(\mathbf G\) uses just one label \(d\), then the [true one-label reverse head and third-debut argument](../dirk12-fifteen-rank3-one-active-third-debut/README.md) applies to this **reverse** cubic. Its only support premises are that \(\mathbf G\) is local at \(d\), and zero on every zero-weight row; both are established here from \(\mathcal B_5\) without requiring \(\mathbf G\) to be proportional to the invisible forward \(\beta\mathbf h\). The first head \(N\mathbf G-(d/2)\mathbf G\) is nonzero at the next live distinct nonzero clock \(v\). The same head is nonzero at the following live distinct nonzero clock \(u\) by the fixed-grid arithmetic \(10v^2-12uv+3u^2\ne0\); both \(u,v\) are fixed nonzero grid nodes in the reflected grid. Since the head belongs to \(\mathcal B_4\), both of these are repeated live labels, different from the repeated label \(d\). This again contradicts at most two. The argument does not assert any full rooted order for a transposed numerical method.

Thus there are three or four repeated live labels. Since there are eleven live physical rows over seven sites, the only residual multiplicity distributions for repeated sites are \((3,2,2)\) and \((2,2,2,2)\), with the remaining sites singletons. The terminal is one of the singletons. No exclusion of those distributions is claimed here.
