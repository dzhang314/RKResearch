However there are only three distinct nonzero ghost interpolation conditions on \(S/X\in P_3\). They leave one further degree of freedom, the bare quartic
\[
               P_4(X)=X\prod_{\text{nonzero ghosts }g}(X-c_g).
\]
Thus the exact scalar mask space is \(\mathscr S=\langle S_3,P_4\rangle\), where \(S_3\in XP_2\) is the unique scalar cubic with \(S_3(c_g)=c_g h_g\) at each of the three nonzero ghosts. It is nonzero by the third nonzero value, while its degree could a priori be below three. Every actual corrected grade-four state is zero at the origin and all four ghosts.

This is precisely the dimension and genuine scalar premise of the earlier no-late proof. The degree-four leading coefficient map on this space is nonzero because it contains \(P_4\), and its degree-at-most-three kernel is the line of \(S_3\); the previously used nonzero-ghost *cubic-fit* guard is unnecessary here. Thus:
