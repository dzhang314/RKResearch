# DIRK12/15: one noncancelling dirty label on a variable eight-site grid is impossible

Consider ordinary real DIRK with order twelve, fifteen physical stages, \(C(2)\), and \(f_5=3\). Suppose it has three distinct active cubic-dirty clock labels and just one of these labels has nonzero aggregate ordinary dirty weight. The fixed seven-site case was excluded by the [small exact cuts](../dirk12-fifteen-rank3-three-active-fixed-grid/README.md). The variable eight-site case is excluded here without any fixed-grid or polynomial elimination calculation. Thus the \(q=1\) case is entirely closed. Three all-cancelling dirty labels with a variable eight- or nine-site grid and the overall real interval **15–16** remain open.

Write \(T_j,F_j\) for genuine numerical and pure forward spaces at grade \(j\), where grade is tree order minus one. Write \(\mathbf h=A\mathbf c^2-\mathbf c^3/3\), \(C=\operatorname{diag}(\mathbf c)\), \(\beta\mathbf v=\mathbf b\odot\mathbf v\), and \(JS(X)=\int_0^X S\). The actual equality frame gives the literal zero origin at clock \(0\), the live terminal at clock \(1\), and the ranks \(f_3=1,f_4=2,f_5=3,f_6=5\). In particular \(F_j=\mathbf hP_{j-3}(\mathbf c)\) for \(3\le j\le5\), from the independent powers at the three active dirty labels.

## All rows and the one-dimensional clean grade-six space

Let \(u\) be the noncancelling dirty label, and let \(z_1,z_2\) be the cancelling labels. The [count](../dirk12-fifteen-rank3-three-active-fixed-grid/README.md) gives at least four live dirty rows at \(u\), and at least two each at \(z_1,z_2\). Of the eight candidate scalar labels exactly seven are different from \(u\); each requires its own physical row outside these dirty rows. That row is active clean, except possibly the separately included literal origin. Thus these eight plus seven physical rows exhaust the fifteen slots. Neither boundary clock can equal \(u\), because its actual clean boundary row would then be extra, beyond the seven outside-\(u\) labels and the eight active dirty rows.

There are therefore seven physical clean rows at distinct clocks, including the literal origin and terminal, with nonzero weights except possibly the origin. There are four live dirty stages at \(u\) with four distinct nonzero amplitudes, and two live cancelling pairs at \(z_1,z_2\) with equal amplitudes within each pair. The label \(u\) differs from every clean label, since any such overlap would similarly require an extra physical row. The cancelling labels may overlap with clean coordinates. No subsequent argument divides by the origin weight or assumes the quadrature has a nonzero coefficient at \(0\).

The genuine order pairings \((\beta\mathbf h) C^j\perp T_6\) for \(j=0,1,2\) separate the three dirty labels. Thus every \(T_6\) vector has equal entries within either cancelling pair, and on the four rows at \(u\) its entries lie in \(\langle\mathbf 1,\mathbf h,\mathbf h^2\rangle\). Indeed their sole tested equation there is \(\sum_{i:c_i=u,\;i\ \mathrm{dirty}}b_i h_i v_i=0\), and the independent three columns \(1,h_i,h_i^2\) satisfy it by the paid first through third amplitude moments on that label. The fourth weight and all four amplitudes are nonzero, so this is a genuine codimension-one constraint on that fiber.

For any \(\mathbf v\in F_6\), take its **weighted scalar aggregates** on the eight candidate sites, ignoring the two uniform cancelling pairs since their contributions vanish. On nonorigin clean rows these aggregates are \(b_i v_i\), and at \(u\) the aggregate is the value of the constant coefficient of the quadratic in \(h_i\) times the nonzero ordinary mass there. The aggregate at \(0\) is zero by the literal zero origin (or its zero weight). This eight-entry aggregate vector annihilates scalar powers of degrees \(0,\ldots,5\) by the actual \(6+5=11\) order pairing. If \(H\) is the monic nodal hull of the eight sites, the Vandermonde description is the line with entries \(X/H'(X)\) at the respective sites: the degree-five annihilator is spanned by \(1/H'\) and \(X/H'\), and the coordinate at \(0\) kills the first coefficient. All entries of this line at nonorigin clean sites are nonzero for a nonzero coefficient. In particular the restriction of \(F_6\) to the nonorigin clean rows has rank at most one, and a pure grade-six column vanishing at one such row vanishes at **all** such clean rows. This conclusion uses no sign restriction on a dirty amplitude or clock.

## A physically zero diagonal at the second interior clean stage

Write \(a,b\) for the first and second interior clean clocks in physical forward order. They are distinct and nonzero, and distinct from \(u\). A scalar profile \(S\in P_4\) has a genuine clean-supported state \(\mathbf v_S=S(\mathbf c)-\mathbf hQ(\mathbf c)\), \(Q\in P_1\), if \(S(u)=0\) and one linear relation on its two cancelling-label values holds. Indeed at \(u\) choose \(Q(u)=0\); then \(Q(X)\) is a scalar multiple of \(X-u\), and matching the required values \(Q(z_j)=S(z_j)/h_{z_j}\) at \(z_1,z_2\) gives one linear compatibility equation. Start with the two-dimensional space \(X(X-a)(X-u)P_1\) and impose that equation to choose a nonzero \(S\). Thus the actual grade-four input vanishes at every dirty stage, at the literal origin, and at the first interior clean row. It is zero at all physical rows through that first interior stage.

The actual lower triangular commutator
\[
                          C A-A C-A^2
\]
on this state is pure of grade six, because its formal scalar profile \(X J S-J(XS)-J^2 S\) is zero. It is zero through the earlier actual prefix; in particular its zero at the first interior clean row forces it zero on all nonorigin clean rows by the one-dimensional clean projection proved above.

If \(S(b)=0\), genuine chronology also gives \(A\mathbf v_S=0\) through the second interior clean row. On the clean rows \(A\mathbf v_S\) is exactly \(JS\), as \(F_5\) is pure dirty. Thus \(JS\) has double roots at \(0,a,b\), from the primitive vanishings and \(S(0)=S(a)=S(b)=0\). This is impossible for its degree at most five unless \(JS=S=0\). Hence \(S(b)\ne0\): the row at \(b\) is the first nonzero numerical coordinate of this actual forward vector. The commutator diagonal is \(-A_{ii}^2\) at the physical row \(i\) of \(b\). Its zero there forces \(A_{ii}=0\).

## Grade-five primitive rank now contradicts the same clean space

For **every** \(S\in X(X-a)(X-u)P_2\) there is an actual grade-five clean-supported state. Choose the quadratic \(Q\), on the three distinct dirty labels, with \(Q(u)=0\), \(Q(z_j)=S(z_j)/h_{z_j}\). The resulting actual vector is zero on every physical row strictly before the second interior clean row. As that row's diagonal is zero, its actual primitive is zero there as well. Thus both \(JS(a)\) and \(JS(b)\), up to sign, must be the two coordinates of a genuine pure grade-six state. Their joint linear map on this three-dimensional scalar space would have rank at most one.

On the other hand, any \(W=JS\) in its kernel is divisible by \(B=X^2(X-a)^2(X-b)\) and has degree at most six. This follows from the two double roots at \(0,a\) and the root at \(b\). It is constrained by \(W'(u)=0\), since the input scalar \(S\) vanishes there. On the two-dimensional space \(B P_1\) the derivative-at-\(u\) linear functional is nonzero: if \(B'(u)\ne0\) take \(B\); if \(B'(u)=0\) take \(XB\), whose derivative there is \(B(u)\ne0\). Thus the primitive kernel has dimension at most one, so the two component evaluations have rank two. This contradicts the actual grade-six clean rank.

This proof is mathematical, with no arithmetic extrapolation or new DIRK stage lower claimed. The two final true primitive conditions cost grades five and six, respectively; neither is an assumed \(C(6)\) property.
