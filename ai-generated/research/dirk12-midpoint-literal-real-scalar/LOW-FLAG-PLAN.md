the named real roots exist, all ten support labels are distinct,
the weight split is proved, and the scalar degree-eleven obligations
and true size-thirteen bush defect are certified. The order-twelve

The remaining proof must define the actual lower-triangular causal
matrix and show the following for its literal entries, not merely for
a model of the support.

   as the declared clock minus all other taps. Prove that all needed
   ordinary and reverse denominators and nonzero output weights are

   mathematical construction has these guards by arithmetic

   identities, including the selected zero fourth diagonal. The
   external exact checker has these literal prefix gates.
3. **Reverse grades three through five.** Take the genuine weighted

   native. The five physical opening-stage quartic gates still need
