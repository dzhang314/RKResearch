# Ordinary scalar premise from the verified midpoint quadrature

The polynomial linear integral satisfies
\[
    \mathcal I(P')=P(1)-P(0).
\]
Lean proves this on monomials and extends by linearity. Applying it to
\((1-X)^{j+1}\) proves that the reflected scalar quadrature
implies the exact ordinary normalization
\[
   (j+1)\sum_i b_i c_i^j=1,\qquad 0\le j\le11.
\]
Both the reusable field theorem and its specialization to the literal
physical midpoint weights are checked. The latter still takes the
matching non-ghost row sums of \(A\); it does not assume or claim full
rooted order. This supplies precisely the scalar field of the reviewed
sufficient flag record for those row sums.
