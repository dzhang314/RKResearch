# A canonical rank-three third-copy DIRK12 obstruction

This excludes one sufficient construction for single-tableau width 19
over \(\mathbb Z[1/12!]\). It is already impossible on its regular
rational parameter chart. It is **not** a DIRK12 stage lower bound,
an exclusion of the width-19 portfolio, or an exclusion of a
third-copy chart using a different amplitude, asymmetric mini ports,
or a size-seven rank-four space.

## Hypotheses

Use the ten-stage old decoder with six ports at original 0-based
indices \(0,5,6,7,8,9\). Normalize the old clock at index 5 to 1
before applying a nonzero common rescaling. The normalized clocks
through index 8 are
\[
 0,\ a/4,\ a/2,\ a,\ d,\ 1,\ t,\ 2t-1,\ 2t,
 \qquad d=(3-5a)/(5-10a).
\]
The physical clocks at indices 8 and 9 are \(z,u\). All old clocks
are distinct. Use the actual Lagrange-integral row supports from
the two-copy construction: at indices 1–4 respectively
\((0,1),(0,1,2),(0,2,3),(0,2,3,4)\); at 5 use \((0,3,4,5)\);
at 6 and 7 use respectively \((0,3,4,5,6)\) and \((0,3,4,5,7)\).
The six old ports are exact through tree size six, and the final
two uncorrected ports also through seven. Set \(r\) to an earlier
true port, distinct from \(0,1,z,u\), where 1 is the scored terminal
clock. Use the usual rank-one injection at \(r\):
\(\boldsymbol\sigma\) is row \(r\) minus its integrated decoder
row, the cross-block change is
\(\boldsymbol\eta\boldsymbol\sigma\), and the old final row receives
\(\eta(u)\boldsymbol\sigma\). For literal merges one needs
\(\eta(r)=1,\eta(z)=0\).

The *additional* hypotheses tested here are that the actual
corrected old order-seven residual polynomial space \(\mathcal F_6\)
has dimension at most three, and the injection
\(\boldsymbol\eta\) is evaluation of some \(f\in\mathcal F_6\)
plus a correction supported only at scored clock 1. Enforce the
six lower weighted reverse moments of that injection against
\(1,(1-X),\ldots,(1-X)^5\). These are the construction's paid
sufficient conditions; they are not asserted for arbitrary
DIRK tableaux.

## The unique lower polynomial correction

Every \(f\in\mathcal F_6\) has degree at most six and \(f(0)=f(z)=0\).
Power moments of the scalar degree-eleven quadrature identify the
five lower constraints of positive exponent with
\(\int_0^1 f(X)(1-X)^j\,dX=0\) for \(1\le j\le5\);
the terminal correction disappears there, and the unscored origin
does not contribute. On \(f=Xq\), these are the five pairing
conditions of \(q\) with the degree-\(\le4\) polynomials for the
positive density \(X(1-X)\). They are independent even over
\(\mathbb Q\): the restriction to degree-\(\le4\) polynomials is
a nonsingular Gram pairing (or use the explicit monomial
moments). The common kernel is consequently generated by
\[
 H=X(2X-1)(66X^4-132X^3+84X^2-18X+1),
 \qquad \int_0^1 H(X)X^j\,dX=1/42\quad(0\le j\le5).
\]
Since \(\eta(r)=1\), the multiple of \(H\) cannot be zero. The terminal
pad for the \(j=0\) condition does not affect \(\eta(z)=0\).
The quartic factor has leading coefficient nonzero modulo 13 and no
root there. A rational root with denominator divisible by 13 would
likewise violate the leading homogeneous congruence. Therefore the
only nonzero rational root of \(H\) is \(z=1/2\).
The normalized mini clocks \(1,t,2t-1\) now have physical scale
\(1/(4t)\).

## Actual mini-port rank

Let \(\mathbf M,\mathbf C,\mathbf D\) denote, respectively, the
three mini rows' values of \(A\mathbf c^5-\mathbf c^6/6\), column 3
taps, and column 4 taps in the normalized matrix. For a normalized
mini clock \(v\in\{1,t,2t-1\}\), the \(\mathbf M\) entry is
\[
 -\int_0^v X(X-a)(X-d)(X-1)(X-v)\,dX .
\]
At \(v=1\) this agrees with its four-donor row since
\(\int_0^1 X(X-a)(X-d)(X-1)\,dX=0\). The same vanishing lets
one compute both actual taps at \(v=1\) using the five-factor
cardinal formulas (neither tap attaches to the doubled node).

Precisely the following *two actual size-six profiles* recover
\(\mathbf C,\mathbf D\) when read by the mini rows:
\[
 A\mathbf c^4-\mathbf c^5/5,\qquad A^2(A\mathbf c^2-\mathbf c^3/3).
\]
Both are differences of genuine elementary differentials with their
ideal coefficient subtracted, or their successive linear root
extensions. They vanish at the pure ports through size six. The old
trapezoid has the only size-four deviation seen upstream,
\(a^3/384\) at index 1: row 2 is Simpson through the cubic,
row 3 is Simpson through the cubic, row 4 integrates through the
cubic, and the other ports are pure. Its next linear read at index 2
is through the actual index-1 tap; two reads at indices 3 and 4
are via their actual index-2 taps. The determinant on indices
3 and 4 of the two displayed profiles is
\[
 -\frac{a^5(5a-3)^3(10a^2-10a+3)^3}
 {54000000(2a-1)^5(10a^2-15a+6)} ,
\]
which is nonzero on the regular rational chart. The excluded rational
linear zeros give \(a=0\), \(d=0\), or undefined \(d\); the quadratics
in this expression have negative discriminants. The subsequent
linear root extension yields \(\mathbf C,\mathbf D\) in the actual
size-seven mini projection, up to span, not in a hypothesized
unpaid defect band. The vector \(\mathbf M\) is independently
a size-seven difference.

The size-seven star has exact value \(\mathbf c^6\) at *every*
stage, so its residual polynomial is the nonzero, monic old port
nodal polynomial. It lies in the kernel of projection to the three
mini clock evaluations. Changing only the final old row cannot
affect those mini evaluations, and the change does not affect the
star (the row sum is unchanged). It follows from
\(\dim\mathcal F_6\le3\) that this projection has dimension at most
two. In particular \(\det[\mathbf M,\mathbf C,\mathbf D]=0\).

The direct tap determinant in replay.py reduces, up to regular
nonzero factors, to \(-5a^2+6at+a-3t+1\). Its other numerator
factors \(t,2t-1,t-1,2a-1\) are all forbidden by the actual distinct
clocks or the definition of \(d\); its linear denominator factors
are the used tap gaps or those already excluded, and
\(10a^2-10a+3>0\). Thus
\[
 t=(5a^2-a-1)/(6a-3).
\]
The \(1,t\) minor of \([\mathbf C,\mathbf D]\) becomes
\(125(5a^2-a-1)^3/[1944a(2a-1)(5a-3)(10a^2-10a+3)]\),
nonzero since \(t\ne0\). This pays the comparison with the
actual rank-two mini projection.

## Obstruction

If \(H\in\mathcal F_6\), the vector of its values at the three
physical mini sites must lie in the span of
\(\mathbf C,\mathbf D\). The corresponding determinant after
substitution for \(t\) is
\[
 -\frac{125(a-1)(5a-2)(10a^2-8a+1)P(a)}
 {2239488a(2a-1)^3(4a-1)(5a-3)(10a-7)
       (5a^2-a-1)^2(10a^2-10a+3)} .
\]
Here \(P\) is the explicit degree-twelve numerator in replay.py.
The extra denominator zeros \(a=1/4,7/10\) collide \(2t-1\) with
\(a,d\), respectively. The numerator factors \(a-1\) and \(5a-2\)
collide clock 1 with \(a,d\); the quadratic \(10a^2-8a+1\) has
nonsquare discriminant 24. The leading coefficient of \(P\) is
nonzero modulo 11, and none of its eleven residues is zero.
For a rational root \(m/n\) in lowest terms, \(11\mid n\) would
contradict the leading term after homogenizing, and \(11\nmid n\)
would give a forbidden root modulo 11. This rules out every
regular rational parameter in this chart.

These are polynomial coefficient tests in finite fields, not
reductions of an unknown tableau or assumptions about individual
score-weight residues. The replay checks the exact rational
identities and the two prime residues. The core-only Lean module
independently certifies the finite nonroot and leading coefficient
checks without an axiom on all four listed Lean releases. The
preceding analytic determinant argument is not claimed as native
Lean.
