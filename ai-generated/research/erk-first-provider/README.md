# Ordinary formulas found inside an explicit Runge–Kutta tableau

A precise local consequence of separation is that a suitable row at a nonzero clock yields an ordinary Runge–Kutta method on its own earlier physical stages. The row applied after \(A\) gives the primitive of the exact polynomial at that clock. Truncate to the earlier stages and divide both \(A\) and the output weights by that clock. The resulting method satisfies the ordinary rooted order conditions, so ordinary stage and defect-rank lower bounds apply to the actual prefix.

The two arguments here use degree \(j\) for tree size minus one. Thus \(F_j\) is formed from trees through size \(j+1\).

- [Clock-invariant defect space](INVARIANT-DEFECT-HERMITE-PROVIDER-RENT.md). When \(F_j\) is invariant under multiplication by the clock vector, the prefix at the \(M\)-th nonzero clock has at least \(d_j+2M\) rows, for \(M\le\lfloor j/2\rfloor\). Here \(d_j\) is a pure-defect rank lower bound for ordinary order-\(j\) methods, measured through tree size \(j\). The \(2M\) independent functionals are the primitive at each clock and the evaluations at the preceding clocks and the origin. The resulting bound is \(\dim F_j\ge d_j+\lfloor j/2\rfloor\).
- [One-head defect space](FIRST-CLEAN-PROVIDER-AND-RESIDUE-RENT.md). With a core of dimension \(u\) and one complementary polynomial profile of degree \(d\), the first clean nonzero clock gives an actual ordinary method on at most \(u+d+3\) predecessors. At equality the same early nodes force divisibility relations between successive primitives. These give an [exclusion in characteristic zero](QUARTIC-CHARACTERISTIC-ZERO-EXIT.md) for the specified rank-three quartic profile. They do not exclude all degree-eight profiles.
- [A paid degree-seven exclusion](PAID-DEGREE-SEVEN-FIRST-PROVIDER.md). In the tight order-15 forward space, either a level-three defect dimension of at least three, or a rank-four \(F_4\) with a nonzero level-three head, forces at least 23 stages. The proof uses the actual first prefix even when two earlier reads at the same clock must be combined to obtain it. The case where the level-three defects lie in the invariant core is not excluded.

Over a nonzero commutative ring, the invariant rank proof can be applied in each residue field when separation holds there. Ordinary order \(2j\) ensures this: a polynomial of degree at most \(j\) representing a pure defect vanishes at the origin, so it is \(X\) times a polynomial of degree at most \(j-1\). Its first \(j\) moments vanish by the order conditions. The corresponding matrix of moments for weight \(X\) is a Cauchy matrix with a unit determinant after the factorial payment, so the polynomial is zero. The resulting residue-wise ranks imply a unit ideal of minors over the ring. Mere separation over the original ring does not by itself guarantee this residue-wise hypothesis.

These results give conditional refinements, not new unconditional entries in the stage table. The proofs were checked independently; no Lean certificate is claimed.
