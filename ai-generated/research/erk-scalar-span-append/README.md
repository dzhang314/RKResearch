# A scalar-span obstruction to short append-only order lifts

This is a **fixed-prefix** theorem. It leaves arbitrary coefficient
retuning outside its scope. Its concrete new application is that
the 13-stage split Shanks order-eight tableau cannot be raised to
order nine by appending at most two strict rows and changing all
weights, over any nonzero commutative ring paying \(8!\).
For the unsplit 12-stage Shanks the same conclusion holds if \(41\)
is paid too. This does not change a general ERK stage bound.

## Monic recurrence with no extra units

Work over any commutative coefficient ring. Suppose an old tableau
\(A\), with weights \(\boldsymbol b\), has ordinary order at least
\(p\). For \(1\le r<p\), let \(V\) be the module generated by its
actual rooted derivative columns through size \(p-r\).
Assume the coordinatewise scalar powers \(\boldsymbol c^k\)
lie in \(V\) for every \(k\ge0\). It is enough that every linear
row annihilating \(V\) also annihilate these powers.
In particular the full-module hypothesis is sufficient but
unnecessarily strong.

Append \(r\) arbitrary strict rows with actual clocks
\(z_1,\dots,z_r\) and new weights \(\beta_1,\dots,\beta_r\),
keeping **all** old entries of \(A\) unchanged.
Let \(\boldsymbol u=\boldsymbol b'-\boldsymbol b\) be the
change in the old weights. If order \(p\) is preserved, comparison
of the true normalized order conditions gives the unnormalized
balance
\[
 \boldsymbol u^\top\boldsymbol\Phi_\tau^{\rm old}+
        \sum_j \beta_j \Phi_{\tau,j}^{\rm new}=0
        \qquad(|\tau|\le p).
\]
The relevant factorial is already a unit because the true order
equation equals one, so its cancellation requires no new unit.

Put \(F(X)=\prod_j(X-z_j)=X^r+\sum_{j<r} f_j X^j\).
A root leaf mark multiplies every actual derivative by that
stage's clock. Use the coefficients of \(F\) to combine the
versions of the preceding equation with \(0,1,\ldots,r\) additional leaves, for each base tree
\(|\tau|\le p-r\). All new contributions vanish since
\(F(z_j)=0\). Thus
\[
           \boldsymbol u^\top F(C)\boldsymbol\Phi_\tau^{\rm old}=0 .
\]
The hypothesis gives
\(\boldsymbol u^\top F(C)\boldsymbol c^k=0\) for every \(k\).
No assumption on the distinctness of the new clocks enters.

Define the scalar moment change
\(m_k=\boldsymbol u^\top\boldsymbol c^k+\sum_j\beta_j z_j^k\).
Multiplication by \(F\) yields the monic recurrence
\[
       m_{k+r}+\sum_{j<r} f_j m_{k+j}=0
       \qquad(k\ge0).
\]
The ordinary bush conditions through order \(p\) give
\(m_0,\ldots,m_{r-1}=0\). Induction therefore gives all
\(m_k=0\), without division by any new coefficient, weight
or clock difference. Hence an unpaid next bush cannot be
repaired by this append-only lift.
The proof for fewer than \(r\) new stages follows either by
using its shorter propagation budget or padding with zero
weights and unused strict rows.

## Literal split and unsplit Shanks

The [split Shanks literal](../erk-eight-ring-witness/README.md)
has 13 rows and ordinary order eight over every factorial-admissible
ring. Take \(p=8,r=2\). Its true profile space through tree size six has rank
12, rather than full rank 13; physical stages 10 and 11 have the
same rooted values through size six and both clock \(5/6\).
The replay checks these equalities for all 37 rooted trees.
All old scalar probe columns obey the same physical equality.

On the collapsed coordinate module (delete physical column 11),
two actual selected profile minors, with sizes and root-child indices
from [the exact audit](exact-audit.txt), have determinants
\[
 \frac{-6839}{655033836209580319703040000},\qquad
 \frac{-4781}{436689224139720213135360000}.
\]
Both denominators use only \(2,3,5,7\); their numerators satisfy
\(151(-6839)-216(-4781)=7\).
For each minor, its adjugate places its determinant times each
coordinate vector in the profile row span. Taking the indicated
integer linear combination after clearing the paid denominators
therefore places a unit times every coordinate vector in the span.
This proves that the collapsed profile module is full over the
factorial localization, not just over \(\mathbb Q\), and it
remains full after every allowed base change.
The full fixed-prefix profile module thus contains all the
scalar probes. The normalized ninth bush \(1/4320\) is a unit,
so it survives in every nonzero such ring and cannot be repaired
by two new rows.

The unsplit Shanks tableau has the same equality of columns
10 and 11 through size six. Its two analogous determinant
numerators are \(1127,2429\) with \(-125(1127)+58(2429)=7\);
the denominators are paid after also inverting \(41\).
The identical ninth bush defect is therefore unchanged there too.

## Other exact seeds

For both rational 11-stage order-eight seeds, dispersal and
rank-three four-support, the true size-six space also has
one-dimensional annihilator with zero sums at each scalar clock.
The same holds through size seven for both 14-stage order-nine
rational seeds. Thus the weaker scalar-span criterion applies to
two new rows over \(\mathbb Q\) in all four cases.
The [existing pure-relation certificates](../erk-short-append-witnesses/README.md)
already give stronger fixed-prefix append counts for those four
seeds; the present argument supplies independent arithmetic and a
general degree-by-degree scalar invariance criterion.
