# A fifteen-row paid chart through rooted order seven

The grade-seven chart in the signed reverse construction needs four new primitive evaluations, not five. The saved coordinate is an exact linear combination of three earlier literal rows. Their first unequal stage derivatives arise from the same primitive error, and that error is even after the shift to the centered clock.

Use the nine-row grade-five chart \(\Pi_5\) and the eleven-row grade-six chart \(\Pi_6\) in Section 2 of [the signed reverse construction](CONSTRUCTION.md). The old output has ordinary order six. The clock-one endpoint is its complete literal output row, and its derivative values are exact through tree order seven. The centered coordinate and the boundary factor are
\[
 u\coloneqq2x-1,\qquad \omega(u)\coloneqq(1-u^2)^2.
\]

## Three earlier rows realize the same primitive

One of the three rows is the new midpoint \(I_{1/2}\Pi_4\) of the grade-five construction. On arbitrary data the maps \(\Pi_5,\Pi_4\) agree in the endpoint and first two moment reads, so their difference is a multiple of the fixed polynomial
\[
 (1-u^2)(u^2-1/5).
\]
That polynomial is even and has zero integral. Its primitive at the midpoint is therefore zero, and \(I_{1/2}\Pi_5=I_{1/2}\Pi_4\) as literal padded rows, not only on lower tree data. The other two rows \(I_{2/3}\Pi_5,I_{5/6}\Pi_5\) were already specified by \(\Pi_5\). Thus the three numerical stages are all pure rows \(I_x\Pi_5\), at centered clocks \(0,1/3,2/3\).

Let \(g_t(x)\coloneqq |t|x^{|t|-1}/t!\) be the exact derivative polynomial of a rooted tree. At any child of size six the discrepancy \(g_t-\Pi_5\boldsymbol{\Phi}_t(A)\) vanishes at both endpoints and at the midpoint. These are actual exact size-six evaluations: the endpoint comes from the old output, and the midpoint is exact through size six by its self-promotion from \(\Pi_4\). The discrepancy also has zero mean because the old output is order six and the mean is one of the pinned reads. Its degree is at most five. The even part after centering is \((1-u^2)(a+b u^2)\); its midpoint value gives \(a=0\), and its mean then gives \(b=0\), using the unit \(2/15\). The discrepancy is odd. Its primitive from zero in \(x\) is therefore even, with double endpoint zeros, and has degree at most six. It belongs to \(\omega\,\operatorname{span}(1,u^2)\).

The first possible nonexact derivative at any of the three pure rows is at tree size seven and can only come from a unary root with a child of size six. All other children that can occur at a size-seven branching root have size at most five and are already exact. Thus, on every size-seven test, the discrepancy of these three old derivative reads is sampled from the same space \(\omega\,\operatorname{span}(1,u^2)\).

## A paid exact contrast and the four new rows

Let \(a_0=0,a_1=1/3,a_2=2/3\) denote the centered clocks of the three pure rows. Define the scalar read \(\ell\) by
\[
 \ell(f)\coloneqq \sum_{i=0}^2
 \frac{f(a_i)}
 {\omega(a_i)\prod_{j\ne i}(a_i^2-a_j^2)}.
\]
This is the leading coefficient of the quadratic interpolating the normalized readings \(f(a_i)/\omega(a_i)\) as a polynomial in \(a_i^2\). It vanishes on \(\omega\,\operatorname{span}(1,u^2)\). Its three weights are \(81/4,-2187/64,2187/100\), all paid; thus the same linear combination of actual old rows gives precisely \(\ell\) on the exact polynomials through tree order seven. In particular no new evaluation is needed to obtain this observation.

Append only the four rows \(I_x\Pi_6\) at centered \(-2/3,-1/3,1/3,2/3\), in that order. Their new derivative reads are exact through order seven. Use those four point evaluations, the two endpoint reads, and \(\ell\) to determine \(\Pi_7\) on polynomials of degree at most six. The six point reads have paid gaps; their remaining one-dimensional kernel is generated by
\[
 D(u)\coloneqq(u^2-1)(u^2-1/9)(u^2-4/9).
\]
Since \(D(a_1)=D(a_2)=0\) and \(D(0)=-4/81\), the missing pivot is \(\ell(D)=-1\). This chart is therefore paid and exact through all ordinary rooted trees of size seven. It has \(11+4=15\) physical rows. Its union of clocks is exactly the same as the previous sixteen-row chart: \(0,1/6,1/3,1/2,2/3,5/6,1\). No row of the first eleven stages changes.

For order 14 use the reversed orientation \(m=6,r=7\); its /18 grid already contains the newly required reverse clock \(1/6\) (index 3). For orders 15 through 17 use the orientations and grids of the master family, changing only the appropriate 16-row grade-seven chart to the 15-row one. At the extra literal merges, the pure suffix row has zero suffix part and its complete prefix part is the stated old output row. Sending its taps and mass to that earlier physical row therefore preserves every rooted weight. No corrected donor is included in these extra merges.

The construction uses only the first-excess parity of the actual three earlier rows. It does not remove the need for a genuine order-six forward chart and it does not assume that the new contrast remains exact through size eight.
