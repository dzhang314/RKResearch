# An explicit cubic residue example at 181

The fixed 15-stage signed-pair tableau has exact order ten over
`F_181[X]/(X³+85X²+125X+29)`. The cubic is irreducible. The
[tableau](literal-p181.json) gives strictly lower-triangular rows
and output weights in the ascending power basis. The
[exact replay](verify-p181.py) checks all 1,205 rooted order
conditions through order ten and the nonzero cleared eleventh-bush
defect `101+136X+106X²`. Run `python3 -B verify-p181.py` with
SymPy. The rooted replay itself uses explicit cubic multiplication,
not numerical arithmetic or a floating-point tableau.

For the signed-pair tuple, the recovered actual reflected stage
time `a` must satisfy the [published nonic](README.md). The
monic greatest common divisors of that nonic with
`X^(181^k)-X` for `k=1,2,3` are respectively 1, 1, and the
displayed cubic. Thus this fixed tuple cannot specialize over the
prime field or its quadratic extension. The two literal tail roots
and their simple quadratic relation are also checked directly.

For the fixed [Stepanov tuple](../erk10-stepanov-all-primes/README.md#exact-degree-of-this-literal-family),
the actual coefficients recover the generators with squared values
3, 7, and `147+42√7`. At 181 the first is a square, 7 is not, and
the norm of the third radicand from the quadratic field is `21³`,
again a nonsquare. Thus the Stepanov tuple needs degree four.
Consequently **the least residue degree across these two fixed
tableaux is exactly three at 181**. This realizes the degree-three
class in the [density calculation](RESIDUE-DEGREES.md). It does not
rule out a different 15-stage tableau over either smaller field.
