# Eight nonzero clocks: one new multicolor closure

Retain the rational actual split host and width eleven.
Assume the unique endpoint is physically first in
reverse (as earned earlier), exactly eight distinct
nonzero high clocks, and no physical high zero unless
expressly stated. The actual host error is
\(\boldsymbol\eta\); "multicolor" means it is not
single-clock-supported, so the
[weighted support theorem](../erk10-fifteen-split-host-weighted/README.md)
gives at least seven nonzero pooled weighted dirt masses.

**New closed case.** With a unique endpoint, no high
zero and a sole ordinary fourfold label, multicolor
dirt is impossible over \(\mathbb Q\). The proof
also eliminates some additional active-pair and
high-zero configurations stated below. It is not
a complete eight-label exclusion: in particular
the mono-host case is not claimed excluded in
the leaking grade-three branch.

## The actual grade-three leaking direction

There are seven effective visible ordinary labels.
Thus the seven positive moment tests and the unique
endpoint imply the real grade-two seed
\(\boldsymbol u\) lies in the pure label-sum
kernel \(S\). This remains true if there are high
zeros, which are unprobed individual axes in \(S\).
The nonzero \(\boldsymbol u\) gives at least one
pure dimension. The known cap is \(\dim D_4=3\).
If grade three leaks visibly, its image cannot
have rank two: those two directions would fill
the cap of two visible directions in \(D_4\),
making the visible image invariant under the mark
inside the kernel of the first positive probe.
Vandermonde would make that image zero. Thus
the grade-three aggregate rank is exactly one,
and its first mark forces grade-four aggregate
rank two. In particular
\(D_4\cap S=\langle\boldsymbol u\rangle\).
As the actual mark of \(\boldsymbol u\) is in
\(D_3\cap S\), \(\boldsymbol u\) is supported at
just one clock, possibly a high zero. It cannot
be at the unique endpoint (all defects zero
there).

Moreover \(\boldsymbol h=K\boldsymbol u\)
leaks. If it were pure it would be proportional
to \(\boldsymbol u\), and its first nonzero-\(u\)
coordinate would make that proportionality zero.
At the first live nonendpoint of a different
reverse ordinate, all prior live nonendpoint
scores are at the active reverse label \(a\ne0\):
the first live nonendpoint necessarily is active
by the literal nonzero seed there. On the
active preceding strict block \(N\),
\[
 \boldsymbol u_*=a(N-aI/2)\boldsymbol w_*,
 \qquad N\boldsymbol u_*=0
\]
would force \(N\boldsymbol w_*=0\) by strict
triangular invertibility. The paid first primitive
at the different live label would then require
nonzero tap on those same weighted donors
proportional to \(\boldsymbol u_*\), contradicting
the assumed \(K\boldsymbol u=0\).
Different live labels exist by the scalar support
bound. No assumption of nonzero individual
weights in the active group is used.

Thus \(\boldsymbol h\) has a constant cofactor
numerator on the seven ordinary visible labels
\(\mathcal T\):
\[
 H_z=\sum_{i:x_i=z}h_i=\frac{L}{z F'(z)},
 \quad L\ne0,\quad F(X)=\prod_{z\in\mathcal T}(X-z).
\]
This uses its six positive tests after dividing
out the forward \(X\) factor.

## The general one-exception closure

Assume in addition that:

* all physical high zeros, if any, have \(\eta_i=0\);
* the high dirt is constant on each ordinary group
  except possibly one group at a label \(c\).

The literal \(\boldsymbol\eta\) annihilates
\(C^j\boldsymbol h\) for \(j=0,1,2\)
(grade three plus at most two marks). Because
\(\boldsymbol h=0\) at the endpoint and
\(\eta_i=0\) at every high zero, these three
ordinary tests imply
\[
 \sum_{i:x_i=z}\eta_i h_i=\frac{G(z)}{F'(z)},
                        \qquad \deg G\le3 .
\]
At each constant group, where \(H_z\ne0\), its
common \(\eta\) is therefore \(\Delta(z)=zG(z)/L\).
The only possible exceptions to this polynomial
dirt are the endpoint \(1\) and the one
nonconstant ordinary group \(c\). The opening
origin and high zeros all have zero true dirt
and \(\Delta(0)=0\).
Multicolor dirt forces \(G\ne0\), since otherwise
only the one ordinary exceptional group could
be dirty (the live first endpoint is clean).
The actual degree-five weighted dirt tests and
degree-nine scalar exactness now give
\[
 I\bigl(X(1-X)(X-c) G(X) P(X)\bigr)=0
                             \quad(\deg P\le3).
\]
The nonzero polynomial \((X-c)G\) is degree at
most four and orthogonal to cubics under the
positive formal weight \(X(1-X)\).
It must be proportional to the previously
[verified rationally rootless Jacobi quartic](../erk10-fifteen-tilt-fullmark-closure/DENSE-JACOBI.md#three-allowed-exceptional-site-sets-all-impossible),
contradicting its rational root \(c\).
If there is no exceptional group the square
test with \(X(1-X)G\) already contradicts
nonzero \(G\). Thus both hypotheses close
the multicolor, leaking-grade-three case.

Consequences:

* With no high zero and a sole ordinary fourfold
  label, there is at most one nonconstant group,
  so the leaking case closes. If \(D_3\) is pure
  instead, it is supported on that single group.
  The exact
  [one-label nilpotent and feedback proof](../erk10-five-opening-fourfold-interior/README.md#the-first-live-singleton-forces-a-two-dimensional-nilpotent-block)
  applies unchanged: the premises used by that
  proof are \(D_3\subseteq S\) supported on one
  nonendpoint label, \(\dim D_4\le3\), and at
  least two other live singleton nonendpoint
  labels. Its old seven-clock count was used only
  to earn these premises. Here they hold directly
  (at least six live ordinary labels by the scalar
  floor). Its contradiction closes the pure case,
  even without the multicolor hypothesis.
* With no high zero, one triple and one pair,
  if grade three leaks and \(\boldsymbol u\)
  occupies the pair, its two components are
  nonzero and opposite by purity. The condition
  \(\boldsymbol\eta\cdot\boldsymbol u=0\)
  makes the two dirt values equal. The triple is
  then the only possible exceptional group;
  the multicolor case closes.
* With one high zero, one ordinary triple and
  all other visible labels singleton, if grade
  three leaks and \(\boldsymbol u\) occupies
  that high zero, \(\boldsymbol\eta\cdot\boldsymbol u=0\)
  makes the literal high-zero dirt zero.
  The triple is again the only possible
  exceptional group, and the multicolor case closes.

This exact smaller-clock transfer needs no new
numerical search or scalar root certificate.
The unclosed mixed multiplicities and the
