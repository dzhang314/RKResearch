# Additional true nonlinear tests at the split host

The [raw order-ten short words](README.md) do not use
all actual rooted-tree tests. In the split reclassification
the *same* genuine size-five early-null zero-model
source \(\boldsymbol d\) also supplies nonlinear constraints.
Writing products componentwise,
\[
 \boldsymbol w^T \boldsymbol d^{\odot 2}
 =\boldsymbol w^T C\boldsymbol d^{\odot 2}
 =\boldsymbol w^T B\boldsymbol d^{\odot 2}=0 .
\]
Indeed the product of two actual normalized tree
profiles grafted by identifying their roots has size
at most \(5+5-1=9\), with exact scalar model the
product of their scalar models. A linear combination
is bilinear in this operation, so the nominal model
of this source is zero. One more real mark or unary
fits order ten. The retained old prefix is zero
and remains zero on unary application. In the
\(p\)-host case the erased \(q\) coordinate is zero
on \(\boldsymbol d^{\odot2}\); hence the literal
extra raw \(B\)-read is on the right principal
block. No assertion for a generic raw degree-nine
budget is being made.

There is a related linear restriction on
\(\boldsymbol w\odot\boldsymbol d\):
\[
 \langle \boldsymbol w\odot\boldsymbol d,\boldsymbol a\rangle=0
 \quad\text{for all }\,
 \boldsymbol a\in V_*:=
  \left\langle\boldsymbol d,C\boldsymbol d,B\boldsymbol d,
                         T(X{\cal P}_3)\right\rangle=V .
\]
For \(f\in X{\cal P}_3\), the raw residual \(T(f)\)
is the restriction of a genuine size-at-most-six
early-null zero-model source: take a size-five
pure input \(Ef\), apply a real unary, subtract a
pure source \(EJf\). The comparison terms of degree
at most four are themselves size-five sources;
the possible \(X^5\) term is a real mark on the
size-five \(X^4\) pure source, still within size six.
At the erased \(q\) coordinate, when present, set
the source entry to zero before these unaries or
marks by subtracting the corresponding multiple
of its actual size-five silent coordinate source.
This ensures the raw restrictions are exactly the
principal row calculation without the \(q\)-tap.
There is no need for the resulting size-six source
to be zero at \(q\): its product with \(\boldsymbol d\)
is zero there. The vectors \(C\boldsymbol d,B\boldsymbol d\)
are actual size-six sources by a mark or unary
with the same restriction on the retained prefix.
Their products with \(\boldsymbol d\) therefore
fit order \(5+6-1=10\), as do the products
with each \(T(f)\).

The equality \(V_*=V\) here is earned, not assumed:
the [whole rank-two contradiction](README.md)
used only those four kinds of vectors.
It gives \(\dim V_*\ge3\) inside \(\dim V=3\),
independent of the nonlinear argument.
Thus \(\boldsymbol w\odot\boldsymbol d\) belongs
to the combined observer space \(V^\perp\).
Its first five positive moments are zero by the
literal length-five marks of \(\boldsymbol d\).
The shifted Hilbert matrix removes every nominal
component modulo \(D_4\), giving the stronger
actual consequence
\[
                         \boldsymbol w\odot\boldsymbol d\in D_4 .
\]

## Immediate consequences at the physical endpoint and repetitions

Every actual defect in \(D_4\) vanishes at the first
reverse endpoint. Because this row is live, the
last **physical** raw row has zero dirt. There are
at least six live ordinary groups in addition to
this physical live endpoint. Thus at most four
physical high scores are zero. The option of dirt
on at least seven clocks therefore has
\(\boldsymbol w\odot\boldsymbol d\ne0\).
In particular, if there are only seven distinct
nonzero clocks, no high zero and a unique endpoint,
the multicolor option is impossible: dirt misses
that unique endpoint and there are at most six
other clocks. This does not yet exclude the
monochromatic option in that geometry.

If the grade-two aggregate leaks, the now
unconditional equality takes the form
\[
   \boldsymbol w\odot\boldsymbol d
        = Q_2(\boldsymbol y)\odot\boldsymbol u,\qquad
      Q_2\in{\cal P}_2 .
\]
In particular \(Q_2(1-r)u_r=0\) at the unscored host.
If dirt is monochromatic in this branch, then
\(Q_2=0\): a nonzero quadratic multiple of
\(\boldsymbol u\) is supported on at least six
different visible labels, since the grade-two
aggregate has at least eight. Hence all nonzero
dirt coordinates have physical score zero.
At the monochromatic host the [model-blind cut](COLOUR-SUPPORT.md#what-survives-in-the-single-color-case)
requires the old host and at least one further
coordinate with dirt value \(1\). There cannot
be three nonzero dirt coordinates: on their
single-color zero-score support, all \(D_4\)
columns are restrictions of multiples of one
\(\boldsymbol u\)-coordinate vector. Three such
coordinates would give at least two independent
single-clock directions in \(V\). Thus in this
branch exactly two dirt coordinates survive,
both at the host clock, both unscored, both of
dirt value \(1\); their \(\boldsymbol u\)-entries
are nonzero and opposite (otherwise the two
coordinate axes would themselves be annihilators).

More generally under grade-two leakage, each
single-clock block of observers has rank at most
two, that of the two vectors
\((\boldsymbol w,\boldsymbol u)\) on the block.
A group with at least four physical copies, or
two separate groups with at least three each,
would therefore yield two independent single-clock
directions in \(V\), forbidden. No assigned clock
or output sign assumption is used in this count.
