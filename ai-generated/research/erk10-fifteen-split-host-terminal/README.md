# A genuine host-error probe restores the terminal cut at eleven raw stages

Retain the rational split host and the
[remaining ten-label geometry](../erk10-fifteen-split-host-weighted/README.md):
exactly one ordinary pair, a unique reverse-first
endpoint, no high zero, and a singleton unscored
host with nonzero grade-two defect. There is no
assumption of live weights at every other singleton.

This note proves the forced zero of the first new
tail donor (and of an actual tap), and gives an explicit
rational cubic for the four-tail interpolation. It closes
the vanishing-interpolation-multiplier subchart and
rules out a late cofactor zero in seven of the eight
2-integral residue classes of the pair. It does **not**
exclude the remaining ordinary-pair chart.

## Notation and actual paid inputs

Use normalized reverse ordinates as in the original
[one-pair head proof](../erk10-five-opening-nine-label-one-pair/README.md):
the eleven physical reverse rows have labels
\[
             0,1,b,c,c,d,e,f,g,h,R .
\]
All displayed labels except the pair \(c,c\) are distinct.
The old external origin is at \(H\ne0\) (not a raw
clock); \(R\) is the unscored physical host. The symbol
\(\boldsymbol\eta\) below denotes the actual size-five
host error, to distinguish it from the scalar new
site \(d\). Thus \(\eta_R=1\).
Write \(I(p)=H^{-1}\int_0^H p(Y)\,dY\) and \(J\)
for the lower primitive from \(0\). These are formal
polynomial operations with exact rational coefficients.
In these coordinates the forward origin is \(Y=H\),
so the four usual grade-five tests are all polynomials
\((H-Y)\mathcal P_3\).

Crucially \(\boldsymbol\eta\) is **one more true
grade-five test**, not just an arbitrary interpolation
device. It annihilates the actual grade-five reverse
defects by its five-letter size budget, and
\(\boldsymbol w\odot\boldsymbol\eta\) annihilates
every scalar polynomial of degree at most five.
Thus both facts can be used with
\[
       \boldsymbol t=E\nu-\gamma\boldsymbol\eta,
              \qquad \nu\in(H-Y)\mathcal P_3 .
\]
All these probes are on genuine tree profiles, including
after the earlier \(p\)-host word-budget erasure; a zero
external origin contribution to them is ensured by
\(\nu(H)=0\) and the zero-model host input.

Let \(\boldsymbol u\) be the true grade-two seed.
An affine nonzero \(A\) (degree at most one), a quartic
\(G\) (degree at most four), and the ordinary node
polynomial \(F=\prod_{z\in\{1,b,c,d,e,f,g,h,R\}}(Y-z)\)
give the two exact aggregate cofactors
\[
 \sum_{i:Y_i=z} u_i=\frac{A(z)}{(H-z)F'(z)},\qquad
 \sum_{i:Y_i=z}\eta_i u_i=\frac{G(z)}{F'(z)}.
\]
They are the same cofactor identities as in the weighted
packet, after the change of coordinate and scalar
normalization. At a singleton this gives, without
inverting either \(A(z)\) or its score,
\[
 t_z u_z=\frac{\Pi(z)}{F'(z)},\qquad
 \Pi=A\,\frac{\nu}{H-Y}-\gamma G,\quad \deg\Pi\le4 .       \tag{1}
\]

## Literal head donors and an extra transport identity

Here \(c=4b\kappa/(2\kappa-1)\) with
\(\kappa\ne0,1/2\), and the head predicts
\(w_z/u_z=N(z)/(z^2(z-c))\) at \(z=1,b,d\), with
the affine \(N\) of the
[three-anchor receipt](../erk10-five-opening-nine-label-one-pair/README.md#the-exceptional-repeat-supplies-two-actual-early-null-donors).
The nonzero \(u_d\) guard follows even for this
larger cofactor: if \(u_d=0\), the literal after-pair
five-by-five matrix in the prior proof has all
homogeneous right sides, forcing \(w_d=0\).
Then the sixth reverse row is blind on the full
grade-four and nominal observers. The five
remaining raw forward-prefix clocks are distinct
and nonzero, so the
[local rank-three witness](../erk10-fifteen-split-host-rank/LARGEST-CLOCKS.md#three-annihilators-independent-off-the-early-reverse-head)
excludes this blindness. The \(1,b\) entries
already have \(u\ne0\) by the earlier head proof.

Put \(M=Y^2(Y-c)^2\), \(G_0=(Y-d)M'/4\), and define
the actual vectors
\[
\begin{split}
 \boldsymbol L&=\boldsymbol w\odot M'(\boldsymbol Y)/4
                   +(2-c)(\boldsymbol Y-c)\odot\boldsymbol u,\\
 \boldsymbol Z&=\boldsymbol w\odot M(\boldsymbol Y)
                   -(\boldsymbol Y-c)\odot N(\boldsymbol Y)
                                           \odot\boldsymbol u,\\
 \boldsymbol G_*&=(\boldsymbol Y-d)\odot\boldsymbol L .
\end{split}
\]
The two earlier head ratios make \(\boldsymbol L\)
zero through the first five rows, and the factors at
\(d\) and the head relation make \(\boldsymbol G_*\)
and \(\boldsymbol Z\) zero through the first six.
There is a stronger exact identity
\[
                              4K\boldsymbol L=\boldsymbol Z . \tag{2}
\]
Indeed \(K\boldsymbol L-\boldsymbol w\odot M/4\)
is grade four, hence a scalar quadratic multiple of
\(\boldsymbol u\). On the first six rows strictness
gives \(K\boldsymbol L=0\), and the three nonzero
\(u\)-anchors at \(1,b,d\) identify the quadratic
as \(-(Y-c)N(Y)/4\). In particular this is an
identity on all physical rows. The exact replay
checks
\[
 N(Y)(Y-c/2)+(2-c)Y(Y-c)
                  =-\frac{c^2}{2b-c}(Y-1)(Y-b),
\]
with \(2b-c=-2b/(2\kappa-1)\ne0\). At \(d\) the
ratio \(L_d/u_d\) is nonzero by this factorization.

## Augmented four-tail blind test

Choose the unique polynomial
\(\nu\in(H-Y)\mathcal P_3\) with
\(\nu(z)=\eta_z\) at \(z=f,g,h,R\). Existence
follows simply by cubic interpolation after dividing
only by the nonzero \(H-z\) at these singletons.
It is nonzero because \(\nu(R)=1\).

Take \(\gamma=1\) in the test \(\boldsymbol t\).
It kills all these four physical tails. Since
\(\boldsymbol Z,\boldsymbol G_*\) vanish through
\(d\), their \(K\)-reads vanish through \(e\);
hence the grade-five tests
\(K\boldsymbol Z-\boldsymbol w\odot JM\) and
\(K\boldsymbol G_*-\boldsymbol w\odot JG_0\) give
\[
      I(\nu JM)=I(\nu JG_0)=0 .
\]
The \(\boldsymbol\eta\) part of each nominal
primitive pairing is zero by its true moments,
and the scalar precision suffices since the two
primitives have degree at most five. Consequently
\(I(\nu(Y-d)M)=0\), using \(4JG_0=(Y-d)M-JM\).

There is an exact cardinal simplification for
**any** \(\boldsymbol t=E\nu-\gamma\boldsymbol\eta\)
as above, not just the chosen one:
\[
             I(\nu Y^j M)
               =\sum_i t_iY_i^j Z_i,\qquad j=0,1 .       \tag{3}
\]
To see this, scalar precision and the ordinary
dirt moments identify its left side with
\(\sum_i t_iY_i^j w_iM(Y_i)\).
Subtract the right side, then use (1) at every
ordinary singleton. Both entries at the repeated
pair have the vanishing \(Y-c\) factor regardless
of their individual \(t_i\), and the initial
endpoint contributes zero because \(u=0\) there.
The difference is exactly
\[
       \sum_{z\in\mathrm{roots}(F)}
                 \frac{\Pi(z)z^j(z-c)N(z)}{F'(z)}=0 .
\]
The numerator has degree at most \(7\), so the
ordinary nine-site cofactor annihilates it. No
smoothness or sign of physical scores is involved.

For the chosen four-tail test, (3) and the blind
moment \(I(\nu(Y-d)M)=0\) say
\((e-d)t_e Z_e=0\), and in particular
\[
                    I(\nu M)=I(\nu YM)=0 .                \tag{4}
\]
By (1), its \(\Pi\) vanishes at the four tails,
so is \(\sigma(Y-f)(Y-g)(Y-h)(Y-R)\). If
\(\sigma\ne0\), then \(t_e u_e\ne0\),
and (4) forces \(Z_e=0\).

### Why the interpolation multiplier cannot vanish

If \(\sigma=0\), then \(G=A\,\nu/(H-Y)\).
The [rational Jacobi exit](../erk10-fifteen-split-host-weighted/README.md#the-unscored-host-necessarily-has-nonzero-grade-two)
already excludes this polynomial dirt unless \(A\)
is genuinely affine with a single root at a live
ordinary singleton \(a\), and the polynomial
\(\nu(a)\ne0\) there. Indeed otherwise the only
possible weighted exceptions to \(\nu\) are the
pair and endpoint, so the degree-nine Jacobi
test applies. At that live root the actual dirt
is zero, because \(w_a\eta_a=Q(a)u_a=0\).
Thus \(a\) cannot be one of the four matched
tails. It cannot be \(1,b,d\) (their \(u\)
is nonzero); the only remaining location is \(e\).
But then \(t_e=\nu(e)\ne0\) and
\(Z_e=w_e M(e)\ne0\), contradicting
\((e-d)t_e Z_e=0\). This rules out \(\sigma=0\).

Therefore the **actual** \(u_e\ne0\), \(Z_e=0\),
and \(K_{e,d}=0\): equation (2) reads at \(e\)
using the single prior nonzero \(L_d\).
The ratio formula at \(e\) now agrees with the
anchors, and \(L_e\ne0\) by its displayed
factorization. None of these conclusions requires
that \(w_e\ne0\).

Two more ordinary blind moments follow. Set
\(S=(H-Y)(Y-g)(Y-h)(Y-R)\). The grade-five
\(K\boldsymbol Z\) response is zero through
\(f\), so the pure test \(S\) gives \(I(SJM)=0\).
Its two identities (3) have support only at \(f\),
giving
\[
           I(SJM)=0,\qquad I(S(Y-f)M)=0 .                  \tag{5}
\]
Unlike the earlier ten-row closure, \(\nu\) need
not be a product of the other tail factors or
a linear polynomial times a single cubic test.
Thus (4)–(5) do not simply reduce to the
old rational nonic.

## Explicit cubic and a uniform rational residue exclusion

Rescale now to \(y=Y/H\) and \(C=c/H\);
let \(M_0(y)=y^2(y-C)^2\). Equations (4) and
\(I(\nu JM)=0\) say that the nonzero cubic
factor of the rescaled \(\nu\) belongs to the
kernel of the three by four matrix of the forms
\[
          \int_0^1 (1-y)y^j (M_0,\ yM_0,\ JM_0)\,dy,
                         \qquad 0\le j\le3 .
\]
The exact minor calculation shows that this
kernel is the line of
\[
\begin{split}
 V_C(y)={}&75C^3-108C^2+45C-5\\
 &-3(175C^3-244C^2+97C-10)y\\
 &+3(350C^3-476C^2+183C-18)y^2\\
 &-15(42C^3-56C^2+21C-2)y^3 .
\end{split}
\]
The first and last coefficients are nonzero for
rational \(C\) by projective tests modulo \(2\)
and \(17\), respectively (and \(C\ne0\) is already
guarded). Thus the moment matrix has rank three,
and \(\nu\) has degree exactly four.
The rescaled \((1-y)V_C(y)\) is normalized
by \(\nu(R)=1\), which in particular
requires \(V_C(R/H)\ne0\).

If \(C\) is 2-integral and \(C\not\equiv2\bmod8\),
\(V_C\) has **no rational root**, by the exact
homogeneous modulo-eight certificate. For example
at an integral odd \(C\), the reduction is the
projectively rootless \(y^3+y^2+1\); the same
modulo-eight computation covers the allowable
even residues and nonintegral \(y\).
A live singleton at a root of \(A\) must now be
impossible: after the head and the \(u_e\) result,
such a root could only be \(f,g,h\); its actual
\(\eta\)-value is zero by \(w\eta=Qu\), forcing
a rational root of \(V_C\). An unscored ordinary
singleton at such a root was already excluded
by the Jacobi argument, since \(G\) also vanishes
there. The repeated pair's pooled numerator
may still vanish, which is not an individual
singleton conclusion.

This arithmetic subcase does **not** assume
\(A(H)\ne0\) (equivalently the old forward \(A(0)\)).
If \(A(H)=0\), one instead knows \(A\) is a nonzero
multiple of \(H-Y\) and \(G(H)\ne0\) by the earlier
Jacobi exit, so all ordinary aggregate \(u\)
values are nonzero. In this exceptional branch
\(Q\) is only required to vanish at \(R\), not at
\(H\); the terminal proof and all forms (3)–(5)
are unchanged. When \(A(H)\ne0\), the more
specific \(Q=\lambda(Y-H)(Y-R)\) is still
available for follow-on cardinal arithmetic.
