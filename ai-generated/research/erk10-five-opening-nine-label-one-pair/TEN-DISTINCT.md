# Ten distinct high labels are impossible over every paid field

Keep the same actual five-opening short word budget, high width
ten, nonzero forward clocks, and unique clock 1 in the first
reverse row. If all ten high forward clocks are distinct, no such
continuation exists over any field with \(10!\ne0\), regardless of
which physical scores vanish.

There are now nine interior scalar sites. The actual grade-two
defect \(\boldsymbol u\) is nonzero by the first live interior
score. Its aggregate satisfies the seven positive forward moments
and hence is the interior cofactor times a scalar polynomial
of degree at most one. There are no repeats, so this says
\(\boldsymbol u\) is nonzero at at least eight distinct labels.
Its first two clock marks are independent and give the full
grade-four space of dimension at most three. Thus every actual
grade-four profile is a quadratic multiplier times \(\boldsymbol u\).

In fact every grade-three profile is an **affine** multiplier
times \(\boldsymbol u\): its clock mark is still grade four,
and a nonzero quadratic term would produce a cubic multiplier,
independent of the three grade-four basis profiles on at least
four distinct nonzero-\(\boldsymbol u\) labels. This argument
works whether the original cofactor multiplier was constant or
genuinely linear, and does not assume that \(\boldsymbol u\)
is nonzero on every label.

Now apply the [actual first-four row argument](README.md#leaking-grade-two-normalized-first-three-interiors).
The first three \(u\)-entries cannot vanish by early observer
blindness and the two mark reads; that proof used no global
cofactor nonvanishing at those individual locations. All first
four interior labels are distinct here. The raw compatibility
determinant therefore forces \(\kappa=1/2\) even when the
fourth \(u\)-entry might initially be zero. Its causal induction
contradicts the first scalar \(u\) moment. The argument stops
before any nonic, so is uniform over paid fields.

## Complete rational exclusion of this decoder architecture

Consider this particular five-opening decoder with ten physical high rows, all high forward clocks nonzero, the unique high clock (1) at the first reverse row, and only the opening origin allowed any output weight among the opening rows. There is **no rational fifteen-stage continuation** in this architecture.

The remaining case of at most six distinct high labels is excluded by the stronger [two-endpoint rational quadrature obstruction](../rational-endpoint-moment-nine/README.md), not just the origin-only bound. The allowed set of scalar nodes is the opening origin together with the distinct high labels, hence contains both (0) and (1), even when one or both endpoint weights vanish. If there were at most six high labels the total allowed set would have at most seven sites. The degree-nine scalar rule imposed by order ten needs at least eight such rational sites. There are no sign or nonzero-weight hypotheses on this argument. Seven high labels are excluded by the [complete multiplicity census](../erk10-five-opening-triple-pair/ZERO-TRIPLE-POOL.md); eight by the [one-triple](../erk10-five-opening-eight-label-triple/README.md) and [two-pair](../erk10-five-opening-eight-label-pairs-zero/README.md) proofs; nine by the main packet; ten by the argument above.

This closes only the stated architecture over the rationals. A rational tableau with a different opening, nonorigin weights in the opening, a zero high clock, or a missing, repeated, or displaced endpoint is not ruled out here. The general real interval remains 14–15, and the general rational interval remains 14–16.
