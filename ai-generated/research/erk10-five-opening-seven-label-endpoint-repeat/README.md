# Seven labels, exactly two endpoint copies: the last double-endpoint case

Use the actual five-opening order-ten short-word budget,
ten physical high rows, all high forward clocks nonzero,
and only the opening origin as a possibly scored opening
row. Let exactly two high forward clocks equal \(1\),
one being first in reverse, and let there be exactly
seven distinct high labels. Then the continuation is
impossible over \(\mathbb Q\), with arbitrary physical
and pooled scores and any placement of the second endpoint.
The other multiplicity is one interior triple or two
interior pairs. This is an architecture exclusion, not
an unrestricted stage lower bound.

All physical rank, chronology, and cofactor arguments
below use an arbitrary field with \(10!\ne0\); the last
nonic gives the rational restriction, not a uniform
paid-field exclusion in its leakage branch.
Use the raw reversed \(K=B^T\), ordinate \(y=1-x\),
defect spaces \(D_j\), rank-three bound and early
contrast as in the
[first endpoint jump](../erk10-five-opening-first-endpoint-jump/README.md).
There are at least five high labels with live pooled
scores, by shifted Hilbert \(5\). In particular at least
one interior singleton is live.

## Aggregate-exact grade three

With seven high labels, all grade-two aggregates vanish
by \(X,\ldots,X^7\). The first physical endpoint has
all positive defect values zero by causality, so each
grade-two defect, including the nonzero \(\boldsymbol u\),
is individually zero on both endpoint copies. Earlier
interior scores at nonzero \(y\) are all zero at the first
live interior row, so its \(u=-w y^2/2\ne0\).

Assume also that \(D_3\) has zero aggregate. Then its
endpoint coordinates vanish individually as well, and
\(D_3\) is contained in the two-dimensional zero-sum
space \(S\) of the interior repeated labels. It cannot
have dimension one: its marked \(\boldsymbol u\) would
confine support to the first live label \(a\), and at the
first live interior row of a different clock its compulsory
nonzero scalar tap would contradict \(K\boldsymbol u=0\).
On \(a\)-rows before that target, only scalar label \(a\)
is available in positive degrees, so there
\(g=a u+a^3w/6\) and proportionality
\(\boldsymbol g\in\langle\boldsymbol u\rangle\)
makes their score vector proportional to
\(\boldsymbol u\). The endpoint copies have \(y=u=0\)
and cannot alter the comparison. Therefore \(D_3=S\).

* If there is one interior triple, the
  [single repeated label nilpotent and feedback proof](../erk10-five-opening-fourfold-interior/README.md)
  applies: the first live singleton \(t\) pays \(t=3a/2\),
  and \((K\boldsymbol g)_t\ne0\) forces the two independent
  primitive restrictions before it. The local proof uses
  only that the two first live singleton targets are clean
  in \(\boldsymbol u,\boldsymbol g,\boldsymbol h\), that
  the positive scalar donors before the first are all at
  \(a\), and that \(\dim D_4\le3\). Both physical endpoints
  contribute neither positive scalar input nor any \(D_3\)
  input, so the proof remains unchanged even if the late
  endpoint is between the two live singletons. There are
  at least three live singleton labels by the scalar
  support bound (only endpoint and triple can occupy the
  other two live labels).
* For two pairs use the
  [raw aggregate-exact two-pair argument](../erk10-five-opening-eight-label-pairs-zero/README.md#all-grade-three-aggregates-exact).
  With both separate opposite modes in \(D_3\), an
  ordinary nonzero-ordinate target means a live singleton
  or a live-mass pair closure. There are at least two.
  Both pairs must have opened before the first ordinary
  target; with only one label the available \(u\)-mode
  is proportional to its one live scalar tap (or folded
  zero-total pair), which would make \(K u=0\) incompatible
  with the nonzero first primitive. The first ordinary
  mean is thus a nonzero calibration of \(D_4/S\) by
  its two tracked modes. At the next ordinary target
  all added untracked scalar input is at the reference
  label alone; the endpoints still contribute neither
  positive scalar nor \(D_3\) input regardless of their
  position or scores. The same three scalar equations
  force the two target clocks to be equal.

Thus aggregate-exact grade three cannot occur.

## The sole leakage line and the actual early heads

If grade three leaks, its aggregate is the seven-high-site
cofactor line; its first mark has independent aggregate.
This gives \(D_4\cap S=\langle\boldsymbol u\rangle\).
The support of \(\boldsymbol u\) is one interior repeated
label \(a\): two distinct pure label directions from it
and its mark would exceed the rank bound in addition
to the two leakage directions.
The composed defect \(\boldsymbol h\) itself leaks:
otherwise it is proportional to \(\boldsymbol u\) and zero
at the first live interior row, hence zero globally.
On the earlier \(a\)-copies before the first live
different interior label, the raw equation
\((N-aI/2)N\boldsymbol w_*=0\) for their strict block
forces \(N\boldsymbol w_*=0\). That different label would
then need a nonzero scalar tap to a multiple of
\(\boldsymbol u\), contrary to \(K u=0\).
Consequently \(D_4=\langle\boldsymbol u,\boldsymbol h,
\boldsymbol y\odot\boldsymbol h\rangle\), the same
three actual low identities \(g=(2a/3)u+\beta h\),
\(k=(a^2/2)u+\phi(y)h\), \(Kh=\psi(y)h\) apply,
and the first interior is a live active opener. An
inactive first interior or zero score would have
\(w=u=h=0\), an early observer null coordinate.
The crucial extra condition here is that \(\boldsymbol h\)
has **nonzero second endpoint value**, by the seven-site
cofactor on all high labels and the first endpoint’s
zero entry.

Before the late endpoint, the exact nonzero-ordinate
classifications of the
[one-triple](../erk10-five-opening-eight-label-triple/README.md)
and
[two-pair](../erk10-five-opening-eight-label-pairs-zero/README.md)
early heads apply. Here is the check that potential early
endpoint insertion does not add a hidden branch.
After a single live active opener, \(u=0\) at the late
endpoint kills its sole scalar tap and hence \(h=0\).
After two leading active copies the same scalar argument
kills the total \(a\)-tap, and \(\beta=-2/3\) gives \(h=0\)
at the endpoint. If they have zero first effective tap,
the two rows would already have been an early
observer contrast. After \(a,b\) with \(a\) still open,
one earliest active \(u\) donor gives
\(h_z=w_z a z^2(2z-3b)/(4b)\), zero at \(z=0\).
For the three-interior prefixes \(a,a,b\), \(a,b,a\),
\(a,b,c\), the two literal left coefficients at \(z=0\)
are the
[previously replayed pairs](../erk10-five-opening-eight-label-endpoint-repeat/README.md#zero-grade-two-aggregate-the-late-endpoint-cannot-have-the-required-jump).
None can vanish simultaneously, so \(h=0\), impossible.
These tests do not divide by the late endpoint score.

The only possible first-five physical reverse rows are
therefore one of
\[
       (0,a,b,a,a),\ a=2b,\qquad
       (0,p,p,q,q),\ q=2p,\qquad
       (0,\alpha,p,q,q),\ q=2p
\]
arising from the respective old one-triple or two-pair
head. In the first case put \(p=b,q=a\).
A second endpoint immediately next is impossible as well.
At \(Y=0\) all nominal positive primitive terms vanish,
and the adjusted cubic's left coefficient multiplying \(h\)
is, respectively,
\(q/2\), \(q\), or \(pq/\alpha\).
These are all nonzero. They are the evaluations at zero
of the same next-row scalar identities
\((q-Y)/2,\ q-Y,\ -p(Y-q)/\alpha\)
from the literal donor polynomials.
Thus the sixth physical ordinate \(c\) is nonzero.
In the third head it may be the active closure; the other
two have only singleton possibilities.

## The same terminal collapse, with the endpoint in the cofactor

Rescale the actual grade-three profile as before:
use \(\boldsymbol H=\boldsymbol h/2\) for the first triple
head, \(\boldsymbol H=\boldsymbol h\) for immediate active
closure, and \(\boldsymbol H=(p/\alpha)\boldsymbol h\)
for the still-open active pair. In all three heads the
actual pooled values are
\[
                  H_p=-W_p p^3/4,\qquad
                  H_c=W_c c^2(c-q)/4 .
\]
These are the same raw computations from the previous
packets; a \(c\) that closes \(\alpha\) is treated by
summing its two rows, with \(u\)-aggregate zero, not by
dividing by its own second score.

Let \(M=Y^2(Y-q)^2\), \(G=(Y-c)M'/4\)
so \(M'/4=Y(Y-p)(Y-q)\).
Use the same actual donor corrections to \(\boldsymbol
G_*=\boldsymbol w G\) and
\(\boldsymbol Z_*=(\boldsymbol y-q)\boldsymbol H-
\boldsymbol w M/4\) from the three archived heads.
For the triple no correction is needed.
For the early active pair subtract \(p^2\boldsymbol u/2\)
from \(Z_*\); \(G_*\) already vanishes.
For the open \(\alpha\)-pair add
\(2G(\alpha)\boldsymbol u/\alpha^2\) to \(G_*\) and
subtract \((\alpha-q)^2\boldsymbol u/2\) from \(Z_*\).
They vanish on the first six physical rows, and their
\(K\)-actions with nominal \(JG\), \(-JM/4\) removed
are grade-five tests. If \(\alpha\) closes later,
its actual \(Z_*\) at that second copy is exactly
\((\alpha-q)H_\alpha-W_\alpha M(\alpha)/4\) by
the raw closing-coordinate identity, independent of
its score. Thus every later nonzero ordinate is a
single physical donor for the **pooled** expression
in \(Z_*\), including a delayed \(\alpha\) closure.

Write the last four physical reverse labels as
\(s,t,u,v\), one of which is \(0\) (the late endpoint).
They are pairwise distinct and distinct from \(p,q,c\).
Let \(\nu=(Y-1)(Y-t)(Y-u)(Y-v)\).
The two \(\nu\) tests show
\(I(\nu JG)=I(\nu JM)=0\), using the actual zero
donors and killing the last three rows.
Hence \(I(\nu(Y-c)M)=0\).
With \(P_8=\nu(Y-s)G/Y\), every distinct scalar
label occurs once; the quotient removes the one
extra displayed high zero ordinate. Put
\(\mu_i=I(P_8(Y)/(Y-i))\).
Cardinal exactness gives \(W_i=\mu_i/P_8'(i)\).
The seven-high-site **grade-three** cofactor includes
the late endpoint; hence \(H_i=-L/P_8'(i)\) for
a common nonzero \(L\), again with **no** extra numerator
\(i\). The displayed early anchors give
\[
              D(p)=D(c)=-4L,\qquad
              D(i):=\mu_i\,i^2(i-q).
\]
Because \(\nu(Y-s)\) includes that late zero factor,
the scalar polynomial \(\nu(Y-s)M\) has only
the two contributions at \(p,c\), with numerators
\(D(p),D(c)\) and opposite two-site cofactors.
Its integral is zero. As \(s\ne c\), comparing with
the blind derivative moment yields
\(I(\nu M)=I(\nu YM)=0\).

If \(s=0\), however, \(\nu M\) has no such tail-zero
factor and its two numerators are \(D(i)/i\) instead.
They contribute \(D(p)[1/(p(p-c))+1/(c(c-p))]
=-D(p)/(pc)\ne0\), contradiction over paid fields.
If \(s\ne0\), then \(\nu\) contains the late zero
factor and \(I(\nu M)\) has the three contributions
at \(p,c,s\) with numerators \(D(i)\).
The three-site cofactor and the zero integral imply
\(D(s)=-4L\). Thus the literal \(Z_*{}_s=0\),
including when it is the delayed \(\alpha\)-closure,
by its raw pooled value. Finally test with
\(U=(Y-1)(Y-u)(Y-v)\): last two are killed and
all earlier test rows use zero input through \(s\).
It gives \(I(U JM)=0\), and \(\nu=(Y-t)U\) gives
\(I(YU JM)=0\). These are the
[old rational nonic equations](../erk10-fifteen-two-pair-rational-closure/README.md#the-unconditional-collapse-and-rational-exclusion)
with small parameter \(p\), allowing either tail
root of \(U\) to be zero. Their projectively rootless
nonic excludes the rational chart. No score was normalized
at the duplicated endpoint or at a delayed active closure.

## Combined exactly-two conclusion

With two copies of \(1\) and ten physical high rows,
there are at most nine distinct high labels.
The anchored degree-nine rational scalar floor excludes
at most six. This packet treats seven, the
[eight-label proof](../erk10-five-opening-eight-label-endpoint-repeat/README.md)
treats eight, and the
[nine-label paid theorem](../erk10-five-opening-nine-label-endpoint-repeat/README.md)
treats nine. Thus **all positions of exactly two high
copies of \(1\)** are excluded over \(\mathbb Q\) in
this actual five-opening architecture, whenever one copy
is first in reverse and every high clock is nonzero.
Three or more noncontiguous endpoint copies remain
separate.
