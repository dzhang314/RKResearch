# Stepanov's fifteen-stage ERK10 tableau above every admissible prime

The [already kernel-checked literal tableau](../erk10-stepanov-native-upper/README.md)
gives exact order ten in 15 explicit stages over
\(\mathbb F_{p^4}\) for **every prime \(p>7\)**. It also works over
the unramified degree-four extension of \(\mathbb Z_p\), and at
every finite precision in that coefficient ring. The same construction
gives order ten in 15 stages over every algebraically closed field
in which \(10!\) is invertible. The unit order-eleven defect remains
nonzero after transfer to any nonzero algebra over these coefficient
rings. This does not give the tableau over every prime field itself
or over the factorial localization.

## Roots at the good primes, cancellation at the bad primes

Use the [archived source](../erk10-stepanov-five-opening/stepanov15-source.txt)
with coordinates \(1,x,y,xy,z,xz,yz,xyz\), where
\(x^2=3, y^2=7, z^2=147+42y\). Let \(D\) be the common source
denominator in this basis, and put \(S=\mathbb Z[1/10!]\).
The norm identity
\[
(147+42y)(147-42y)=21^3
\]
means the third radicand is nonzero modulo every admissible prime.
For \(p\nmid D\), the first two square roots already lie in
\(\mathbb F_{p^2}\); a further quadratic step suffices for the third.
All three roots are simple, so they lift by Hensel's lemma to the
unramified degree-four ring of integers over \(\mathbb Z_p\). Since
\(D\) is a unit in that ring, the existing exact root-conditional
order certificate applies directly.

The [factor and branch table](seeds.json) accounts for every prime
factor of \(D\): 21 primes in all, of which 17 exceed seven. For 16
of those 17, the selected roots lie in \(\mathbb Z_p\), and the
tableau's actual coefficients are integral there even though \(D\)
is not a unit. At \(p=523\), choose \(x^2=3\) in the unramified
quadratic ring, and lift scalar roots \(y\equiv141\) and
\(u\equiv218\) with \(3u^2=147+42y\); take \(z=ux\). This
gives the remaining integral branch. Both the degree-one and
degree-two unramified rings embed into the degree-four one.

For the exact cancellation test write \(D=p^e D_0\) with
\(D_0\) a local unit. Each source entry is a polynomial numerator
with integer coefficients divided by \(D\). Lift the seed roots
to precision \(p^{e+2}\); the replay evaluates **every** numerator
and checks divisibility by \(p^e\) (both coordinates at 523).
Because the approximate roots agree with the simple exact Hensel
roots to that precision, these are exact integrality checks, not
inferences from low-order tableau replays. The normalized equations
are valid in the local fraction field by the root-conditional
certificate, and hence in its integer subring by injection.
The cleared eleventh-bush defect is exactly \(1/52920\), a unit
at every \(p>7\). Larger precision follows from the exact roots and
integrality, not from extrapolating the finite checks.

## A single faithfully flat coefficient extension

In the characteristic-zero coefficient field \(K=\mathbb Q(x,y,z)\),
let \(B\) be the \(S\)-subalgebra generated by the roots and all
actual tableau entries. It is finitely generated over the Noetherian
ring \(S\), hence finitely presented, and is torsion-free over the
PID \(S\), hence flat. For each maximal ideal \((p)\) of \(S\),
the integral branch constructed above maps \(B\) to a local
integer ring with nonzero residue. In particular \(B/pB\ne0\).
Thus \(B\) is faithfully flat over \(S\). The literal tableau
lies in \(B\); its validated polynomial equations descend along
\(B\hookrightarrow K\) and its next-order defect is already an
\(S\)-unit. This yields a 15-stage method after faithfully flat,
finitely presented coefficient extension of any nonzero paid
coefficient ring by base change. It does **not** descend a tableau
to \(S\) itself.

## Exact degree of this literal family

The clock entries at physical rows 8 and 7 are respectively
\(1/2+z/42\) and \(1/2+zx(y-2)/126\). They recover \(z,y,x\)
in that order: use \(y=(z^2-147)/42\), and the factors \(z\)
and \(y-2\) are nonzero in every characteristic under discussion,
with \((y-2)(y+2)=3\). Over \(\mathbb Q(y)\) the norms of
\(147+42y\) and \((147+42y)/3\) are \(21^3\) and \(21^3/9\),
neither a rational square. A square in
\(\mathbb Q(y)(x)\) lying in \(\mathbb Q(y)\) must be a square
or three times a square there, by comparing its \(x\)-coefficient.
Thus the final step is quadratic and \([K:\mathbb Q]=8\).
The alternative radicand has root \(21xy/z\), so the field is
Galois. The map fixing \(x\), sending \(y\) to \(-y\) and
\(z\) to \(21xy/z\) has order four; changing the sign of \(x\)
alone gives an involution conjugating this map to its inverse.
The group is dihedral of order eight.

For \(p\nmid D\), the **minimum extension degree of this literal
family** is 1 when all three roots are available in \(\mathbb F_p\),
4 when 3 is a square and 7 is a nonsquare in \(\mathbb F_p\),
and 2 otherwise. When 7 is a nonsquare, the third radicand has norm
\(21^3\) from \(\mathbb F_{p^2}\); it is a square there exactly
when this norm is square in \(\mathbb F_p\), that is, when 3 is
also a nonsquare. When 7 is a square, the radicand is a base
field element and becomes a square by degree two. Clock recovery
gives necessity. These are not lower bounds for other tableaux.

## Verification

Run `python3 -B check.py ../erk10-stepanov-five-opening/stepanov15-source.txt seeds.json`
with SymPy available. The replay independently decodes and hashes the
source, checks the full factorization with recursive Lucas primality
certificates, checks every cancellation, and computes the 1,205 literal
rooted conditions and the unit bush failure modulo \(p\) and \(p^2\)
for each of the 61 scalar cases and the quadratic case at 523: 124
direct exact replays. The [Lean scalar file](../../lean/RKCert/ReviewedNative/ERK10StepanovDomainScalars.lean)
checks the norm and recovery identities on all four target releases;
the new local integrality and faithfully flat extension arguments are
mathematical extensions of the original tableau's native theorem.
