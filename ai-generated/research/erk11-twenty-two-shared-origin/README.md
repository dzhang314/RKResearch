# Universal exact order eleven in twenty-two explicit stages

The [literal tableau](tableau.json) is strictly explicit and has
exact order eleven in **22 stages over \(\mathbb Z[1/11!]\)**.
It therefore works over every nontrivial commutative ring in
which \(11!\) is a unit. This improves the
[23-stage general-ring upper bound](../erk-signed-reverse/tableau-11-weighted.json).
No minimum or new real-algebraic upper is asserted.

The common denominator is
\(941072888448000=2^{10}3^{11}5^3 7^3 11^2\), dividing \((11!)^3\).
Let \(s\) be the five-vertex bush and \(u=[[[s]]]\) its
three successive unary grafts. Take the tree whose root
has three leaf children and child \(u\). Its size is twelve,
its tree factorial is \(20160\), and its raw output is
\(2657/53887680\). The factorial-scaled residual from one is
\(-16/2673\), a unit in the same ring. The two other exact
twelfth-order witnesses in the fixture also have coprime
numerators, but the single unit witness suffices.

## Move the old ports and share the reverse origin

Start from the
[weighted reverse construction](../erk-signed-reverse/WEIGHTED-ORDERS-11-16.md)
with its nine-row forward opening and six-row reverse decoder.
Scale the **forward** opening by \(\xi=2/3\), leaving its
literal origin, endpoint and half-port at \(0,2/3,1/3\).
Scale its polynomial readouts on \(X^j\) by \(\xi^j\),
leaving their actual coefficient rows unchanged. The first
forward discrepancy has the usual endpoint zeros at
\(0,\xi\) and its mean on \([0,\xi]\) is zero. With
\(v=2y-1\) the centered reverse clock, its reflected
endpoint factor is \((1-v)(v+1/3)\); the late primitive
correction uses the scaled forward mean.

Use an asymmetric scalar rule at the eleven **forward**
clocks
\[
 (-2/3,-1/2,-1/3,0,1/3,1/2,2/3,5/6,1,7/6,4/3).
\]
It has precision ten by exact cardinal integration and all
differences are units in \(\mathbb Z[1/11!]\).
The reverse origin, \(y=0\), is the forward clock \(1\),
where the scalar mass is the unit \(243/1925\).
Unlike the previous chart, this is **not** one of the three
old forward ports.

Retune the two movable reverse clocks to \(a=5/3\) and
\(r=3/2\). The six-stage reverse raw clocks are
\(0,a,1/2,1,1,r\). The initial four-stage seed still has
ordinary order four. The scalar high observation represented
on its first six raw stages is
\[
 \mathcal R(P)=P(r)-16r^2(1-r)^2 P(1/2)
                    -2r^2(r-1)(2r-1)P(1).
\]
The replay explicitly checks that observation through size
five on actual rooted vectors. The endpoint observations
and the seed mean then give the same cubic reverse decoder
through size four. The forward decoder and all exact
first-excess reads are checked on actual rooted inputs as well.
In centered reverse chronology the three live scalar probes
are at \(-5/3,-4/3,-2/3\), and the four late correction
sites are \(0,5/3,2,7/3\). The weighted probes plus the
mixed read have a unit-ideal maximal-minor certificate.
The six-row raw reverse matrix and all these identities
are regenerated by the companion.

The old scheme balances each live reverse donor by a
\(+1,-1\) pair. At the reverse origin the donor and its
balancing copy have the zero incoming row, and so does the
pure scalar row at that site. Only the donor is ever read
as a reverse input. The other two entries can be absorbed
into its mass:
\[
             +1-1+243/1925=243/1925.
\]
This preserves its weighted elementary reverse derivative
at every grade. The origin's live forward-transpose row
is then normalized by the actual mass \(243/1925\) rather
than by 1; this division is paid. It removes the redundant
physical pure row at forward clock 1 without removing a
readable reverse donor.

Finally the unchanged full-row merger algorithm redirects
every outgoing coefficient and output weight at the three
scaled forward ports. The verifier checks that the
corresponding complete rows equal old rows 1, 8 and 9,
respectively. This gives 22 distinct strict rows.
The method uses signed masses and internal clocks outside
\([0,1]\); no stability or positivity assertion is made.

## Exact reconstruction and independent order verification

The reconstruction pins the unmodified neighboring
[base construction](../erk-signed-reverse/construct.py) and
[weighted reverse source](../erk-signed-reverse/weighted_regular.py)
by SHA-256. The exact substitutions and new data are in
regenerate.py; the checked input sources are not edited.
The independent integer verifier reads only the literal
tableau, checks all 3,047 ordinary rooted conditions, the
complete denominator factorization, and the single
order-twelve unit defect. Neither step uses any
floating-point decision.

    python3 -B regenerate.py
    python3 -B audit_tableau.py
    sha256sum -c arithmetic-sha256.txt

The [end-to-end Lean certificate](LEAN.md) checks the reviewed rooted order predicate, a unit twelfth-order defect, and exact existence at width 22 if and only if 11! is invertible.
