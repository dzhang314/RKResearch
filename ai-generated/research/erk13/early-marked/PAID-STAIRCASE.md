## 5. Remaining exact extra mode and terminal consequences

If dim F₇=8 then it is necessarily an early terminal case. In the actual E₇^+ expansion of an F₇ vector, subtracting its head band and mark leaves a degree≤a polynomial whose constant coefficient is zero and whose shifted tests λ_0,…,λ_(a−2) vanish. Its polynomial component is therefore in the single rational line generated by K_a=X H_(a−1), where H_(a−1) is monic orthogonal for weight X(1−X) through degree a−2. If this component were zero for every F₇ vector, pure-band recovery and the a=5 refund would force rank seven. In the rank-eight case the line is genuinely present. At a first physical mismatch off the core and head, the F₇ transport can therefore give only primitive(TSP)(t)=κ K_a(t) off the terminal, not the unqualified zero. The clean joint-mask proof does not remove this mode. The remaining radius-four possibility (F₂=U of rank one) is confined to this early rank-eight branch, not dismissed.
