# The marked degree-six Lobatto obstruction in Lean

The original width-19 tableau is expressed with the reviewed `HasOrder 13 A b`, over a field of characteristic zero. In the early branch where the first escape polynomial has degree six, the checked sources construct the actual clean head and its first three grades from the original stage columns. The [square projection](../../lean/RKCert/ReviewedNative/ERK13NativeMarkedLobattoSquare.lean) supplies the radius-three conclusion and the radius-two low-leading conclusion, given the now checked scalar input.

That [scalar input](../../lean/RKCert/ReviewedNative/NativeLobattoPurePort.lean) is a nonvanishing theorem for a primitive evaluated at a nonzero node of the literal Lobatto mesh. The proof scales the mesh to a monic integer polynomial, reduces one marked factor at a separating prime, and replays [160 complementary factor certificates modulo 181](../../lean/RKCert/ReviewedNative/NativeLobatto181.lean). The [general reduction lemma](../../lean/RKCert/ReviewedNative/NativeModularFactorLift.lean) uses the integral closure and preserves the marked-root distinction.

The [final assembly](../../lean/RKCert/ReviewedNative/ERK13NativeWidth19Assembly.lean) shows exactly what is still needed for an unconditional Lean theorem: the two early escape degrees must both be closed. This commit does not yet check the radius-two high-leading class argument, nor the other early escape degree. It therefore does not certify the full [twenty-stage lower bound](order-thirteen-twenty-stages.md) end to end. The eight modules passed independent kernel replay on Lean 4.36.0.
