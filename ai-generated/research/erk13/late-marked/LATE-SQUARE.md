# The late clean square: radius-three cutoff and radius-two exhaustion

Assume the genuine late width-19 chart proved in [paid staircase](PAID-STAIRCASE.md). We have a=6, rank F₇=7, r∈{2,3}, and rank F₄=4. Notation is unchanged: c=A1, C=diag(c), V₆=e(K[X]≤6), D_n=U+span(w,…,C^(n−1)w), and E_j⁺=E_j+Kℓ. In particular F₇=D_(r+2). Both e(G) and its proposed head representative d are actual grade-seven vectors; since ℓ is absent from E₇, the first-escape equation e(G)=d+γℓ has γ=0. Thus e(G)∈F₇, G(0)=0, deg G=7, and μ₀(G),…,μ₅(G)=0 by the exact F₇ tests. If G(1)=0, write G=X(1−X)H with deg H≤5 and use six weight-X(1−X) tests to kill H. Hence G(1)≠0. This endpoint is not a stage-count assumption.

## 1. Actual clean representative, and no true terminal at E₈

Apply the native flat interval at grades 4,5,6, whose ranks are 4,5,6. Its maximal invariant core is the same U: its core is contained in the maximal invariant subspace of F₅, and every invariant subspace of F₅ drops into F₄ by the flat-chain drop with the next unpaid clock power (equivalently compare the last coefficient of x and Cx on the two chains). Its head can be chosen in actual F₄. In the radius-two case the F₄ band has length one; in the radius-three case it has length two, so Cw also belongs to F₄. The new head agrees on the band at F₅ and then each later band by clock advancement.

The actual clock slices of uu′ or uw with u,u′∈U all lie in F₈, by 4+4 payment and C-invariance of U. They belong to U by [single-clock purity](PAID-STAIRCASE.md#2-adjacent-shifts-force-marked-purity-including-single-clock-vectors). Assemble the slices to obtain pointwise closure of U and U·w⊂U. The native disjoint-head lemma replaces w by its representative zero wherever U is live without changing any band. This preserves membership of w, and of Cw when r=3, in actual F₄.

If ℓ∈E₈, then ℓ·F₄⊂F₁₂ and b_L≠0 force all F₄ columns to vanish at L. Since c_L=1, all head powers and U vanish at L and so does e(G)∈F₇=D_(r+2), contradicting G(1)≠0. Thus E₈=V₆⊕D_(r+3), free of the mark. At j=9,10 we have actual V₆⊕D_(r+j−5) inside E_j and the direct marked extension V₆⊕D_(r+j−5)⊕Kℓ. We do not assume a true entry at either of these grades.

## 2. Radius three: the additional paid square shift

Its two paid shifts also belong to D₆: successively they lie in D₇ and F₈, and directness of the marked E₉ chart, compared to E₈, removes their polynomial component and extra power. The actual E₈-to-E₉ marked step gives more than six head clocks by the clock-fibre count. Apply the native multiplier theorem at band length six and shift depth two to get a polynomial R of degree≤3 with w=R(c) wherever w is nonzero (the square equation has the form (w−R(c))w∈U, and both head and its products are zero on U). Let e_U∈U be the live indicator. For S=X or S=X(X−t₀) put z=e(SR)−S(c)w−e(SR)e_U. Since S(c)w∈F_(3+deg S) and e(SR)e_U∈U, separation in degree≤5 proves that z does not vanish. It is zero on both actual live supports. At its first mismatch t=c_i we have S(t)R(t)≠0, so t≠0, and t≠t₀ for the second mask. For T=X^k with deg(TS)≤3, the correction (TS)(c)w+e(TSR)e_U is in F₆ because TS is divisible by X and Cw itself is in F₄. The input polynomial TSR has degree≤6. Strict causality makes A[T(c)z] vanish there, giving primitive(TSR)(t)=0. The two masks provide three and two tests at their respective distinct nonzero clocks. The [cubic two-port scalar gate](interfaces/CUBIC-TWO-PORT.md) excludes this R. No true terminal entry or count of nine head clocks was used.

## 3. Radius two: both leading coefficients

Here D₁=F₄, D₄=F₇ and D₅⊂F₈. Since E₈=V₆⊕D₅, decompose the actual square and G:
```text
w² = e(P) + R(c)w + u,       e(G) = Q(c)w + v,
u,v∈U,       deg P≤6, deg R≤4, deg Q≤3.
```
The square is paid at 4+4, so e(P)∈F₈. Consequently P(0)=0 and μ_k(P)=0 for k=0 through 4, at 8+k+1≤13. Multiplying the mesh by w and using vw=0 yields e(G−RQ)w−e(PQ)∈U. The paid E₁₀⁺ chart makes the seven head powers independent modulo U.

Write γ=coeff₇(G−RQ). Divide PQ=GH+J, with deg H≤2 and deg J≤6. In both cases e(GH) is already in D₆. If γ=0, the transport gives e(PQ)∈D₇, so e(J)∈D₇, and E₁₀⁺ directness and polynomial separation force J=0. If P≠0 its exact degree is six, since at degree≤5 its five weight-X tests on P/X would kill it. The polynomials W=G/X and V=P/X have degrees six and five and are consecutive for the same weight-X form. They are coprime. Indeed at a putative common root a in an algebraic closure let V=(X−a)v and W=(X−a)z. For L(H)=primitive(XH)(1), their tests give L(X^kv)=a^k L(v) through k=5 and L(X^kz)=a^k L(z) through k=6. The degree-four v has L(v)≠0 by the five-test detector. Now L(v)z−L(z)v is a nonzero degree-five polynomial with six vanishing tests, impossible. Thus G|P Q would yield W|Q, impossible. It follows that P=0. Since γ=0, R and Q have exact degrees 4 and 3. The transport vanishes on at least seven head clocks, where the square fixes w=R(c)≠0. Its degree≤6 gives G=RQ. The [physical factor ports](interfaces/FACTOR-PORTS.md) exclude both R(0)=0 and R(0)≠0 under the actual F₄,F₇ hypotheses just earned.

If γ≠0, the same division gives e(PQ)∈V₆+D₇ and the transport solves for C⁷w there. C V₆ also lies there by the actual degree-seven G payment. It follows that B_alg=V₆⊕D₇ is clock invariant, consists of all sums of a clock polynomial, a clock polynomial times w, and U, and is a unital pointwise algebra by closure, disjointness and the square. This deduction is inside the unmarked summand, even when true E₉ or E₁₀ contains ℓ.

## 4. Exact 17/18-class chart and all noncore masses of the signed adjoint

A finite coordinate subalgebra contains all indicators of its own classes: multiply finitely many separating functions normalized to one on the chosen class and zero on one other class each. For the nonunital U use its live indicator as the identity on the live support. The three U atoms are entire B_alg classes (clock is constant on each by U-stability and w is zero there). All other classes are exactly the distinct pairs (c,w) off U. Since B_alg has rank 17, there are 14 such pairs, including zero-head pairs. The unmarked grade-nine chart V₆⊕D₆ is a hyperplane there; its annihilator vanishes on the core and is nonzero at every noncore pair by the [all-target selector](interfaces/ALL-TARGET-SELECTOR.md). This includes double clocks and identically zero Q.

The mark ℓ is outside B_alg by directness and splits the terminal class to make the full marked 18-class algebra E₁₀⁺. The physical pair contrast and D are produced in [final three, §2](LATE-FINAL-THREE.md#2-fold-on-the-actual-18-classes-not-the-unmarked-17). Grouping D on B_alg gives the nonzero annihilator of the unmarked grade-nine chart: it annihilates because D belongs to L₂=ann(E₉⁺). If its grouped row were zero, D would be a linear combination of the two physical pair contrasts ρ₁ and δ of that section. D_L=0 eliminates δ and D_m=0 eliminates ρ₁, contradicting D≠0. Hence the unmarked signed carrier is nonzero on all 14 noncore pairs. Splitting L does not change any surviving entry of this carrier because D_L=0.
