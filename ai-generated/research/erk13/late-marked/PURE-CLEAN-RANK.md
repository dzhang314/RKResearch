# Independent exact modular obstruction for the pure late radius-two chart

This is an independent backup for **P=0** in the radius-two leading-mismatch residual, with true terminal entry at E₉ or E₁₀. It is a paper proof with an exact standard-library checker, not a Lean theorem. The [common final-three argument](LATE-FINAL-THREE.md) does not need this backup and also covers P≠0 and a terminal absent through E₁₀.

## 1. Exact clean masses, including common head and clean clocks

As in the prior late rank proof, G=X W with W the monic degree-six weight-X orthogonal polynomial; the six literal native moments identify it. Let V be the monic degree-five polynomial for the same weight and H₅=∫₀¹ X V². Use S=G−RQ, whose degree is exactly seven in the leading-mismatch residual. With P=0, clean support means actual head w=0; at each live head w=R(c)≠0. Thus every live head clock is a root of S. The measured seven head powers give at least seven numerical head clocks; exact degree implies exactly seven distinct simple head roots. The nine native F₄ moments of b w annihilate each head-clock weighted mass, hence each group's output weight is zero. The three U atom indicators also have weight zero. Scalar quadrature supports at least seven nonzero aggregate numerical output clocks, so the clean classes are exactly all seven simple roots of G, all carrying the unique clean unweighted Radau rule. There is no active clock one in this unmarked 17-class picture because G(1)≠0. The terminal original row is not a clean class.

For a nonzero clean clock x the exact Radau mass is
β_C(x)=H₅/[x V(x) W′(x)] = H₅/[V(x)G′(x)]
with G monic. This follows from the six-node weight-X Gaussian rule at roots of W: dividing by W gives exactness through degree eleven, and the cardinal Lagrange polynomial at x paired with monic V gives Gaussian mass H₅/(V(x) W′(x)). The unweighted rule adds the origin and divides these masses by x; writing a polynomial as its constant plus X times another polynomial proves degree-twelve exactness. The actual original scalar law therefore has these grouped masses.

Let λ be the grouped unmarked D row from the exact residual, annihilating the unmarked E₉ hyperplane with nonzero entries at all noncore classes. Head tests w·c^k, k=0,…,5 give λ_H(h)R(h)=η/S′(h) with one nonzero η, by the degree-five Vandermonde relation on the seven roots of S. At a clean root x, test with its degree-six cardinal polynomial G(X)/[(X−x)G′(x)]. This yields
λ_C(x)=−Σ_(S(h)=0) [η/(R(h)S′(h))]·G(h)/[(h−x)G′(x)],
where the cardinal polynomial is evaluated directly if h=x. If x is not a head root, substitute G(h)=R(h)Q(h) and use the exact simple-pole expansion Q(X)/S(X)=Σ_h Q(h)/[S′(h)(X−h)] to obtain λ_C(x)=−η/[R(x)G′(x)].

If x is both a clean and head root, its two physical amplitudes are distinct, so R(x)≠0 and Q(x)=0. The h=x contribution is −η/[R(x)S′(x)]. The other terms give η Q′(x)/[G′(x)S′(x)] by the removable value of Q/S at x. Since S′(x)=G′(x)−R(x)Q′(x), the sum is again **λ_C(x)=−η/[R(x)G′(x)]**. This includes Q identically zero. At any clean x where R(x)=0, S(x)=0 too and x would have to be a physical head root with nonzero amplitude R(x), impossible. Thus no denominator R(x) at a clean class is zero. There is no assumption that head and clean clocks are disjoint.

## 2. The 21 homogeneous causal conditions

For this independent check fold on the actual **unmarked 17 classes** by earliest physical occurrences, and denote the resulting strict matrix by B; this is not the 18-class fold of the simpler final-three proof. D=A_m−A_a, D_T=A_L−A_j. Grouping the original reflected rows on the 17 classes gives
(k+1)Bᵀ(β y^k)−β y^(k+1)=F_k(y) λ,   k=0,…,5,
with degree caps (2,0,0,1,2,3), in that order. For k=0 exact firstSeed=0 and the differing m,L representatives give F₀=−b_m−b_L P_T(y), with P_T from the literal terminal opener proved in the appendix. This is degree one for E₁₀ entry and two for E₉ entry. For k≥1 the terminal row correction is absent because y_L=0; the off-one row m subtracts (k+1)b_mY^k from the grouped original residual coefficient. The latter is zero for k=1 and degree≤k−2 for k=2,…,5 by the paid L₂,…,L₅ spine. The scalar check uses degree two for k=0 and hence covers both entry grades without distinguishing them.

Only the seven clean classes carry aggregate output. The origin is their first representative, literally stage 0, so its row contains no edges to any of the six nonzero clean input classes. Thus for each input class i among those six, the left transpose in the displayed equations sees only their six clean output rows z. Let y_i=1−c_i and let ℓ_z be the degree-five Lagrange cardinal polynomial of those six reflected clocks. Put a_k=F_k/(k+1). Inverting the Vandermonde gives
β_z B_(z,i)= β_i ∫₀^(y_i) ℓ_z(T)dT + λ_i Σ_(k=0)^5 ℓ_z[k] a_k(y_i).
The strict chronological order of the six clean representatives forces this quantity zero whenever z is earlier than or equal to i. This is true whether head and clean representatives are interlaced or not. Divide by λ_i≠0; the exact ratio in section 1 is β_i/λ_i=−(H₅/η)R(c_i)/V(c_i). Absorb −H₅/η and the normalization of V into a polynomial B₄(y) of degree≤4 (nonzero because R≠0). We obtain the **21 forbidden-entry equations**
[B₄(y_i)/V_raw(c_i)] ∫₀^(y_i) ℓ_z(T)dT + Σ_(k=0)^5 ℓ_z[k] a_k(y_i)=0
for z no later than i, with the five coefficients of B₄ and the 14 coefficients of a₀,…,a₅ as unknowns. This is a homogeneous 21 by 19 matrix. There is no fit depending on b_a's individual sign or on a choice of R's normalization.

Why this is a characteristic-zero proof, not a random finite-field surrogate: given any actual labelled tuple in characteristic-zero K, all six clean roots lie in its finite rational splitting subfield E. Since W_raw's leading coefficient is invertible in Z_(p), every root is integral over Z_(p). The algebra Z_(p)[c₁,…,c₆] is integral and finite over Z_(p), hence has a prime ideal above p. Reduce at that prime (or localize there first). The six reduced roots are distinct because the reduced degree-six polynomial is squarefree, and all are the six displayed elements of F_p because it splits there. Their inherited chronological ordering is one of the tested 720, regardless of which prime was chosen and regardless of the Galois group. Coefficient denominators in the interpolation matrix are only V_raw(c_i), differences y_i−y_j, and primitive divisors 1,…,6, with also an invertible leading normalization as needed. All are units in the localization. A nonzero full-rank minor of the reduced matrix is therefore a nonzero minor of the actual exact matrix over E, and remains nonzero in K. Thus all nineteen coefficients vanish. This contradicts the required nonzero R and proves the pure late residual impossible.

This argument transfers rank by denominators and exact minors, not by choosing one convenient ordering of algebraic roots or assuming that arbitrary permutations are Galois conjugate. It is not an exclusion of the nonpure residual and is superseded for pure purposes by the shorter common final-three reverse adapter if that adapter is used.


## Appendix: literal terminal opener for this backup

In the unmarked 17-class algebra, L and its earlier twin j are identified (see [final-three, §2](LATE-FINAL-THREE.md#2-fold-on-the-actual-18-classes-not-the-unmarked-17)). In the original coordinates the mark separates them. The same two marked strict clock steps and the step at E₇⁺ used for L₄ give L₅=span(ρ₁,D,yD,y²D,y³D). The reflected row ρ_k∈L_k has coefficient Y^(k−1) on ρ₁ by evaluating at m; all other terms are P_k(y)D with deg P_k≤k−2. The discussion below uses the first true terminal-entry grade t; use the main proof instead if no such t≤10 exists.

The signed terminal row difference is D_T=A_L−A_j=Aᵀδ. Let t∈{9,10} be the first true terminal-entry grade. Then D_T annihilates E_(t−2) because A E_(t−2)⊂E_(t−1)⊂B. It annihilates ℓ by Aℓ=0. It does **not** annihilate E_(t−1): otherwise A of every lower-grade tree agrees at the terminal twins and belongs to B on those twins. The rooted-tree construction then produces no grade-t terminal split, since pointwise products preserve equality on the twins and the actual grade-t columns already agree on the other duplicate pair. The latter equality is paid by E_t⊂E₁₀^+. Equivalently the first failure of equality on twins in any elementary weight needs an A application to a column of the previous grade, because the lower inputs and all their pointwise products already agree there. Thus D_T∈L_(13−t) but D_T∉L_(12−t): for t=10 this is L₃\L₂, and for t=9 it is L₄\L₃.

Therefore D_T=κρ₁+P(y)D with **deg P=11−t**, exactly one for E₁₀ and two for E₉. The remaining scalar κ is not free. Every live row after m has clock one. Saturated reverse suffix payment puts its compensated contrast e_i−(b_i/b_L)e_L in the true U⊂B: every row past m is outside the support of all paid L₅ rows by literal reverse tail vanishing, so its coordinate is in E₆⁺; subtract the indicated mark by the constant Cauchy probe, placing the contrast in F₆. If j<m, no other later live row besides L occurs; if j>m the two later outputs cancel. In both cases exact firstSeed=0 at m gives b_L D_T(m)=b_m Y. Since ρ₁(m)=−b_m Y², this forces
