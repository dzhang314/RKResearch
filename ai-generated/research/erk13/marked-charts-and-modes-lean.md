# The actual marked chart, early ports, and late mass law in Lean

These certificates use the original tableau, the reviewed rooted-tree order predicate, and the literal last-stage coordinate. They do not assume that adjoining the last-stage coordinate preserves the original unmarked tree space.

* [Marked adjacent shifts](../../lean/RKCert/ReviewedNative/PaidMarkedAdjacentClock.lean) and the [native first-escape chart](../../lean/RKCert/ReviewedNative/ERK13NativeMarkedEscapeChart.lean) prove that a consecutive sequence of paid defects remains in the head band. The [shift locality theorem](../../lean/RKCert/ReviewedNative/ERK13NativeMarkedShiftLocality.lean) removes the terminal coordinate when the original membership pays it, including the degree-six one-slot refinement.
* The [first original pivot](../../lean/RKCert/ReviewedNative/ERK13NativeMarkedPrefixPort.lean) and [rank-seven or extra-mode dichotomy](../../lean/RKCert/ReviewedNative/ERK13NativeMarkedEarlyMode.lean) give the actual preterminal equations. The [two physical ports](../../lean/RKCert/ReviewedNative/ERK13NativeMarkedCubicPorts.lean) share the same leakage coefficient. The [degree-six endpoint](../../lean/RKCert/ReviewedNative/ERK13NativeMarkedLobattoGradeThree.lean) uses the checked Lobatto gate to prove that every degree-three defect in that branch lies in the core.
* In the late nonzero-leading radius-two case, the [clean head and unmarked chart](../../lean/RKCert/ReviewedNative/ERK13NativeLateLeadingChart.lean) produce the observed class algebra without assuming that the unmarked E9 or E10 is exactly that algebra. The [pair count](../../lean/RKCert/ReviewedNative/NativeQuadraticPairBound.lean) accounts for repeated clocks by multiplicity. The [physical mass and ratio argument](../../lean/RKCert/ReviewedNative/ERK13NativeLateMassRatios.lean) then supplies the distinct-response condition for the checked final-three argument.

These are new checked parts of the [20-stage lower bound](order-thirteen-twenty-stages.md), not an end-to-end Lean proof of that bound.
