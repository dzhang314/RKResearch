# Checked bridges for the marked order-thirteen proof

The [early cubic gate](../../lean/RKCert/ReviewedNative/NativeLobattoCubic.lean) proves that the five primitive equations at the two paid Lobatto clocks force the cubic profile to vanish, even with the shared unknown leakage coefficient. The [mod-43 check](../../lean/RKCert/ReviewedNative/NativeLobatto43.lean) checks all 20 ordered distinct pairs of the five residue roots. The [reusable lifting theorem](../../lean/RKCert/ReviewedNative/NativeModularRootLift.lean) takes two distinct roots of a monic integer polynomial and reduces them in one residue field. Separability after reduction prevents them from colliding: a collision would give a squared linear factor of the residue polynomial. Thus the finite check applies over every characteristic-zero field, not only over the reals.

On the late side, the [literal label theorem](../../lean/RKCert/ReviewedNative/ERK13NativeLatePhysicalLabels.lean) constructs the unique omitted representative from the paid first reflected row and the 18 marked classes. The [reverse spine](../../lean/RKCert/ReviewedNative/ERK13NativeLatePairSpine.lean) uses the checked marked ranks to identify the needed adjoint columns. The [physical fold](../../lean/RKCert/ReviewedNative/ERK13NativeLatePhysicalFold.lean) computes the effect of merging the labels directly from the original tableau. The only correction involves the difference of the two original rows, multiplied by the original weight of the omitted row. The result supplies the finite causal theorem without asserting that the folded matrix is itself a Runge–Kutta method.

Both paths use the reviewed definitions; these are checked implications from the stated inputs, not yet a native proof of the entire marked case. The full mathematical stage bound and evidence are linked from [the proof index](order-thirteen-twenty-stages.md).
