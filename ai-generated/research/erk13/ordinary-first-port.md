## Optional degree-independent, arithmetic-free root cut

Only the rational identification and irreducibility of H₆ are needed for this alternative, not reciprocal arithmetic. Over ℝ, the six orthogonality tests prove H₆ has six distinct roots in (0,1): if at most five signs changed at its real roots in (0,1), multiply by the product S of those distinct odd-multiplicity factors. The polynomial X H₆ S has one weak sign and is nonzero in the interval. Its integral is therefore strictly nonzero, contradicting the six paid moments since deg S≤5. Degree six now forces simple positive real splitting.

Normalize both factors over the original characteristic-zero K *before* restricting fields. In a splitting extension of K, the coefficients of these monic factors are symmetric polynomials in the corresponding roots of H₆, hence algebraic over ℚ. The selected t is algebraic too. They therefore lie in the finite rational splitting field generated by the six original ambient roots, regardless of whether K is ordered or embeds in ℝ. This splitting field admits a ℚ-embedding into the real splitting field of H₆. Irreducibility and normality allow choosing it to send t to the smallest positive real root v. Equivalently invoke the already checked native root-transport theorem with target ℝ, once splitting is shown. Primitive evaluation commutes with the embedding, and scaling the factors did not affect a zero. In the target the primitive factor has no root in (0,v); it has strict constant sign there and X>0, so its first primitive at v is nonzero. This contradicts the transported port. This argument works for every factor degree of a rational monic irreducible fully real-split polynomial with a positive root. It is not on the critical Lean staging path and involves no embedding of arbitrary K.
