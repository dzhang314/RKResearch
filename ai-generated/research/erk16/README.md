# Two order-sixteen quartic exclusions

* [One inner exterior clock](one-inner-exterior/PROOF.md). The actual exterior primitive forces a continuous two-parameter positive density. A finite exact enclosure contradicts a paid endpoint word in each of the two root orders. [The chronology proof](one-inner-exterior/CHRONOLOGY.md) and [rational checker](one-inner-exterior/check_uniform_outer.py) are included. Running the checker with Python 3 verifies all 15 intervals, with worst upper bounds of −490/1,000,000 and −38/1,000,000 on the two normalized integrals.
* [Strict interior crossed positive tail](crossed-interior-positive-tail.md). The second and fifth nodes of the relevant positive Gaussian rule give a negative mixed moment, but the actual endpoint pair forces it to be positive.

These are mathematical proofs for the stated sectors of the weak-five quartic case. Their full integrations have not been checked in Lean, and they do not give an unconditional stage bound.
