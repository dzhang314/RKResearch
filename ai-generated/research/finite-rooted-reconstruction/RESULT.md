1. **Filtered effective reconstruction.** With effective stage bound r, a nonzero r-by-r one-hole block at reachability/observability grades rho,sigma and a stage separator of grade delta reconstructs the full observable tableau from order rho+sigma+max(1,delta)+1. Only multiplication by the separating generators is needed. The proof gives exact degree recursions and a separate stage-level partition saturation test. This is a sufficient grade witness, not an assertion of optimality for every actual filtration.
2. **Sharp known-clock triangular result.** Given ordered distinct clocks, weights nonzero on varying rows, and the stated triangular support, the single-link double-bush values through order s determine a strict ERK (or known/common diagonal), and through s+1 determine a free-diagonal DIRK. These thresholds are sharp against all rooted output data in the fixed-clock classes; small actual exact-order examples also checked. General known sparsity has an exact rectangular linear rank test and a simple row-degree peeling budget.
3. **Classical paid borderline, used as a sharp benchmark.** At s effective stages, paid order 2s reconstructs the Gaussian collocation tableau; this is the classical maximal-order uniqueness, not claimed novel. At 2s−1, fixed Gaussian b,c still allow a one-parameter rank-one family by the classical B(2s−1), C(s−1), D(s−1) conditions (proved directly). Two nonzero perturbations have exact order 2s−1 and different order-2s marked outputs, so the 2s cutoff is genuinely necessary without extra support assumptions.

The full-rank filtered criterion requires at least 108 despite clock separation at grade one; that is not a claim that the first distinguishing tree costs 108 (it is order 55).
