# Early transport: the ordinary one-hole span, all-native-read blindness, and honest two-hole rank tests

**Source-only mathematics over the rationals or a characteristic-zero field.** “Early” means the physical prefix is discarded and actual suffix *columns* are counted. No assertion about a new compiled endpoint, an irreducible method, a minimal tableau, or a stronger unconditional stage floor. This extends, rather than rewrites, the separately replayed finite paid-packet certificate.

## 1. One marked path has a literal partial-fraction formula

Write $c=A1$, $D=\mathrm{diag}(c)$ and $Q_k(v)=b^T D^k v$. For a rooted tree $t$, let $n=|t|$, let $\gamma(t)=n\prod_{u\prec t}\gamma(u)$ be its tree factorial, and let $\Phi(t)=\prod_{u\prec t}A\Phi(u)$ be its actual stage column. Products of stage columns in this note are pointwise. A column of order $n$ has **grade** $n-1$. Formal evaluation in the exact integration model sends a tree column to $\alpha(t)x^{n-1}$ with $\alpha(t)=n/\gamma(t)$, pointwise product to ordinary product, $A$ to $I:f\mapsto\int_0^x f(z)dz$, and output to $\int_0^1$. This follows by induction. A homogeneous stage expression, obtained by linear combinations, products and $A$, is a linear combination of actual trees of its grade: products merge roots, and $A$ adds a unary root. These statements concern *expressions*, not a purported evaluation map on every coordinate vector of a given tableau.

For $\operatorname{HasOrder}(p)$, one scalar read of an expression of grade $g$ is its formal integral whenever $g+1\le p$. A one-hole ordinary tree context is linear in its hole: traverse its marked path outward, alternating applications of $A$ with multiplications by fixed side-branch factors $A\Phi(u)$. This describes every such context, including a hole merged with other root branches. Each $A$ step on the path costs one; each off-path branch costs $|u|$; the total is $C$. Start the cost counter at zero. Immediately **before** each path $A$ record the counter value, and finally record $C$. This is a strictly increasing list $H$ (an $A$ itself advances the counter). Write $S=\prod_{\rm side}\gamma(u)^{-1}$, with repeated branches repeated in the product. Then, on the span of all actual input trees with $n+C\le p$,

$$ b^T\mathcal C = S\sum_{h\in H}\frac{Q_h}{\prod_{k\in H,\ k\ne h}(k-h)}. \tag{1} $$

Indeed, on $\Phi(t)$ of order $n$, each recorded $A$ divides the formal coefficient by $n+h$, and the final read divides by $n+C$. All side factors multiply by $S$. Thus the scalar is $S\alpha(t)/\prod_{h\in H}(n+h)$. On the same paid input, $Q_h\Phi(t)=\alpha(t)/(n+h)$ for $h\le C$. The elementary partial fractions of a product of distinct linear factors in $n$ give (1). In particular, $b^TA=Q_0-Q_1$ on the paid grade; $b^TA^2=Q_0/2-Q_1+Q_2/2$ there. This is **not** an identity on all coordinate vectors or above the order budget. In a field of positive characteristic it also suffices that all divisions used be legitimate; no such extension is needed here.

For an actual mask $P$ of degree $d$ covering the discarded prefix, and a transported suffix column $T^a(P(c)c^e)$ with $T=DA-AD-A^2$, $a\ge1$, the formal value of each homogeneous summand is zero. In fact $xIf-I(xf)-I^2f=0$ identically. The expression costs at most $g=d+e+2a$; it equals its actual zero-extended counted version because the initial mask vanishes on the prefix and lower triangularity preserves suffix support. Thus every decorated read of cost $C$ annihilates it if $g+C+1\le p$. In particular replacing the ordinary paid rows by all rooted decorations of the same budget supplies no new transport rank.

## 2. Exact order p, but order-only normalized data through any prescribed N

Here and below an upper order horizon is chosen **before** constructing the example, not afterward on one fixed table. For any $M\ge N>p$, use explicit (left) or implicit (right) Euler extrapolation with $m=1,\ldots,M$ microsteps. On block $m$, $A_{ij}=m^{-1}1_{j<i}$ (ERK) or $m^{-1}1_{j\le i}$ (DIRK), and $b$ before extrapolation is $1/m$ at each node. Left nodes are $0,1/m,\ldots,(m-1)/m$, right nodes $1/m,\ldots,1$. Use disjoint blocks with the output in block $m$ multiplied by its Lagrange weight at zero on mesh parameters $1/m$:

$$\omega_m=(-1)^{M-m}m^{M-1}/((m-1)!(M-m)!).$$

For a tree of order $n$ the block stage value at grid index $j$ is $m^{-(n-1)}f_t(j)$, with $f_t$ a polynomial of degree $n-1$ and leading coefficient $n/\gamma(t)$. Summing a child polynomial over strictly earlier left nodes, or over right nodes through the present node, raises its degree by one and divides its leading coefficient by its new degree; multiplying branches supplies the induction. The sum over the $m$ output nodes is polynomial in $m$ of degree $n$ with zero constant term. Consequently the scalar block read is a polynomial in $1/m$ of degree $\le n-1$ with value $1/\gamma(t)$ at mesh zero. Extrapolation integrates every tree of order $\le M$ exactly.

Take $p$ distinct nonzero rational scales $s_i\ne1$, e.g. $i/(p+1)$, and their Lagrange weights $w_i$ at 1. Use a disjoint copy of this high-order tableau with matrix $s_i A$ and weights $w_i b$ in each scale block. Since tree stages have grade $n-1$,

$$\gamma(t)b_{\rm all}^T\Phi_{\rm all}(t)=\mu_{n-1}:=\sum_i w_i s_i^{n-1}\qquad(n\le M). \tag{2}$$

We have $\mu_0=\cdots=\mu_{p-1}=1$, but $\mu_p=1-\prod_i(1-s_i)\ne1$ by the interpolation error for the monic degree-$p$ polynomial. This is an actual RK tableau of **exact** order $p$ with all normalized tree reads shape-independent through $M$. Its weights are signed, but every individual weight is nonzero; the DIRK variant has nonzero diagonal at every stage.

A homogeneous expression of grade $g$ read against this tableau therefore has scalar $\mu_g$ times its formal integral whenever $g+1\le M$. This holds by linearity on raw trees. If a rooted **multi-hole** context is formed with ordinary products and $A$ and has even one hole filled with a transported expression whose formal value vanishes in each degree, its resultant formal value is again zero in each degree. Its single native $b$ read vanishes whenever the largest fully filled order is $\le M$. The same reasoning covers $b^T(vw)$, $b^T((Av)w)$, and products of scalar results with a vanishing factor. An arbitrary polynomial in other measured scalars need not itself vanish; the separate pair below establishes that even all native scalars need not identify the packet loss. For an inhomogeneous fixed mask, expand it degree by degree. The finite obstruction is **not specific to one hole**. This does not prohibit independent stage measurements, sign hypotheses, or special irreducible branches, and the cost bound does not prohibit a higher-order probe of one fixed example.

## 3. The invisible early packet can have full actual coordinate rank

Choose $d\ge1$. In the first scale place the microblock $m=d$ first and discard its $d$ physical stages. Their $d$ clocks are distinct. For the positive-degree mask $P(x)=\prod_{i\,\rm discarded}(x-c_i)$, any later complete microblock on that scale uses spacing $h=s_1/m$. Its lower triangular Volterra matrix is **exactly** $T=+hA$ for left Euler, $T=-hA$ for right Euler. On subdiagonal entries this follows by counting intermediate indices contributing to $A^2$; on the diagonal it is respectively $0$ or $-h^2$. The step uses the full-tableau clocks, which on a complete block equal its own clocks.

On any such microgrid, the Euler summation $S_h=A$ sends $x^j$ to a polynomial $F_j(x,h)$ of total degree $j+1$, $x$-degree at most $j+1$, whose constant coefficient as a polynomial in $h$ is $x^{j+1}/(j+1)$. Indeed $h^{j+1}\sum_{k=0}^{x/h-1} k^j$ or $h^{j+1}\sum_{k=1}^{x/h} k^j$ has exactly these properties (Faulhaber); at $j=0$ both equal $x$. Extend coefficientwise to polynomials in $h$. Hence the coordinate values of every column with pair $(a,e)$ are samples of

$$V_{a,e}(x,h)=(\pm h)^a S_h^a(P(x)x^e)
 =(\pm1)^a h^a I^a(P(x)x^e)+O(h^{a+1}). \tag{3}$$

Any finite set of distinct pairs $(a,e)$ gives linearly independent polynomials: in a relation take the smallest surviving $a$. Its $h^a$ coefficient gives $I^a(P(x)\sum_e v_e x^e)=0$. Integration on characteristic-zero polynomials is injective, and $P\ne0$, contradicting survival. Degree bounds are $D_x=\max(d+e+a)$ in $x$ and $D_h=\max(d+e+2a)$ in $h$. If $M\ge D_x+D_h+1$, the complete blocks $m=D_x+1,\ldots,D_x+D_h+1$ occur after the discarded one. At each spacing $m>D_x$ distinct sampled nodes determine the polynomial in $x$; each coefficient in $x$ then vanishes at $D_h+1$ distinct spacings, and so vanishes identically. The sampled matrix therefore has full column rank. These are literal physical coordinates and involve no output or generic-rank argument.

**Order 20.** For $d=4$, terminal grade 14, take all 34 old even-chain pairs $(a,e)$, where $t=0,2,\ldots,12$, $a=(14-t)/2$, $0\le e\le\min(t,15-t)$. Then $D_x=14$, $D_h=18$. With $M=54$, $s_i=i/21$, $m=4$ first, each example has $20\cdot54\cdot55/2=29{,}700$ physical stages. Every native one-hole context of cost through 35 is blind to *all* 34 old columns ($18+35+1=54$), including the pure degree schedule $2,\ldots,35$ realized in the earlier, different example. All ranks of paid widths 1 through 8 are full, as pinned by the exact modular checks. On the 456 physical rows of blocks $m=15,\ldots,33$ in the first scale, the two-hole matrix $G_{ij}=\sum_k (V_i)_k(V_j)_k$ has determinant **565 for ERK** and **881 for DIRK**, modulo 1009. The denominators of the rational inputs are units at 1009; a nonzero modular determinant proves an exact rational nonzero minor. All 34 old columns are thus independent despite all these scalar probes vanishing. This is an information example, not a competitive stage number or a lower-bound proof.

## 4. What a genuinely different multi-hole experiment can measure

Products inside an ordinary rooted read do not solve the information problem universally. Instead **coincide the physical stages of two separately weighted reads**:

$$\mathcal H(v,w)=\sum_{i\in\mathrm{counted}} b_i^2 v_iw_i. \tag{4}$$

The two output factors are paired at *the same physical coordinate*; this is neither $b^T(vw)$ nor $(b^Tv)(b^Tw)$. It requires actual stage incidence. On the rational examples in §2, all $b_i\ne0$, so a nonzero vector has strictly positive square (4). Arbitrary positive weights on a physically selected row set give the same test on that set, as used in §3. If weights can vanish, unit coordinate weights count all rows, or use $1+b_i^2$ over the reals. For $X$ a matrix of selected transported columns and positive row weights, $X^T\operatorname{diag}(w)X$ is nonsingular precisely when the columns are independent; over $\mathbb Q$ this follows by embedding in $\mathbb R$. This is nonlinear in the unknown transports and returns a direct rank certificate. For the reviewed quartic target it suffices to pick $28-h_{14}$ old columns. No scalar order identity grants these additional measurements.

For a general field where sums of squares may cancel, introduce physical stage indeterminates $z_i$. For $n$ chosen columns the Cauchy–Binet identity

$$\det(X^T\operatorname{diag}(z_i)X)=\sum_{|J|=n}\det(X_J)^2\prod_{i\in J}z_i$$

is nonzero as a polynomial exactly when $X$ has rank $n$. Distinct squarefree monomials prevent cancellation. These labels mark the independent information; they cannot be treated as available rooted order data. **No** finite rooted one- or multi-hole rule is claimed to provide them. The separate construction in `QUARTIC-INVISIBLE-PAIR.md` is sharper: for each admissible order, even every scalar tree at *every* order and fixed $b,c$ can fail to determine a proper early positive-mask packet rank, while (4) distinguishes it.

## Reproduction and limits

With only Python's standard library run `python3 -B check_exact.py` and `python3 -B check_quartic_pair.py`. `EXACT.txt` gives the observed output. The former evaluates all 200 rooted trees through order 8 on both Euler conventions and on the exact-order blends, checks 63 words and 1,121 paid pairings per convention, and computes the two order-20 Gram determinants directly from the defining commutator, not from the simplification $T=\pm hA$. The latter verifies physical rows, exact moments and changed packet loss of the separate all-orders-indistinguishable pair. Finite checks illustrate the proven inductions; they are not a substitute for them. The companion theta example with fixed clocks and weights and different positive-mask collision squares is an additional low-order check, not the proof of the stronger pair.
