# Net clock gaps and arithmetic bands in Runge–Kutta order

- [Paid net clock-gap moments and support](NET-GAPS.md) derives the
  one-step and higher-step laws from literal paid rooted-tree words,
  then proves the finite support obstruction by a unit Hankel
  determinant over a nonzero commutative ring.
- [Consecutive arithmetic gap bands](CONSECUTIVE-BANDS.md) uses the
  leading coefficients of the full allowed gap hull to sharpen the
  one-step bound over ordered fields. Physical lag requires actual
  intermediate-level occupancy and increasing level order; a separate
  weaker initial-band result uses only paid ring units.

- [Omitted lattice strides](LATTICE.md) excludes even saturation for
  any real affine lattice with at least two distinct net gaps. The
  [rational filter](RATIONAL-FILTER.md) treats the odd endpoint.
- [Two composable hops](COUPLING.md) separates a shared incoming
  stencil from the actual joint paid moments. The [lane
  construction](DIAGNOSTIC.md) shows why total-gap moments alone do
  not recover the middle-stage data.

- [Multihop joint gaps](DIRICHLET.md) gives the full paid Dirichlet
  moments and a unit Gram determinant. The [scope](MULTIHOP-SCOPE.md)
  separates path-label diversity from stage counts; the [independent
  calculation](UNIT-GRAM-CHECK.md) verifies the exact factorial budget.

The masses are aggregated at numerical clock differences before
support is counted. The bounds are necessary conditions, not method
constructions or attainment claims. The colored version requires
mixed literal payment with one common endpoint clock.
