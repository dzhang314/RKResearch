# The open signed-ghost extension of the SDIRK9 twin-port family is excluded

There is no thirteen-stage method of order nine in the following **specified** extension of the regular two-port bridge. Its first physical reverse clock is a distinct, noncontributing site with a nonzero old share and a fresh share making the aggregate zero. This extra site is not a scalar quadrature node. Old terminal shares at either or both of the protected sites may overlap the literal forward merges. A protected reverse column can send strict taps to the other protected representative, but not to an unmerged row. The arithmetic is characteristic zero; the proof in particular excludes the entire regular real-algebraic port tower in this chart. The unrestricted real interval remains 13–14. A ghost paid entirely within the old reverse prefix, changing the forward stem, or abandoning the grouped law below is not covered.

## Actual raw-weight construction

The forward bank is the six-row dense size-four return of the [six-site three-terminal packet](../real-sdirk9-six-site-three-terminal/README.md), with its regular unit-diagonal ports $v,w$ and relative size-five defect space the full $\mathcal P_4$ vanishing at both ports. The decoder $D_f$ satisfies $E_vD_f=\mathbf e_v^{\mathsf T}$ and $E_wD_f=\mathbf e_w^{\mathsf T}$ on all six old coordinates, and its shifted clock rows give the literal strict rows of the original protected stages. Thus an intra-protected suffix tap contributes only a multiple of $\mathbf e_j^{\mathsf T}-E_{x_j}D_f=0$ after redirect. This is why the proof admits these taps: it never assumes either protected column is isolated from the other one. The other strict entries of a protected reverse column outside its protected cluster must be zero for this literal merge.

Use physical reflected clocks $Y$ on $[0,1]$, with common diagonal $\gamma\ne0$, $\alpha=1-\gamma v$, $\beta=1-\gamma w$. Five distinct clocks are contributing precision-eight quadrature sites, including $\alpha,\beta$. The sixth site is $\gamma$, a signed zero-aggregate site between the first reverse row and one fresh opposite-mass representative, also allowing other old occurrences at that site. All individual reverse-stage weights used in the transpose are nonzero. There are $s_r$ old reverse rows and $t$ distinct, fully old contributing terminal groups. Each other site, including the ghost, has exactly one fresh representative made after the old block. The physical count after the two literal merges is $6+s_r+(6-t)-2$, requiring $s_r\le t+3$.

Let $L$ be the actual reverse lower triangular matrix with diagonal $\gamma$, $C$ its clock diagonal, $J$ the primitive from zero, $M$ the **raw** site sum (row $i$ adds the actual physical shares at site $Y_i$), and
$$
 (FP)_i=W_iP(Y_i),\qquad
 ML=FJD_r+S(M-FD_r),\qquad S_{ii}=\gamma.
$$
Here $W_i$ is the aggregate, in particular $W_{\rm gh}=0$; the expression never divides by $W_i$. The old return $D_r$ takes actual packets to degree at most two and is exact through two primitive/clock letters. A terminal row of $S$ is $\gamma$ times its coordinate row, and the actual old group is already exact through length three. At any open site the column for its fresh representative determines the corresponding column of $S$ on all its predecessors: it must vanish there, because the fresh physical share is nonzero and $D_r$ uses only old coordinates. A fresh protected column may respond at the other protected site, but not at any unmerged site. At an old protected terminal the top residual polynomial vanishes at that site, which gives the same zero input to the off-port tests. Clock multiplication respects the raw sums.

These equations imply $(M\mathbf v)_i=W_iP(Y_i)$ on all reverse words through length three. On length-three words let $V$ be the span of the genuine $K=P-D_r\mathbf v$. The next primitive's raw discrepancy vector is $\boldsymbol\delta(K)=(SF-FJ)K$. Taking the actual weight-conjugated reverse transpose for the forward suffix and the literal feed $(E_XJ-BE_X)D_f$, every new root is pure through size five. Through order nine at most one later error appears at a root: two children of size at least five would cost at least eleven. One unary attachment to an actual size-five source, then three exterior primitive or leaf marks, isolates the top mixed pairing from lower exterior lengths, where the word identities already pay integration by parts and quadrature. Thus order nine requires
$$
 \sum_i H(Y_i)\delta_i(K)=0
 \quad(H\in \mathcal P_4,\ H(\alpha)=H(\beta)=0,\ K\in V).
$$
This is the same actual-rooted reduction as in the disjoint packet, using raw site sums instead of means. Affine reflection of the clock letter introduces only already paid shorter words. The ghost's physical shares participate in this pairing; its nominal polynomial row and nominal primitive row are both zero.

Write $B(Y)=\prod_{W_i\ne0}(Y-Y_i)$ and $T(Y)=(Y-\gamma)B(Y)$. The four nonport sites have mixed rank three by interpolation; their weighted annihilator has coordinates $1/T'(Y_i)$ by the leading coefficient formula for degree-three interpolation. Thus for each $K$ the quantities $T'(Y_i)\delta_i(K)$ agree at all four nonport sites. Put
$$
 \mu_x K=(JK)(x)-\gamma K(x),\qquad
 D_x=(x-\gamma)\int_0^1 B(Y)/(Y-x)\,dY
       =W_x T'(x)
$$
for contributing $x$. At a nonport terminal the common quantity equals $-D_x\mu_xK$ (there $K(x)=0$). At the earliest contributing nonport fresh representative it also equals $-D_x\mu_xK$. At the next such representative the same formula applies on every $K$ vanishing at the earlier one. These claims allow a preceding fresh ghost, whose incoming $FK$ coordinate is zero. In both fresh cases the only other possible earlier contributors are terminals or protected columns; the former evaluate $K$ to zero and the latter do not tap an unmerged row. No assumption on the signs of physical shares is used.

## The paid stage budget forces the exact polynomial space

The dense lower reverse return needs at least four rows: independent $\mathbf1,\mathbf c,\mathbf c^2$ polynomial profiles and the genuine pure quadratic defect $\mathbf g=L\mathbf c-\mathbf c^2/2$, nonzero on the initial row. Thus $t\ge1$. If $t\ge4$, the cubic residual vanishes on four distinct terminals and is zero, refunding a whole extra primitive grade to each of those terminals. For the $t\le5$ independent degree-four evaluation targets, the invertible commutator $CL-LC-L^2$ (diagonal $-\gamma^2$ and formal action zero) gives the three disjoint dimension contributions $t,3,1$. In a relation, read the first block with the terminal length-four targets, divide by the commutator, read with the dense degree-two decoder, and then divide again. This requires $s_r\ge t+4$, impossible. Hence $1\le t\le3$.

The computation below verifies, for every permitted scalar geometry, that no contributing clock $x$ has $x^2-4\gamma x+2\gamma^2=0$, and that no two distinct contributing clocks $x,z$ satisfy $z=2x-\gamma$ or $z=4\gamma-x$. The first two reverse rows cannot complete a true length-three terminal, and a terminal completed by a third effective row must have three distinct clocks forming $\gamma,x,2x-\gamma$. This follows from the [actual third-row tests](../real-sdirk9-seven-site-three-terminal/README.md#the-actual-first-four-reverse-rows-are-forced), including their repeated signed means. Since some terminal completes by chronological row four, $s_r=t+3$. Moreover the first three physical rows cannot include literal twins: coalescing twins in those early word columns would give a terminal completed by at most three effective rows, already impossible. This compression is only a proof about the word data of the early prefix, not a modification of a hypothetical tableau.

To determine the actual pure rank, rescale these first three rows to unit diagonal and write their clocks as $1,x,z$; the third-to-second strict tap is $u$. The second clock is not 1, since the first two rows would then be twins. The second quadratic defect component is $g_2=2x-1-x^2/2\ne0$. Hence $\mathbf g$ and $C\mathbf g$ are independent on the first two rows. If the four genuine pure columns $\mathbf g,C\mathbf g,L\mathbf g,\mathbf d$ (where $\mathbf d=L\mathbf c^2-\mathbf c^3/3$) had rank only two on the first three rows, comparison on those first two rows would give
$$
 L\mathbf g=\mathbf g+(C-I)\mathbf g/(2g_2),
 \qquad \mathbf d=\tfrac23(L+C)\mathbf g .
$$
The third coordinate of the latter identity forces $u(x-1)(2x-z-1)=0$. If $u\ne0$, the two distinct effective clocks are an excluded arithmetic progression (the equation itself makes $z$ neither 1 nor $x$). If $u=0$, then $z\ne1,x$ by absence of twins, and the first identity forces $g_3=g_2$, hence $z=4-x$, again excluded. Thus the old pure rank is at least three. The surjective degree-two return on $s_r=t+3$ coordinates has kernel dimension $t$, so the image of these actual pure columns provides at least $3-t$ independent residuals of degree at most two. The cubic clock word supplies one more independent residual. The $t$ terminal evaluations give the opposite bound, and therefore
$$
 V=\left(\prod_{\text{old terminals }a}(Y-a)\right)\mathcal P_{3-t}.
$$

## Exhaustive top primitive tests

Let $o$ count the protected sites that are old terminals. Use $r,s$ for two ordered distinct nonport contributing nodes as appropriate; the arithmetic below covers all their permutations.

- $(t,o)=(1,0)$: the terminal $t_0$ and first contributing live $r$ would have proportional primitive functionals on $(Y-t_0)\mathcal P_2$. The two minors on basis pairs $(Y-t_0,Y(Y-t_0))$ and $(Y(Y-t_0),Y^2(Y-t_0))$ have nonzero prefactors times $r^2-rt_0-3\gamma r+\gamma t_0$ and $r^2-rt_0-4\gamma r+2\gamma t_0$, respectively. The prefactors are nonzero since $r,t_0$ are distinct and nonzero. The bracket difference is $\gamma(r-t_0)\ne0$.
- $(1,1)$: if $a$ is the old protected terminal and $r,s$ are the first two contributing live sites, take $K=(Y-a)(Y-r)$. The required difference $D_r\mu_rK-D_s\mu_sK$ is nonzero.
- $(2,0)$: at the two distinct disjoint terminals, the primitive determinant on their quadratic hull and its first mark has nonzero factor $r^2s^2(r-s)^3/24$.
- $(2,1)$: let $a$ be the old protected terminal, $r$ the other terminal and $s$ the first contributing live. The same test $K=(Y-a)(Y-r)$ has nonzero difference.
- $(2,2)$: both protected are terminal; use $K=(Y-\alpha)(Y-\beta)(Y-r)$ at the first two contributing live sites $r,s$. The difference is nonzero, with the tap from $r$ killed by $K(r)=0$.
- $(3,0)$: the only nonport open site is the ghost; it has zero discrepancy since all contributing nonprotected inputs are terminal. Thus all three disjoint terminal primitives must vanish. The nonzero cubic terminal hull $K$ would give the degree-four $JK$ a double root at three distinct nonzero terminal sites, impossible.
- $(3,1)$: with one protected old terminal $a$ and two disjoint ones $r,s$, use $K=(Y-a)(Y-r)(Y-s)$ and the nonzero difference.
- $(3,2)$: for the disjoint terminal $r$ and earliest contributing live $s$ use $K=(Y-\alpha)(Y-\beta)(Y-r)$. Again the difference is nonzero.

All cases permit arbitrary same-site signed rough shares and repeats among old occurrences, and all allowed taps between the two protected representatives.

## One finite splitting algebra proves all residue claims

For a five-node precision-eight rule the node hull is proportional to $\mathrm L_5+\lambda\mathrm L_4$, the shifted Legendre pencil. This follows by orthogonality of the monic hull to $\mathcal P_3$. Use $\mathrm L_4=70Y^4-140Y^3+90Y^2-20Y+1$ and $\mathrm L_5=252Y^5-630Y^4+560Y^3-210Y^2+30Y-1$. The anchors impose
$$
 \frac{\mathrm L_5(1-Gv)\mathrm L_4(1-Gw)-\mathrm L_4(1-Gv)\mathrm L_5(1-Gw)}{G}=0,\qquad G=\gamma.
$$
The quotient has degree eight. Over the regular port field, define its monic quotient algebra, adjoining first $r$ a root of the remaining monic cubic and then $s$ a root of that cubic divided by $Y-r$. This is a free algebra of rank $8\cdot3\cdot2=48$, even if it is not a field. Reduction at $p=13$ uses the simple port tower residues $(s_f,v,w)=(9,6,2)$, with the same irreducibility witnesses at primes 31 and 17 as the disjoint packet. The leading coefficient of the degree-eight polynomial and the resultant denominator $\mathrm L_4(1-Gv)$ are units here; the monic cubic and quadratic can therefore be formed over the localized characteristic-zero coefficient ring before reducing. A real candidate with any ordering of the three remaining nodes would be an evaluation homomorphism out of this algebra.

The replay verifies 37 multiplication operators are invertible in that single rank-48 specialization. These include nonzero/inequality clocks, every ordered progression and sibling pin, the quadratic exceptional second-clock tests, and the literal primitive differences above. It uses signed residues only; every modular matrix product has inner length at most 48 and terms below $13^2$, so there is no integer overflow. The characteristic-zero determinants are nonzero because they have nonzero reduction. This proves the exclusions for every branch without assuming that a candidate clock itself reduces to a specific finite-field element. Each arithmetic gate is separately certified by the Lean kernel on its emitted explicit multiplication matrix and a computed inverse of the unit coordinate. The arithmetic receipt formalizes neither the mathematical mixed reduction nor the generation of matrix entries.
