# No arithmetic S,D,S opener opposite a doubled endpoint in the affine seven-clock case

Keep the characteristic-zero, all-dirty, strict ten-stage ordinary-order-at-least-eight hypotheses and **exactly seven** distinct physical clock labels. In the [affine graph](../sdirk-eight-all-dirty-seven-affine-openers/README.md), each of the four singleton labels (S) has its quadratic residual on one affine nonconstant graph \(R\). Neither copy at any of the three doubled labels (D) has residual \(R\) there. All weights may be signed; nonzero weights follow from the complete intersection.

**Result.** If the physical endpoint labels are one singleton and one double, the orientation starting at the singleton must begin **S,D,D**. Earlier packets eliminated S,S,D and S,D,S with star; this packet eliminates S,D,S with a genuine arithmetic third read. It does not exclude S,D,D, both-singleton endpoints, the full affine graph, or ten-stage tableaux globally.

## Exact mesh projection and the otherwise valid \(a=1\) cubic

Normalize the diagonal to one, the interval length to \(\ell=1/\gamma\), and the original clock to \(x\). Use doubled-centered coordinate \(y=2x-\ell\). Let the affine graph zero be \(x_0=1-a\), with \(R(x)=(x-1+a)/(2a)\) from the first singleton row, and let \(b=2-\ell-a\), so \(z=b-a\) is the doubled-centered coordinate at the graph zero. No nonzero premise on \(b\) is imposed. These are the same coordinates as the [affine opener packet](../sdirk-eight-all-dirty-seven-affine-openers/README.md); \(L_4(x-\ell/2)=J_4(2x-\ell)/560\) and the D clock polynomial is its exact Christoffel divided difference \(K(y,z;\ell)\). At a possible common root of the D clock polynomial and \(R\), evaluate \(K\) as a polynomial without dividing by \(y-z\).

The physical prefix is S,D,S with gap \(u=x_2-1\), \(x_3=1+2u\), \(g_2=\kappa(u)/2\), \(g_3=q_{\rm num}(u)/(2D(u))\), where
\[
 \kappa(u)=1+2u-u^2,\quad D(u)=-2+u+4u^2-u^3,\quad
 q_{\rm num}=(1+4u-4u^2)D+4u^2(3u-2).
\]
The third stage is on the affine graph, so the exact identity from the [arithmetic singleton packet](../sdirk-eight-all-dirty-seven-affine-two-singletons/README.md) gives
\[
              a=\frac{D(u)}{2(u-2)(u^3-3u^2+1)}.
\]
Its denominator is nonzero; the literal arithmetic guards also require \(u,\kappa,q_{\rm num},D,3u-2\ne0\). The actual D label encountered in the opener is the **second** physical row, at \(x_f=1+u\). The terminal is D at \(x_e=\ell-1\). Therefore both
\[
              K(a+b+2u,b-a;2-a-b)=0,\qquad
              K(-a-b,b-a;2-a-b)=0
\]
are mandatory. They do not require that these two D labels differ.

The script reconstructs every necessary fibre of the exact resultant in \(b\) of the two polynomial numerators. Up to nonzero scalars its irreducible factors are
\[
  (u-1)^{12},\quad (u-2)^{45},\quad
  (2u^3-7u^2+u+2)^{12},\quad (u^3-3u^2+1)^{45},
   \quad \Phi_{18}(u),\quad \Phi_{58}(u).
\]
Here the two last factors have the displayed degrees and exponent one; they are regenerated by the check. The \(u=2\) and \(u^3-3u^2+1=0\) factors are forbidden by the denominator. The \(u=1\) specialization gives \(a=1\), \(x_2=2\), \(g_2=1=R(2)\), contradicting that the second row has a D label. **One must not prune \(a=1\) in general.** The other possible \(a=1\) factor is \(2u^3-7u^2+u+2\); this follows from
\[
 D(u)-2(u-2)(u^3-3u^2+1)=-(u-1)(2u^3-7u^2+u+2).
\]
In that exact cubic field the two numerator equations have gcd \((b-1)^3\), so \(b=1\) and \(\ell=0\), impossible.

In each of the exact degree-18 and degree-58 fields the gcd in \(b\) is linear. Recover \(b\), check both equations, the nonzero interval and distinct endpoint clocks, and the nonzero two Legendre values at the graph zero. In degree 18 the two known D labels coincide; the remaining two D labels are obtained over an irreducible quadratic extension (degree 36 overall), retaining both choices. In degree 58 the two known D labels are distinct; the third follows from the cubic coefficient sum. No field embedding or sign choice is excluded by these reconstructions.

## Every reverse three-row phase fails

The actual reverse prefix begins at the doubled terminal, and there are now two observed singleton labels in the original prefix, \(x_1=1,x_3=1+2u\). At the reverse end write \(v=x'_2-1\). The known literal phase relations are the same as forward, and \(g_{\rm orig}=\lambda g_{\rm rev}\), \(\lambda\ne0\), at every corresponding row.

- If the reverse second and third are both S, they cannot be a star (its repeated residual would be at different clocks on a nonconstant affine graph). In genuine arithmetic their original clock coordinates are \(\ell-1-v,\ell-1-2v\), with original residuals \(-(b+v)/(2a),-(b+2v)/(2a)\). Thus \(q_{\rm num}(v)(b+v)-\kappa(v)D(v)(b+2v)=0\). These two stages consume the two other singleton labels, whose complete quartic evaluated at \(x_0\) must equal \(J_4(z)/560\). The gcd of these two necessary polynomials in \(v\) is one in each exact base field. This does not require a choice of D-root extension.
- If the reverse second and third are both D, try both ordered assignments of the other two D labels and both literal phases. No matching physical phase exists.
- If exactly one of the reverse second and third is D, that label and the phase fix both clocks and reverse residuals. The observed reverse singleton determines \(\lambda\) by its nonzero graph value and literal reverse residual, and supplies a third singleton. The three known singleton locations interpolate \(C=A_2+B R\) through \(C=L_4/R\) **only at singleton rows**. The known literal forward D residual \(g_f=g_2\) then gives
\[
              B=\frac{L_4(x_f-\ell/2)-g_f C(x_f)}
                        {g_f(g_f-R(x_f))}.
\]
Here \(g_f\ne0\) and \(g_f\ne R(x_f)\). With these values, the two reverse D pairs must both satisfy \(U=L_4-gC-Bg(g-R)=0\). The code counts eight possibilities in each field extension and all fail. It checks physical pair uniqueness if the reverse D label equals the known forward D label, and never divides by \(R\) at any D label.

These exact arguments use actual prefix phases and complete clock masks, not trace approximation or a numeric full-tableau solve. The [corrected normalized trace formula](../sdirk-eight-all-dirty-seven-affine-two-singletons/AFFINE-TRACE.md) is available for the remaining S,D,D chart but is unnecessary here.
