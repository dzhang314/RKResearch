# Exactly seven all-dirty clocks: both physical endpoints must be singleton

**Theorem.** A strict ten-stage SDIRK tableau over a characteristic-zero field of ordinary order at least eight, with all ten literal quadratic defects nonzero and exactly seven distinct abscissae, must have **singleton first and last clock labels**. The result uses no ordering, positivity, splitting or assumed symmetry. It is a restriction on the ten-stage equality case, not a general ten-stage impossibility.

1. The [complete seven-clock pair decomposition](../sdirk-eight-all-dirty-seven-clock/README.md) gives four S (singleton) and three D (doubled) labels, no higher multiplicity. Every physical weight is nonzero in the all-dirty equality case, so the genuine weighted mirror is available; it reflects clocks and multiplies defects by a nonzero scalar. [Constant](../sdirk-eight-all-dirty-seven-constant/README.md) and [affine nonconstant](../sdirk-eight-all-dirty-seven-affine-final/README.md) singleton residual graphs are already excluded. Thus the singleton graph is genuinely quadratic.
2. The [both-doubled-endpoint synthesis](../sdirk-eight-all-dirty-seven-quadratic-final-both-d/README.md) excludes D at both physical endpoints, including a shared endpoint clock. If one endpoint alone were S, orient from it via the true mirror. By the [one-singleton topology ledger](../sdirk-eight-all-dirty-seven-quadratic-two-s-dss-arithmetic-new-a2-final/LEDGER.md), the actual forward first three have exactly two S and one D; the actual reverse first three have two D and one S. The distinctness within an opening is supplied by the true arithmetic/star phase identities, not assumed for the rest of the tableau.
3. Each opening has a single **non-initial** D and a single **non-initial** S. Its options are true arithmetic with that D second (A2), arithmetic with D third (A3), or either physical star ordering. In the star, the two consumed pair data coincide under the two true formulas; checking them is not permuting an arbitrary tableau. The two prefixes involve six actual rows. A singleton cannot occur in both. The one observed forward D can be the reverse terminal D, the reverse nonterminal D, or neither. Since the two reverse D labels are distinct, this trichotomy is disjoint and exhaustive.

The [terminal overlap](../sdirk-eight-all-dirty-seven-quadratic-last-rear-terminal-overlap/README.md) and [nonterminal overlap](../sdirk-eight-all-dirty-seven-quadratic-last-rear-extra-overlap/README.md) each exclude all nine algebraic phase pairs. In the disjoint case, [two star charts](../sdirk-eight-all-dirty-seven-quadratic-last-rear-disjoint-two-star/README.md), [five further charts](../sdirk-eight-all-dirty-seven-quadratic-last-rear-disjoint-more/README.md), and [the final two arithmetic charts](README.md) cover all nine. Remainder identities and masks keep genuine clock, slope and paid residual guards; no at-infinity, missing-root or field-order shortcut is taken in these exact replays. Hence exactly one S endpoint is impossible, proving the theorem.

Both-S endpoint geometry is **not** excluded here. The other all-dirty distinct-count branches and the general 10–11 stage interval are not settled by this theorem.
