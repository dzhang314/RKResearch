# Pure polynomial support is impossible at four physical SDIRK8 quadratic zeros

Let a strict ten-stage SDIRK tableau have common nonzero diagonal $\gamma$ and ordinary order at least eight over a field of characteristic zero or greater than seven. Define $\mathbf c=A\mathbf 1$, $\mathbf g=A\mathbf c-\mathbf c^{\odot2}/2$, $C=\operatorname{diag}(\mathbf c)$, $G=\operatorname{diag}(\mathbf g)$, and $U=\ker G$. Suppose exactly four physical $g_i$ vanish. There is no nonzero actual supported column $\mathbf v=r(\mathbf c)\in U$ for a scalar polynomial $r$ of degree at most two. Together with the [nonzero-$\mathbf g$-coefficient exclusion](../sdirk-eight-four-supported-paired-zero/README.md), this says **$E_2\cap U=0$ in every exactly-four-zero case**, since $E_2=\langle\mathbf 1,\mathbf c,\mathbf c^{\odot2},\mathbf g\rangle$. This is a support-rank restriction, not an exclusion of every four-zero tableau or a general ten-stage lower bound.

Use the genuine saturated forward and reverse spaces from the [two-sided flags](../../lower-bounds/characteristic-zero/sdirk_saturated_weighted_mirror_duality_20261123.md), valid over these paid fields by the ordinary Hilbert determinants and the triangular commutator with unit diagonal. In particular
$$
\dim(W_j\cap U_{\rm row})=4-\dim(E_{5-j}\cap U).
$$

## 1. Two physical off-zero clocks, at least one live

Every off-zero clock is a root of $r$, so there are at most two. There cannot be only one, say $d$: the ordinary size-four Hilbert rank forces at least three live clock groups other than $d$, all on zero rows. Thus $\mathbf c-d\mathbf1$ and $(\mathbf c-d\mathbf1)^{\odot2}$ are independent supported $E_2$ columns, while $\mathbf b^{\mathsf T}(C-dI)^j$, $j=1,2,3$, give three independent supported $W_3$ rows. The restriction identity at $j=3$ forbids this. Therefore there are exactly two physical off-zero clocks and we normalize $r(X)=(X-d)(X-e)$.

There are at least two distinct live zero clocks outside $\{d,e\}$ by ordinary rank; consequently the supported columns $\mathbf v,C\mathbf v$ are independent in $E_3$. If no off-zero row were live, $\mathbf b^{\mathsf T},\mathbf b^{\mathsf T}C,\mathbf b^{\mathsf T}C^2$ would be three independent supported $W_2$ rows, contradicting the restriction identity at $j=2$. Thus $n=1$ or $2$ distinct off-zero clocks are live.

Since the two physical off-zero clocks are distinct, $F_3=G E_1=\langle\mathbf g,C\mathbf g\rangle$ is clean at all four zeros. Also the first two actual forward rows are off-zero: otherwise $\kappa=0$ in $(C-\gamma I)\mathbf g=\kappa(A-\gamma I)\mathbf g$ would localize both off-zero clocks to $\gamma$. Their clocks differ and the third forward clock is distinct from both by the actual star/arithmetic identities. Thus physical row 3 is zero, with nonzero normalized second gap $u$, arithmetical third clock $\gamma(1+2u)$, and
$$Q(u)=4u^3-12u^2+3u+2=0.$$
The last physical stage is off-zero since some off-zero row is live: $Z_2$ is spanned by both $\mathbf b^{\mathsf T}G$ and $\mathbf b^{\mathsf T}M$, whose terminal entry is nonzero. The terminal clock is $1-\gamma$. The last two output weights are nonzero and their physical clocks differ by the literal degree-one reverse rank argument. None of these deductions assumes live zero-row weights.

## 2. Two live off-zero clocks

All six actual off-zero rows must carry nonzero weights. To see this, the two separate clock groups each have zero first through third weighted residual powers, by the six moments $\mathbf b^{\mathsf T}C^jG^\ell\mathbf1=0$ for $0\le j\le1$, $1\le\ell\le3$. Each group needs at least two live rows. If a group has nonzero ordinary aggregate, its annihilating residual polynomial forces at least four different live residual values there, and the other group uses at least two remaining live rows. If both aggregates are zero, each group has at most four rows, and the residual Vandermonde (through degree three including the ordinary mass) forces each distinct live $(c_i,g_i)$ subaggregate to vanish. In that case a varying-residual group still needs at least four live physical stages.

There must be one varying-residual group. Weighting $E_3$ onto $W_3$ is an isomorphism here: the Hilbert test detects the trace, and the two live off-zero clocks detect the remaining $\mathbf g$ times an affine function. The live off-zero restriction of $E_3=\langle 1,\mathbf c,\mathbf c^{\odot2},\mathbf c^{\odot3},\mathbf g,C\mathbf g\rangle$ has rank two if each group has constant residual. This would make the supported $W_3$ dimension four and $E_2\cap U=0$, a contradiction. So the varying group consumes four live rows, the other two, and only one group varies. Call the varying clock $d$. This conclusion concerns the six off-zero weights, not the four zero weights.

Both rear edge rows are off-zero because $Z_3=W_1G$ is clean and the terminal tap into row 9 is nonzero. The actual physical row 8 is zero: if it is live its literal normalized three-row suffix cannot have three distinct off-zero clocks; if it is silent it must be zero since all off-zero rows are live. In fact row 8 is **not silent**. If it were, the exact rows $\mathbf b^{\mathsf T}A$ and $(\mathbf b^{\mathsf T}G)A$ both vanish at its column. Their two tap constraints force $g_9=g_{10}$ (otherwise that column is inaccessible), so the reverse second gap is 2. The forward second gap equals that reverse gap if $\gamma\ne1/2$, or its negative if $\gamma=1/2$, because the two literal off-zero clocks are used at both ends. Thus the forward gap must be 2 or $-2$, but $Q(2)=-8$ and $Q(-2)=-84$, both nonzero. Hence the latest zero is live, and the actual normalized reverse third zero pays its own cubic $Q$.

If $\gamma=1/2$, the second gaps are $u,-u$; $Q(u)=Q(-u)=0$ gives $6u^2=1$ and $4u^2=-3$, requiring characteristic 11. The two physical zero thirds then share the same literal clock, whose centered value is $2u$ and whose Gaussian polynomial value is $-13/9$. Ordinary quadrature has at most four nonzero sites (three distinct zero clocks and at most one nonzero off-zero mass), so all live ordinary sites are fourth Gaussian. The latest physical zero is live, giving a contradiction at characteristic 11. For the rest of this section $\gamma\ne1/2$. The off-zero sites are $\gamma,1-\gamma$, the two second gaps coincide, and with $s=2\gamma-1=-u/(u+2)$ the two centered outer zero clocks are $-3s,3s$.

### Gaussian ordinary quadrature

If the ordinary quadrature has only four active clocks, they are exactly the fourth Gaussian roots. In particular the latest physical zero, which is live, is Gaussian; hence its reflected mate at the first physical zero is Gaussian even if that first weight is zero. The Gaussian pin at the outer nodes is $L_h(3u,u+2)=0$, with $L_h(Y,D)=35Y^4-30Y^2D^2+3D^4$. Its resultant against $Q(u)$ has paid-prime-stripped support **103**. The complete splitting field has the sole accepted $u=28$, $\gamma=79$, $s=54$ modulo 103. Neither off-zero clock $\pm s$ is Gaussian. Hence any nonzero ordinary aggregate at an off-zero site is impossible in this four-site subcase; only canceled aggregates survive, with four distinct live Gaussian zero rows.

### A five-site moment determinant and the supported diagonal read

With a nonzero off-zero aggregate at $d$ and five ordinary sites, the four zero rows are live and distinct, and $d$ does not coincide with a zero. The five-site nodal polynomial is $(y-d_y)(y^2-9s^2)Q_2(y)$ in centered time, where $d_y=\pm s$ and $Q_2$ is monic quadratic. The ordinary moments through degree seven force its orthogonality to degrees 0, 1, 2. Writing $m_j=I((y-d_y)(y^2-9s^2)y^j)$, the $3\times3$ Hankel of these moments must be singular. The exact determinant at $d_y=s$ is
$$\frac{4s(21s^4-14s^2+1)(945s^4-90s^2+1)}{3675};$$
reflection changes only an overall nonzero factor. The exact resultant after putting $s=-u/(u+2)$ against the cubic $Q(u)$ leaves only primes 103 and 149. The three moment equations determine $Q_2$ uniquely in every remaining fiber. At 103, the reconstructed fifth ordinary weight is zero, a contradiction.

There is a common protected diagonal test for the remaining canceled Gaussian and the 149 five-site cases. In centered coordinates, every cubic profile $H(y)$ with $H(d_y)=0$ belongs to an actual supported degree-three column. To see this, a general grade-three column has profile $H$ and pure part $\mathbf g$ times an affine correction. At the varying-residual off-zero group the correction and trace must both vanish; at the other (constant-residual) clock the affine correction can be chosen uniquely to cancel $H$. Thus on the ordered four-zero block these supported columns span the hyperplane of values of cubics vanishing at $d_y$. Its defining form is $\nu_i=\ell_i(d_y)$, the degree-three Lagrange cardinal values at that separate clock. All $\nu_i$ are nonzero in the used controls.

Let $A_U$ denote the actual strictly triangular-ordered four-zero block (with common diagonal $\gamma$), $B_U$ its weight diagonal, and $\mathbf b_U$ the four zero weights. Define the symmetric defect on this block by
$$S=B_U A_U+A_U^{\mathsf T}B_U-\mathbf b_U\mathbf b_U^{\mathsf T}.$$
It vanishes as a bilinear form on the supported hyperplane by ordinary grade-seven pairing. The column with values $r_c(y)=y^2-s^2$ is in its kernel: the clean $F_3$ and $Z_3$ supported primitive reads add to the ordinary mean. Any symmetric form vanishing on $\ker \nu$ in characteristic different from two is $\nu \omega^{\mathsf T}+\omega\nu^{\mathsf T}$ for some $\omega$ (evaluate it at one vector where $\nu$ is 1). Its kernel condition on the supported column gives $\sum_i r_c(y_i)\omega_i=0$. Since $S_{ii}=b_i(2\gamma-b_i)$, the paid scalar diagonal gate is
$$\sum_i r_c(y_i)\frac{b_i(2\gamma-b_i)}{\nu_i}=0.$$
The exact residual is **85** in the canceled Gaussian control at 103 and **62** in the five-site control at 149, in both choices of $d_y$. This excludes two live off-zero clocks.

## 3. One live off-zero clock: penultimate zero and the five-site projection

Let the only live off-zero clock be $d=1-\gamma$. Its physical penultimate row is a live zero (its weight is nonzero and its clock differs from $d$). The true normalized second gap $v$ on the live two-row suffix pays $\kappa(v)=1+2v-v^2=0$. This uses the actual degree-two reverse proportionality and only the live two-row suffix. It does not reflect a silent tableau. The other physical off-zero clock $e$ has no live off-zero rows. In centered coordinates the two chronologically outer zero clocks and the ordinary off-zero clock have the following exact values:

| Case | $\gamma$ | $d_y$ | $e_y$ | First zero $a_y$ | Last zero $b_y$ |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| first clock is $d$ | $1/2$ | $0$ | $u$ | $2u$ | $-v$ |
| first clock is $e$ | $1/(u+2)$ | $u/(u+2)$ | $-u/(u+2)$ | $3u/(u+2)$ | $(u-2v)/(u+2)$ |

The denominator $u+2$ in the second line is nonzero from the first diagonal and the actual two clocks. In both lines $Q(u)=\kappa(v)=0$.

If the off-zero aggregate cancels, the four zero clocks are live Gaussian nodes. In the midpoint line the first pin $L(2u)$ against $Q(u)$ allows only characteristic 267613, while the last pin $L(v)$ against $\kappa(v)$ allows only 71; here $L(y)=35y^4-30y^2+3$. In the other line the first pin allows only 103, whose complete splitting field has no accepted last pin. If the off-zero ordinary aggregate is nonzero but there are only four ordinary sites, then $d_y$ and the **live** last zero $b_y$ are both Gaussian, even if the first zero is silent. In the midpoint line $d_y=0$ is impossible. In the other line the two Gaussian pins on $d_y,b_y$, using $Q(u),\kappa(v)$, have exact integer resultant gcd supported only on paid primes. This covers repeated zero clocks, overlaps with $d$, and a silent zero weight whenever only four ordinary sites occur.

Thus five ordinary sites are necessary: all four zero weights are live, their clocks are distinct, and $d$ is not a zero clock. The nodal polynomial is
$$(y-d_y)(y-a_y)(y-b_y)Q_2(y),$$
where $Q_2$ is monic quadratic for the two interior zero clocks. As before its degree-seven ordinary moments give a three-by-three signed Hankel determinant zero. To project, form the three linear factors homogeneously using the displayed denominators and take resultants first against $\kappa(v)$ in $v$ and then against $Q(u)$ in $u$. Only paid rational denominators occur; no ordered or prime-field root is assumed. The stripped supports are respectively **770514337** and **26254266039604698273071**, each prime by a full recursive exact Lucas certificate.

## 4. Literal four-zero block at the last two prime candidates

The exact field replay computes a splitting field for $Q,\kappa$ at each prime and then doubles that extension degree, so every surviving $Q_2$ is split. This covers arbitrary extensions: both actual outer clock parameters lie in the field generated by the two gap roots, and the paid moment equations fix the remaining monic quadratic (their actual $2\times2$ pivot is nonzero in both controls). For each accepted pair of gaps it recovers the five ordinary weights by the degree-four Lagrange cardinal formula. There is exactly one such control at each characteristic; the two possible inner orders are both tested.

On the actual ordered four-zero submatrix $A_U$, use the clean forward read $A_U r_c=Jr_c$, where $r_c(y)=(y-d_y)(y-e_y)$ is the supported pure polynomial. Use also the supported reverse row of trace $y-d_y$, with actual values $b_i(y_i-d_y)$: its degree-two pure part vanishes at every zero column, so its read of $A_U$ is $b_i J^*(y-d_y)(y_i)$. The three supported reverse rows of traces $(y-d_y)^j$, $j=1,2,3$, pair with $A_U$ of the supported forward traces $r_c,y r_c$ by ordinary rooted pairing. All of these are literal equations because the forward and reverse support masks remove the off-zero coordinates, no matter how the zero rows are interleaved.

The six strict entries of $A_U$ are the unknowns, with diagonal $\gamma$ fixed. In both possible orders at each prime, the same seven actual rows of the augmented linear system (indices 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 11, 13 in zero-based construction order) give an invertible $7\times7$ minor. Its determinant as the two interior roots are exchanged has nonzero product: **170900925** at the first prime and **306422335611480264695** at the second. This is an exact quadratic norm, not a numerical residual; the code recovers the affine determinant in the chosen first interior root and verifies the norm $a^2-ka b+qb^2$ for its monic polynomial $X^2+kX+q$. Consequently neither chronology has a triangular supported block, over any extension field.

## Domain and replay

No real or ordered-field hypothesis is used. As a conditional ring corollary to both supported-chart exclusions, if $8!$, $\gamma$, all six off-zero residuals, and at least one value of a supported column $\mathbf v\in E_2\cap U$ are units in a nonzero commutative ring, reduction at a maximal ideal preserves the exact zero support and the nonzero supported column. This is impossible by the two paid-field results. Without that value-unit guard the entire supported column may reduce to zero.
