# Coprime eight-active arithmetic rear exclusion

At strict SDIRK8 with ten stages over a characteristic-zero field, use the [eight-active reverse-silent reduction](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-eight-reverse-vanish/README.md). The sole nonzero quadratic defect is at the weight-zero first stage. There are eight individually active rows with distinct clocks, both literal fronts active, and the other ordinarily silent stage has actual reverse read zero, so it is physical four. Here the affine reverse numerator \(T\) is nonconstant and coprime to \(L_4\); assume the actual active rear three rows are in their reverse arithmetic phase. The packet excludes this entire arithmetic phase, retaining separately a Gauss terminal, penultimate, or eighth. Together with [constant](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-eight-reverse-vanish/README.md), [shared-root](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-eight-shared-gauss/README.md), and [coprime reverse-star](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-eight-coprime-star/README.md) packets, this closes the specified **eight-distinct-active, both literal fronts, actual \(w_4=0\)** chart. It is not an exclusion of all eight-active tableaux and does not change the unconditional **10–11** interval.

Write \(Q(Y)=q_0+q_1Y\) in normalized time \(Y=c_i/\gamma\), with the two literal front anchors; set \(q(X)=\gamma q_0+q_1X\) in physical time. Then \(q,T,w_i\) are nonzero at every actual active site, since an active root of \(q\) would also be a root of \(L_4\). The cubic profile is proportional to \(L_4/q\), and \(\boldsymbol h^{\mathsf T}B=\boldsymbol wP_1(C)\) with \(P_1\) affine nonzero. Thus a Gauss active stage is a root of \(P_1\); there is at most one and cannot be either literal front. At such a clock the real cubic amplitude is zero but its ordinary and reverse weights are both nonzero. No Gaussian ordinary mass is imposed.

## Fifth projection with five non-Gauss pegs

Write \(x=c_{10}=1-\gamma\), \(c=x-\gamma u\), \(d=x-2\gamma u\), \(k=1+2u-u^2\), \(H=-2+u+4u^2-u^3\), and \(J=4u^3-12u^2+3u+2\). The [actual reverse ratios](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-eight-reverse-vanish/README.md) give the cleared arithmetic equation
\[
 H L_4(d)q(c)q(x)+2J L_4(c)q(d)q(x)-kJ L_4(x)q(c)q(d)=0 .
\]
Divide the exact nonzero \(u^2\) factor to obtain \(F(\gamma,u)\), of bidegree \((14,5)\) in \((\gamma,u)\). The actual terminal and penultimate reads give the affine shape
\[
 \rho(X)=(X-c)kL_4(x)q(c)+(x-X)L_4(c)q(x)
\]
of \(P_1\), without dividing a tail defect. On this chart the [eight-site nodal equation](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-eight-reverse-vanish/README.md) gives \(D\bmod L_4\) proportional to \(\rho q^2 W_{L_4}^{-1}\bmod L_4\), with an irrelevant sign. Use monic \(L_4\) for the quotient.

For an occupied peg \(p\), the divided difference \(K_p=(L_4(X)-L_4(p))/(X-p)\) is the inverse of \(X-p\) modulo \(L_4\) up to the nonzero scalar \(-L_4(p)\). Multiply by the five \(K_p\)'s, \(p=\gamma A_0,\gamma B_0,x,c,d\), to get the missing cubic up to a nonzero scale. Its coefficients have the exact common factor \(u L_4(\gamma A_0)L_4(\gamma B_0)L_4(x)L_4(c)\), in the chosen normalization. All five factors are nonzero in this branch; no division by \(L_4(d)\) was made in this construction. The actual fifth must satisfy the [literal fifth cross after elimination of silent fourth](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-eight-coprime-star/README.md), clearing \(Q(Y)\) and removing only its two padded occupied front roots. Its resultant with the missing cubic gives \(M(\gamma,u)\), bidegree \((78,15)\). No additional two-variable factor is deleted from \(M\).

The exact diagonal resultant of \(F,M\) is degree 600 in either front phase, computed by fraction-free elimination over the exact field \(2t^2=1\). ### Keeping the exact contact diagonal under control

Write \(F_d=2L_4(c)q(x)-k L_4(x)q(c)\). The separately retained diagonal factor is the exact \(u\)-resultant of \(F_d,L_4(d)\) after the same true independent guards. That root has \(u\ne0\) by \(L_4(x)\ne0\); it solves \(F=0\) by the cleared polynomial equation. At this root \(\rho(d)=0\). Thus \(\rho q^2 W_{L_4}^{-1}\bmod L_4\) vanishes at \(d\) and is annihilated by multiplication by \(K_d=L_4/(X-d)\). All divided content factors of the missing cubic are units on this contact fiber by the full reduced contact tests, so the normalized missing cubic is zero there and \(M=0\).

To show there is **no other** common gap, use the five-dimensional monic gap quotient over the entire contact diagonal, with actual \(F\) leading coefficient a unit. In every reduced diagonal factor the common gcd of \(F,M\) has degree at most one. Equivalently multiplication by \(M\) has rank at least four, with the four-minor ideal a unit over the local finite diagonal algebra by reduction. Therefore every characteristic-zero fiber has at most one common gap. The known contact is that gap. A genuine non-Gauss solution cannot have gone missing merely because its diagonal agrees with the contact diagonal.

## Keeping Gauss pegs as actual physical clocks

Use the actual Gauss root \(r\), \(L_4(r)=0\), as a monic degree-four coefficient parameter. If the terminal is Gauss, \(\gamma=1-r\) and use \(F(\gamma,u)\). If penultimate is Gauss, \(u=(x-r)/\gamma\) in the same equation; clear precisely powers of the strict \(\gamma\). If eighth is Gauss, \(u=(x-r)/(2\gamma)\), and use the stronger \(F_d=0\) directly: \(c=(x+d)/2\), \(P_1(d)=0\), so \(2h_9=k h_{10}\). This last equation does not permit an artificial \(J=0\) root from the cleared rear formula. The actual rear \(H,J\) cannot vanish, since the nonzero actual eighth reverse ratio satisfies \(H k_8=-kJ\), \(k\ne0\), and the fixed polynomials \(H,J\) are coprime. The exact monic parameter degrees after structural removal are \(5,12,9\), respectively, in either front phase.

Every physical Gauss contact passes this exact exclusion, with no ordinary Gaussian weight assigned and no Gaussian atom subtracted.
