# Exact finite diagonal projection for the one-front coprime branch

Consider strict SDIRK8 with ten stages over a characteristic-zero field. The sole physical nonzero quadratic defect is at the ordinarily weight-zero first stage. Exactly eight ordinarily active physical stages have distinct clocks. The extra silent row has actual reverse read zero, only one literal front is active, and the affine reverse numerator \(T\) is nonconstant and coprime to \(L_4\), as in the [eight-active reduction](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-eight-reverse-vanish/README.md). This packet proves that each early and rear phase admits only **finitely many possible strict diagonals**: it specifies a nonzero exact polynomial via companion matrices and a witnessed square minor. It does **not** expand or exclude all roots of that polynomial, and makes no unconditional change to **10–11**.

## The common equations, without a Gauss or ordering assumption

The actual fourth and fifth are ordinary. Use the homogeneous normalized profile from the [one-front coprime terminal packet](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-eight-coprime-one-front-terminal/README.md),
\[
 Q_*(Y)=(s-Y)H_4(s)L_4(\gamma a)+(Y-a)H_aL_4(\gamma s),
 \qquad P_*(Y)=H_aH_4(s)(s-a)L_4(\gamma Y).
\]
Here \(a\) is the active literal clock, \(s\) actual normalized fourth and \(y\) actual normalized fifth. This time \(\gamma\) is an indeterminate. The actual \(H_4\) and \(A\boldsymbol h\) fourth polynomials are coprime, so \(H_4(s)\ne0\). The physical fourth cannot duplicate either earlier literal clock. Both padded factors \(Y-a,Y-s\) divide the actual normalized fifth equations exactly, yielding cubics \(E_T,E_A\). Their exact \(Y\)-resultant has bidegree \((24,21)\) in \((\gamma,s)\). The first removed factor is the exact sixth power of the physical fourth collision with the silent literal; the second is the exact normalized homogeneous slope-pole coprimality factor. The latter is normalized only by already nonzero factors, so is nonzero at an actual candidate. The surviving exact equation \(D(\gamma,s)=0\) has bidegree \((12,7)\).

Write \(q(X)=\gamma Q_{*,0}+Q_{*,1}X\) in physical time, and \(x=1-\gamma,c=x-\gamma u,d=x-\gamma(2-u)\) in rear star or \(d=x-2\gamma u\) in rear arithmetic. The exact cleared actual rear identity, after dividing only the forbidden physical factors \(u(u-1)(u-2)\) or \(u^2\), is \(F(\gamma,s,u)=0\) of \(u\)-degree two or five. It uses no terminal cubic-amplitude inverse. The actual affine reverse multiplier is proportional to
\[
 \rho(X)=(X-c)k L_4(x)q(c)+(x-X)L_4(c)q(x).
\]
This polynomial is nonzero: the actual two trailing reverse ratios are nonzero, \(x,c\) are distinct, and the actual affine multiplier cannot vanish at both. A Gauss peg is not assumed absent.

The six ordinarily occupied pegs \(\gamma a,\gamma s,\gamma y,x,c,d\) are distinct. Write \(T_6\) for their monic product. The actual nodal \(D_8=T_6 R_2\) has monic quadratic \(R_2\), and by the [eight-site Bézout relation](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-eight-reverse-vanish/README.md) its \(L_4\) residue is proportional to the residue of \(V=\rho q^2 W_{L_4}^{-1}\). In the fixed monic \(L_4\) quotient, the four coefficient vectors of \(T_6,XT_6,X^2T_6,V\) therefore have determinant \(\mathcal N=0\). This necessary determinant does not divide by a Legendre value, a masked signed mass, or any reduced collision factor.

## The exact nonzero polynomial specified by a residue witness

Work first over \(K_0(\gamma)\), \(K_0=\mathbb Q(t)\), \(2t^2=1\). The prospective model is obtained by the monic equations \(D\) in \(s\), \(E_T\) in \(y\), and \(F\) in \(u\), after inverting their actual leading coefficients in the appropriate algebras. The degree-seven leading coefficient of \(D\), and the norms of the leading coefficients of \(E_T,F\) in the seven-dimensional \(s\)-algebra, are nonzero rational functions of \(\gamma\). This is certified by one degree-preserving split in each phase: evaluate at \((1097,t=345,\gamma=2)\) except \(\gamma=3\) for arithmetic front with physical two active and rear star. The replay builds the **full** finite \(s\) algebra and checks both leading coefficients are units there; it does not throw away smaller-degree factors. These same lead inversions exist in characteristic zero over \(K_0(\gamma)\).

In the resulting finite free algebra use the monomial basis \(s^i y^j u^k\), \(s\) fastest, then \(y\), then \(u\). Its size is \(42\) in rear star and \(105\) in arithmetic. Concatenate the multiplication matrices of \(E_A\) and \(\mathcal N\) in that basis. The replay gives an explicit ordered set of square columns, from elimination in the single degree-preserving residue, with nonzero determinant. These column indices specify an exact square minor of the *symbolic* multiplication matrix over \(K_0(\gamma)\). Its determinant is a nonzero rational function, since the same monic companion matrices and unit leading inverses commute with the verified specialization. This certifies the existence of the nonzero coefficient polynomial; it is not a claim that a random scan over \(\gamma\) found no tableau.

A definite necessary nonzero diagonal polynomial can be obtained by multiplying three factors and the numerator of that minor: the leading coefficient of \(D\), and the numerators of the two leading coefficient norms in the monic \(s\)-algebra. If one of those leading guards vanishes at an actual \(\gamma\), the product vanishes already. Otherwise all exact matrices specialize, and evaluation at actual \(s,y,u\) is a row functional annihilating both multiplication blocks, since \(E_A=\mathcal N=0\). The row has first coordinate one (its value on the constant monomial); it annihilates the chosen square columns and their determinant is therefore zero. Thus every genuine \(\gamma\) is a root of the specified nonzero polynomial over \(K_0\), without claiming the exact roots have been excluded. No actual tableau is reduced modulo the witness prime.
