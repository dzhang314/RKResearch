# Physical pure-four graph and fifth clock in the corrected shared-quintic chart

Work over a characteristic-zero field, not necessarily ordered. Consider the ten-stage strict SDIRK8 chart with sole nonzero quadratic defect at physical stage one, ordinarily and reversely silent. The other nine physical clocks are distinct. Eight are ordinarily weighted and the extra ordinary-silent row \(\zeta\) is actually reverse-live. The affine reverse numerator \(T\) and genuine quintic mixed numerator \(S\) share the root \(r\) of \(T\), a genuinely weighted clock with both actual reverse weight and normalized cubic amplitude zero. Assume \(H(r)\ne0\) for the continued quartic \(H=(S/T)/\gamma^3\). Use the [ordinary-hole source](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-eight-reverse-live-affine-shared-quintic-hole-literal-closed/README.md) for \(H=\alpha L_4+\beta K_3(X,r)\), \(\alpha\ne0\), \(\beta=b_rH(r)\ne0\). Agreement and the earlier positional exclusions are separate. These are **necessary gates**, not exclusions of actual hole six or seven. The unrestricted **10–11** interval remains.

Write \(\boldsymbol h\) for the **actual** normalized cubic amplitudes, so \(h_i=H(c_i)\) for \(i\ne1\) except at the ordinary hole \(r\), where \(h(r)=0\). In particular \(h_\zeta=H(c_\zeta)\) since \(T(c_\zeta)\ne0\). Write \(C=\operatorname{diag}(\boldsymbol c)\), \(A'=A/\gamma\), and \(I(X^j)=1/(j+1)\). The ordinary rule pays degrees zero through seven, not eight or nine.

## The physical pure-four graph

The [saturated duality](../sdirk-eight-saturation/README.md), [reverse pure-four rows](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-eight-reverse-vanish/README.md), and [nine-site construction](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-nine-site-pade/README.md) give
\[
 F_4=\operatorname{Ann}(W_3),\qquad
 W_3=\langle \boldsymbol b^{\mathsf T}C^j\ (0\le j\le3),
                 \boldsymbol w,\boldsymbol wC\rangle .
\]
Here \(k=1+2u-u^2\ne0\) by nine-site chronology. All these rows vanish at physical one. For \(\boldsymbol v\in F_4\), interpolate \(S_v(c_i)=D_9'(c_i)w_i v_i\) over the nine sites. The two paid reverse moments kill the two top coefficients, giving \(\deg S_v\le6\), and \(S_v(r)=0\). Thus \(V=S_v/T\) has degree at most five and agrees with \(\boldsymbol v\) at every nonhole site, including \(\zeta\).

Subtract a scalar multiple of \(C\boldsymbol h\in F_4\) to remove the fifth coefficient of \(V\), giving \(\boldsymbol v'\) with continued \(V'\). Since \(\deg V'\le4\), precision seven and the paid ordinary probes give
\[
 I(X^jV')=d r^j,\qquad d=b_r(V'(r)-v'_r),\quad 0\le j\le3 .
\]
Consequently \(V'=fL_4+dK_3(X,r)\). Subtract \((d/\beta)\boldsymbol h\); the continued polynomial becomes \(fL_4\). At \(r\) it agrees with the physical column as well: \(v'_r=V'(r)-d/b_r\), and \((d/\beta)H(r)=d/b_r\) while actual \(h(r)=0\). Thus every pure-four column has the actual graph
\[
 v_i=\ell(c_i/\gamma)h_i+fL_4(c_i)\qquad(2\le i\le10),\qquad \deg\ell\le1 . \tag{1}
\]
The first coordinate was not identified with the graph, and its clock may coincide with a zero-row clock. Only two reverse moments and ordinary degrees through seven were used.

## True fourth rank and the next-clock fiber

Set \(s=c_4/\gamma\). The early literal clock ratios are \(a=2+2t\), with \(b=2-2t\) (star front) or \(b=3+4t\) (arithmetic front), \(2t^2=1\). The actual \(A'_{32}\) is \(m=0\) or \((1+6t)/2\). Let \(h_a=(a-1)/2,\ h_b=(b-1-m)/2\); both nonzero. These remain literal when an early row is \(\zeta\). Use the genuine columns
\[
 \boldsymbol v_A=A'\boldsymbol h,\qquad
 \boldsymbol v_\tau=(A\boldsymbol c^{\odot3}-\boldsymbol c^{\odot4}/4)/\gamma^4 .
\]
Let \(\ell_v(Y)\) interpolate \(v_2/h_a,v_3/h_b\) at \(a,b\), and \(\ell_L(Y)\) likewise interpolate \(L_4(\gamma a)/h_a,L_4(\gamma b)/h_b\). The three quantities
\[
 M_0=Y-1-2h,\quad M_1=Y^2/2-Y-2h,\quad
 M_2=Y^3/3-Y^2-\tfrac43h
\]
are the strict predecessor moments in \(A'\) **after separating actual stage one**. Indeed \(A'_{i1}=2h_i\) and the quadratic residual on this row is zero. The cubic residual equals \((2/3)\gamma^3h_i\), since it belongs to the pure-three space generated by the first axis and \(\boldsymbol h\), with scalar fixed by literal stage two. These moments also apply when \(h=0\) or the row is ordinarily silent.

At literal four the two other predecessors force \(M_2-(a+b)M_1+abM_0=0\). Write \(H_4(Y)\) for its cubic solution for \(h\), not the continued quartic \(H\). Solve both literal fourth reads \(v_{\mathrm{four}}(Y)\) by recovering the two taps from \(M_0,M_1\). With exact pad \((Y-a)(Y-b)\), put
\[
 R_v(Y)=\frac{v_{\mathrm{four}}(Y)-\ell_v(Y)H_4(Y)}{(Y-a)(Y-b)},\quad
 D(Y)=\frac{L_4(\gamma Y)-\ell_L(Y)H_4(Y)}{(Y-a)(Y-b)}.
\]
\(D\) is of degree at most two. Actual distinctness and (1) give \(R_v(s)=f_vD(s)\). Both \(R_v\) are \(1/4\) in the star front; in the arithmetic front they are coprime quadratics over \(\mathbb Q(t)\). Thus \(D(s)\ne0\) on this whole actual chart and
\[
 D(s)\big(v_i-\ell_v(Y_i)h_i\big)
   =R_v(s)\big(L_4(\gamma Y_i)-\ell_L(Y_i)h_i\big),\quad Y_i=c_i/\gamma . \tag{2}
\]
This pivot does not divide the old \(L_4,K_3\) anchor determinant. In fact if that determinant \(\Delta=0\), then \(\ell_L=L_4(\gamma a)/h_a\) is constant. At true fourth,
\[
 h_a\big(L_4(\gamma s)-\ell_L(s)h_4\big)
 =\beta\big(K_3(\gamma a,r)L_4(\gamma s)-L_4(\gamma a)K_3(\gamma s,r)\big).
\]
Hence the alternate \(a,s\) anchor is nonzero, even if true fourth is ordinarily silent or \(h_4=0\). This recovers the profile if needed on the earlier rank-loss locus, rather than excluding that locus.

At true fifth \(Y=y=c_5/\gamma,\ h=h_5\). For holes six and seven, its three other predecessors and fifth itself are nonhole, even if fifth is \(\zeta\). Set \(E_3(Y)=(Y-a)(Y-b)(Y-s)\). The degree-at-most-two interpolation polynomials for the actual earlier amplitudes and cubed clocks are
\[
 P_h=H_4-[Y^3]H_4\,E_3,\quad P_c=Y^3-E_3 .
\]
With \(\mathcal M(P)=\sum_{j=0}^2 [Y^j]P\,M_j\), the actual reads are \(v_A=h+\mathcal M(P_h)\), \(v_\tau=y^3-y^4/4+2h+\mathcal M(P_c)\). Equation (2) gives two equations affine in the **same actual \(h\)**, \(a_0+a_1h=b_0+b_1h=0\). Their determinant in the two columns has degree at most five in \(y\). Each genuine earlier row pads the determinant by substituting its own literal amplitude and taking the extra taps to be zero. Also the determinant has exact factor \(D(s)\), since both lines use the same \(L_4(\gamma y)-\ell_L(y)h\) column. Both \(D(s)\) and \(E_3(y)\) are actual nonzero factors; their removal gives
\[
 Q_5(\gamma,s,y)=\frac{a_0b_1-a_1b_0}{D(s)E_3(y)}=0.
\]
It has degree at most two in \(y\) and is **never the zero polynomial in any characteristic-zero fiber**:
- Star front: the linear coefficient equals \(-s/2\) times the quadratic coefficient plus a polynomial in \(\gamma\) coprime to that quadratic coefficient. If the quadratic degree drops, the linear term remains.
- Arithmetic front: the quadratic coefficient is \(p(\gamma)s+q(\gamma)\), with coprime \(p,q\). If it vanishes, \(p\ne0\), and the degree-twelve homogeneous evaluations of the other coefficients at \(s=-q/p\) are coprime. Both linear and constant-degree fibers are retained.

## Stronger fifth amplitude gates

Cross multiplying the true graph rows by \(R_\tau(s),R_A(s)\) cancels the common \(L_4\) term, then cancels actual \(D(s)\ne0\). In the star front this gives
\[
 (y-(3s-1)/2)(h_5-H_4(y))=0,\qquad
 Q_5=\tfrac12(y-(3s-1)/2)\,\frac{D(y)-D(s)}{y-s}.
\]
Thus \(y=(3s-1)/2\) is an entire exceptional branch (do not set its \(h_5=H_4(y)\)). Otherwise \(h_5=H_4(y)\) and \(y=-s-d_1/d_2\), where \(D(Y)=d_2Y^2+d_1Y+d_0\). The polynomials \(d_1,d_2\) in \(\gamma\) are coprime, so \(d_2=0\) cannot host a nonexceptional root. In this branch \(H(\gamma Y)=H_4(Y)+\lambda E_3(Y)(Y-y)\), \(\lambda\ne0\), by its four actual values.

In the arithmetic front let \(J(s,y)\) be the coefficient of \(h_5\) in the cross-multiplied equation, with combination \(R_\tau G_A-R_A G_\tau\) for \(G_v=v-\ell_v h\). Then
\[
 J(s,y)(h_5-H_4(y))+\kappa E_3(y)(s+y-6-4t)=0,\qquad
 \kappa=729/56-(129/7)t\ne0 .
\]
This recovers true \(h_5\) independently of \(\gamma\) if \(J\ne0\). If \(J=0\), the second term gives \(y=6+4t-s\). The resulting \(J\) is a nonzero constant times
\[
 (s-2+2t)\big(s^2-(6+8t)s+237/17+(380/17)t\big).
\]
All three \(s\) roots are retained, with actual graph reads still required. Neither front changes any physical label.

## Actual sixth and projection ledger

At true sixth take \(Y=c_6/\gamma,\ h=h_6\) and the same \(M_0,M_1,M_2\). Its four nonfirst predecessors are \(a,b,s,y\), all distinct. Equation (2) determines \(D(s)v_A,D(s)v_\tau\). Put
\[
 E_4(X)=(X-a)(X-b)(X-s)(X-y),\quad
 P_3(X)=H(\gamma X)-[X^4]H(\gamma X)\,E_4(X),\quad
 M_3=Y^4/4-Y^3-2h+v_\tau .
\]
All four earlier amplitudes are true values of \(H\) for both holes. \(P_3\) has degree at most three and interpolates these actual amplitudes. Hence
\[
 D(s)(v_A-h)=\sum_{j=0}^3[X^j]P_3(X)\,D(s)M_j .               \tag{3}
\]
At actual hole six use \(h=0,\ Y=r/\gamma\), **not** \(h=H(r)\). At actual hole seven keep true \(h_6=H(c_6)\), including when sixth is \(\zeta\). No artificial pad is removed at the hole. With \(h_5=H_4(y)\) in the star front, \(P_3=H_4\); (3) at true hole six gives the especially small gate
\[
 9L_4(r)+D(s)(Y^4-16Y^3+72Y^2-96Y+24)=0.
\]
This is not a literal hole-seven condition.
