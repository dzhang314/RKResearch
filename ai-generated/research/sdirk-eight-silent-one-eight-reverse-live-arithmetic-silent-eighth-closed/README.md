# Arithmetic literal front with actual ordinarily silent eighth

This excludes strict ten-stage SDIRK8 over a characteristic-zero field. Physical first is the **sole nonzero quadratic-defect row**, ordinarily silent. The eight ordinary active clocks are distinct. Physical eighth is ordinarily silent but has its own actual nonzero reverse read at a ninth distinct site. The [nine-site reverse numerator is constant nonzero](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-eight-reverse-live-constant/README.md); the cubic profile is a genuine mixed quintic, with regular fourth anchoring and both literal early quartic evaluations nonzero. The literal front is arithmetic. There is no ordinary star/arithmetic rear choice when eighth is silent. Together with the earlier star-front case, this excludes the entire regular constant-numerator actual-silent-eighth placement. It does not cover ordinary arithmetic-rear cases or change the unrestricted **10–11** stage gap.

## Four-peg pivot and actual ordinary fifth

The four fixed ordinary pegs \(a=\gamma A_0,b=\gamma B_0,q=\gamma s,x=1-\gamma\) give the same [fixed-peg kernel](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-eight-reverse-live-star-silent-eighth-closed/README.md#fixed-known-peg-pivot-not-a-residue-assumption). The true monic ordinary \(D_8\) satisfies \(H_*W_{D_8}-D_8 W_{H_*}=\lambda\ne0\) using only ordinary scalar precision **seven**. The homogeneous profile \(H_*\) has degree five and is nonzero at each ordinary site. If the four lower columns had a kernel, its \(D'\) would be divisible by the four-peg nodal \(E_4\), would have degree at most seven, and would have constant read \(\lambda'\). Since \(\gcd(H_*,W_{H_*})=1\), the difference \(\lambda D'-\lambda'D_8\) is divisible by \(E_4H_*\), whose degree nine exceeds the bound eight. Thus it is zero and \(D'=0\) by degree. The fixed four-peg leading pivot is consequently nonzero before reduction.

The monic missing quartic's scalar multiple \(N(u)\) is the five-peg determinant with fifth peg \(x-\gamma u\), after the exact homogeneous normalizations of the [silent-eighth finite reduction](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-eight-reverse-live-regular-silent-eighth-finite/README.md). It has true degree four in \(u\) because the fixed four-peg pivot is nonzero and \(\gamma\ne0\). The actual penultimate root \(u=(x-c)/\gamma\) must also satisfy the degree-five \(F(u)\) there. The other physical silence is not a peg: its actual clock is \(z=x+\gamma k(u-2)/2\), \(k=1+2u-u^2\). The whole exact tail-and-nodal resultant \(D=\operatorname{Res}_u(F,N)\) after its licensed guard divisions has \((s,\gamma)\)-bidegree \((32,90)\).

Physical fifth is ordinary. Its actual \(Y=c_5/\gamma\) satisfies \(N(x/\gamma-Y)=0\) as well as the separate [arithmetic-front literal fifth cubic](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-eight-reverse-live-arithmetic-star567-regular-closed/README.md#actual-fifth-separately-for-its-two-physical-roles), which already retains the arithmetic third coupling. In particular the fifth determinant is divided only by known literal pads \(Y=A_0,B_0,s\) and its listed nonzero scales, not by a hypothetical coincidence with silent eighth. Transform \(N\) to a polynomial in \(Y\) by clearing \(\gamma\); the result is a quartic of degrees \((Y,s,\gamma)=(4,8,23)\). Its resultant with the actual fifth cubic has bidegree \((42,105)\) in \(s,\gamma\). The only further divided factors are \(\gamma^6 V_0^2([X^5]H_*)^2\), where \(V_0\ne0\) is the normalized regular fourth anchor and the profile is a genuine quintic. The remaining independent fifth projection \(J\) has bidegree **(32,69)**.

The two small exact quadratic-field resultants are reconstructed after clearing rational scalars in the basis \(1,z_0\), \(z_0=2t,z_0^2=2\). The determinant coefficient norm is bounded by the product of the row norms using \(\lvert a\rvert+2\lvert b\rvert\). The two small rectangular bidegree caps are \((\gamma,s)=(164,52),(105,44)\), with coefficient bounds of 593 and 416 bits and 10 and 7 full-degree split primes. CRT beyond twice each bound certifies full integer pairs; an independent split prime verifies them. This is an exact reconstruction, not saturation at a sample diagonal.
