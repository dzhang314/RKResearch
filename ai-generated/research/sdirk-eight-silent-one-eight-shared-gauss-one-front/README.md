# Shared Gauss root with one active literal front

At the ten-stage strict SDIRK8 floor over a characteristic-zero field, assume that only the ordinarily weight-zero first stage has nonzero quadratic defect. Exactly eight ordinarily active physical stages have distinct clocks; the extra silent row has actual reverse read zero, \(w_\zeta=0\), and exactly one of the two literal front stages is active. This packet excludes **nonconstant reverse numerator \(T\) sharing its root with \(L_4\)**. The other literal front keeps its actual early defect and taps, not an ordinary or weighted virtual amplitude. Together with the prior two-front [shared-root exclusion](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-eight-shared-gauss/README.md), this eliminates shared-root \(T\) in the eight-distinct-active reverse-silent chart. One-front nonconstant coprime \(T\), repeated active clocks, genuinely reverse-active silence, and the nine-active branch are separate. The unrestricted strict SDIRK8 interval stays **10–11**.

## One active Gauss hole, with its actual amplitude zero

Use the [eight-active support and nodal reduction](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-eight-reverse-vanish/README.md). The active pure-four space is spanned by \(\boldsymbol h,C\boldsymbol h\), the cubic defect is detected, \(T\ne0\), and \(T(c_i)h_i=\sigma L_4(c_i)\) on active axes. The affine reverse row is \(\boldsymbol h^{\mathsf T}B=\boldsymbol wP_1(C)\) with \(P_1\ne0\) and nonzero rear factor \(k\). If \(r\) is the shared Gauss root, the [same exact active-hole proof](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-eight-shared-gauss/README.md) applies without two active fronts. It makes \(r\) one of the active physical clocks, with \(w_r=h_r=0\) and \(b_r=\nu=I(P)/P(r)\), where \(P=L_4/(X-r)\) and \(I\) denotes formal integration over \([0,1]\). The cubic profile on nonhole active stages is proportional to \(P\). An active zero of \(P\) away from \(r\) is permitted, and no absolute nonzero-defect assumption is made at any nonhole tail.

The active literal \(a\) and terminal and penultimate cannot be the hole: the first has nonzero actual cubic amplitude and the other two have nonzero actual reverse weights. The literal fourth cannot be the hole either. If its actual \(H_4(s)=0\), the active pure-four line implies its \(A\boldsymbol h\) read also vanishes, but the exact gcd of the actual normalized fourth read polynomials is one. Physical row four also cannot repeat either actual literal clock \(A_0,B_0\) by the [literal unique three-tap solve](../sdirk-eight-silent-two-unequal/README.md). Thus the initial possible hole positions are physical 5, 6, 7, 8.

## Direct fifth-hole cut and exact nonhole fifth projection

Write normalized clock \(Y=c/\gamma\) and actual normalized cubic defect \(H_a\ne0\) at \(a\). The nonhole profile is
\[
 H(Y)=\frac{H_a P(\gamma Y)}{P(\gamma a)} .
\]
Here \(P(\gamma a)\ne0\) by the active anchor, and the literal fourth normalized \(s\) is nonhole. Give its exact cubic read \(H_4(s)\) this profile. After removing the padded factor \(s-a\) the resulting equation is the quadratic \(\Phi(\gamma,s)=0\). The two actual pure-four lines for \(\tau_4=A\boldsymbol c^{\odot3}-\boldsymbol c^{\odot4}/4\) and \(A\boldsymbol h\) are anchored at \(a,s\). Their literal fifth constraints are those in the [one-front constant packet](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-eight-constant-one-front/README.md), now with the given cubic \(P\). In particular the *actual* preceding clock set is \(A_0,B_0,s\) after removal of the stage-one feed, and its cubic defect interpolant is the affine literal profile plus \(e(s)(X-A_0)(X-B_0)\) with an exact affine \(e\).

If fifth is the hole, its actual normalized amplitude is zero, not a cubic evaluation. Its two literal pure-four reads before dividing by the active profile are \(T_0(s,r/\gamma)=0\) and \(A_0^{\rm read}(s,r/\gamma)=0\). Both are affine in \(s\); their \(s\) coefficients are \(J_0(r/\gamma)\) and \(e_1 J_0(r/\gamma)\), where \(e_1\) is the slope of \(e\). Thus their difference \(A_0^{\rm read}-e_1T_0\) is an actual equation independent of \(s\). Homogenize at \(Y=r/\gamma\), and also project \(\Phi,T_0(\cdot,r/\gamma)\) in \(s\). The two diagonal projections have degrees four and eleven; their exact common gcd is one after genuine \(\gamma,P(\gamma a)\) guards, in every early phase. This excludes the actual fifth hole without extrapolating the nonhole profile.

Otherwise fifth has the true profile \(H(Y)\). Clear the two literal equations with \(H_4(s)\), the active anchor denominator, and \(s-a\) as in the earlier literal proof. Divide the exact padded \(Y-a\) root. The two remainders on division by \(Y-s\) are exactly multiples of \(\Phi\), so on \(\Phi=0\) the actual nonzero \(Y-s\) is dropped. There is no \(Y-B_0\) or \(Y-A_0\) deletion at a *silent* earlier literal. The two retained fifth polynomials are quadratic in \(Y\). Their \(Y\)-resultant, after exact removal of the forbidden fourth factors \(s-A_0,s-B_0\), has bidegree \((12,16)\) in \((\gamma,s)\). Its \(s\)-resultant with \(\Phi\) is of degree 72, leaving degree 66 after the actual \(\gamma\ne0,P(\gamma a)\ne0\) guards.

For clarity, the last resultant is exact even at lowered \(s\) degree. If \(\Phi=C_0+C_1s+C_2s^2\) and the other polynomial has \(s\)-degree \(n\), use the monic variable \(Z=C_2s\), \(Z^2+C_1Z+C_0C_2=0\). Reduce its \(C_2^n\)-scaled read to \(a+bZ\), take the exact norm \(a^2-C_1ab+C_0C_2b^2\), and divide by \(C_2^n\) **as an exact polynomial identity** before specializing. The possible actual equations with \(C_2=0\) are explicitly retained. The computations use both exact relations \(2t^2=1,L_4(r)=0\); no ordering of Gauss roots or of tableau clocks is used.

## Rear and lowered fourth: exact, not modular removal

When all three rear stages are nonhole, substitute the cubic \(P\) at the actual tail ratios. In reverse star divide the exact collision factor \(u(u-1)(u-2)\). The quotient is constant in \(u\); its diagonal condition has exact gcd one with the degree-66 early projection. In reverse arithmetic the cleared gap has exact \(u^2\) factor and leaves a cubic; its leading coefficient has exact gcd one with the retained diagonal. No tail cubic value is divided.

If physical eight is the hole, reverse star would violate \(w_8=0\) because \(k\ne0\). In reverse arithmetic the actual identity \(H k_8=-kJ\) with \(k_8=0\) forces \(J=4u^3-12u^2+3u+2=0\). Here \(d=x-2\gamma u=r\), so homogenize \(J((x-r)/(2\gamma))\). Its exact gcd with the early projection is already one in the arithmetic front cases, and is degree three in each star front case. On those degree-three loci the actual fourth has no admissible root: remove only its actual forbidden clocks/zero read, and test all remaining fourth roots with the first fifth resultant. This is not a generic tail formula evaluated at a nonhole amplitude.

For the remaining reverse arithmetic scan classify every exceptional early diagonal using the true fourth leading coefficient and the exact projected forbidden factor
\[
  H_4(s)(s-A_0)(s-B_0)(\gamma s-r).
\]
The possible leading-drop diagonals have degree three, the possible geometric-shadow diagonals degree nine. Work in these exact coefficient quotients, retaining any linear fourth polynomial when the leading quadratic vanishes. Remove only actual forbidden fourth roots, then take the gcd of what remains with the genuine fifth resultant. All exceptional models are empty. Thus only the degree-30 diagonal with full quadratic fourth and cubic gap needs finite residue testing; the multiplicities of the eliminated exceptional diagonals, not the possibility of other fourth roots on them, account for the degree drop from 66.

## Six nonhole pegs and a signed singleton mask

After the exact fifth- and eighth-hole cuts the six known nonhole pegs \(\gamma a,\gamma s,\gamma Y,x,c_9,c_8\) are all active and distinct. Remove the known Gaussian atom from the ordinary rule, \(I_\circ(f)=I(f)-\nu f(r)\), precise through degree seven at seven active nonhole clocks. With \(D_6\) the monic product of the six pegs, let \(m_0=I_\circ(D_6)\), \(m_1=I_\circ(XD_6)\). Only one nonhole stage remains, so \(D_6(m_0 X-m_1)\) is its full nodal polynomial times a nonzero scalar. This does not divide by the signed mass or any \(P\) value.

The [deflated affine Bézout identity](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-eight-shared-gauss/README.md) gives \(D_7\bmod P\) proportional to \(P_1(W_P^\circ)^{-1}\), with \(P\) rescaled monic as needed. The inverse is checked in the cubic quotient. The earlier two actual rear ratios give the full affine shape
\[
 \rho(X)=(X-c_9)kP(x)+(x-X)P(c_9).
\]
It is valid even if a nonhole tail is a second Gaussian site with zero cubic amplitude, but it is not zero as a polynomial. The two residue vectors of \(D_6(m_0X-m_1)\) and \(\rho(W_P^\circ)^{-1}\) must be proportional; test their three two-by-two minors as a span of residuals. This uses exact signed degree-seven data only and does not infer a Gaussian mass for a second zero-amplitude site.
