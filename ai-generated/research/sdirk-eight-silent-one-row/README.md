# One invisible physical stage: the first undetected cubic-defect closures

Consider ten-stage strict SDIRK8 over a characteristic-zero field. Suppose exactly one physical stage has nonzero quadratic residual and that stage is weight-zero. This stage must be physical stage one, at the nonzero common diagonal \(\gamma\). Use the same setup as the [one-clock packets](../sdirk-eight-silent-oneclock/README.md), with \(\boldsymbol g\) supported at stage one, \(\boldsymbol h=(A-\gamma I)\boldsymbol g\), and \(\boldsymbol w=\boldsymbol b^{\mathsf T}M\).

**Result.** If \(B\boldsymbol h=0\), seven active stages are impossible. With six active, if physical rows four and five are active, the third silent zero must have a clock outside the six distinct active clocks, with nonzero silent aggregate of \(\boldsymbol w\). With five active, at least one extra silent zero is physical row four or five. With four active, the four active positions are exactly \(6,7,9,10\). The latter three statements leave further charts. No conclusion for \(B\boldsymbol h\ne0\), multiple invisible physical clocks, or the unrestricted **10–11** interval.

## Common literal front

The first two nonfirst stages have distinct clocks \(a,b\ne\gamma\), zero quadratic residual and nonzero \(h\) in either literal phase. So if \(B\boldsymbol h=0\), both are silent. The grade-two reverse row annihilates \(\boldsymbol g\), hence \(w_1=0\); \(Z_3\) also vanishes there. The active restriction of \(F_4\) has rank at most one, since \(F_4|_U\) has rank three and the two independent columns \(\boldsymbol h,C\boldsymbol h\) vanish on active stages. Here \(U\) consists of the nine physical quadratic-zero coordinates.

When physical rows four and five are active and \(B\boldsymbol h=0\), they have no strict stage-one tap (there is only this one invisible donor). Their actual scalar reads are consequently exactly the [equal-tag finite front chart](../sdirk-eight-silent-two-equal/README.md): row-four cubic \(H_4(s)=0\) for \(s=c_4/\gamma\), row-five residual cubic for \(y=c_5/\gamma\) after the literal duplicate root is deleted, and a quartic in \(\gamma\) from the common pure-four line. In particular \(s\ne y\), both actual \(A\boldsymbol h\) reads are nonzero, and \(c_4,c_5,x\) are distinct, with \(x=c_{10}\). There is no new assumption about how many later active rows there are in this transfer. The active \(L_4\) vector cannot vanish identically: in that event all active clocks would be Gauss, giving both \(\gamma,c_4\) Gauss, contrary to \(H_4(c_4/\gamma)=0\) by the exact Gauss front test. So \(L_4\) spans the pure-four active line, making both literal clocks \(c_4,c_5\) non-Gauss. Four ordinary sites are then impossible with at most seven active physical rows (each of these two different sites would need a separate canceled pair in addition to the four Gauss sites). Five ordinary sites are excluded by the signed \(3\times3\) Hankel on the three fixed pegs \(c_4,c_5,x\) in the same earlier exact front fiber. Thus there are at least six ordinary sites.

## Seven active: first exclude repetition

Both \(a,b\) are silent and are the only silent zeros. From the paid moments \(\boldsymbol w\boldsymbol h=\boldsymbol w C\boldsymbol h=0\) we have \(w_2=w_3=0\), so \(\boldsymbol w\) is supported on the seven active stages. If any active clocks repeat, there are exactly six ordinary sites by the preceding bound. The six scalar zero moments of \(\boldsymbol w\) imply that its aggregate is zero at each of the six sites, despite nonzero \(w_{10}\). Hence the only physical repeat must be at \(x\). The penultimate \(c_9\ne x\), so \(w_9=0\), giving \(k=1+2u-u^2=0\) for \(u=(x-c_9)/\gamma\). As the last three physical weights are nonzero, the literal reflected front fixes \(c_9,c_8\) in the two phase shapes with the two orientations. The five pegs \(c_4,c_5,x,c_8,c_9\) leave one unknown site. The signed rank-one minor of the first three masked moments must vanish. It is a unit in every \((3,3,4)\) front fiber at \(1103,t=161\).

## Seven active without repetition

The active distinct clocks are physical \(4,\ldots,10\). The six vanishing scalar moments of \(\boldsymbol w\) on these seven sites make its numerator \(T(c_i)=w_i D'(c_i)\) a nonzero constant, for \(D\) the active monic nodal polynomial. Write \(W_D(X)=I_t((D(X)-D(t))/(X-t))\). Ordinary precision gives \(W_D(c_i)=b_iD'(c_i)\) by the Lagrange cardinal. Thus the actual reverse normalized quadratic ratio at each active tail satisfies
\[
  k_i W_D(c_i)=W_D(x).
\]
This reciprocal relation does not assert an independently normalized reversed method is order eight.

The first three active clocks are known in fixed finite fibers. For the third, the first four scalar moments at the normalized strict clock set \(A_0,B_0,s,y\), with normal \(\tau_4\) fixed by the active \(L_4\) line, solve its strict aggregate taps. The prescribed \(A\boldsymbol h\) yields a quartic. Its padded earlier roots \(s,y\) divide exactly, and are not actual because of distinctness. This is the same guarded quadratic for the third active clock \(z=c_6/\gamma\) as in the earlier finite six-active chart; there is no extra invisible predecessor in this chronology.

For either actual reverse star or arithmetic phase, put \(c=x-\gamma u\) and \(d=x-\gamma(2-u)\) or \(x-2\gamma u\). The six-site mask has pegs \(\gamma s,\gamma y,\gamma z,x,c,d\). Write it as \(P_6\) and define \(m_j=I(X^jP_6)\). The only missing clock is the singleton \(c_7\), so \(m_0\ne0\), \(m_1=c_7m_0\). To avoid dividing by this pivot in the subsequent check, take the scaled total node polynomial \(\widetilde D=P_6(m_0X-m_1)\). The two actual reciprocal tail relations become
\[
 F_9=k W_{\widetilde D}(c)-W_{\widetilde D}(x)=0,\qquad
 F_8=k_d W_{\widetilde D}(d)-W_{\widetilde D}(x)=0 .
\]
Use the literal cleared arithmetic identity \(H k_d=(1+4u-4u^2)H+4u^2(3u-2)\), \(H=-2+u+4u^2-u^3\); star has \(k_d=k\).

The true degree of \(F_9\) is at most four: when computing \(W_{\widetilde D}(c)\) by deleting the marked factor \(X-c\), the first two resulting masked moments cancel the \(c\)-dependence from \(m_0 W_1-m_1 W_0\), so \(W_{\widetilde D}(c)\) has degree at most two in \(u\), whereas \(W_{\widetilde D}(x)\) has degree at most four. The artificial factor \(u\) is exact, since \(u=0\) makes \(c=x,k=1\), and is not admissible by literal penultimate chronology. The resulting cubic retains its leading degree in each fixed monic \((3,3,4,2)\) front fiber. The multiplication determinant of the cleared \(F_8\) there is a unit at \(1129,t=203\) in every front/rear phase. This excludes seven distinct active clocks without any modular-only saturation.

## Lower-active gates

With six active and physical rows four and five active, the common front argument makes all six active clocks distinct and ordinary. There are exactly three silent zero rows, at \(a,b\) and some clock \(\zeta\). Since \(B\boldsymbol h=0\), the paid \(\boldsymbol w\boldsymbol h\) moments through degree two show \(w_2=w_3=0\) except for possible coincidence with \(\zeta\); this uses the individual quadratic clock masks and does not discard either entry at a repeated silent clock. Thus the scalar aggregate support of \(\boldsymbol w\) is among the six active sites and possibly one extra site at \(\zeta\). If \(\zeta\) is an active clock or its aggregate is zero, then all aggregates vanish by the six moments. Nonzero \(w_{10}\) forces \(\zeta=x\), and \(w_9=0\) gives \(k=0\). If stage eight is active, the same five-peg rank-one minors above exclude it. If stage eight is the third silent zero, physical row six is active and the guarded third-active quadratic applies. Then the five pegs \(c_4,c_5,c_6,x,c_9\) leave one missing clock, and the corresponding rank-one minor is again a unit in both front phases and both penultimate orientations at \(1103,t=161\). The only surviving six-active possibilities with rows four and five active therefore have a separate \(\zeta\) with nonzero silent aggregate. If row four or five is silent, no six-active conclusion is asserted.

With five active, making both rows four and five active would give at least six ordinary sites by the common front, impossible. With four active, all four sites are distinct Gauss and all active grade-four pure columns are zero. If row four is active, the Gauss \(H_4\) test excludes it. If physical row five is the earliest active, its three strict scalar sites are \(a,b,c_4\); the exact Gauss earliest-active \(A\boldsymbol h\) test in [equal_four.py](../sdirk-eight-silent-two-equal/equal_four.py) applies because the sole invisible input has \(h=0\) and its active strict feed is zero. It excludes row five. The literal reverse triple cannot all be active and Gauss, so stage eight is silent; the terminal pair remains active. Hence the other two active stages must be physical six and seven. This is a physical census, not a closure of the four-active branch.
