# Census: seven active physical stages with one invisible quadratic defect

At the ten-stage strict SDIRK8 floor over a characteristic-zero field, suppose the only physical nonzero quadratic residual occurs at stage one and \(b_1=0\). **There cannot be exactly seven active physical stages.** Here physical activity means \(b_i\ne0\), regardless of a repeated clock or cancellation of the total weight at that clock. This is not an exclusion of eight or nine active physical stages, and does not change the unrestricted **10–11** stage interval. The proof is the following source-level case census, with the exact arithmetic and scalar Lean receipts in the linked packets.

## Number of distinct active clocks

Write \(\boldsymbol h=(A-\gamma I)\boldsymbol g\), \(B=\operatorname{diag}(\boldsymbol b)\). If \(B\boldsymbol h=0\), the [undetected seven-active proof](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-row/README.md) excludes both the repeated and distinct clock cases. Otherwise the [small-multiplicity census](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-small/README.md) excludes at most five distinct active clocks, including clock groups of canceled ordinary mass. For six distinct active clocks, the [independent](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-six-last/README.md) and [dependent](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-six-dependent/README.md) active \(\boldsymbol h,C\boldsymbol h\) proofs give the two exhaustive cases. Only seven distinct active clocks remain to be considered below. In this last count every active stage contributes its own nonzero ordinary scalar weight, even if its cubic defect is zero.

## Reverse factor and active profile at seven distinct clocks

Use the [seven-distinct anchors and active profile classification](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-anchors/README.md). The reverse factor \(k=1+2u-u^2\) vanishes in three literal-front activity counts; they are ruled out by the [both-active anchors](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-anchors/README.md), [one-active](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-one-front-zero/README.md), and [neither-active](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-no-front-zero/README.md) proofs. These keep the actual weighted balance at the terminal clock, including multiple physical silents there.

For \(k\ne0\), the affine profile \(L_4=Qh\) is classified by \(Q\):
* **Constant.** The [both-active anchors](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-anchors/README.md) and [literal reverse-root](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-literal-reverse/README.md) cases are supplemented by the [nonliteral constant closure](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-constant-nonliteral/README.md).
* **Linear with a Gauss root.** The active root is a genuine hole \(h=0\), not a canceled ordinary weight. The [both-active hole](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-hole/README.md), [same-root](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-gauss-root/README.md), [different-root literal](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-gauss-distinct-literal/README.md), and [remaining different-root nonliteral](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-gauss-nonliteral/README.md) closures cover all positions.
* **Linear and coprime to \(L_4\).** Both literal fronts active is [already excluded](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-coprime/README.md), with or without a separate nonzero reverse aggregate. When at most one literal front is active, the [no-extra-aggregate closure](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-literal-pole/README.md) handles a common pole with opposite individual silent contributions. The separate nonzero aggregate cases are below.

## Why there is no uncounted multiple reverse contributor

Put \(\boldsymbol w=\boldsymbol b^{\mathsf T}M\) and \(C=\operatorname{diag}(\boldsymbol c)\). There are precisely two further silent physical stages after stage one. The paid \(\boldsymbol w\boldsymbol g=0\) gives \(w_1=0\). At \(k\ne0\), \(\boldsymbol h^{\mathsf T}B=\boldsymbol wP_1(C)\) with a nonzero affine \(P_1\). Thus all further silent stages with nonzero individual \(w_i\) must be at its unique root \(z\) (a constant \(P_1\ne0\) permits none). This locates the reverse contributors, not just their aggregate. In the coprime chart every active \(h_i\ne0\), so \(z\) is outside all active clocks.

* With **both literal fronts active**, the preceding both-active proof already allows a weighted mixture at \(z\). The two possible silent contributors here need not be literal.
* With **neither literal front active**, those two literal stages exhaust the further silent indices and have different clocks. They cannot both contribute at \(z\). Their only remaining separate-aggregate cases are the [star](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-coprime-no-front-star/README.md) and [arithmetic](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-coprime-no-front-closed/README.md) rear closures.
* With **one literal front active**, there is one silent literal and one other silent physical row. If both contribute, their common \(z\) is therefore the silent literal clock. Physical fourth cannot repeat that earlier literal clock by the exact literal chronology; the terminal pair is active. Thus the possible other contributor is covered by the shared-literal [fifth](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-coprime-shared-fifth/README.md), [sixth/seventh star](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-coprime-shared-star/README.md), [sixth/seventh arithmetic](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-coprime-shared-arithmetic/README.md), or [eighth](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-coprime-shared-eighth/README.md) proof. These shared-clock arguments use only a nonzero combined reverse aggregate, not equal individual amplitudes or two individually nonzero reverse weights. In particular they also cover a sole contributing nonliteral row whose clock happens to equal the silent literal clock. If just the literal contributes, use the [sole literal](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-coprime-literal-sole/README.md) proof. If just the distinct nonliteral stage contributes, use the [fourth](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-coprime-nonliteral-fourth-closed/README.md), [fifth star](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-coprime-nonliteral-fifth-star/README.md) or [fifth arithmetic](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-coprime-nonliteral-fifth-arithmetic/README.md), [sixth/seventh](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-coprime-nonliteral-six-seven/README.md), or [silent eighth](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-coprime-nonliteral-eighth/README.md) proof.

All branches of **exactly seven physically active** stages with the single invisible first defect are thus excluded. Detected higher-active counts and the remaining [undetected five-site chart](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-five-cuts/README.md) are separate.
