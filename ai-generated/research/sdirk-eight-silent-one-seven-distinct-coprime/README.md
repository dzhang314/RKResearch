# Closing the coprime affine profile with both literal fronts active

Use the [seven-distinct active profile](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-anchors/README.md) at the ten-stage strict SDIRK8 floor over a characteristic-zero field. Exactly the weight-zero first stage has nonzero quadratic residual, there are seven distinct active clocks, both literal front rows are active, and the reverse factor \(k\ne0\). Here the affine \(Q\) is nonconstant and coprime to \(L_4\). This packet excludes that last both-front chart, with both physical eighth placements and either count of reverse aggregate sites. Together with the earlier constant and [Gauss-hole](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-hole/README.md) packets, every seven-distinct case with both fronts active is closed. Fewer active literal fronts remain separate, and the unrestricted interval is still **10–11**.

Write \(x=c_{10}=1-\gamma,\ c=c_9=x-\gamma u,\ k=1+2u-u^2\). For an active eighth its actual clock is \(d=x-\gamma(2-u)\) in star or \(d=x-2\gamma u\) in arithmetic. Write \(H=-2+u+4u^2-u^3,\ T=4u^3-12u^2+3u+2\) for the two literal cleared arithmetic factors. With \(N=A-\gamma I,\ M=CA-AC-A^2,\ \boldsymbol w=\boldsymbol b^{\mathsf T}M\), the actual reverse row is \(\boldsymbol h^{\mathsf T}B=\boldsymbol wP_1(C)\) for a nonzero affine \(P_1\). Every active \(h,w\) is nonzero. There is at most one distinct inactive clock carrying nonzero individual \(w\)'s, at a root of \(P_1\); its aggregate may be zero.

Normalize clocks and cubic defects by \(\gamma\) and \(\gamma^3\). Write \(A_0,B_0,H_a,H_b\) for the two literal front clocks and defects. They fix \(Q(Y)=q_0+q_1Y\) by \(Q(A_0)=L_4(\gamma A_0)/H_a,\ Q(B_0)=L_4(\gamma B_0)/H_b\). Denote its physical version by \(q(X)=\gamma q_0+q_1 X\), so the profiles \(L_4/q\) and \(L_4(\gamma\,\cdot)/Q\) differ by a nonzero common scale. All active clocks have \(q,L_4\ne0\), by coprimality and the nodal profile classification. The literal two front ratios fix an affine \(\ell_v\) for each pure-four read, giving \(v_i=\ell_v(c_i/\gamma) h_i\) on the active set. The literal first-four rank test forces physical row four silent, as in the [six-clock anchor](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-six-anchored/README.md).

## No separate reverse aggregate: two opposite physical silents

On the seven active sites \(D'(c_i)w_i\) is a nonzero constant by the six scalar reverse moments, where \(D\) is the active nodal polynomial. The active first defect numerator \(D'(c_i)b_i h_i\) is a nonzero multiple of \(Q(c_i/\gamma)\). Hence \(P_1\) is a nonzero multiple of \(Q\); their common root is the inactive pole \(z=-\gamma q_0/q_1\).

There must be a nonzero **defect-weighted** silent aggregate there, even though its scalar \(w\)-aggregate is zero. Otherwise all three paid \(\boldsymbol w C^j\boldsymbol h\) moments, \(0\le j\le2\), would reduce to the seven active sites. With the nonzero constant \(w\)-numerator, this would interpolate \(h\) by a cubic \(S\), forcing \(QS=L_4(\gamma\,\cdot)\) by the seven distinct nodes, contrary to coprimality. Thus both physical silents are at \(z\), with opposite nonzero \(w\)'s and distinct actual \(h\)'s. For \(v=Ah,\tau_4\), the paid pairings with \(v,h,Ch\) give the actual **difference** \(v_4-v_j=\ell_v(z/\gamma)(h_4-h_j)\), not identical stage amplitudes or positive averages.

If the other silent is physical five, the literal row-five pure-four expressions at this common clock are affine in its actual defect. The padded earlier row four is the expression at its own actual \(H_4\). The two difference slopes minus the respective \(\ell_v\)'s are coprime affine polynomials outside the forbidden literal front clocks. A different \(h_5\) is therefore impossible (an equal one would already contradict the defect-weighted balance). This tests actual affine slopes, not a pole value of a rational profile.

Otherwise row five is active ordinary. Row four has the known normalized pole \(Z=-q_0/q_1\) but its own literal \(H_4(Z)\), not a rational evaluation at that pole. The three strict moments for fifth \(y\) are affine in actual \(H_5=L_4(\gamma y)/Q(y)\). Its \(Ah,\tau_4\) reads are also affine in the prior clock \(Z\), by the literal measurement of \(H_4\) and the three-clock cubic relation. Multiply by \(q_1 Q(y)\) to clear precisely those two known nonzero denominators. The two resulting literal conditions have the exact padded factors \(y-A_0,y-B_0\), forbidden because both those earlier stages are active. Their resultant in \(y\) has, after the true nodal and coprime guards, monic diagonal degree 20 or 24 for the two front phases.

Here \(w_i/b_i\) is proportional to \(L_4(c_i)/q(c_i)^2\) on the active sites. Comparing its actual penultimate and terminal values gives the cubic gap polynomial after its exact zero-gap factor, which is inadmissible by chronology. The leading coefficient is a unit on the precise diagonal projection. Active eight adds its actual cleared ratio and the signed five-peg moment gate. With silent eight the actual silent clock equation at \(z\) gives \(q(x)+\gamma q_1 k(u-2)/2=0\). If this alone is not used, the signed moments on the four known active pegs recover a missing cubic and test the actual fifth-row polynomials on it. [Replay](no_extra.py).

## Separate nonzero aggregate: honest virtual transport

Now there is one extra aggregate site \(z\) with nonzero total \(w\), separate from every active site. On \(D(X)(X-z)\) the numerator for \(w\) has degree at most one by the six paid scalar moments. Its seven active values make it a nonzero multiple of \(q\), since the extra nodal factor cancels the root factor of \(P_1\). Its value at \(z\) is nonzero, so \(q(z)\ne0\). For any actual pure-four \(v\), the two paid reverse moments of \(w_i v_i\) give a degree-at-most-five numerator on this same eight-site set. On all seven active sites it equals a common multiple of \(\ell_v(X/\gamma)L_4(X)\), hence that polynomial everywhere. At \(z\) the **weighted virtual** \(v\) and \(h\) therefore have the rational active profile. When only one stage carries the nonzero reverse balance there, its actual values equal the virtual ones; we never use that inference for two occupants.

Physical row four cannot be the sole transported stage, by the literal first-four rank. If physical row five is silent, it is at \(z\), either singly transported or sharing with physical four. In the shared case its actual two pure-four expressions are affine in \(h_5\) at that fixed clock; the earlier row four is the padded read in the same formulas. Therefore the weighted mixture satisfies the same formulas at its virtual \(h\). If row five is instead active, it has the rational active profile in the usual sense.

Eliminate the unknown prior fourth clock \(s\) from these two literal fifth-stage reads. In the cleared scalar strict moments their dependence on \(s\) has coefficients \(J,e_1J\) respectively, where \(e_1\) is the slope of \((H_4(Y)-\operatorname{affine}(H_a,H_b)(Y))/((Y-A_0)(Y-B_0))\). Subtract the required \(Ah,\tau_4\) affine profile and eliminate \(s\) by cross subtraction. Substitute the actual or virtual \(H_5=L_4(\gamma y)/Q(y)\), clearing the nonzero \(Q(y)\). The resulting \(E_5(\gamma,y)\) has the exact padded factors \(y-A_0,y-B_0\). These are impossible for an active later site or the separate inactive \(z\). The resulting condition applies to the actual ordinary fifth or the weighted virtual one; no \(h_4\) is assigned by transport in the shared chart.
