The [same-root packet](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-gauss-root/README.md) is complementary.

The reverse scalar law requires at least seven nonzero aggregate sites; there are six ordinary active sites, so there is a separate nonzero reverse aggregate at \(z\) outside **all** the active sites. ## Physical front alternatives

1. **One active literal, second physical silent outside \(z\).** The actual silent literal at \(z\) is sole contributor. Its actual defect and pure-four ratios therefore transport, with the active literal fixing the other pure-four anchor. The literal first-four rank test forces fourth silent, at a different clock (it cannot repeat a preceding literal clock), so eighth is active. Equality of the actual two normalized literal \(h/p_0\) ratios supplies a cubic diagonal polynomial. Its exact gcd with the corresponding 6- or 24-degree tail polynomial is one.
2. **Neither literal active.** The two literal clocks are distinct, so the actual literal at \(z\) is sole transported. Rows four through ten are active, with eighth active. Its known nonzero defect supplies the profile scale and pure-four anchor.
3. **One active literal, both physical silents share \(z\).** No individual silent virtual amplitude or pure-four value is assigned. Fourth cannot repeat the earlier literal silent clock, so fourth is active. Use the actual active literal as sole earlier profile anchor. Eighth may be active or may itself be the second physically silent stage.

In cases 2 and 3 fourth is ordinary: an active hole there would force both its actual \(H_4\) and \(Ah\) to vanish, forbidden by their exact literal gcd. With \(a\) the active or sole-transported literal anchor and \(h_a\) its known actual defect, take \(Q=p_0(\gamma a)\), \(P(Y)=h_a p_0(\gamma Y)\). The normalized fourth equation \((QH_4-P)/(Y-a)=0\) gives its entire quadratic after removing the earlier padded clock. If fifth is active ordinary, use the two [cleared fifth reads](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-gauss-root/README.md) at \(h=P/Q\). If fifth is the actual active hole, put its actual \(h=0\) in the unpadded strict reads and check their values at \(r/\gamma\) jointly. Both alternatives are tested.

If fifth is instead the second physically silent stage in case 3, it shares \(z\) with the earlier literal silent. At this fixed clock the actual literal fifth pure reads are affine in the actual defect. The earlier literal has the padded values there at its own actual defect, by uniqueness of the three strict scalar moments at the distinct preceding clocks.
