# The nonliteral distinct reverse root of a Gauss profile

This packet completes the Gauss-hole case of the [detected seven-distinct active chart](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-anchors/README.md) at the ten-stage strict SDIRK8 floor. Work over a characteristic-zero field. The only nonzero quadratic residual occurs at the individually weight-zero physical first stage, and \(k\ne0\). A shared Gauss root \(r\) of \(Q,L_4\) gives one active hole with actual \(h_r=0\) and six ordinary active sites with \(h=\lambda p\) for \(p=L_4/(X-r)\), \(\lambda\ne0\). The affine reverse root \(z\) is **different from \(r\)** and is not either literal front clock. There is exactly one active literal front, since both-front-active is already excluded, and if both were silent there would be no nonliteral physically silent stage to carry the separate reverse balance.

Complementary cases: [zero reverse factor](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-one-front-zero/README.md) and its [two-silent-literal companion](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-no-front-zero/README.md); [same Gauss/reverse root](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-gauss-root/README.md), including the [literal placement](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-literal-reverse/README.md); and [different root at a literal silent](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-gauss-distinct-literal/README.md). Constant and coprime profiles and other support charts are separate. This is not an unconditional stage-bound improvement.

## Honest sole transport

Write \(\boldsymbol h=N\boldsymbol g,\ \boldsymbol w=\boldsymbol b^{\mathsf T}M\), where \(N=A-\gamma I,\ M=CA-AC-A^2\). Under the actual equation \(\boldsymbol h^{\mathsf T}B=\boldsymbol wP_1(C)\), the active hole contributes zero reverse weight. The six ordinary active sites and the sole nonliteral silent at \(z\) supply the seven nonzero aggregate reverse sites. The literal silent outside \(z\) has zero reverse weight. The [different-root profile argument](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-gauss-distinct-literal/README.md) therefore transports the **actual** \(\boldsymbol h\) and pure-four reads of the sole silent at \(z\): they equal the cubic active profile and its affine slopes. Do not transport the other literal silent, or assume \(p(z)\ne0\).

Let \(a\) be the active literal normalized clock and \(h_a\ne0\) its known normalized defect. For every ordinary active stage and the sole silent at \(z\), set \(Q=p(\gamma a)\), \(P(Y)=h_a p(\gamma Y)\), so \(Qh(Y)=P(Y)\). Physical fourth, whether ordinary or sole transported, therefore lies on this profile. It cannot instead be the active hole: \(H_4=0\) and actual \(Ah=0\) are inconsistent by their exact literal gcd. The same gcd forbids \(H_4=0\) at an ordinary/transported fourth. Its exact equation is
\[
 \Phi(\gamma,s)=(QH_4(s)-P(s))/(s-a)=0.
\]
Physical fourth cannot repeat the active earlier literal clock. The two literal fifth pure equations may both be padded and divided by \(y-a,y-s\) if fifth is ordinary active or the sole transported silent. In either case \(a,s,y\) are distinct: at most one is at the separate silent clock \(z\), and the active clocks are distinct. The nominal quotient after \(y-a\) has remainder exactly \(V_0(s)\Phi(\gamma,s)\) on division by \(y-s\), where \(V_0\) is the defect-free literal strict read. No resultants presume this remainder vanishes before imposing \(\Phi\). If fifth is the actual active Gauss hole, use \(h=0\) and the two original strict reads \(T_0=A_0=0\) at \(r/\gamma\).

## Tail cuts

Put \(x=1-\gamma,\ c=x-\gamma u,\ k=1+2u-u^2\). Here \(x,c\) are always ordinary since their actual \(w\)'s are nonzero. When eighth is active star, \(d=x-\gamma(2-u)\) is also ordinary. The actual ratios at ordinary sites are \(w_i/b_i\propto p(c_i)/(c_i-z)\). Cross eliminating \(z\) gives
\[
 (2-u)p(c)-u p(d)+k(2u-2)p(x)=0.
\]
For a cubic this factors exactly as
\[
 u(u-1)(u-2)\bigl[-2p(x)-\gamma^2p''(x)+4p_3\gamma^3\bigr].
\]
The three gap factors are forbidden collisions; \(p_3=70\). The residual pins the diagonal by an exact cubic independent of \(u,z\). Its full finite fibers give unit fourth leading coefficients, and the ordinary-fifth resultant and the two possible Gauss fifth reads exclude all four orientations.

If active arithmetic eighth \(d=x-2\gamma u\) is the Gauss hole, its \(w_8=0\) and the actual \(H k_d=-kT\) require \(T(u)=4u^3-12u^2+3u+2=0\). Its clock gives \(\gamma=(1-r)/(1+2u)\); \(1+2u=0\) would be \(r=1\), not a Gauss root. In this exact cubic gap quotient, the two star-front orientations have a fourth-clock factor in common with \(H_4=0\). Remove that factor **over the exact algebra** (degree two becomes degree one), never by discarding a reduced point. The arithmetic-front quadratics retain both roots. The ordinary-fifth resultants are units on all genuine retained components. Fifth cannot be another active hole at \(r\).

Otherwise active arithmetic eighth is ordinary. Since \(d=2c-x\), the actual ninth and eighth comparisons jointly give \(H p(d)+2T p(c)-kT p(x)=0\). Silent eighth has nonzero individual reverse balance at \(z\) and \(2(x-z)+\gamma k(u-2)=0\); substitution into ninth gives \((u-2)p(c)-(2u+k(u-2))p(x)=0\). In each case remove exact powers of the forbidden zero \(u\). The resulting gap polynomial is cubic. These two modes use the independent front-only diagonal projection below.

## Literal front-only diagonal projection and exact guards

The ordinary or transported fifth has two degree-at-most-two pure equations after the exact padding above. Their resultant is a polynomial in \(\gamma,s\). Remove each coefficient content only by recording it as an included diagonal factor; use a pseudo-remainder by the fourth quadratic and a second resultant to remove \(s\), including its possible leading-zero diagonal factor. The exact guarded diagonal polynomial has degree 114 before repeated factors/leading splits. If fifth is the active hole, evaluate its two unpadded pure equations at \(r/\gamma\), clearing \(\gamma\)-powers. Their affine-in-\(s\) resultant gives degree 7. Their exact diagonal gcd is one.

The diagonal guards removed here are only \(\gamma,\ p(\gamma a),\ p(x),\ x-\gamma a\). All hold by the occupied active clocks. Intersect the projected diagonals with the exact \(H_4(s)=p(\gamma s)=0\) possibility; it is forbidden at actual fourth. In that small exact intersection (degree nine for ordinary fifth), divide only common exact \(H_4\) factors from \(\Phi\) in the monic diagonal quotient, splitting if an exact coefficient pivot is a zero divisor. The complementary fourth components retain all true degree-two and lowered degree-one branches. The proof also splits the gap by its actual exact leading degree, not a reduced degree guess.

## Masked signed and actual chronology gates

Write
\[
 d_9=p(c)-k p(x),\qquad n_9=xp(c)-kcp(x)
\]
so that \(d_9z=n_9\); \(d_9\ne0\) for an actual tableau, since \(d_9=0\) would leave \(k p(x)(x-c)\ne0\). The implementation uses homogeneous tests, not an inverse of \(d_9\) in the reduced ring. The six ordinary active nodes have the signed degree-seven moment functional \(I-\nu\delta_r\), \(\nu=I(p)/p(r)\) being the actual weight at the Gauss hole. A mask on known ordinary pegs leaves exactly the remaining ordinary sites. For four known pegs the four masked moments give the cleared two-node polynomial by the Hankel/Cramer rule. For five known pegs the one remaining ordinary site implies the signed rank-one identity \(m_0m_2-m_1^2=0\). No positivity or pivot inversion is imposed in these masks.

* **Arithmetic, silent fourth**: check its actual clock \(\gamma d_9s=n_9\), and either the active Gauss fifth's two pure reads, or the ordinary fifth in the signed two-missing-site quadratic on the actual pegs \(\gamma a,x,c,d\).
* **Arithmetic, silent fifth**: apply the exact two (divided) fifth equations to \(n_9/(\gamma d_9)\) by homogeneous evaluation. This fifth is transported by the sole reverse balance, not mistaken for an active node.
* **Arithmetic, silent sixth or seventh**: fourth and fifth are active. The five ordinary pegs are \(\gamma a,x,c,d,\gamma s\); test the signed rank-one condition together with the appropriate fifth pure gate.
* **Silent eighth**: fourth and fifth are active. For ordinary fifth, use the cleared two-node polynomial on pegs \(\gamma a,x,c,\gamma s\) and its two padded fifth resultants. For a Gauss fifth, check instead its two actual zero-defect reads.

In every case the relevant residuals generate the unit ideal on the entire retained finite algebra after reduction.
