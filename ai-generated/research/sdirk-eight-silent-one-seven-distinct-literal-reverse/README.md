Write \(\boldsymbol h=N\boldsymbol g,\ \boldsymbol w=\boldsymbol b^{\mathsf T}M\) and \(\boldsymbol h^{\mathsf T}B=\boldsymbol wP_1(C)\), with \(N=A-\gamma I\), \(M=CA-AC-A^2\). ## Constant \(Q\): separate aggregate at a literal silent front

First, constant \(Q\) cannot have no separate aggregate. In that event the seven active numerators \(D'(c_i)b_i h_i\) and \(D'(c_i)w_i\) are nonzero constants, so \(P_1\) would be a nonzero constant and all silent \(w\)'s would be zero. The three paid \(\boldsymbol wC^j\boldsymbol h\) moments would interpolate the active \(h\)'s by a cubic, contrary to \(h\) proportional to the genuine quartic \(L_4\) on seven distinct sites.

Now suppose the separate reverse aggregate is at \(z=\gamma a\), where \(a\) is the normalized clock of an actual silent literal front. The extra site has nonzero aggregate \(w\) and is not active. Here \(w_i/b_i\) at active sites is proportional to \(L_4(c_i)/(c_i-z)\). The exact ninth equation, removing only the zero-gap padding, is
\[
 [L_4(c)(x-z)-kL_4(x)(c-z)]/u=0.
\]
The star and arithmetic eighth equations use the identical literal tail comparisons as the [coprime no-extra proof](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-literal-pole/README.md) but with one power of \(c_i-z\), not two. Their exact guarded diagonal resultant has degree 16 or 35 before squarefree decomposition. The removed factors are only actual \(\gamma,\ x-z,\ L_4(x)\), plus \(L_4(z)\) when there is **actual sole** literal transport. The tail polynomial cancellations are the forbidden coincident-tail gaps.

If both literal fronts are silent, there is exactly one silent at \(z\) because the two literal clocks differ. Rows four through ten are all active; their normalized pure-four profile is anchored to the **actual** literal silent at \(z\). This is legitimate because the sole actual reverse balance there equals the weighted virtual profile. Its literal defect is nonzero, so \(L_4(z)\ne0\).

If exactly one literal front is active and the other physical silent has a **different** clock from \(z\), the literal silent is the sole nonzero contributor there. The active and transported literal ratios fix both pure-four affine slopes. The literal first-four rank condition forces physical fourth silent, at a different clock by literal three-tap uniqueness. Thus physical eight is active. The equality of the two actual normalized \(h/L_4\) ratios supplies a quartic diagonal equation whose exact gcd with the guarded tail resultant is one.

For both literal fronts silent, actual fourth \(s\) satisfies the cubic \(\Phi=(L_4(z)H_4(Y)-h_aL_4(\gamma Y))/(Y-a)\). Physical fourth cannot repeat the earlier literal anchored clock. Its leading coefficient is a unit in every reduced tail component. The four known active pegs \(x,c,d,\gamma s\) leave exactly three active ordinary nodes. The scalar moments through degree nine are known from \(h^2\) and \(Ch^2\) and the degree-seven quadrature condition. Using their four-peg nodal mask gives the signed cleared missing cubic by Cramer. With the actual transported literal and actual fourth as slope anchors, the two literal fifth polynomials are the [denominator-cleared pure reads](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-distinct-literal-pole/README.md), now with constant \(Q\). Their joint padded resultant test against the missing cubic gives the unit ideal in every reduced component. No stage weight, difference of nodes, or moment determinant is inverted in these reads.

Finally, exactly one active literal may have both physically silent stages at \(z\). Literal fourth cannot repeat the silent literal clock, so it is active. The active literal alone anchors \(h/L_4\); assign **no** virtual value to either silent, whether one or both contributes a nonzero \(w\). Do not remove \(L_4(z)=0\). For active eight the known four ordinary pegs (active literal, terminal, ninth and eighth) give the cleared missing cubic, and its padded resultant with the actual fourth cubic is a unit. For silent eight its nonzero individual \(w_8\) gives \(2(x-z)+\gamma k(u-2)=0\); the guarded diagonal polynomial has degree 14. First retain the entire actual fourth cubic, then the constant-profile masked moments on four known ordinary pegs (active literal, terminal, ninth and actual fourth) give the missing cubic containing actual fifth. Its two literal pure-read resultant conditions generate the unit ideal, with precisely the same clearing as above. This covers every physical sharing of the literal reverse root.

## Gauss hole and reverse root both at the silent literal clock

Let \(r\) be the shared root of \(Q,L_4\), and suppose \(P_1\) also has this root, at the clock of either actual silent literal front. Set \(p_0(X)=L_4(X)/(X-r)\). On the active sites *other than the Gauss hole* the ratios \(w_i/b_i\) are proportional to \(p_0(c_i)/(c_i-r)\). At the hole no such ratio is substituted. Since \(r=\gamma a\) for a known nonzero normalized literal clock, \(\gamma=r/a\) is fixed in the exact quotient of \(\mathbb Q(t)[r]\) by the Gauss quartic. The terminal is not the hole: \(x-r\) is an exact unit in that quotient.

If both active \(c,d\) are ordinary, their two actual tail comparisons with terminal have exact gcd one as polynomials in \(u\). If \(c=r\) or \(d=r\), substitute its fixed gap \(u=(x-r)/\gamma,\ 2-(x-r)/\gamma,\) or \((x-r)/(2\gamma)\), as appropriate, into the other comparison. Each remaining polynomial value is an exact unit. They cannot both be the hole because the active clocks are distinct. If physical eight is silent its nonzero individual \(w_8\) forces its clock to be \(r\), with \(2(x-r)+\gamma k(u-2)=0\); gcd with the ordinary ninth comparison is one. If the ninth is the hole, direct evaluation at its fixed gap instead gives an exact unit. No silent defect is transported in this Gauss-hole argument, even if the hole is shared by both actual silents.
