# Anchored front cuts for the detected six-clock Gauss double

At the ten-stage strict SDIRK8 floor over a characteristic-zero field, suppose only stage one has nonzero quadratic residual and its weight is zero. In this note there are seven active physical stages at six distinct active clocks, with the active values of \(\boldsymbol h=(A-\gamma I)\boldsymbol g\) and \(C\boldsymbol h\) independent. We exclude:

1. Both literal front rows \(2,3\) active, for every physical eighth placement and zero or nonzero reverse quadratic factor.
2. Exactly one literal front row active, reverse quadratic factor nonzero, and the other literal front row the **sole** silent stage at the separate root of the affine reverse multiplier.
3. Both literal front rows silent, reverse quadratic factor zero.

This is a partial six-clock result, not an exclusion of the dependent \(h,Ch\) branch, the other physical silent placements, or seven distinct active sites. The unrestricted bound is still **10–11**.

## The actual Gauss double and silent transport

On seven active rows the pure-four rank is two and the active values of \(h,Ch\) span it. Thus \(L_4(c_i)=Q(c_i)h_i\) at actual active rows for an affine \(Q\). Let \(D_A\) be the monic polynomial of the six distinct active clocks. Write \(R_b,S_b\) for the numerators \(D_A'(c)\sum_{c_i=c}b_i h_i,\ D_A'(c)\sum_{c_i=c}b_i h_i^2\). The five and two paid scalar moments give \(\deg R_b\le0,\deg S_b\le3\). \(R_b\ne0\): otherwise every detected clock would need a physical repeat, but there is only one double and two distinct detected clocks. The pointwise relation \(Q S_b=L_4R_b\) is a polynomial identity, since both sides have degree at most four. Therefore \(Q\) is a nonconstant affine polynomial whose root \(r\) is Gauss, and \(S_b/R_b=P=L_4/Q\) is a nonzero cubic. At every singleton \(h_i=P(c_i)\) by the two moments and nonzero first mass. If the double had equal amplitudes, then it too would equal \(P\); the paid ordinary scalar probes and nonsingular cubic Hilbert Gram would kill \(P\). Thus the double is at \(r\), with different actual physical amplitudes. The value \(P(r)\) is **not** substituted for either active amplitude at \(r\).

Let \(x=c_{10}=1-\gamma\), \(u=(x-c_9)/\gamma\), \(k=1+2u-u^2\), and \(\boldsymbol w=\boldsymbol b^{\mathsf T}M\). For nonzero \(k\), \(\boldsymbol h^{\mathsf T}B=\boldsymbol wP_1(C)\) with \(P_1\) affine, from the actual \(\boldsymbol w,\boldsymbol w(A-\gamma I)\) basis. At every active site the signed first defect mass is nonzero. Therefore \(P_1\) is nonzero there and the aggregate of \(w\) is nonzero there. The six scalar zero moments of \(\boldsymbol w\) force a separate inactive site \(z\) with nonzero aggregate; all silent \(w_i\) are supported at this root of \(P_1\) (and the invisible \(w_1=0\)). This root may have either one or two actual silent occupants. On the seven distinct sites the \(w\) numerator is constant. For any pure-four column \(v\), the numerator of \(w_i v_i\) has degree at most four from its two zero moments. After dividing by the constant numerator of \(w\), its polynomial values at the five singleton active clocks agree with an affine multiple of \(P\), namely that actual \(v\)'s active formula from \(h,Ch\). They therefore agree everywhere. At \(z\) the **\(w\)-weighted aggregate** of every pure-four value gives precisely this common affine \(h\) profile at \(h=P(z)\). This does not assign equal amplitudes to two physical silents at \(z\).

At zero \(k\), the actual \(\boldsymbol w\) is supported at \(x\). When \(x\ne r\) its terminal coordinate is singleton active. Thus its nonzero \(w_{10}\) is balanced by at least one silent physical occupant at \(x\); the \(w\)-weighted silent values of \(h\) and every pure-four column there agree with the actual terminal values. The same virtual-read conclusion holds at \(x\).

## Two pinned literal fronts

When both front rows are active, their actual nonzero \(h\)'s fix \(Q\). The same is true if one literal front is the **sole** silent at the nonzero-\(k\) root \(z\), because its actual values then equal the transported profile. The two literal clocks \(a,b\) cannot coincide. For each front phase the prior [shared-root diagonal projection](../sdirk-eight-silent-two-high/shared_proxy.py) gives the exact factors of degrees \(4,4,12\) over \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt2)\). The exact field reads keep \(x\ne r\) and \(P(x)\ne0\). Within these fields the literal front pure-four reads determine affine functions \(Q_T,Q_A\) for the actual \(\tau_4\) and \(Ah\) on the active profile. The same reads describe any virtual silent at \(z\) (or \(x\) at zero \(k\)). Physical row four cannot be active and on that profile: the actual first-four rank test would force a literal duplicate at \(a\) or \(b\). Thus row four is silent. If it were the sole silent at the reverse root or the sole silent balance at \(x\), its actual profile would also imply that duplicate. If the two silents share the same clock and are physically rows four and five, their virtual profile is retained.

In normalized time use capital \(X=x/\gamma\), \(Z=z/\gamma\) where applicable, \(A=a/\gamma,B=b/\gamma\), and for finite arithmetic scale the defect polynomial by \(\gamma^{-3}\). The literal row-five test works without recovery of the exceptional fourth clock. Write its normalized clock \(y\), normalized \(h\)-value \(H\), and normalized exceptional fourth clock \(z_4\). The three strict aggregates at \(A,B,z_4\) have moments
\[
 m_0=y-1-2H,\quad m_1=y^2/2-y-2H,\quad m_2=y^3/3-y^2-4H/3.
\]
Define \(J=m_2-(A+B)m_1+ABm_0\). The \(\tau_4\) read minus its required active value has form \(T_0+z_4 J\), by the cubic nodal relation. Let \(L_h\) be the affine interpolant of the literal normalized \(h\) at \(A,B\). The quadratic interpolant of \(h\) at the actual three prior clocks is
\[
 H_2(X)=L_h(X)+(\eta_0+\eta_1 z_4)(X-A)(X-B),
 \qquad
 \eta_0+\eta_1 z=\frac{H_4(z)-L_h(z)}{(z-A)(z-B)} .
\]
The division is exact in the symbolic variable \(z\), by the padded earlier \(H_4\) reads. Thus \(Ah\) minus its required value has form \(A_0+\eta_1 z_4 J\). Their elimination \(A_0-\eta_1 T_0=0\) is affine in \(H\), and is a quartic in \(y\) when \(H=P(y)\) in normalized units. If row five is silent and both silent rows share its clock, the very same polynomial applies to its weighted virtual \(h=P(y)\): padded row four at that clock is the row-five affine read at \(H=h_4/\gamma^3\), and averaging the two physical affine reads gives the same formula.

When a subsequent row-six check is needed, a nonzero row-five coefficient \(J\) fixes \(z_4=-T_0/J\). For row six, its first four normalized strict moments \(m'_0,\ldots,m'_3\) are prescribed by its \(h\), the \(\tau_4\) active line, and the known stage-one strict feed. On strict clocks \(A,B,z_4,y\) mark the input \(y\) by \(F_3(X)=(X-A)(X-B)(X-z_4)\). The row-six tap into row five times \(F_3(y)\) equals the marked scalar moment \(\sum_j[F_3]_j m'_j\). The same tap times \(h_5/\gamma^3-H_2(y)\) equals the difference of the prescribed \(Ah\) and its \(H_2\) strict read (including the row-six diagonal separately). Cross multiplying gives the necessary row-six mask. It does not divide by \(F_3(y)\), so possible equal clocks at rows four and five are not assumed away.

### Nonzero reverse factor and physical eighth active

On all singleton tails use the actual ratios
\[
 (X-Z)P(c)=k(c-Z)P(X),\qquad
 (X-Z)P(d)=k_d(d-Z)P(X),
\]
where \(c=X-u\), \(d=X-(2-u)\) in star or \(X-2u\) in arithmetic, in normalized units. The common normalized scale of \(P\) cancels. In star \(k_d=k\); in arithmetic use the literal cleared numerator and denominator, with no division by the reverse third residual. If both ninth and eighth are singleton, elimination of \(Z\) gives an exact constant projection in star and cubic in arithmetic after removing only the exact impossible factors \(u,k\), the coincident active tail clocks or a tail Gauss-double clock, and the zero coefficient of \(Z\) in the ninth row together with zero singleton \(h\)'s. That coefficient is nonzero at a genuine solution: if \(P(c)-kP(X)=0\), the remaining term is \((X-c)P(c)\ne0\). If either eighth or ninth is the double \(r\), use its fixed gap and only the other singleton relation.

With both literal fronts active, count the known physical \(r\)-copies among \(a,b,x,c_9,c_8\). With one copy the known five distinct pegs leave a single missing ordinary singleton, and the signed rank-one Hankel minor is a unit. The first literal physical row five is either one of those singleton roots, or a silent at the separate root (including the virtual same-clock case). ## Zero reverse factor, neither literal front active

Both silent zero rows are then the actual front stages and all stages from physical four onward are active. If \(x=r\), then \(\gamma\) is Gauss, and neither literal front clock is Gauss at a Gauss diagonal. Thus \(\boldsymbol w\) on \(x\) has only the two active occupants; its two entries cancel and \(\boldsymbol w\boldsymbol h=0\) forces their \(h\)'s equal, contradiction.

Otherwise \(x\) is singleton active. Nonzero \(w_{10}\) requires one of the two distinct literal front clocks equal \(x\), fixing \(\gamma=1/(1+A_0)\) in the quadratic front field. Its actual \(h=P(x)\) and all pure-four reads coincide with the terminal ones by the two-coordinate \(\boldsymbol w\) pairing. The exact Gauss quartic is irreducible over that quadratic field. The fixed active tail clocks \(x,c_9,c_8\) and literal clocks are therefore not \(r\). On the ordinary six sites those three pegs plus \(r\) reconstruct the two missing singleton roots. The literal \(H_4-P\) is a unit at either singleton, so row four is \(r\) with its actual \(h=H_4(r/\gamma)\ne0\). This actual pure-four read and the transported front at \(x\) fix the two active \(Ah,\tau_4\) affine profiles. Their actual row-five comparisons at the singleton clock are a unit on the two missing sites. If physical row five is the second \(r\), at least one literal affine derivative differs from the required active slope, forcing the padded earlier row-four value and a literal duplicate.
