# Additional one-front anchors for the detected six-clock Gauss double

Use the [anchored six-clock setup](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-seven-six-anchored/README.md): one invisible stage, seven active physical stages and six distinct active clocks, with independent active \(h,Ch\). There is exactly one active double at a Gauss clock \(r\), its physical amplitudes differ, and singleton defects match the cubic \(P=L_4/Q\). This packet assumes exactly one literal front row active and excludes:

* \(k=0\) and terminal \(x=r\), for either physical eighth placement.
* \(k=0\) and terminal \(x\) equal to the literal silent front clock, for either physical eighth placement.
* \(k\ne0\), the active literal front clock is \(r\), and the reverse-root clock equals the literal silent front (including a second silent sharing it at physical eight).

These are additional scoped exclusions only. The unrestricted **10–11** interval is unchanged. The active one-clock defect branch, other independent silent placements, and larger distinct-clock counts are not included.

## Zero reverse factor, Gauss terminal

At \(k=0\) the actual reverse quadratic row \(\boldsymbol w\) is supported at \(x\), including its nonzero terminal coordinate. If \(x=r\), then \(\gamma=1-r\) is Gauss. Neither literal front clock is Gauss at a Gauss \(\gamma\), as the [fixed split guard](check_one_front.py) checks for all front coefficients. Hence the active literal front is a singleton with known \(h\), which fixes the scale of \(P\). If only the two active \(r\)-rows carried \(w\), their entries would cancel, and \(\boldsymbol w\boldsymbol h=0\) would force equal physical \(h\)'s, impossible. Thus the second silent zero is at \(r\) and its own \(w\) is nonzero. For any pure-four \(v\) the actual two active values at \(r\) are the same affine-clock multiplier times their respective \(h\). Pairing with \(\boldsymbol w\) and subtracting that multiplier times the paid \(\boldsymbol w\boldsymbol h\) shows the **actual silent** stage at \(r\) is on this same pure-four line. It is not assigned the average of the two active \(h\)'s.

If physical eight is active, the fixed reverse \(k=0\) tail clocks \(c_9,c_8\) are both non-Gauss by the same guard applied to the reflected ratios. The four ordinary pegs consisting of the active front, \(x,c_9,c_8\) (with \(x=r\) pegged even if its ordinary mass is zero) reconstruct the two unpegged **singleton** nodes via the signed two-by-two Hankel. Physical row four may be the other active \(r\)-copy, the silent at \(r\), or one of those singletons. The cubic \(H_4-P\) is a unit at the missing singleton roots, so row four is at \(r\). Its actual \(h=H_4(r/\gamma)\) is nonzero, and whether that row is active or silent, its pure-four values are on the active profile. This actual read and the known active front fix both \(Ah,\tau_4\) affine profiles. The literal row-five singleton read fails at both reconstructed nodes; if row five is another occupant of \(r\), at least one affine derivative differs from the required slope, forcing a literal duplicate of row four. [Replay](one_front_k0.py).

If physical eight is silent, the other silent zero precisely occupies that stage, so rows \(4,\ldots,7\) are active. If row four is the second active \(r\), its actual normalized \(h=H_4(r/\gamma)\) fixes both pure-four slopes with the active front. Row five is singleton; its two actual pure-four equations with \(h=P(c_5)\) are polynomials in its normalized clock. Their exact padded active-front factor is removed (that earlier singleton cannot repeat), and the two residuals have unit resultants. If row four is singleton, its literal necessary \(H_4-P\) cubic, after removing the exact active-front root, gives a monic quadratic for row four in the fixed Gauss field. For row five a singleton, remove the exact padded active-front and row-four factors from the two literal equations; the remaining quadratics are coprime. For row five the other active \(r\) occupant, use the physical \(h\) as an unknown, not the value \(P(r)\). Its two actual affine scalar equations have a unit inconsistency determinant. [Replay](gauss_terminal_silent8.py).

## Zero reverse factor, literal silent front at terminal clock

Here \(x\) equals the literal silent front, \(\gamma=1/(1+A_0)\) in the quadratic front field, and \(x\ne r\) since the Gauss quartic is irreducible there. If the literal front is the sole silent at \(x\), the two opposite actual \(w\)-values at that clock force its \(h=P(x)\) and its pure-four values onto the active profile. Together with the active literal front this pins \(Q\) in the quadratic field, impossible since its root \(r\) has degree four. Therefore **both silent physical zero stages** share \(x\); in particular physical row four is active (it cannot copy either literal front clock). The active front and all fixed tail clocks are in the quadratic field, so none equals \(r\). Their normalized \(P\) scale is fixed using the active literal front.

If eight is active, the four singleton pegs (active front, \(x,c_9,c_8\)) and \(r\) leave one missing ordinary singleton, and the signed rank-one Hankel is a unit. If eight is silent, the other silent is physically eight, and \(x,c_9\), the active front and \(r\) make four pegs. The signed two-node reconstruction and actual \(H_4-P\) unit force physical row four to be \(r\), then its actual pure-four read fixes the slopes with the active front, as in the previous section. The singleton and copy row-five gates close this case. [Replay](one_front_k0.py).

## Nonzero reverse factor, two silents may share a literal front root

The active literal front here is the Gauss double, fixing \(\gamma\) by \(L_4(\gamma A_0)=0\) for its normalized front clock \(A_0\). This exact degree-four factor is one of the earlier anchored diagonal factors; the two scaled front Gauss factors are coprime. The model in that field supplies a cubic proportional to the actual predictor \(P=L_4/Q\) since both have the same Gauss root division, but **does not assume the literal silent front's own \(h\) matches that predictor**. The singleton-tail equations are homogeneous in that scale, so the same exact projections apply.

If physical eight is active, the normalized reverse-root clock is the other literal front \(Z\). At a fixed \(Z\), the actual required singleton-tail equation is a unit in the earlier tail factors, even if both physical silents share that clock. If physical eight is silent, its \(w_8\ne0\) forces it to be a second silent occupant of the same root. Substitute \(Z=X+k(u-2)/2\) to obtain the exact earlier cubic when ninth is singleton; when ninth is \(r\) its gap is fixed directly. The specific literal front root \(Z\) never satisfies the physical condition in those fields. [Replay](front_shared_root.py). No normalization of the silent eighth is made.
