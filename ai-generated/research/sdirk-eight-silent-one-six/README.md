# Six active stages with one invisible donor are impossible when the cubic defect is undetected

At the ten-stage strict SDIRK8 floor over a characteristic-zero field, suppose stage one is the only physical stage with nonzero quadratic residual, its weight is zero, and \(B\boldsymbol h=0\) for \(\boldsymbol h=(A-\gamma I)\boldsymbol g\). This packet excludes **every six-active case**, extending the [seven-active and attached-six packet](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-row/README.md). Four or five active stages with undetected defect and every case with detected defect remain separate. This does not concern multiple invisible clocks or change the unrestricted **10–11** interval.

## 1. Physical support and a useful virtual read

The silent quadratic-zero rows are the actual front pair \(a,b\), with \(h_a,h_b\ne0\), and one extra physical row at clock \(\zeta\). Write \(\boldsymbol w=\boldsymbol b^{\mathsf T}M\), \(x=c_{10}=1-\gamma\), \(u=(x-c_9)/\gamma\ne0\), \(k=1+2u-u^2\). The single invisible stage has \(w_1=0\). The three tests \(\boldsymbol w C^j\boldsymbol h=0\) for \(0\le j\le2\) localize the signed \(w h\) weight at each of these at most three silent clocks. In particular the weights \(w_a,w_b\) vanish separately whenever their clock differs from \(\zeta\), since their \(h\)'s are nonzero. The scalar support of \(\boldsymbol w\), after aggregation, is therefore among the active clocks and possibly the single silent clock \(\zeta\). Let \(\omega\) be the sum of the silent physical \(w_i\)'s at \(\zeta\), including a coincident front row if present. This is a signed aggregate, not a claim that the underlying entries vanish when \(\omega=0\).

The active pure-four space has rank at most one and is spanned by \(L_4(\boldsymbol c)\), which is nonzero there. To see nonzero, if all active clocks were Gauss and stage eight active, the literal three-clock reverse incidence would fail. If stage eight were silent, it would be the extra zero and literal row four would be active; the Gauss row-four cubic already excludes that placement. Hence every \(v\in F_4\) agrees on the active coordinates with \(\rho_v L_4(\boldsymbol c)\) for some scalar \(\rho_v\). Since \(\boldsymbol w\) annihilates \(F_4\), for \(\omega\ne0\) the virtual read
\[
 v_{\rm vir}(\zeta)=\frac{\sum_{i\ {\rm silent},\ c_i=\zeta}w_i v_i}{\omega}
\]
has \(h_{\rm vir}=0\) and \(v_{\rm vir}=\rho_v L_4(\zeta)\) for every pure-four read. When \(\zeta\ne a,b\), this is the actual single silent row, not a new tableau stage.

If \(\omega\ne0\), \(\zeta=a\) or \(b\), and the extra silent stage is early, it cannot be physical row four: its unique modified strict moment solve at either literal earlier clock would be a duplicate. If it is row five, row four is active with its \(H_4\) cubic. At the fixed clock \(\zeta=a\) or \(b\), both actual grade-four reads \(A\boldsymbol h,\boldsymbol\tau_4\) in row five depend affinely on its \(h\), by the unique three strict aggregates at \(a,b,c_4\) after the invisible feed is prescribed by \(h\). The padded earlier front row is the same affine formula, evaluated at its real \(h_a\) or \(h_b\). Thus the virtual weighted \(h=0\) evaluates those reads by that exact formula at \(h=0\). The preceding row-five residual cubic would have the normalized root \(a/\gamma\) or \(b/\gamma\), excluded by the already verified exact front guards. This argument applies whether or not the clock \(\zeta\) coincides with an active clock.

## 2. Nonzero silent aggregate on an active clock

Suppose \(\omega\ne0\) and \(\zeta\) is an active clock. If the extra silent row is after physical five, the first two active rows already give at least six ordinary sites. If it is physically four or five, the coincident front cases were just excluded; otherwise it has its actual \(h=0\) and pure-four line. The two actual or virtual early low-profile reads are the same front cubic/cubic/quartic as before. Their two clock values are distinct active *sites*, since the one silent clock coincides with an active one. Both are non-Gauss by the nonzero \(A\boldsymbol h\) reads. Four ordinary sites would require two canceled pairs in addition to four Gauss sites, and five are excluded by the fixed three-peg Hankel at those two clock values and \(x\). Thus in any case all six active clocks are distinct and ordinary. The six zero moments of \(\boldsymbol w\) force its scalar aggregate to be zero at every active site. Terminal nonzero \(w_{10}\) forces \(\zeta=x\), and \(w_9=0\) forces \(k=0\). For an early silent row this is excluded because the finite early clock reads are both different from \(x\). For later silent rows it is the [previously closed attached case](../sdirk-eight-silent-one-row/README.md).

## 3. Nonzero silent aggregate on a separate clock

Now \(\omega\ne0\) and \(\zeta\) is outside the active clocks. The six scalar zero moments require at least seven aggregate sites, so the six active clocks are distinct and the aggregate numerator of \(\boldsymbol w\) on the seven sites is nonzero constant. Include the zero-weight site \(\zeta\) in an ordinary seven-site nodal polynomial \(D\). Although its weight is zero, the exact cardinal identity \(W_D(c_i)=b_iD'(c_i)\) holds at all seven sites. In particular \(W_D(\zeta)=0\), and the reciprocal reverse readings from the preceding packet hold at the actual active tail.

**If \(\zeta\ne a,b\).** All actual rows \(4,\ldots,10\) now have \(h=0\) and the pure-four line, including the silent row physically. Their clocks are distinct. The first three clocks are in the same finite front fibers as the seven-active proof. If physical eight is active, the two earlier reciprocal polynomials exclude this case without requiring the seventh physical clock's weight nonzero: if that missing clock (physical seven) is silent, its masked moments \(m_0,m_1\) are both zero, and the scaled nodal polynomial \(P_6(m_0X-m_1)\) still satisfies both necessary tail equations. There was no division by \(m_0\) in the exact determinant gate.

If physical eight is silent then its actual clock is \(\zeta=x+\gamma k(u-2)/2\) from the two weighted taps. The six known physical clocks \(c_4,c_5,c_6,x,c_9,\zeta\) leave \(c_7\). Use the scaled nodal polynomial \(P_6(m_0X-m_1)\) as before. Its \(W_{\widetilde D}(\zeta)\) has degree at most two in \(u\): deleting the marked factor \(X-\zeta\) gives the marked moment cancellation, leaving dependence only on affine \(c_9\). The leading unit makes this a fixed monic quadratic; its common ideal with the penultimate polynomial (after exact removal of \(u=0\)) is the unit ideal at \(1129,t=203\) in both front phases. [Replay](six_separate.py).

**If \(\zeta=a\) or \(b\).** The early placements were excluded by the virtual affine read. For physical six, the six pegs \(c_4,c_5,\zeta,x,c_9,c_8\) leave actual active \(c_7\), giving the scaled nodal polynomial as above. The penultimate cubic after exact \(u=0\) division and \(W_{\widetilde D}(\zeta)=0\) have unit resultant. For physical seven, the third active clock \(c_6\) is already in the finite third-active quadratic (before the extra silent row), and all seven sites are pegged. Ordinary degree seven requires \(I(D)=0\), together with \(W_D(\zeta)=0\); the first has preserved degree two in \(u\). For physical eight, the same \(c_6\) field applies and six pegs leave \(c_7\). The monic cubic from \(2(\zeta-x)-\gamma k(u-2)=0\) is coprime to \(W_{\widetilde D}(\zeta)=0\). All twenty literal orientations are verified at \(1103,t=161\). [Replay](six_front_clock.py). Neither these masks nor the virtual read assumes that the two physical silent defects at the coincident front clock are equal.

## 4. Zero effective silent aggregate

Finally let \(\omega=0\). The six vanishing scalar moments then make each active-clock aggregate zero. Nonzero \(w_{10}\) requires an active repeat at \(x\), leaving at most five distinct active clocks. If there are four, all active clocks are Gauss: either actual stage eight is active (impossible reverse Gauss triple) or it is silent and actual row four is active (impossible Gauss row-four cubic). With five distinct active clocks, \(x\) is the sole doubled group and \(w_9=0\), so \(k=0\). The extra silent zero must be physical four or five, since otherwise the already checked first two active rows require at least six ordinary sites. Thus physical eight is active, with literal \(c_8,c_9\) fixed by the \(k=0\) tail. There must be five ordinary sites: if \(x\) canceled, both \(c_8,c_9\) would be Gauss, rejected by the fixed tail gcd.

The three pegs \(x,c_8,c_9\) leave two ordinary nodes. Their signed \(3\times3\) Hankel is singular while its leading \(2\times2\) block is invertible by signed Vandermonde, giving an explicit projection on \(\gamma\). Strip only the impossible factors \(\gamma=0\), zero signed pivot, and repeated factors. The retained exact factor degrees are \(9\) in each reverse-star orientation and \(1,8\) in each reverse-arithmetic orientation. The \(2\times2\) pivot reconstructs the two missing nodes.

If physical five is silent, physical row four is active and \(H_4(c_4/\gamma)=0\). Its clock is \(x\) or one of the two reconstructed roots (the only actual repeat is \(x\)). This cubic is a unit on all those roots. [Replay](early_repeated.py).

If physical four is silent, let \(Z=c_4/\gamma\) and \(y=c_5/\gamma\), the earliest active clock. The values of \(y\) are \(x/\gamma\) or the two finite reconstructed normalized nodes. The actual \(\tau_4,Ah\) reads at silent row four follow from its three taps and \(H_4(Z)\). At the first active row the unique strict scalar aggregates are at \(a,b,c_4\) (the stage-one feed is zero since \(h=0\)). For any pure-four column \(v\), the difference \(v-\rho_v L_4\) is in the kernel of the active restriction, spanned by \(\boldsymbol h,C\boldsymbol h\). Interpolate that affine multiple using the two known front defects. With the two choices \(v=\tau_4,Ah\), write \(\widehat v(Z)=v_4-h_4(Z)\operatorname{affine}(v_a/h_a,v_b/h_b)(Z)\) and \(\widehat L(Z)\) likewise in scaled units. The actual first-active reads then require
\[
 L_4(\gamma y)\widehat v(Z)-v_5(Z)\widehat L(Z)=0 .
\]
We write the row-five tap formulas with a common mask \(J=(Z-a/\gamma)(Z-b/\gamma)\). Both numerator comparisons exactly contain \(J^2\). One factor comes because the padded literal \(H_4\) and pure reads at the earlier clocks agree with those earlier stages; the other comes because, at a padded collision with \(A_0\) or \(B_0\), the two masked taps there have opposite amplitudes and agree in the fed \(c^3\) and \(h\). Therefore the masked scalar and \(h\) outputs also vanish there. Neither earlier clock is admissible for the actual row four by the literal twin obstruction. The two cleared cubic gates have unit gcd in every retained field at \(1103,t=161\); the first preserves full degree three. No division by \(L_4(c_5)\) is needed. [Replay](early_fourth.py).
