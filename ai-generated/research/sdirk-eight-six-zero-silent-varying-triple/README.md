# The silent varying triple cannot complete six physical zeros

Consider a strict ten-stage SDIRK tableau with common nonzero diagonal \(\gamma\) and ordinary order at least eight over a characteristic-zero field. Use \(\mathbf c=A\mathbf1\), \(\mathbf g=A\mathbf c-\mathbf c^{\odot2}/2\), \(C=\operatorname{diag}\mathbf c\), \(G=\operatorname{diag}\mathbf g\), and \(U=\ker G\). Assume exactly six physical zeros, no active off-zero weights, and two physical off-zero clocks. One clock is a singleton and the other occurs on three off rows with three different nonzero residuals.

**Result.** Every such chart is impossible in characteristic zero, allowing silence and repeated clocks among the zero rows. There is no positive-characteristic or new overall stage-bound claim.

## 1. The reverse pure measure has a nonzero atom on the off triple

The [support census](../sdirk-eight-six-zero-census/README.md) gives \(d_2=1,d_3=3\). In particular the \(W_2\) off restriction has rank one and is generated by the off part of the reverse pure row \(\mathbf z^\mathsf T=-\mathbf b^\mathsf T(CA-AC-A^2)/2\), since all off weights vanish. Normalize \(\mathbf x=\mathbf c/\gamma,\ A'=A/\gamma,\ D=1/\gamma\), and let \(d\) denote the normalized off triple clock. The annihilation \(\mathbf z^\mathsf T G E_3=0\) separates the two off clocks by its constant and linear probes. Hence \(z\) vanishes at the singleton, and at the triple its first two residual-power moments vanish:
\[
 \sum_{i\ \mathrm{in\ triple}} z_i g_i=0,\qquad
 \sum_{i\ \mathrm{in\ triple}} z_i g_i^2=0 .
\]
Its ordinary triple aggregate \(Z=\sum_{i\ \mathrm{in\ triple}}z_i\) is nonzero: otherwise the three distinct residuals would give a square Vandermonde and \(\mathbf z\) would vanish off. Since \(Z_2=\operatorname{Ann}(E_5)\), the moments of \(\mathbf z\) on the six zero rows are
\[
 \sum_{i\ \mathrm{zero}} z_i x_i^j=-Z d^j,\qquad 0\le j\le5.
\]
If \(d\) is not a zero clock, the six literal zero clocks must be distinct; a nodal polynomial of degree at most five on fewer labels would contradict this nonzero evaluation. Then the reverse pure row has nonzero value at each zero and the exact Lagrange formula
\[
 z_i=-\frac{Z P_6(d)}{(d-x_i)P_6'(x_i)}
\]
on them, where \(P_6\) is their monic nodal polynomial. If \(d\) is a zero clock, Vandermonde uniqueness makes every other zero-clock aggregate of \(z\) vanish. These are different cases; no division by a clock difference is made in the coincident chart.

The first two physical rows are off at the two different off clocks and the third is zero. Thus \(x_1=1,\ x_2=1+u,\ a=x_3=1+2u\) and \(Q(u)=4u^3-12u^2+3u+2=0\). In particular \(d=1\) or \(1+u\). The supported pure scalar trace is \(P(T)=(T-1)(T-1-u)\). Its grade-three primitive is clean on the zero rows; the grade-four discrepancy is the line of the scaled \(L_4\) whenever some zero clock is not Gaussian, since \(GE_2\) has rank three and \(L_4(\mathbf x)\in F_4\). At first zero \(a\), the \(M P(\mathbf x)\) diagonal reads \(-\gamma^2P(a)\ne0\) in unscaled \(M\), so \(L_4(a)\ne0\). We use the usual [second-zero causal gate](../sdirk-eight-five-zero-graph-arithmetic-silent/README.md#2-three-supported-equations-in-the-same-arithmetic-pencil) for this pure polynomial. The last two rows are live zeros at \(s=D-1,\ t=D-1-v\), with \(v=(c_{10}-c_9)/\gamma\).

## 2. Zero rear ratio and coincidences with a zero clock

If \(\kappa_R=1+2v-v^2=0\), the literal reverse relation makes \(z\) supported at the terminal clock. Its nonzero triple values force \(d=s\), hence \(D=2\) or \(2+u\). Row 8 is live, and the last three zero clocks are \(s,t,r=D-1-w\), \(w=2v\) or \(2-v\). In both \(D\) choices and both rear phases the four fixed literal zero clocks \(a,r,t,s\) are exactly distinct. The four-clock mask has nonzero \(2\)-by-\(2\) ordinary pivot through degree seven, forcing two further distinct live zero labels outside it. This remains valid if the first clock \(a\) is silent. Recover the monic quadratic on those labels; its gcd with the second forward gate is constant in all four exact fields.

Now suppose \(\kappa_R\ne0\) and \(d\) is a zero clock. Both \(z_{10},z_9\) are nonzero. If \(d\) is neither terminal zero clock, both their clock groups require duplicates to cancel their \(z\)-aggregates. With six zero rows this leaves at most four ordinary labels, all of them live by ordinary rank. Both terminal zero clocks are therefore Gaussian, contradicted by the [characteristic-zero Gaussian rear projections](../sdirk-eight-four-zero-native-rear/README.md).

If \(d\in\{s,t\}\), the other terminal zero clock must have a duplicate. Having only four nonzero ordinary labels with both terminal masses nonzero would again make both Gaussian. The only other four-mass possibility has the required duplicate at the other terminal clock **canceled as ordinary mass**, and all four remaining singleton labels live and Gaussian. Its canceled label is then the only non-Gaussian zero clock (the four distinct live Gauss labels exhaust the quartic). The first \(M P(\mathbf x)\) diagonal forces \(a\) to equal that canceled label.

In the five-mass case, or that exceptional canceled-duplicate case, there is exactly one duplicate label, at the other terminal clock. A silent off row 8 would require its clock be \(d\) (because \(z_8\ne0\)), but its arithmetic ghost clock is distinct from \(s,t\). A silent zero row 8 likewise has \(z_8\ne0\) at a clock distinct from \(s,t\), which would require a second duplicate. Thus row 8 is live. Its \(z_8\) is zero because it is a singleton zero at a clock different from \(d\). The reflected third phase is therefore arithmetic and \(Q(v)=0\) (the star would have nonzero third reflected residual \(\kappa_R\)). This leaves eight directed frames: both choices \(d=1,1+u\), either \(d=s\) or \(t\), and the two exact tensor components of the shared gap cubic \(Q\), degrees three and six for equal and distinct roots.

The eight-frame replay checks the complete ordinary tests. Only one frame has \(a\) equal to a known rear label, namely \(u=v,\ d=t,\ D=2+2u,\ a=s\). In it \(d=D/2\), so \(L_4(d)\ne0\); thus \(d\) cannot be one of the four live Gauss singleton labels in the canceled-duplicate case. This disposes of that exceptional possibility. In the five-live-label case, all four-clock frames fail the ordinary single-unseen-label moment relations. In the one frame with three known labels, ordinary recovery yields a monic quadratic, and the second forward gate has constant gcd with it.

## 3. The two kinds of literal silent eighth

Suppose now \(d\) is not a zero clock, so there are six distinct literal zero labels and the Lagrange formula of section 1 applies. If row 8 is silent, the literal ghost conditions give \(v^3-4v^2-v+2=0\) and \(x_8=D-1-2v\). The exact joint gap field with \(Q(u)\) has degree nine, with a verified primitive element \(u+2v\).

If stage 8 is off-zero, its \(z_8\ne0\) forces \(x_8=d\), so \(D=2+2v\) or \(2+u+2v\). At any zero label \(x_i\), write \(B_i=I_D(P_6/(T-x_i))\) for its ordinary cardinal numerator, \(I_D(T^j)=D^j/(j+1)\). Comparing \(z_9/z_{10}\) with the normalized literal ratio \(\kappa_R b_9/b_{10}\) gives
\[
 \kappa_R(d-t)B_t=(d-s)B_s .
\]
Here it simplifies to \(\kappa_R B_t=2B_s\). Mask the fixed labels \(a,s,t\) to write \(P_6=P_3 Q_3\) with a monic cubic \(Q_3\) on the three remaining literal zeros. The ratio is a linear equation in its coefficients, and \(I_D(P_6)=I_D(TP_6)=0\) give the other two linear equations. Their pivot is nonzero in both fixed frames. The exact second forward gate gcd with \(Q_3\) is constant in each; this works even if one of the literal labels initially has zero weight.

If stage 8 is a silent **zero**, there are exactly five live ordinary labels. Having only four would make both rear sites Gaussian (the labels are distinct), contradicted by the silent Gaussian rear projection. Thus \(a,s,t\) are now known live labels, and two live ordinary labels remain. Their ordinary masked Hankel determinant gives a guarded degree-nine polynomial in \(D\). The \(2\)-by-\(2\) pivot is nonzero and its exact inverse is checked in the quotient. Both orders of the two interior live labels are kept; the quadratic norm of the second forward gate has constant gcd with the degree-nine polynomial.

## 4. Live eighth: moment minors and a small or large exact field

If stage 8 is live, its nonzero \(z_8\) gives a nonzero normalized third reflected residual \(\kappa_3\). Use physical (not \(\gamma\)-scaled) clocks in this section, and denote the physical versions of \(a,d,s,t,r\) by the same letters. Thus \(a=(1+2u)\gamma,\ d=(1+j u)\gamma\) with \(j=0,1\), \(s=1-\gamma,\ t=s-\gamma v,\ r=s-\gamma w\). The two remaining literal zero labels satisfy a monic quadratic \(Q_2\). With \(P_4=(T-a)(T-s)(T-t)(T-r)\) there are two ordinary equations
\(I(P_4 Q_2)=I(T P_4 Q_2)=0\). Two further linear equations in its coefficients are the actual reverse cardinal ratios
\[
 \kappa_R(d-t) B_t=(d-s)B_s,\qquad
 \kappa_3(d-r) B_r=(d-s)B_s,\qquad B_i=I(P_4Q_2/(T-x_i)).
\]
They are valid even if an interior label is silent. In arithmetic, \(w=2v\) and \(H(v)\kappa_3=-\kappa_R Q(v)\), \(H(v)=v^3-4v^2-v+2\). Both \(H(v),Q(v)\) are nonzero: \(Q,H\) are coprime in characteristic zero, \(\kappa_R\ne0\), and \(\kappa_3\ne0\). Clear \(H\) and use the two ratios without inversion. In the star, \(\kappa_3=\kappa_R\) and subtraction gives the further moment \(I((T-d)(T-a)(T-s)Q_2)=0\). Averaging the two ratios retains only \((v-1)^2\) since \(t,r\) are symmetric about \(m=1-2\gamma\): the numerator before multiplication by \(\kappa_R\) is
\[
 I\bigl([(d-m)(T-m)-\gamma^2(v-1)^2](T-a)(T-s)Q_2\bigr).
\]
Thus the two star orientations are represented without choosing a sign root. Both remain covered by the subsequent forward gate, which depends only on \(Q_2\).

The resulting \(4\)-by-\(3\) homogeneous matrix on the constant, linear and leading quadratic coefficients must have all four \(3\)-by-\(3\) minors zero. These are necessary even at zero ordinary pivot. Divide their known factors only by physical nonzero guards: distinct known and off clock differences, nonzero diagonal and gaps, and the reflected residual guards above. Exact pairwise resultants eliminate the gap; a gcd of all projections and exact rational factorization over \(\mathbb Q(u)\) gives:

| Live rear phase | Degree of diagonal projection gcd | Exact factor degrees with multiplicities |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Star | 11 | \(1\) twice, \(9\) once |
| Arithmetic | 82 | one linear factor once and one eight times; \(4\) and \(69\) once each |

These counts hold for each \(d\) choice. Substitution kills each linear factor by an identically zero unit guard. The degree-four arithmetic fibers have an exact linear gap gcd entirely removed by local nonzero guards. The degree-nine star fibers each have one exact squared-gap root. Their ordinary \(2\)-by-\(2\) pivot is nonzero, and the norm of the forward gate over the recovered \(Q_2\) is nonzero. This checks both orders of the two unknown early labels and both reflected orientations.

The degree-69 arithmetic factor for each \(d\) is handled by an exact good-reduction certificate. The corresponding field \(\mathbb Q(u,\gamma)\) has a \(p\)-adic embedding: the exact \(Q(u)\) and the monic degree-69 factor both have the displayed simple successive reductions, with all coefficient denominators prime to \(p\).

| Physical triple clock | \(p\) | \(u\bmod p\) | \(\gamma\bmod p\) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| \(\gamma\) | 17 | 16 | 14 |
| \((1+u)\gamma\) | 11 | 3 | 2 |

A fixed first minor retains its full degree in \(v\) at each of these reductions, so every algebraic gap root is integral at the selected prime. In addition to the four necessary minors, use the norm of the forward second-zero gate in the monic **scaled** quadratic from the ordinary Cramer rows. This condition needs no inverse of the principal pivot: if the pivot is zero, both Cramer numerators vanish on a true solution, and the scaled second root is zero; the cleared forward polynomial and its companion norm still vanish. The residue gcd of these five necessary polynomials is constant in both certificates, excluding every common algebraic gap root. This is a characteristic-zero exclusion by simple local embedding, not a claim about methods in either residue characteristic.
