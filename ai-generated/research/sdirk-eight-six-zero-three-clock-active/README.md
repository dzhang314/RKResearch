# Six zeros, three off clocks, and one active pair: complete exclusion

Consider a strict ten-stage SDIRK tableau \(A,\mathbf b\) of ordinary order at least eight with nonzero common diagonal \(\gamma\) over a characteristic-zero field. Write \(\mathbf c=A\mathbf1,\ \mathbf g=A\mathbf c-\mathbf c^{\odot2}/2\). Assume exactly six physical quadratic zeros, genuine quadratic graph support, and three physical off-zero clocks. The [six-zero support census](../sdirk-eight-six-zero-census/README.md) leaves exactly one active canceled pair at a repeated off clock and two silent off singletons.

**Result.** This final six-zero chart is impossible over every characteristic-zero field. This packet includes both literal forward placements, both directed rear phases, ordinary rank drop, and all algebraic gap factors. It does not change the global stage bound.

## 1. Literal support, fixed rear, and exact cardinal mode

Normalize \(A'=A/\gamma,\ \mathbf x=\mathbf c/\gamma,\ D=1/\gamma\), and write \(U=\ker G\) for the physical zero subspace. Let \(\mathbf z^\mathsf T=-\mathbf b^\mathsf T(CA-AC-A^2)/2\), and \(\boldsymbol\eta^\mathsf T=\mathbf z^\mathsf T(A'-I)\). The active pair has equal nonzero residual and opposite weights, so \(\mathbf z^\mathsf T\) is its nonzero difference row. In particular stage 10 is off, and its earlier mate is at \(d=x_{10}=D-1\). The last two rows are live at distinct clocks, making stage 9 a live zero and the reflected ratio zero. A silent row 8 would require \(z_8\ne0\) by [the literal rear gate](../sdirk-eight-four-zero-gaussian-rear/README.md), so row 8 is also a live zero at a third distinct clock. Put \(s=x_8=D-1-w,\ t=x_9=D-1-v\), with \(v^2-2v-1=0\), \(w=2v\) or \(2-v\).

The \(W_3\) off restriction has rank one (\(d_2=1\)), generated by the canceled pair. Its \( \eta_{10}=0 \) forces \(\boldsymbol\eta\) to vanish **coordinatewise** off, not just after taking the ordinary aggregate. The literal reflected first taps give
\[
 \eta_8/\eta_9=b_8 p_R/(b_9v),\qquad
 2p_Rv=2wv+1+2w-w^2.
\]
Both pure coordinates are nonzero. Here \(2p_Rv=1+4v\) in arithmetic and \(-2\) in the star; neither vanishes. If at most five distinct zero clocks existed, \(\boldsymbol\eta\in\operatorname{Ann}(E_4)\) would have zero aggregate at each by Vandermonde through degree four. Two distinct nonzero \(\eta\) coordinates at \(s,t\) would then each require a duplicate mate among the other four zeros. This leaves at most four distinct ordinary labels, all live by ordinary rank, and makes \(s,t\) Gaussian. Their exact two-Gaussian zero-ratio projection is [already excluded in characteristic zero](../sdirk-eight-five-zero-graph-one-active/README.md#2-four-ordinary-sites-are-impossible).

Therefore all six literal zero clocks are distinct, whether or not all carry a weight. Write \(P_6\) for their monic nodal polynomial, \(I_D(T^j)=D^j/(j+1)\), and
\[
 B(T)=I_{D,X}\bigl((P_6(X)-P_6(T))/(X-T)\bigr).
\]
At each literal zero label \(x_i\), \(b_i P_6'(x_i)=B(x_i)\), including a silent label. The nonzero moment-null row \(\eta\) is a common nonzero multiple of \(1/P_6'(x_i)\). The literal ratio above therefore yields the extra linear moment
\[
 p_R B(s)=v B(t).
\]
If \(a\) is the first physical zero, \(P_6=(T-a)(T-s)(T-t)Q_3(T)\) where \(Q_3\) is monic cubic on the other three early zeros. Its coefficients satisfy three linear equations: \(I_D(P_6)=I_D(TP_6)=0\), and this extra ratio. These conditions apply even when a zero label is silent.

## 2. Forward and reverse causal gates

The supported column is \(\mathbf q=P(\mathbf x)-\mathbf g/\gamma^2\), where \(\deg P\le2\) and \(P\) agrees with the normalized residual at the three off clocks. \(F_3=GE_1\) is clean on \(U\). The grade-four restriction is the line of \(L_4\) since \(GE_2\) has rank three and six distinct zero clocks cannot all be Gaussian. The first supported read is \(JP(a)=P(a)\) and the second at \(b\) is the usual [forward one-tap gate \(G(P,b)=0\)](../sdirk-eight-five-zero-graph-arithmetic-silent/README.md#2-three-supported-equations-in-the-same-arithmetic-pencil). Here \(a\ne0\), \(P(a)\ne0\), and \(L_4(a)\ne0\): \(M\mathbf q\) has nonzero restriction on \(U\) by triangularity, and its first zero value is a nonzero diagonal multiple of \(P(a)\). If \(a=0\), the clean first primitive would give \(P(0)=0\), contradicting \(L_4(0)\ne0\). If \(P(a)=0\) at \(a\ne0\), the two clean diagonal primitives force \(P(a)=JP(a)=J(TP)(a)=0\), which makes a quadratic identically zero. This would contradict \(P(1)=1/2\).

A weighted scalar polynomial of degree at most two gives an actual \(W_2\) row supported on \(U\) after subtracting the appropriate multiple of \(\mathbf z^\mathsf T\). Its off support is exactly the one pair and therefore can be canceled; its scalar trace is unchanged. Its right primitive under \(A'\) has trace \(J_D^*R(T)=JR(D)-JR(T)\) and pure error in \(Z_3\), whose restriction to \(U\) is the line of \(\boldsymbol\eta\). Take \(R(T)=(T-s)(T-t)\). At last zero \(t\) and at the **third reverse zero** \(r\), the actual supported reads are purely diagonal, since \(R(s)=R(t)=0\). Multiplying both equations by the ordinary nodal derivatives gives
\[
 [R(r)-J_D^*R(r)]B(r)+J_D^*R(t)B(t)=0.
\]
This equation allows either silent or live labels without dividing by their weights. In particular it uses \(r\), the last of the three roots of \(Q_3\), not the forward second zero \(b\). The exact \(J_D^*R(t)\ne0\) in both rear phases, so the third reverse zero is also live.

For a fourth reverse gate, write the first four **forward** zero labels in order \(a,b,c,r\). Define the off nodal cubic \(R_{\mathrm{off}}=\prod_{\mathrm{three\ off\ clocks}}(T-x_i)\). The supported scalar traces \(P,TP,R_{\mathrm{off}}\) are actual \(E_3\cap U\) columns, and are independent by the three distinct off clocks and their nonzero residual values. The following test profile belongs to their span and vanishes at \(a,b\):
\[
 S(T)=P(a)P(b)(b-a)R_{\mathrm{off}}(T)
   -P(T)\bigl[P(b)R_{\mathrm{off}}(a)(b-T)+P(a)R_{\mathrm{off}}(b)(T-a)\bigr].
\]
Use the supported weighted cubic trace \(R_3(T)=(T-s)(T-t)(T-r)\) in \(W_3\), again killing the off pair by a pure correction. Its actual right read vanishes at \(s,t,r\) and is diagonal \(b_cR_3(c)\) at \(c\). Its pure discrepancy belongs to \(Z_4=\operatorname{Ann}(E_3)\). Pairing it with \(S\) eliminates the two other unknown rows by \(S(a)=S(b)=0\) and gives
\[
 -\sum_{x_i\in\{s,t,r\}}b_i S(x_i)J_D^*R_3(x_i)
 +b_c S(c)\bigl(R_3(c)-J_D^*R_3(c)\bigr)=0.
\]
This is a genuine fourth chronological reverse gate, not a symmetric polynomial assumption about \(b\) and \(r\).

## 3. First zero at physical row 3

Here the literal forward phase is arithmetic with \(Q(u)=4u^3-12u^2+3u+2=0\), \(a=1+2u\). The graph is \(P=P_0+\lambda (T-1)(T-1-u)\) for \(P_0=2T-1-T^2/2\). The base field \(\mathbb Q(u,v)\) is represented by the verified degree-six primitive element \(u+2v\). If the mate is among the first two off rows, then \(D=2\) or \(2+u\). The exact three-by-three coefficient pivot for \(Q_3\) is nonzero in all four fixed fields; the reverse-third polynomial has constant gcd with \(Q_3\).

If the mate is later, \(d\) differs from \(1,1+u\). Use the same ordinary Cramer system with variable \(D\). Its singular projection (simultaneous zero determinant and all three numerators) has gcd supported only on the forbidden \(D=0\). For the regular chart, scale clocks by the ordinary pivot to make the recovered cubic monic without introducing denominators. The reverse-third gate norm over this cubic leaves one exact irreducible factor of degree \(14\) in the star rear and \(18\) in the arithmetic rear after the structural guards (nonzero diagonal and pivot, distinct labels, nonzero ordinary cardinal numerators at the two live rear zeros, and the late-mate condition).

The possible \(\lambda\) is cleared without division: if the forward second equation is \(F_0+\lambda F_1=0\), use \(F_1P_0-F_0(T-1)(T-1-u)=F_1P\) in the fourth reverse test. The gates remain necessary if \(F_1=0\). The two exact projected fields have simple successive reductions:

| Rear | \(p\) | \(u+2v\) | \(u\) | \(v\) | \(D\) | Third reverse zero \(r\) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Star | 89 | 87 | 35 | 26 | 62 | 54 |
| Arithmetic | 31 | 22 | 5 | 24 | 11 | 16 |

All coefficient denominators are units at the selected primes; the fixed simple roots give embeddings into \(p\)-adic fields. The ordinary cubic remains monic and separable with no collisions at the three fixed labels, and its reduced gcd with the reverse-third gate is the indicated single root. Thus any actual \(r\) must reduce to that root, while \(b,c\) occupy the other two roots. With the exact ordinary cardinal weights (only the nodal derivatives are inverted), the fourth reverse equation has constant gcd with the remaining monic quadratic. This excludes both chronological orders even if the quadratic is not split.

## 4. First zero at physical row 4

The three initial off clocks are distinct and include the earlier mate. If their literal phase is star, \(P=P_0\), so \(JP_0(a)=P_0(a)\) makes \(a\) a root of \(T^3-9T^2+18T-6\). The verified base extension with the rear gap has degree six. For each rear phase the same variable-\(D\) ordinary system has no admissible singular projection. The regular forward-second and reverse-third norms have degrees \(27\) and \(14\) or \(18\) after structural guards, with constant gcd in \(D\). The two chronological gates are normed separately; we do not require that \(b\) and \(r\) be the same root. This treats every mate even without using the additional mate clock equation.

The other literal initial phase is arithmetic. Its first three off clocks are \(1,1+u,1+2u\); the choice of mate gives \(D=2+i u,\ i=0,1,2\). The exact interpolant has
\[
 H P=H P_0+(3u-2)(T-1)(T-1-u),\qquad H=-2+u+4u^2-u^3.
\]
There is no actual \(H=0\), since it would also force \(3u-2=0\), and these are incompatible. Its cleared leading coefficient is \(L=(u-1)^2(u-2)/2\). At \(u=1,2\) the graph is \(T/2,1/2\), respectively, and the first zero is \(a=2,1\) by its diagonal gate (the apparent \(a=0\) is excluded as above). The twelve fixed fields have all three-coefficient pivots nonzero, and the reverse-third gcd is already constant in each.

Otherwise set \(A_0=L a\); multiplying the first-zero diagonal by its leading scale gives an exact monic cubic for \(A_0\). The ordinary three-coefficient system can be built using mask \((LT-A_0)(T-s)(T-t)\), which multiplies every ordinary and rear ratio row by the known nonzero \(L\). Denote its pivot by \(\Delta\) and Cramer numerators by \(N_i\). This gives two monic cubic layers after scaling the prospective early clock by \(L\Delta\). The exact singular projection from the norms of \(\Delta,N_0,N_1,N_2\) has constant gcd after the forbidden base guards \(L,D,H,u,1+2u-u^2\).

For the regular projection use the two **inner** norms \(N_F,N_R\) (forward-second and reverse-third) in the first-clock algebra, and also \(N_F+N_R\). All three outer norms must vanish. The code verifies exact polynomial divisors and degree-preserving good reduction over the quadratic rear gap field. In each of the six \((i,\mathrm{rear})\) charts the regular common gcd is supported only on the forbidden base guards (total degree \(339\)) and the non-base part of the norm of \(\Delta\), to the twentieth power. The latter is one irreducible factor of degree \(9\) for \(i=0\) and \(27\) for \(i=1,2\); total gcd degrees are \(519,879\), respectively. The selected reduction preserves all outer leading degrees, so no larger characteristic-zero gcd is possible: monic common factors remain integral and retain their degrees upon reduction. The exact divisor degrees attain the residue gcd upper bound.

It remains necessary to check the **actual first-clock fibers** at these factors; a projection of some singular first clock does not exclude a different nonsingular one. Over each factor field, some first-clock root has \(\Delta=0\) by the exact norm of \(\Delta\). At **any** such root both inner norms vanish: the first scaled clock and diagonal vanish, so the cleared forward gate vanishes; the scaled six-node polynomial becomes \(T^3 Q_3(T)\) and the scaled endpoint is zero, so its cardinal polynomial becomes \(T^2 Q_3(T)\), zero modulo \(Q_3\). Thus the reverse gate also vanishes there. For each of the six factors a simple good reduction certifies that the monic first-clock cubic and the two inner norms have joint gcd of degree exactly one, with that root singular. Since the exact gcd already has a singular common root, no additional nonsingular root exists in the actual field. The exact singular Cramer projection excluded the remaining roots. This checks local fibers rather than stripping projected denominator factors globally.
