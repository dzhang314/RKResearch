# Exact three-quadratic-zero exclusion over 169 paid characteristic domains

For every field of characteristic $p$ in the following list, a strict ten-stage SDIRK tableau of ordinary order at least eight cannot have **exactly three physical quadratic-zero rows**. Here a physical zero is a literal entry $g_i=0$ of $g=Ac-c^{\odot2}/2$, regardless of its output weight, with $c=A1$ and common diagonal $\gamma\ne0$.

The list is all **164 primes $11\le p\le997$**, and **6277, 6359, 43627, 3716728837183, 92194631289141263724905333**. For each listed characteristic this is a statement about all fields, including their algebraic closures and extensions of arbitrary degree. It is not a claim about arbitrary paid characteristics or about all ten-stage tableaux in these fields. In particular zero-free residuals or a larger physical zero set are not addressed here.

## 1. What remains after the paid support and chronology cases

The [two-clock](../sdirk-eight-two-clock-triple-paid/README.md) proof handles multiplicities on one or two clocks (in combination with the exact coincident-clock rank argument). For three different clocks, the [low-support](../sdirk-eight-three-zero-d2zero-paid/README.md) proof excludes $E_2\cap\ker G=0$, where $G=\operatorname{diag}(g)$ and $E_2$ is the actual degree-two stage space. The [constant](../sdirk-eight-constant-zero-trace/README.md), [affine/null](../sdirk-eight-affine-null-trace/README.md), and [silent quadratic](../sdirk-eight-three-zero-silent-paid/README.md) cases reduce a remaining supported vector to a nonzero quadratic graph with all weights live. The [even centered trace](../sdirk-eight-even-quadratic-trace/README.md), [asymmetric midpoint](../sdirk-eight-asymmetric-midpoint-zero/README.md), and [singular pivots](../sdirk-eight-asymmetric-singular-pivots/README.md) results reduce it to
$$r(y)=y^2+ky+q,\quad y=2c-1,\quad k\ne0,\quad
T\ne0,\quad p_0\ne0,\quad \mathcal A\ne0,\quad \gamma=\mathcal B/(180\mathcal A).$$
Here $T$ and $p_0$ are the leading and constant coefficients of the exact Cramer nodal cubic, and $\mathcal A,\mathcal B$ come from the clean grade-seven diagonal identity. The scalar formulas are reconstructed by `chart.py`. Both actual second-gap denominators and numerators are nonzero; write $R,D,U$ for the first residual normalizer, gap denominator, and gap numerator in either orientation. Reflection flips $k$.

The last chart's third rows are arithmetic. An **A** end has a nonzero third residual on the quadratic graph; a **Z** end has an actual zero third residual with its actual clock at the Cramer node. In either end the earliest zero in that orientation pays the primitive anchor $h=\operatorname{Res}_y(P,H)$, where $P$ is the full zero-node cubic, not a guessed midpoint polynomial. For Z one also uses the physical cubic gap equation and third-clock pin. These gates, including the normalized A arithmetic relation, are exactly those reconstructed in `chart.py` and explained in the paid midpoint and singular-pivot papers; no finite-search-only hypothesis is assumed. The companion [ordered-gap proof](../sdirk-eight-both-zero-thirds/README.md) independently closes Z/Z in every paid field, but this finite sweep also tests Z/Z.

## 2. Exact geometric finite-field projection

Homogeneously substitute the prescribed diagonal $\gamma=\mathcal B/(180\mathcal A)$ into each gate, in a single clear power of its denominator. Every rational coefficient denominator is supported in $2,3,5,7$. Retain all integer contents; remove only primitive integral factors that divide a product of actual nonzero guards:
$$k\,T\,p_0\,\mathcal A\,\mathcal B\,(R R^*)_{\rm cleared}\,(D D^*)_{\rm cleared}\,(U U^*)_{\rm cleared}.$$
Both a gate quotient and the corresponding guard quotient are checked to have integral coefficients. If the guard product is nonzero at an actual point, any such removed factor is nonzero there as well; no degree-drop or content prime is silently discarded.

* In AA and ZZ require both signs of the corresponding gates and anchors, take their even and divided-odd parts in $k$, and replace $k^2$ by a new variable $x$. Both parts must vanish because $2k\ne0$. The symmetric guard is the product of the original guard and its reflection.
* In AZ reflect the A end to the front if necessary. Keep $k$ as the variable and use the front arithmetic gate, the reverse Z cubic and physical pin, and both anchors.

In a listed characteristic reduce these integer bivariate systems exactly. Take pairwise resultants of the first gate with the others in the non-$q$ variable. A possible common zero gives a common $q$-root of all the resultants; the checker uses the *specialized* modular polynomials and verifies the resulting univariate projection is not identically zero. Factor its full gcd, with no bound on the algebraic degree of $q$. For each irreducible factor of degree $d$, choose a root in $\mathbf F_{p^d}$ and evaluate all gates and guards there. All the other roots of that factor are Frobenius conjugate, so this representative is sufficient. The resulting univariate gcd in $x$ or $k$ covers all further algebraic extensions of this field. Discard only factors whose zeros force the guard product to vanish, removing their repeated powers if necessary. In every tested phase and characteristic the surviving gcd is constant. This proves that there is no possible scalar pair in any extension field, not merely that no small finite tableau was found.

For arbitrary coefficient fields of the given characteristic, embed the finite algebraic scalar subfield determined by these projections into an algebraic closure of the prime field. All the cleared polynomial relations and nonzero guards are preserved. A hypothetical nonalgebraic $q$ cannot satisfy the nonzero univariate projection, and after $q$ has been fixed the univariate gcd itself rules out every common root over any field. No compatibility check of later rows is needed after this contradiction.

## Reproducibility and native scope

Run `python check_geometric.py` with Python-flint 0.8. The polynomial/guard data are constructed in memory from `chart.py` and `scalar_basis.py`. Primes through 997 are verified by trial division. The five extras have recursive exact Lucas/Pocklington certificates in `prime_certificates.py`, tested before any finite-field arithmetic. The two large modular cases use the arbitrary-precision FLINT modulus routines; the integral projection is the same. The replay gives 169 characteristic checks, all three actual chronology choices in each, and zero guarded geometric roots.

`RKCert.Research.SDIRKEightRegularProjection` checks the abstract even/odd and quadratic norm inference over fields, with a Lean/Mathlib 4.36.0 axiom audit. The full bivariate projection, finite factorization and causal reduction are the supplied source proof and exact replay, not one native `HasOrder` nonexistence theorem. These 169 exact domains do not justify extrapolation to the other paid characteristics.
