# Ordinary two- and three-slot lifts of an actual Laguerre kernel

Pure mathematics. Fix an integer r >= 2 and a sufficiently small positive
rational common diagonal gamma. All stages below have that physical diagonal.
The paper gives ordinary order at least r+2 with two new rows, or at least r+3
with three, and a subsequent optional scalar-only tail. It does not give an
unrestricted ordinary-order table improvement. The auxiliary ordinary and
scalar orders are deliberately stated separately.

## 1. Reused kernel, paid cap, and notation

The input is the actual cached Laguerre kernel of
`LAGUERRE-CACHED-DENSE-KERNEL.md` (sealed SHA
`ad523310296387203bd96ed272a5ca3fcebe1885d1ffc4eff40b7499e6e447db`),
stopped at layer r. The needed results from it are these:

* Put I P(x) = integral_0^x P(t) dt, T = I - gamma Id, and
  W_k(x) = k! T^k 1. These are monic Laguerre polynomials, with
  T W_k = W_(k+1)/(k+1) and simple positive real roots.
* In the full diagram, F_k is the polynomial chart of degree <k
  interpolating the observations at the W_k roots; F_0 = 0. Each layer-k
  observation at z has strict prestate (T F_(k-1))(z). A chart is a
  polynomial-valued **constant row combination of actual observations**
  H_i = h f(Y_i), not an evaluation of f on a virtual interpolation point.
* Fix a paid chart cap P. The cached physical chart equals the full chart
  on every raw tree coefficient of order <= P. It uses
     K(r,P) = sum_(k=1)^r min(k,P+1-k)
  actual predecessor rows when r <= P. Cap P is a statement about
  reproducing the full chart, **not** an assertion of ordinary order P.
  All coefficients are in the field generated by gamma and the sampled
  real roots. No unselected root is needed for reconstruction.
* If F_* is the exact time derivative profile of the flow, the chart error
  of grade n is supported in W_j with j >= 2k+1-n at layer k.
  In the full diagram this also holds for the unprojected stage polynomial.
  In particular F_k is exact through grade k, and its grade-(k+1) error
  is the exact coefficient vector of x^k multiplied by W_k.

Here is a check of the stage counts that will be used. The sum through
layer P is floor((P+1)^2/4). At cap P=r+2 the two omitted layers have
widths 2,1; at cap P=r+3 the three omitted layers have widths 3,2,1.
Thus the actual retained kernel has respectively
`floor((r+3)^2/4)-3` or `floor((r+4)^2/4)-6` rows.
The proofs below do not quietly retain the omitted layers.

Work in the raw elementary-differential vector space (so exact flow
coefficients include the symmetry factors). Write G_n = D_f^n id,
e_n = G_n/(n-1)!, and
    F_*(x) = h f(Flow_(xh)(y)) = sum_(n>=1) h^n e_n x^(n-1).
For a formal series V write V^[n] for the coefficient of h^n.
Let Uv = Df(y)[v] on homogeneous vectors: it adds one unary root.
Write br for projection to branching roots (at least two children),
and L(v) = D^2 f(y)[f(y),v] for graft with one leaf and a large child.
In particular the unary part of e_(n+1) is U e_n/n. Indeed for a
child t of size n the unary-root coefficient in e_(n+1) is
1/(sigma(t)t!), while the coefficient in e_n is n/(sigma(t)t!).

Write H(Z)=h f(Y) for the physical observation at strict increment Z,
where Y=y+Z+gamma h f(Y). Every Z used below is a constant linear
combination of observations physically completed earlier. An O(h^j)
change of Z changes H only from grade j+1, with leading coefficient
U applied to the grade-j increment. Its possible implicit feedback
is one higher grade. The first old row supplies H(0), when needed.

Use the monic recurrence
    x W_r = W_(r+1) + b_r W_r + a_r W_(r-1),
    a_r=gamma^2 r^2, b_r=gamma(2r+1), s_r=gamma(r+1)^2.
The first equality follows from orthogonality; its lower coefficient
is the squared norm ratio, with norm^2(W_r)=(r!)^2 gamma^(2r)
by Rodrigues and integration by parts. The x^(r-1) coefficient of
W_r is -gamma r^2 from its binomial formula, giving b_r and also
the coefficient s_r of W_r in the W expansion of x^(r+1).

Use ell P = integral_0^1 P(x) dx, mu_j=ell W_j,
M_i=ell(x^i W_r), and N_j=ell(x W_j). These are rational functions
(in fact polynomials before division) of gamma. Two different
operators called integration are not being identified: ell is the
fixed output integral, and I is the variable-upper-limit primitive.
The key adjoint identity is
    ell(T P)=ell(g P),   g(x)=1-gamma-x.                           (1)

## 2. The next chart defects and an actual old donor

Let E_k=F_*-F_k. For r>=2 and cap >=r+2, the exact identities are
    E_r^[r+1](x) = e_(r+1) W_r(x),
    E_r^[r+2](x) = e_(r+2)(W_(r+1)(x)+s_r W_r(x))
                                        + S_r W_(r-1)(x),          (2)
    S_r = a_r br(e_(r+2)) + U S_(r-1)/(r-1),
    S_1 = a_1 br(e_3).                                            (3)
All S_j have zero tall coordinate. In (2), the band in Section 1
allows precisely indices r-1,r,r+1. The latter two are the fixed
top modes of the exact model polynomial.

To compute the remaining one, do the comparison in the full
diagram, then use the paid cap. Its unprojected layer-r state error
at grade r is T(E_(r-1)^[r])=e_r W_r/r. At grade r+1 the state
error is gamma times the unprojected output error plus
T(E_(r-1)^[r+1]). The first of these is a multiple of W_r, since
the output error at that grade is Ue_r W_r/r. Modulo W_r the
second is
    C_r W_(r-1),   C_r = -a_r e_(r+1)/(r+1)+S_(r-1)/(r-1).
Indeed T raises each W index and W_(r+1) reduces to -a_r W_(r-1).
At output grade r+2 the only branching contribution of the
earlier state error has a factor x W_r and reduces to zero.
The rest is unary, U C_r W_(r-1). The interpolation error of
x^(r+1) contributes a_r e_(r+2) W_(r-1). Since its unary part
is a_r U e_(r+1)/(r+1), the two indicated unary factors cancel,
leaving (3). At r=1, H(0) has raw grade-three vector
gamma^2 (tall_3 + fork/2). Also W_2+s_1 W_1=x^2-2gamma^2.
This gives S_1=gamma^2 fork/2 as stated.

Define the actual row-combination donor
    S = [W_(r-2)] (F_r-F_(r-1)),                                   (4)
where brackets select a polynomial coefficient in the W basis,
coefficientwise in h. It is O(h^(r+1)), with grade-(r+1)
vector s=S_(r-1). The low modes in the two charts agree through
grade r by the paid band, and (2) at layer r-1 gives the first
difference. The donor is available before any new stage.

For the three-slot lift assume cap >=r+3. Let Q_2(x) be the monic
degree-two quotient of x^(r+2) by W_r. Then
    br E_r^[r+3]
      = br(e_(r+3)) W_r Q_2 + L(C_r) rem_Wr(x W_(r-1)).            (5)
At this grade an unprojected error at a branching root can have
one erroneous child of size r with other size sum 2, or size r+1
with one leaf. The first terms contain W_r and reduce to zero.
The second is x L(C_r) W_(r-1) modulo W_r. When r=2 the possible
product of two earliest state errors also contains W_r^2, so is
zero at every old site; no special-case omission is needed.
This accounts for all branching errors of the unprojected row.
Taking the nodal remainder of the exact branch model adds the
first term in (5).

For a forced off-grid receiver at clock z, let
    D(z,V)=H((T F_r)(z)+V)-F_r(z),   V=O(h^(r+1)), v=V^[r+1].
Put e=e_(r+1) and B_2=br(e_(r+2)). Its grade-(r+1) vector is
W_r(z)e. Its grade-(r+2) vector is
    B_2 (z-b_r+s_r) W_r(z)
    + U[ s_r W_r(z)e/(r+1) + W_(r-1)(z)s/(r-1) + v ].            (6)
To see this, the unprojected new state error begins at grade r+1
as e W_(r+1)(z)/(r+1)-v, by transport through T. Thus the intrinsic
grade-(r+2) output error is unary. Subtract it from (2) evaluated
at z, using the three-term recurrence and (3). At grade r+3
the new row's intrinsic branching error is exactly
    z L(e W_(r+1)(z)/(r+1)-v).                                    (7)
Only its leading erroneous state child of grade r+1 and its
first-degree state child z f can contribute to a branching root.
Terms at grade r+2 in V affect only unary roots at grade r+3.

## 3. Two new rows: all ordinary trees through r+2

Use cap P=r+2. Define the signed centroid and its weight:
    z=M_1/M_0,   w=M_0/W_r(z),   C=mu_(r-1)-w W_(r-1)(z).
They exist for all sufficiently small positive rational gamma:
as gamma -> 0, W_r(x) -> x^r, M_0 -> 1/(r+1), and
z -> (r+1)/(r+2). The denominators are then nonzero, and z
is a rational number in (0,1) for rational gamma.

Physical chronology:
1. Form H_z = H((T F_r)(z)) and D_z=H_z-F_r(z).
   This is the first new actual observation. The first augmented
   output, based on exact old row combinations, is ell F_r+w D_z.
2. From these completed rows form E=D_z/W_r(z) and
       V=mu_(r+1) E/(r+1) + C S/(r-1).
   Choose an already evaluated baseline H_base at either strict
   increment Z_base=0 (clock gamma) or Z_base=(T F_r)(z) (clock z).
   Add the second new observation H(Z_base+V), with output
       H(Z_base+V)-H_base.

Step 1 pays through r+1 by the weighted W_r moment. Its
grade-(r+2) branch part is paid since
w z W_r(z)=M_1 and the remaining branch factor in (6) is linear
in z. The remaining unary residual is
    U[ mu_(r+1)e/(r+1)+C s/(r-1) ].
For the e coefficient, the same first two moments in the
three-term recurrence give
w W_(r+1)(z)-a_r C=mu_(r+1); for the s
coefficient the definition of C gives it directly. So Step 2
pays exactly this vector, irrespective of the reused baseline's
clock. It cannot change any earlier degree. Both prestates
are explicit row combinations of observations already present.

This gives ordinary r+2 with
    floor((r+3)^2/4)-1
actual stages, no exceptional gamma root and no field extension.
If scalar order through p>=r+2 is desired, the second response
has grade-(r+2) tall coefficient mu_(r+1)/((r+1)r!), nonzero
for small gamma. Use it as an onset-(r+2) direction and form
successive responses H(V_old)-H(0), one new row per further
grade, whose lowest terms are its successive unary grafts.
Triangular scalar reweighting pays all tall coefficients
through p and preserves every RK tree through r+2. This
uses p-r-2 extra rows over the displayed count.

## 4. One genuinely mixed row obstructs simply adding delayed responses

This checks the actual two-slot construction at its **minimal** cap
P=r+2. There is no need to reproduce the full chart at r+3. Use
normalized RK stage weights Phi_t (before the symmetry division)
and take the mixed stage expression
    Dmix=(r+1)! Phi_[bullet,tall_(r+1)] - Phi_star_(r+3).           (8)
The second tree has r+2 leaf children. Their exact polynomial
models cancel in (8), so this is not a quadrature marginal test.

Let f_mix(x) be the actual cached layer-r chart of Dmix.
Its coefficients are O(gamma^3) as gamma tends to 0, for each
fixed subset of dimensionless Laguerre roots. Indeed the kernel
tableau scales as A(gamma)=gamma A(1): each T on an x/gamma
polynomial contributes a factor gamma, and the reconstruction
operations commute with that scaling of nodes and polynomials.
Thus a grade n stage profile scales as gamma^(n-1). The
chart at grade r+3 has degree <r, so its coefficients scale
by at least gamma^((r+2)-(r-1))=gamma^3. This holds even
outside the cap where exact chart agreement is not claimed.

At the fresh clock z the actual profile (8) is -z W_(r+1)(z).
In fact the child tall_(r+1) state weight differs from its
ideal primitive by W_(r+1)(z)/(r+1)!, exactly the grade-(r+1)
prestate error from (6). Multiplying by the leaf clock z
and subtracting the star gives the formula. In the second
response at clock c, the contribution to (8) is c mu_(r+1):
the star cancels between same clocks; the tall component of
the grade-(r+1) prestate change is mu_(r+1)/((r+1)r!).
The non-tall donor s cannot contribute to this coordinate.

Consequently the actual output moment of (8) is
    (ell-w ev_z) f_mix - w z W_(r+1)(z) + c mu_(r+1).             (9)
For the y-prestate baseline c=gamma, or any old physical
clock O(gamma), its limit is -M_0 z^2 != 0. Even for the
recycled fresh clock c=z it is nonzero: the recurrence and
centroid give mu_(r+1)-w W_(r+1)(z)=-a_r C, and
    C -> 1/[r(r+1)^2].
Thus (9) is
    -gamma^2 r/[(r+1)(r+2)] + O(gamma^3)
for c=z. Every later difference whose prestate displacement
is O(h^(r+2)) is invisible to (8): both its clock and its
size-(r+1) state child are unchanged. This includes the
scalar tail above, at any response weights. The particular
two-slot recipe therefore has exact ordinary order r+2 for
small gamma even after that tail. This is not a lower bound
on arbitrary reweighting or on receivers added with earlier
mixed prestates. In particular it does not rule out the
different three-clock route below.

## 5. Three staggered new rows: all ordinary trees through r+3

Use cap P=r+3. Choose three distinct clocks z_j, with
z_3=1-gamma and W_r(z_j) nonzero. Select output weights w_j
satisfying the three weighted moments
    sum_(j=1)^3 w_j z_j^i W_r(z_j) = M_i,    i=0,1,2.              (10)
We give a choice with nonzero w_j, rational for rational
gamma, in Section 6. Define
    C=mu_(r-1)-sum w_j W_(r-1)(z_j),
    K=N_(r-1)-sum w_j z_j W_(r-1)(z_j),
    A=mu_(r+1)/(r+1), A_x=N_(r+1)/(r+1).
Choose scalar amplitudes alpha_j,beta_j with alpha_1=0 and
    sum w_j alpha_j=A,       sum w_j z_j alpha_j=A_x,
    sum w_j beta_j=C/(r-1),  sum w_j z_j beta_j=K/(r-1).            (11)
In the formulas below V_j=alpha_j E+beta_j S, where E is
defined from the first new observation; V_1=beta_1 S is
already known and does not depend on E.

First consider the target output ell F_r+sum w_j D_j with
D_j = D(z_j,V_j) as in Section 2, before adding the final
prestate correction. It pays through r+2 exactly: the
first two moments of (10) pay its first defect and all
the next branch profile; (11) pays the remaining unary
factor in (6). It pays every branching-root term at r+3
as well. In detail (5)'s W_r Q_2 factor is paid by (10).
For the L(s) term in (5)-(7), the common moment of W_r
makes the remainder of x W_(r-1) reduce to the difference
K, yielding the required sum w_j z_j beta_j. For the
L(e) term, the three-term recurrence gives
    sum w_j z_j W_(r+1)(z_j) - a_r K = N_(r+1),
using (10) for x(x-b_r)W_r. This is exactly the required
sum w_j z_j alpha_j in (11). These equations exhaust the
branching-root terms of that grade; the argument is not
just about the star or tall rows.

Now the physical chronology, including the outstanding
unary row, is as follows.

1. Compute H_1=H((T F_r)(z_1)+beta_1 S) and
   D_1=H_1-F_r(z_1). Put E=D_1/W_r(z_1), whose lead is
   grade r+1 with vector e. This stage needs only old rows.
2. Compute H_2=H((T F_r)(z_2)+V_2), D_2=H_2-F_r(z_2).
   V_2 uses the old S and newly completed E.
3. Let
       Q_0=sum_(j=1)^2 w_j g(z_j) D_j,
       P_phys=(ell-sum_(j=1)^3 w_j ev_(z_j)) T(F_r-F_(r-1))
                                      -sum_(j=1)^3 w_j V_j + Q_0.
   These are physical row combinations available **before** H_3;
   g(z_3)=0, so Q_0 does not need D_3. Form
       H_3=H((T F_r)(z_3)+V_3+P_phys/w_3).
   Output y+ell F_r+sum w_j(H_j-F_r(z_j)).
   In particular this is one extra row at slot 3, not an auxiliary
   donor or a separate response stage.

Here are the exact checks of Step 3. First, Q_0 equals ell T E_r
through r+2, with no terms through r. Its grade-(r+1) vector is
e ell T W_r by (1) and (10). Its grade-(r+2) branch coefficient
is ell T((x-b_r+s_r)W_r), by (10) since g is linear. For the
remaining coefficients use (6):
    sum w_j g(z_j) alpha_j = z_3 A-A_x
        = ell T^2 W_r = ell T W_(r+1)/(r+1).
The symbol ell T^2 W_r here means ell(T(T W_r)); the second
moment identity follows from (1) applied to T W_r. This verifies
the Ue coefficient, including the already shared s_r term.
Likewise
    sum w_j g(z_j)[W_(r-1)(z_j)/(r-1)+beta_j]
                                 = ell T W_(r-1)/(r-1)
by (11) and the definitions of C,K. This is the US coefficient.
Thus all of the two-jet of Q_0 is established.

It follows directly that P_phys vanishes through r+1. The first
term at grade r is killed by the paid W_r moment, since
(F_r-F_(r-1))^[r]=e_r W_(r-1). At grade r+1 that term contributes
C s/(r-1), by (2) at layer r-1 after canceling the shared W_r
error of F_r; the additional W_r part under T is again killed
by (10). The other two terms contribute -A e-C s/(r-1) and
A e, respectively. Thus H_3's extra increment changes only the
grade-(r+3) unary vector (through the order in question).

To verify that this was exactly the remaining unary residual,
use the literal root Taylor identity. Through the paid cap
    pi_unary F_r = h U(T F_(r-1)+gamma F_r).
For the full layer this holds pointwise at its physical nodes,
then as polynomial charts: T F_(r-1) and F_r both have degree <r.
For the compressed layer it follows from equality of all paid
profiles to the full chart. The exact-flow version is
pi_unary F_*=h U(T F_*+gamma F_*), and the new receiver version
uses its known strict prestate plus gamma H_j. Hence the
unmodified output's only remaining unary grade-(r+3) term is
U times the grade-(r+2) part of
    (ell-sum w ev_z) T(F_r-F_(r-1)) + ell T E_r - sum w V.
The additional gamma times the output residual vanishes because
that output is already paid through r+2. This is precisely
U(P_phys^[r+2]), by the Q_0 two-jet above. The extra increment
P_phys/w_3, weighted by w_3, pays it in the actual physical row.

Thus ordinary order r+3 is obtained in
    floor((r+4)^2/4)-3
stages over the already selected-root field. These three rows
use predecessor data strictly in the order 1,2,3 given above.

## 6. Exact small-rational-diagonal gates and a scalar continuation

Use the following concrete calibration, which also yields a
direction whose first nonzero order is r+3 and whose tall-tree coefficient at that order is nonzero, without any additional old stage:
    N=N_(r+1)/mu_(r+1),
    z_1=(M_2-N M_1)/(M_1-N M_0), z_2=1/3, z_3=1-gamma.
Use the weights from (10). Let l be the affine polynomial with
    ell(W_r l)=mu_(r-1)/(r-1),
    ell(x W_r l)=N_(r-1)/(r-1).
Set
    alpha_j/W_r(z_j) = A(z_j-z_1)/(M_1-z_1 M_0),
    beta_j = W_r(z_j) l(z_j)-W_(r-1)(z_j)/(r-1).
The equations for alpha follow by the definition of z_1:
(M_2-z_1 M_1)/(M_1-z_1 M_0)=N. The equations for beta follow
from those for l. Thus (11) and alpha_1=0 hold.

Here no small-numeric trial is asserted or needed. As gamma -> 0,
M_i -> 1/(r+i+1), N -> (r+2)/(r+3), and the denominator
M_1-N M_0 has limit -1/[(r+1)(r+2)(r+3)]. From
W_j=x^j-gamma j^2 x^(j-1)+O(gamma^2) coefficientwise one obtains
    z_1=(2r+1) gamma+O(gamma^2).
For example the derivative of M_2-N M_1 at zero is
-(2r+1)/[(r+1)(r+2)(r+3)], and its value at zero is zero.
The other two clocks tend to 1/3 and 1. The three masses
m_j=w_j W_r(z_j) in (10) have nonzero limits: their
Lagrange numerators at (0,1/3,1) are respectively
M_2-M_1+(M_0-M_1)/3, M_2-M_1, M_2-M_1/3 at gamma=0.
The first is nonzero since (r+1)/(r+3)>1/3, the second
negative and the third positive. The 2-by-2 moment matrix
for l is invertible near gamma=0 by positivity of the
weight x^r there. The gates involving z_2,z_3 and W_r
are nonzero at gamma=0.

For the only subtler gate W_r(z_1), the rational function
z_1/gamma is nonconstant. Its limit at zero is 2r+1, whereas
at positive infinity its limit is 1. To verify the latter,
W_j(x)=j!(-gamma)^j(1-jx/gamma+O(gamma^-2)) on [0,1], so
the normalized time means are 1/2-j/(12gamma)+O(gamma^-2)
and the variance of W_r/M_0 tends to 1/12. Writing z_1 as
mean minus variance divided by the difference of the
next and current means gives z_1=gamma+O(1). Since
W_r(z_1)=gamma^r W_r|_(gamma=1)(z_1/gamma), it is not
identically zero. The finitely many rational-function
gates thus hold for **all sufficiently small positive
gamma**, after choosing a small enough interval. This
allows rational gamma and ensures all three new clocks are
in (0,1). The old Laguerre clocks can also be made interior
by the same finiteness argument. No additional root is
adjoined: z_j, w_j, alpha_j, beta_j and l are rational
in gamma, and P_phys uses only retained-field operations.

For scalar continuation, the normalization in (6) shows that
the jets of D_j/W_r(z_j) are equal at grade r+1 and affine
in z_j at grade r+2. This uses precisely the affine alpha
and l calibration above; the final P_phys change only
starts at grade r+3. Therefore a constant divided-difference
combination killing {1,x} in these three rows gives an
actual direction of onset >=r+3 and uses H_3 with nonzero
coefficient. Its tall at that grade is nonzero. Indeed the
strict matrix B=A_tableau-gamma Id has at most r+3 levels,
and only H_3 can carry B^(r+2)1. Ordinary order r+3 forces
b^T B^(r+2)1=ell T^(r+2)1, which tends to 1/(r+3)! at
gamma=0, hence is nonzero at small gamma. The divided
difference direction already kills all earlier tall moments,
so its grade-(r+3) tall in A_tableau^(r+2)1 equals its
nonzero B^(r+2)1 value. With that direction one new unary
response per further grade pays scalar order through p>=r+3
by triangular reweighting, without changing the ordinary
order already achieved.

In terms of desired ordinary auxiliary order q, the counts are
    two-slot, q=r+2:   floor((q+1)^2/4)-1+p-q,
    three-slot, q=r+3: floor((q+1)^2/4)-3+p-q,
where p>=q refers only to the optional scalar target. At p=q
both full ordinary counts are above all applicable verified
unrestricted p<=20 entries. For example p=20,q=13 gives
55 and 53 *starting* stages respectively; neither is an
ordinary order-20 tableau.

## 7. Scope and audit handoff

The shortest physical stop for the two-slot template is (8)-(9),
not a stability-function ceiling. For a broad retained-core
iteration of any kind, the depth-mode carrier rank from the
input seal also forces more than k new stages to gain k>=6
ordinary orders at small gamma. For depth r>=2 its count is
sum_(l<=floor((k-1)/2)) min(2l+1,r+k-2l,2k-4l-1).
At k=6 this is 7; thereafter every even-to-odd step increases
the last old block from 3 to 5 and adds a 1-block, every
odd-to-even step increases the last from 1 to 3, and all
other blocks are nondecreasing. This does not rule out a
different phase arrangement or more than k added rows.

Independent audit targets:
* Recheck the coefficient recurrence (2)-(5), especially the
  raw unary factorial division, and the r=2 squared-input
  term at the first mixed branching grade.
* Separate cap r+2 and cap r+3 from actual physical counts.
  Check every prestated row combination is from permitted
  predecessors; no virtual function evaluation is inserted.
* Recheck the q_3=0 adjoint receipt Q_0, the grade-(r+1)
  cancellation of P_phys, and the g-weighted r+2 unary terms.
* Check the rational gates and tall seed (not just a scalar
  order condition on the output), and both anchor cases in
  (9) without relying on unpaid old profiles.
* Preserve the distinction between ordinary order q and scalar
  p. No result assumes the undisclosed forests, solvers, or
  numerical stage proposals.
