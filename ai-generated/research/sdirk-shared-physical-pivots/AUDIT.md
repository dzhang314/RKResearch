## Four-excess two-clock realization and its optional even gate

The auxiliary rows are physically ordered \(x,y\), after all regular rows; their common diagonal and single internal strict tap give \(\nu D=(1-x,\gamma)=\nu(I-C_Y)\) when \(y=1-\gamma\). The length-one decoder is exactly affine interpolation of the actual row coordinates. At length two its errors are \(-W\) for \(CC\), \(W\) for \(BC\), \(R+W/2\) for \(CB\), and \(-R-W/2\) for \(BB\). For example, the second coordinate of \(\nu C_YD\) is \(y\gamma\), so the affine remainder has \(R(y)=y\gamma-(1-y^2)/2=-\gamma^2/2\). The \(BB\) relation follows from \(\nu D=\nu-\nu C_Y\), and uses no additional auxiliary row. The determinant of \(W,R\) at \(z,y\) is \(W(z)R(y)\ne0\) as long as \(z\) is distinct from both auxiliary labels. There are at least three regular sites in both parities, and their physical ordering can put any such \(z\), then \(y\), at the end. This uses the same two pivot rows for both signals, even though the auxiliary row at \(x\) cannot use \(y\) in its co-site difference.

For odd \(p=2m-1\), \(q=2m-5\) and \(\dim U\le m-2\), exactly the untouched earlier count bound. The residual moment functional weighted by \(y-X\) has nonsingular Gram matrix at \(y=1\), hence simple free Radau nodes persist with small positive \(\gamma\). Division by the full hull gives uniform quadrature through \(2m-2\). Its cardinal squares are exact there, proving positive weights. The degree-\(2m-1\) polynomial \((X-y)Q_{m-1}^2\) vanishes at the actual nodes and its nonzero Radau limit integral persists. For even \(p=2m\), \(q=2m-4\) and \(\dim U\le m-1\), again exactly the untouched count bound. The small positive rational subtraction at \(y\) gives a nonsingular residual Gaussian Gram system with simple interior nodes and positive weights by continuity from uniform Gaussian. Restoring the atom makes all \(m+1\) weights positive and pays through \(2m-1\); the next moment mismatch persists. The selected old monic basis has independent restriction on the sufficiently many distinct positive remaining sites in each case. This yields counts \(L(q)+m+2\) and \(L(q)+m+3\), respectively, exactly one below the prior three-auxiliary recipe with the same forward cutoff, regardless of whether it is a best method.

In the optional \(m\)-node even branch, the last \(y\)-entry and preceding \(z\)-entry of \(\Delta_W\) are fixed independently of \(G_{y,z}\), because \(W(y)=0\). The requirement that the two discrepancy rows lie on the annihilator line first forces \(\Delta_W(z)\zeta_y=\Delta_W(y)\zeta_z\). It is sufficient for those **specific rows**: scale \(\zeta\) for each row by its forced last value, use \(\beta_yR(y)\ne0\) to tune the preceding \(R\) entry, and solve all earlier columns by the same two-pivot invertible system. No injection or earlier coordinate rank is assumed in this optional branch beyond its stated actual annihilator-line hypothesis. Failure of this scalar equality rules out that sufficient adjoint payment, not every possibility of cancellations across full rooted outputs.
