## 1. Chronological elimination: exact inputs, output and class

Let \(R\) restrict to retained original labels and \(L\) restore the omitted coordinates by the rightmost relation pivots. For a relation through \(|t|\le p-1\), \(LR\Phi_t=\Phi_t\) is needed only on a child of an extension of total tree order at most \(p\). Restriction commutes with coordinatewise products, so the induction proving \(\Phi_t(RAL)=R\Phi_t(A)\) through order \(p\) needs no multiplicativity of \(L\). Root decomposition removes trivial bullet factors if necessary. The canonical output row is \(L^\top\mathbf b\), and its error through order \(p\) is exactly \(\mathbf b^\top(LR-I)\Phi_t\). This vanishes for all those orders if the relation also holds at order \(p\); otherwise it vanishes precisely when the remaining indicated output discrepancy vanishes (e.g. \(b_i=0\) for one deletion). Thus retaining all tree vectors and preserving the output are separate tests at the top order.

A restored omitted column \(i\) is replaced only by coefficients on earlier retained labels. Removed column \(i\) could have affected a retained row only at a later label. Hence no retained diagonal changes, and strict/lower triangularity, common retained diagonal and nonzero retained diagonal status are preserved. For multiple relations, reduced row echelon in reversed actual chronology (unit pivot and clearing other pivot columns) gives genuinely earlier retained coefficients in each restoration. The argument is exact over a commutative unital ring with this specified unit triangular system. It does not claim arbitrary ring null ranks yield such pivots; the field minimality corollary must not be silently ported.

With free weights over a field, descent through \(R E_{p-1}\) is equivalent to annihilating the actual kernel \(E_{p-1}\cap K^D\). Extension from this subspace to the retained field space is always possible. For a singleton and \(b_i\ne0\), this is exactly \(e_i\notin E_{p-1}\). This is an iff for preserving the original output on this retained tree array, not a characterization of every lower-stage tableau.

Over a ring, the descended functional must actually extend. A direct-summand certificate for \(R E_{p-1}\subset R^m\), or a verified extending row, suffices. Direct-summand status is not asserted necessary for extension of a *particular* functional. The split extension is independent of the unit pivot. Here is an exact paid-ring failure of kernel descent alone. Over \(\mathbb Z[1/2]\), let \(\ell\) be an odd prime and take
\[
 A=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&0\\\ell&0&0\\1/2&0&0\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad \mathbf b=(0,0,1),\qquad c=(0,\ell,1/2).
\]
This is actual order-two ERK, and \(q=e_3-e_1\) annihilates \(E_0\), with a unit rightmost pivot. Deleting row 3 leaves the two stage weights through order two unchanged. The kernel of restriction on \(E_1=\langle\mathbf1,c\rangle_R\) is zero, since the first two retained coordinates of \(\alpha\mathbf1+\beta c\) force \(\alpha=0,\ell\beta=0\). Yet the retained weights must obey \(\ell b_{*,2}=1/2\), impossible in this paid ring. Tensoring with the fraction field loses the obstruction. The fixed and canonical preservation proofs themselves need no module extension beyond the specified pivot divisions.

## 2. The finite two-sided cutoff and unchanged vs mixed contexts

With \(H=p-1\), relation through grade \(h\), and \(r=H-h-1\), write \(T=LR\), \(\widetilde A=AT\), \(\widetilde{\mathbf b}=T^\top\mathbf b\), \(\mathbf u=Ae_i\), and \(\rho_t=q\cdot\Phi_t/q_i\). The extension error is exactly \(\widetilde A\Delta_t-\mathbf u\rho_t\). Up to grade \(h+1\) no error exists. The context condition includes original \(b_i=0\), hence the updated output is exactly the same full \(\mathbf b\); the extinct column \(i\) in \(\widetilde A\) and zero weight make physical restriction equivalent. The irrelevant deleted row need not preserve its own SDIRK diagonal; only retained rows matter.

A direct source \(-\mathbf u\rho_t\) first exists at a child input of grade at least \(h+1\). Its embedding as an extension spends one grade, so any genuine root-to-output path including the final pairing with \(\mathbf u\) has context cost at most \(H-(h+1)=r\). If \(H<2(h+2)\), a product of two changed factors cannot occur at relevant grades; one branches along the single error and uses only original siblings. On transposition,
\[
 (AT)^\top z=N z-q(Nz)_i/q_i .
\]
Pulling the operations towards the source agrees step by step with the original contexts under the actual vanishing hypothesis at the next cost, and the final \(z\cdot\mathbf u=(Nz)_i\) has that included cost. This proves the stated conclusion, including updated weights, in the field and in a ring with the unit pivot.

Without the inequality, exact telescoping expands each product as a sum with just one chosen difference but may put updated siblings to its left. The same path costs hold if the original-\(N\) admissible context set includes **both kinds of** tree multipliers \(A,\widetilde A\), of the measured cost at most \(r\). This is a sufficient vanishing test. Dropping the mixed siblings outside the one-error range has not been proved. For actual grade-\(j\) reverse marks with \(h=p-1-j\) in the stated range, \(r=j-1\) and the condition is exactly \(2j<p+3\). A truncated word space is not enough without the stated containment of all relevant context discrepancies.

For the graph shortcut, newly redirected columns are earlier than \(i\), which an error originating at \(i\) cannot reach. Any strict actual error edge from a reached stage with label at least \(i\) is an original edge (or was deleted), and diagonal steps do not shorten strict distance. Coordinatewise multiplication does not relocate errors. Induction therefore gives stage error grade at least \(h+1\) plus its strict distance, and the possible direct weight correction if \(b_i\ne0\) has grade at least \(h+1\). This checks the exact strict inequality \(h+1+D_i>H\). In the stated range \(h<H\), a case with \(D_i=0\) cannot satisfy that inequality. These are single truncated-deletion tests; using them sequentially requires checking the competing relations/contexts on the updated tableau.

## 3. Finite promotion tests

The supported-character statement is correct by the two restricted kernel dimensions, or by comparing images of \(P_a(B)\subset P_a(M)\). The finite head example needs a genuine clock-invariant complement and distinct effective head labels, as stated. Off the target clock its head cannot cancel with the core, so one needs degree \(|H|-1\) for the literal isolating polynomial. A supported representing row for the character is not a supported vector carrying it.

Cayley–Hamilton proves the finite \((O,ON,\ldots,ON^{s-1})\) kernel is exactly the largest invariant subspace inside \(U_a\). Field row-append rank tests are equivalent to the character being nonzero on that kernel, or on its intersection with an eigenkernel. Given the actual quotient intertwining, the obstruction \((N-\nu I)q_0\bmod(N-\nu I)Q\) is exact. The repeated diagonal example is correct: \(u\) is an eigenvector and the quotient generator maps to a nontrivial Jordan companion; the whole zero-mass plane nevertheless annihilates all rooted values since every row sum is the same and the tree recurrence remains constant. Thus an invariant family can be safe without a split eigenline.

The full rooted space is an invariant unital coordinatewise algebra. Over a field, any genuine block-separating feature yields a normalized affine separator between a chosen block and another, and their product gives block indicators without needing one global generic feature. The recursive summed-row partition is exactly its partition: refining old blocks by the applied block indicators introduces only true feature distinctions, while stability gives an invariant block-constant algebra containing the whole tree algebra. At most \(s-1\) nontrivial refinements. All three finite criteria then check:
1. Paid indicator products have grade at most \((r-1)g\). The stipulated dimension gives equality with the within-block zero-mass space, whose annihilator is invariant and a coordinate algebra.
2. The stipulated actual supported saturation makes multiplication by each measured \(E_g\) vector preserve \(W\). This persists under its generated coordinate algebra, which is the full tree algebra if the measured partition is stable. Zero mass gives all tree annihilations.
3. Supported rank one makes \(E_{g+1}\) genuinely constant on \(S\). The measured external surjectivity then forces every external row contrast to vanish, since the full row sums agree; each total internal sum agrees as well. The resulting stable block is merged into its earliest physical representative by valid chronological relations.

Cayley–Hamilton and literal subgroup/membership implications make sense over rings, but rank comparisons, quotient generators and the finite interpolation steps require actual split matrices, unit determinants/differences, or direct certified memberships. A nonzero coordinate of \(q\) is not by itself cancelable over a ring; a unit coordinate suffices. Distinct clock labels alone need not give unit Vandermonde differences. For example, over \(\mathbb Z[1/2]\) the coordinate algebra generated by \((1,1)\) and \((0,\ell)\) distinguishes both indices but only contains pairs \((a,b)\) with \(b-a\in\ell R\); it does not contain both block indicators. Stable summed-row equivalence is safe over a ring when the block indicators are actually obtained; field rank never substitutes for them.

## 4. Exact nonlinear example and its limitations

The composite midpoint base has rows with diagonal \(\alpha_i/2\), each earlier column equal to its fraction, and \(b_i=\alpha_i\). Its first reverse identity is exact by summing the remaining fractions in a column. The algebraic symmetric threefold composition cancels the first odd local error, and symmetry rules out the following even local error by composing with negative step. It gives a characteristic-zero DIRK base of sufficient order with nonzero (not necessarily common) diagonals.

The probe recursion adds an affine term in its clock and a common-diagonal multiple; its clock polynomial degree is at most rooted grade. The Vandermonde vector therefore first sees the chosen bushy grade \(d-1\) with moment one. At a receiver the first linear dependence on \(\theta\) occurs in an extension of a grade-\((d-1)\) child, hence grade \(d\), and is independent of the common read, clock, and diagonal. A quadratic coefficient at grade \(2d\) can arise only from two grade-\(d\) factors, since an extension of a lower grade child is at most affine. This makes that coefficient identical in the two blocks. The second difference \(q=(1,-2,1)\) sees precisely twice this quadratic coefficient and is a true \(N\) eigenvector because both mass and first \(\theta\)-moment vanish. The single-block third-primitive cancellation is a separate diagnostic; the two-block witness uses its own first residual.

In the two-block array the actual order is at least \(p=2d+1\) by cancellation, and the displayed first reverse residual is a nonzero multiple of \(q_1\) because \(\eta_2=a_2,\eta_1\ne a_1\). The true \(q_1\) annihilates through order \(p-1\) and fails on the order-\(p\) rooted square of the specified extension. Its last pivot has weight \(\delta\). No retained row reads it, so all retained tree vectors stay unchanged beyond the horizon as well. The canonical \(L^\top\mathbf b\) leaves zero weight at both surviving receivers of block 1, and preserves the second block. Its exact output error is \(-\delta q_1\cdot\Phi_t\), equal to \(-2\delta\) at the witness. Thus the canonical substitution preserves through \(p-1\) and loses order \(p\); this is not a demonstration that all free weights fail. In fact setting all receiver weights to zero works, and \(q_1-q_2\) is itself a full through-\(p\) relation with a different (later) pivot. This also shows directly that \(e_i\notin E_{p-1}\) at the deleted first-block pivot, consistent with the field free-output criterion. If "fixed output" is used, specify that it is the prescribed chronological transfer \(L^\top b\), not a universal failure of finding new weights (or a proof about minimal stage counts). There is no new order bound or need to invoke ERK/strict SDIRK existence from this diagnostic.
