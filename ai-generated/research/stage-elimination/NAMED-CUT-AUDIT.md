The output has zero omitted weight and uses the old retained weight unchanged.

The exact difference identity is Delta_[t]=Ahat Delta_t−UQ Phi_t(A). Since Q kills original E_h, every discrepancy through degree h+1 vanishes. The first source consumes h+2 degrees. With delta=max(0,g−h−2) and columns U in E_g, the new tree values of degree k lie in E_(k+delta floor(k/(h+2))). At an extension this uses one degree for A; the correction either vanishes for a child through h or has grade g no larger than the proposed bound. At a rooted product it uses additivity of the old tree grade and superadditivity of the floor.

Telescoping each product gives linear sums of paths with one distinguished source at a time and possibly new values on siblings. A particular path has at most ell=H−h−2 ordinary budget left.
