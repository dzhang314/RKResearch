# Sharp leading quadratic delay with no ordinary visibility

For the illustration with three effective responders at one clock, [use the corrected labels and common diagonal](ERRATUM.md). The delay theorem itself is unchanged.

This is an exact counterdiagnostic to unrestricted visibility-based *restoration*, not an example of a globally stage-minimal method. It works in ERK, DIRK or SDIRK whenever an exact base in the chosen class is supplied. Fix integers \(c\ge2\), \(d\ge1\), and \(p=2c+d\). Work over a field of characteristic zero with enough distinct prescribed labels, and choose one allowed diagonal \(\gamma\) for the new rows. All rows below precede a disconnected exact order-\(p\) base whose output is kept.

Begin with one seed row of diagonal \(\gamma\). Add \(c-1\) unweighted probe rows, each with its own distinct clock, diagonal \(\gamma\), and only one strict feed, into the seed. The numerical value of each tree on the probe rows is the evaluation of a polynomial in their clocks of degree at most its tree degree: extension adds the affine strict feed times the fixed seed value plus \(\gamma\) times the old probe value, and root products add degrees. Take a probe covector \(\mathbf v\) with moments zero through degree \(c-3\) and moment one at degree \(c-2\), by the Vandermonde matrix. The range of required zeros is empty for \(c=2\).

Append unweighted rows \(j,i,i'\) in that order, all with diagonal \(\gamma\) and no mutual reads. Let \(j\) read a common old row \(\mathbf a\), and \(i,i'\) read \(\mathbf a+\mathbf v\). These two latter rows have identical numerical tree histories. Their histories match \(j\) through degree \(h=c-2\) and first differ from it on the extension of the bushy tree of degree \(c-2\): the clock-power moment of \(\mathbf v\) gives difference exactly one, and the equal smaller self terms cancel. This calculation follows inductively from the extension and product recurrences. Stage \(i\) is unread at the cut just after \(i'\).

The first receiver block consists of three rows with a common diagonal and common clock, labelled \(\theta=0,1,2\). Their only differing strict reads are \(\theta\) at \(i\) and \(-\theta\) at \(i'\); all other reads are common and avoid those two indices. Follow the first block with \(d-1\) three-row blocks. Each row reads only its equally labelled predecessor in the previous block with coefficient one, besides its own common diagonal. Give the last block the weights \((1,-2,1)\) and all other added rows zero weights. Every block has common original stage histories across its three labels, because the first contrast is identically zero and propagation respects equality. Thus its weighted output is identically zero at all orders; the method remains an actual order-\(p\) method by the original exact base.

A backward ordinary context cannot see \(i\) at any cost. Indeed, in each block its added part is a scalar multiple of the inherited three-entry contrast, because the original tree multipliers are constant on that block. Transposition of the ordinary strict feeds propagates that contrast back one block. From the first block it produces nothing in the prefix, because both its mass and its \(\theta\) moment vanish. The original exact base is disconnected, so its contexts also cannot see \(i\). Nevertheless, the shortest strict dependency distance \(d_i\) is exactly \(d\): nonzero direct feeds go precisely to the first block and then through every block.

Now delete \(i\) using \(j\) as its valid degree-\(h\) restoration, while retaining \(i'\). The first receiver block obtains an affine correction whose \(\theta\) coefficient is \(-1\) at degree \(c\), for the extension of the first differing child specified above. The rooted sum of two copies of this extension has weighted quadratic discrepancy two in the first block. The \(d-1\) unary extensions carry it to the output at degree \(2c+d-1\), with all linear terms zero and no earlier or additional quadratic term at this degree, by the antichain calculation. Equivalently the actual first-boundary weighted Gram of the strict response is \(0-2+4=2\) at that block. Hence the proposed restoration fails exactly at the predicted first nonlinear order, even though the ordinary weighted lag is infinite.

This example keeps the surviving diagonal class and has a same-clock effective three-row support whose aggregate transported weight is zero, so it also shows why the two-row aggregate exclusion cannot be generalized by clock aggregation alone. The original extra stages are redundant in the unconstrained problem: one may remove the added blocks and keep the base, or restore from the true duplicate rather than the merely low-order match. No claim about globally stage-minimal instances beyond the necessary conditional theorem is made.
