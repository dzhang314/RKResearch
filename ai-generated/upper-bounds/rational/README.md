# Rational witnesses

Examples at current stage counts: ERK orders 1–4; DIRK orders 1, 2, 4, 5, 6 and 7; and strict SDIRK orders 1 and 2. Quadratic radicals are unnecessary for the newer 7-stage order-7 DIRK witness. The order-20 rational midpoint extrapolation fixture below is a certified alternative, not the 42-stage best-known construction in this archive.

- [Even-order midpoint DIRK family over factorial-unit rings](../../research/dirk12-factorial-midpoint-21/MIDPOINT-EVEN-FAMILY.md): exact order 2r in r(r+1)/2 stages, with a unit next tall-tree defect and a matching lower bound only for disjoint constant-square midpoint prefixes.
- [Exact rational DIRK order twelve in 19 stages](../../research/dirk12-nineteen-rational-two-ports/README.md): two full physical row mergers and exact replays of all 7,813 conditions. This is the current rational upper, not a rational minimum or a factorial-only witness.
- [Exact rational DIRK order twelve in 20 stages](../../research/dirk12-twenty-rational-port-splice/README.md): a literal old-port and reflected suffix splice. Exact arithmetic checks every rooted condition and its next-order failure. It requires additional denominator primes; the 21-stage factorial-only result remains separate.
- [Exact rational DIRK order twelve in 21 stages](../../research/dirk12-factorial-midpoint-21/README.md): a literal independently replayed extrapolation tableau over every nonzero coefficient ring inverting 12!. This is not the unrestricted rational minimum.
- [Exact DIRK order ten in thirteen stages with only factorial denominators](../../research/dirk10-factorial-thirteen-and-fourteen/README.md): one literal genuine DIRK tableau applies to every nonzero commutative ring inverting 10!. The 12-stage rational method is still sharper where either of its denominators is a unit.
- [Exact rational DIRK order ten in twelve stages](../../research/dirk10-twelve-rational-three-ports/README.md): three literal whole-row merges, all 1,205 rooted conditions checked exactly, and a unit eleventh-order defect in the coefficient localization. The rational minimum is 12; the real minimum is 11. A [two-tableau portfolio](../../research/dirk10-twelve-field-portfolio/README.md) covers every admissible field and every nonzero local or finite-characteristic ring inverting 10!. Separate earlier [13-stage](../../research/dirk10-thirteen-rational-two-ports/README.md) and [14-stage](dirk-order-ten-overlap/README.md) certificates are retained.
- [Exact ERK order eight in 11 stages over factorial-unit rings](../../research/erk-eight-dispersed/README.md): a single Lean-checked tableau, matching the known ordered-field lower bound. The earlier [eleven-stage rational tableau](../../research/erk-eight-rank3-four/README.md) is distinct.
- [Exact strict SDIRK order seven in nine stages at all powers of 19](../../research/strict-sdirk7-quadratic/README.md): one quadratic construction gives the real quadratic and prime-power witnesses; no rational nine-stage claim.
- [Exact rational strict SDIRK order seven in 10 stages](../../research/strict-sdirk7/README.md): two Lean-checked tableaux cover all admissible local coefficient rings.
- [Exact rational strict SDIRK order thirteen in 35 stages](../../research/fixed-one-odd-continuation/README.md): the tableau and the symbolic stage-merge proof are Lean-checked.
- [Even-order head sharing for exact rational strict SDIRK](../../research/even-terminal-head-sharing/README.md): 28, 38, 49, 61, and 75 stages at orders 12, 14, 16, 18, and 20, with full Lean witnesses.
- [Exact rational strict SDIRK at orders 15, 17 and 19](../../research/fixed-one-higher-odd/README.md): 46, 58 and 72 physical stages, respectively, with Lean-checked symbolic receipts.

- [Exact rational strict SDIRK order eight in 15 stages](../../lean/evidence/reviewed-native-rational-sdirk-fifteen-stage-eight-20261125/README.md): the literal common-diagonal `1/8` tableau and exact order are Lean-checked. This is a rational upper witness, not a stage minimum.
